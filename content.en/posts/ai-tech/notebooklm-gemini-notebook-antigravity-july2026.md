## Core Summary

Google has officially renamed its AI knowledge management tool NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook, integrating Gemini 3.5 and the new Antigravity model into its AI Pro subscription plan. This brand overhaul and product upgrade signals Google's strategic direction of deeply integrating AI-assisted research with knowledge management, further strengthening its competitive position in the AI productivity tools market.

## Event Details

According to 9to5Google, The Verge, and TechCrunch, Google has officially renamed NotebookLM to Gemini Notebook. This renaming is not merely a brand adjustment but a strategic move to fully integrate the AI research tool into the Gemini product ecosystem.

Simultaneously, Google announced that Gemini Notebook will integrate the latest Gemini 3.5 model and the entirely new Antigravity model, with these upgrades available to AI Pro subscribers. Gemini 3.5 features significant improvements in reasoning capability and multimodal processing over its predecessor, while the Antigravity model focuses on deep knowledge analysis and cross-document relational reasoning.

Since its launch, NotebookLM has become an important tool for researchers, students, and professionals conducting knowledge management. The tool can analyze uploaded documents, generate summaries, extract key information, and build knowledge connections. This upgrade brings more powerful analytical capabilities and a smoother user experience.

## Panoramic Analysis

Google's brand overhaul and product upgrade reflects the deeper transformation occurring in the AI tools market. First, AI tools are evolving from standalone innovative products into parts of larger ecosystems. Incorporating NotebookLM into the Gemini brand system means Google is building a unified product matrix centered on AI, where users can seamlessly switch and share data between different tools.

From a competitive landscape perspective, Google's move directly responds to Microsoft's strong positioning in AI productivity tools. By deeply integrating AI into its Office and Edge product lines, Microsoft has already established a first-mover advantage in the enterprise market. Google needs to strengthen its Gemini ecosystem to compete for the knowledge worker market.

From a technology evolution perspective, the introduction of the Antigravity model is noteworthy. This model focused on knowledge analysis indicates that AI is moving from general conversation toward deep applications in vertical domains. In the future, we may see more specialized AI models optimized for specific scenarios.

## Perspectives

**Tech industry analysts** believe this renaming is an important step in clarifying Google's AI strategy. A unified brand helps users understand Google's AI product layout and reduces selection costs.

**Existing user groups** are excited about product upgrades but worry that renaming may bring feature adjustments or pricing changes. Some users hope Google will maintain NotebookLM's core functionality.

**Competitors** including Microsoft and OpenAI may accelerate iteration of their own knowledge management tools. Competition in the AI productivity tools market is entering a white-hot phase, with product differentiation becoming the key to winning.
