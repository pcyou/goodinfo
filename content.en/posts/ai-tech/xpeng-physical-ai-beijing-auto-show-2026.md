## 📰 Main Story

The 2026 Beijing International Automotive Exhibition continued on April 25, with XPENG emerging as one of the most compelling exhibitors at this year's show. Under the theme of "Physical AI," the company presented its comprehensive ecosystem spanning flying cars, humanoid robots, and autonomous driving technology, drawing significant attention from attendees and industry experts alike.

XPENG showcased its latest flying car products alongside multiple humanoid robot prototypes at the auto show. These products embody XPENG's strategic vision of transforming from a traditional automaker into a "Physical AI" company. The company stated that its Physical AI ecosystem aims to apply artificial intelligence technology to real-world physical interactions, spanning ground transportation, low-altitude flight, and intelligent robotics.

According to Stock Titan, nearly 100,000 consumers have already experienced XPENG's AI driving system, with a remarkable 98% satisfaction rate. This data reflects XPENG's leading position in autonomous driving technology and the market's strong recognition of its AI capabilities.

During the auto show, XPENG also demonstrated its fully self-developed AI technology architecture. The system integrates perception, decision-making, and execution modules, enabling a complete AI loop from environmental awareness to autonomous decision-making to precise execution. In autonomous driving, XPENG's AI system has already achieved full self-driving capabilities on urban roads.

This year's Beijing Auto Show is also regarded as an important platform for showcasing the AI transformation of China's automotive industry. According to Technology Org, driven by the Chinese government's policy push, China's car industry is integrating AI technology into every vehicle. From smart cabins to autonomous driving, from supply chain management to manufacturing, AI is reshaping the entire automotive landscape.

XPENG's "Physical AI" strategy not only demonstrates the innovation capabilities of Chinese tech companies in AI applications but also provides new perspectives on the future direction of the global automotive industry. The simultaneous debut of flying cars and humanoid robots signals that AI technology is rapidly moving from the virtual world into the physical realm.
