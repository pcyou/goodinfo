**[Core Summary]**
OpenAI has published a position paper on EU AI Act text-provenance rules, saying it will rely on watermarking and metadata to ensure that AI-generated text is identifiable in the EU market, and calling for cross-jurisdictional regulatory coordination to avoid fragmented compliance.

**[Event Details]**
- **Official Stance**: OpenAI stated in an official blog post that it supports the EU's identifiability requirement for AI-generated text and will gradually implement the relevant technical mechanisms across its product lineup.
- **Technical Means**: The plan emphasizes watermarking and metadata-based identification covering ChatGPT, APIs and third-party applications embedding its models.
- **Regulatory Call**: OpenAI called on EU regulators to converge on unified standards for text provenance markers, disclosure formats and enforcement mechanisms, to avoid fragmented compliance burden.
- **Industry Impact**: This plan could become an important reference for other major AI vendors deploying text-provenance mechanisms in the EU market.
