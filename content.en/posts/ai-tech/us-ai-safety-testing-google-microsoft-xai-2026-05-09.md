## [Flash] US to Safety Test New AI Models from Google, Microsoft, xAI

The US Commerce Department has reached agreements with Google, Microsoft, and xAI to conduct safety tests on their next-generation AI models. Building on the AI safety framework established during the Biden administration, the initiative aims to ensure frontier AI systems are safe and reliable. Analysts see this as a key milestone in the maturation of US AI governance.
