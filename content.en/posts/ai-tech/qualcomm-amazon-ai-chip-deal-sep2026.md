## Qualcomm Strikes AI Chip Deal With Amazon, Grants $4 Billion Stock Purchase Right

**[Core Summary]** US chip giant Qualcomm announced on September 8 an AI chip agreement with Amazon to provide custom AI inference chips for Amazon Web Services (AWS), used to accelerate generative AI workloads. As part of the deal, Amazon received an option to purchase approximately $4 billion in Qualcomm stock at a preset price. The deal marks Qualcomm's formal transformation from a smartphone-centric chip maker to an AI infrastructure supplier serving hyperscale data centers. Following the news, Qualcomm shares jumped about 10 percent in after-hours trading, adding tens of billions of dollars to its market capitalization in a single session.

### Latest Developments

**Core deal contents:** According to Qualcomm's official statement, the heart of the collaboration is the custom development of specialized AI inference chips for AWS, optimized for large language model inference, video generation, and recommendation systems. First chip samples are expected to be delivered in 2027, with mass deployment timelines to be adjusted based on partnership progress.

**Stock option terms:** As the non-cash consideration component of the deal, Amazon obtained an option to purchase approximately $4 billion in Qualcomm stock at a preset strike price. This arrangement means that if Qualcomm's share price rises during the option's validity period, Amazon will share in Qualcomm's market value appreciation. Qualcomm uses this structure to secure long-term commitment from Amazon on chip procurement without significantly diluting existing shareholder equity.

**Financial impact assessment:** According to analyst estimates cited by Reuters, the AI chip agreement could generate approximately $8 to $12 billion in incremental revenue for Qualcomm over the next three years. When combined with the potential capital inflow from the Amazon stock option, Qualcomm's overall financial position will be significantly enhanced. Market analysts believe the deal marks a fundamental shift in Qualcomm's strategic positioning in the AI chip field.

**Market reaction:** Following the announcement, Qualcomm shares rose rapidly by about 10 percent in after-hours trading, reflecting strong Wall Street endorsement of the deal. Shares of competitors NVIDIA and AMD saw modest pullbacks, suggesting the market views Qualcomm's entry as a challenge to the existing competitive landscape. Amazon shares remained relatively stable, reflecting market approval of AWS's diversified chip supply strategy.

**Strategic context:** Qualcomm's entry into the AI data center chip market represents a stark departure from its smartphone-centric business model of the past decade. While Qualcomm has been expanding in automotive, XR, and IoT chips, data centers have remained a relatively weak area of its portfolio. By securing an exclusive collaboration with the world's largest cloud provider, Qualcomm has gained an entry ticket into the hyperscale data center market in one move.

### Comprehensive Analysis

Qualcomm's AI chip collaboration with Amazon is far more than an ordinary commercial transaction. It is a critical node in the deep adjustment of the global AI computing competitive landscape.

From an industry chain perspective, the deal signals that the AI chip market is formally evolving from "NVIDIA dominance" toward "diversified supply." Over the past two years, NVIDIA GPUs have nearly monopolized the generative AI training and inference market. But as custom chips demonstrate increasing advantages in energy efficiency and cost control, hyperscale cloud providers are actively promoting chip supply diversification. Amazon has already reduced its NVIDIA dependence through self-developed Trainium and Inferentia chips, AMD MI300 adoption, and Anthropic investment. The Qualcomm deal further enriches its chip portfolio.

From a technology evolution perspective, custom AI inference chips represent the broader industry migration from "general-purpose computing" toward "specialized computing." Unlike the training phase's pursuit of extreme computing power, the inference phase emphasizes energy efficiency, latency control, and cost optimization, areas where Qualcomm has traditional strengths. Qualcomm's accumulated expertise in low-power mobile chip design and system-level optimization will become its core moat in the AI inference chip market.

From a geopolitical perspective, against the backdrop of escalating US chip export controls on China, the global AI computing supply chain is undergoing profound restructuring. US chip makers NVIDIA, AMD, and Qualcomm are all accelerating product portfolio adjustments, channeling more resources into data centers and AI-dedicated chips. The deal can be seen as an important signal of how US AI chip makers are redividing spheres of influence under the new landscape.

From a corporate strategy perspective, Qualcomm's transformation reflects the survival pressure facing traditional chip makers in the AI era. With smartphone market growth slowing and automotive chip business still being nurtured, AI data centers have become one of the few areas offering exponential growth opportunities. By deeply binding with hyperscale customers like Amazon, Qualcomm has managed to leap across the high entry barrier of the AI chip market, avoiding the risk of becoming a marginal participant in an NVIDIA-dominated market.

From an industry ecosystem perspective, AI chip diversification will drive broader application innovation. When computing costs continue to decline and inference efficiency improves significantly, AI applications will penetrate from leading internet companies to small and medium enterprises and traditional industries, triggering explosive growth in the AI application ecosystem. Such growth will in turn further expand AI chip demand, forming a positive feedback loop.

### Multi-Stakeholder Perspectives

**Qualcomm management** expresses strong confidence in the deal's outlook. Qualcomm's CEO stated in the announcement that the collaboration with Amazon is an important milestone in the company's AI strategy, marking a key step in its transformation from "mobile chip leader" to "edge computing and AI infrastructure leader." Qualcomm expects the deal to bring "exponential growth opportunities" to its data center business.

**Amazon AWS team** emphasizes the strategic value of diversification. AWS executives have stated on multiple public occasions that reducing dependence on a single chip supplier is the core of the company's long-term strategy. By introducing Qualcomm custom chips, self-developed Trainium and Inferentia, and AMD GPUs, AWS aims to build the most resilient and cost-competitive AI infrastructure portfolio.

**Wall Street analysts** generally view the deal positively for Qualcomm's strategic value. A Morgan Stanley analyst noted that the collaboration with Amazon signals Qualcomm's "successful opening of the door to the AI data center market," raising the target price by approximately 15 percent. A UBS analyst pointed out that the $4 billion stock option arrangement reflects Amazon's confidence in Qualcomm's long-term development.

**NVIDIA and competitors** remain alert to changes in the market landscape. Industry observers note that after Qualcomm's entry into the AI data center chip market, NVIDIA's pricing power and market share may face new pressure. However, some analysts believe that Qualcomm's initial focus on the inference market is differentiated from NVIDIA-dominated training market, with limited direct competition in the short term.

**Cloud service customers** welcome chip supply diversification. Multiple AWS customers revealed that they have long hoped AWS could offer more cost-effective AI computing options. The introduction of Qualcomm custom chips may further reduce AI inference costs, thereby promoting the deployment of generative AI applications across broader scenarios.

**Academic and policy researchers** focus on AI computing monopoly concerns. The head of Stanford's AI Index project noted that excessive concentration in the AI chip market may affect the inclusive development of AI technology. The entry of more players such as Qualcomm and AMD helps build a more competitive AI computing ecosystem, thereby lowering the overall cost threshold for society to use AI technology.
