Waymo has issued a recall for thousands of autonomous vehicles after reports that its system could drive into flooded roads during heavy rain. The recall, one of the largest in Waymo's history, affects robotaxis operating in multiple cities.

The company said its autonomous system failed to properly assess water depth in several incidents during heavy rainfall. Waymo has paused operations in affected areas and said a software update will address the issue.
