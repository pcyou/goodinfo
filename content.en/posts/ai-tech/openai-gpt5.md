## 📰 Full Story

OpenAI has officially released **GPT-5**, its most powerful AI system to date.

### Key Breakthroughs

- **Reasoning**: 40% improvement on MATH and GSM8K math benchmarks
- **Code Generation**: First to exceed 95% on HumanEval, capable of independent complex engineering projects
- **Multimodal Understanding**: Native support for images, audio, and video input without additional plugins
- **Context Window**: Expanded from 128K to 100,000 tokens, capable of processing entire books

### Industry Impact

Multiple tech companies have begun integrating GPT-5 into their API pipelines. Microsoft announced GPT-5 will be integrated into the Copilot product line, with a full rollout expected next month.

> "GPT-5 represents another significant step in our exploration toward artificial general intelligence." — OpenAI Technical Blog
