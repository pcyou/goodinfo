## Summary

MacRumors reported on September 12 that Apple recently confirmed that the iPhone 18 Pro Max sold in the US market has one hardware-level difference from the overseas version. The difference involves a specific connectivity module and antenna configuration, and Apple said the adjustment matches the network environment and frequency allocation strategy of US domestic carriers and does not affect the core performance and user experience of the device.

## Details

According to MacRumors, Apple confirmed in internal communications with developers and partners that the US-market version of the iPhone 18 Pro Max has specific antenna and connectivity module optimizations tailored to the frequency allocation strategies of major US carriers. Apple emphasized that the adjustment is a routine hardware customization designed to meet local communications compliance requirements, and does not affect the device's processor performance, imaging capabilities, battery life, or software feature experience, nor does it alter the key indicators in the consumer-facing official specifications. Industry analysts note that similar country-level hardware tweaks are not uncommon across Apple's recent flagship lines.
