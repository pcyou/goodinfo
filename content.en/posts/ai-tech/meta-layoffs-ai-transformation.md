## Meta Announces Massive Layoffs as Over 20,000 Face AI-Driven Restructuring

Meta announced on April 25 that it will lay off approximately 10% of its workforce, affecting more than 20,000 employees — the largest labor restructuring in the company's history as it aggressively pivots toward artificial intelligence.

According to The Wall Street Journal, the layoffs are driven by Meta's "relentless shift toward AI." CEO Mark Zuckerberg stated in an internal memo that the company will redirect more resources toward AI research and infrastructure development, rather than traditional business areas.

Affected employees span multiple departments, including content moderation, advertising sales, and infrastructure teams. Meanwhile, Meta is significantly expanding its AI engineering hiring, planning to add thousands of new positions related to artificial intelligence.

Business analysts note this trend is not unique to Meta. Microsoft simultaneously offered buyout packages to its staff, reflecting an industry-wide labor restructuring driven by AI adoption. CNBC analysis suggests this marks the moment when the "AI-driven labor crisis" has moved from theory to reality.

Meta employees face a stark choice: adapt quickly or seek employment elsewhere. According to Business Insider, some employees have been placed on performance improvement plans (PIPs), which insiders describe as a "slow-swinging axe" — a disguised layoff mechanism.

Labor rights organizations are calling for greater government oversight of such large-scale layoffs, ensuring affected workers receive adequate severance and reemployment support. Several members of Congress have also announced plans to hold hearings on AI's impact on the job market.
