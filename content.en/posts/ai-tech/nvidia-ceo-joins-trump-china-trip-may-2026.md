## Jensen Huang Joins Trump's China Delegation

Bloomberg reports that Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang joined President Trump's delegation to China as a last-minute addition. This arrangement highlights the central role of semiconductor export controls and AI chip trade in US-China relations.

### Chip Controls in Focus

Nvidia is the world's largest AI chip supplier, and its products are subject to strict US export controls to China. Huang's presence suggests the US may be seeking adjustments or dialogue space on chip export policy. China, as one of the world's largest AI application markets, has enormous demand for high-end chips.

### The Main Battlefield of Tech Competition

This visit comes as US-China technological competition continues to escalate. AI chips have become the core bargaining chip between the two countries. Previous export control measures from the Trump administration directly impacted Nvidia's revenue in the Chinese market, and Huang has publicly expressed concerns about the policies.

### Delegation Signal

Huang's last-minute addition also sends a signal: technology and trade are among the top priorities of this summit. Beyond chips, AI technology cooperation, data security, and cloud computing are also likely to be on the agenda.
