**[Core Summary]** According to The Mobile Report and CNET, T-Mobile, the third-largest U.S. wireless carrier, plans to automatically migrate over 8 million customers still on legacy plans to current, higher-priced plans. This means significant monthly bill increases for many long-term subscribers.

T-Mobile says the legacy plans are no longer maintained or supported, and the migration ensures customers receive the latest service features. Consumer rights organizations, however, criticize the move as a "disguised price hike," particularly impacting low-income and elderly users.
