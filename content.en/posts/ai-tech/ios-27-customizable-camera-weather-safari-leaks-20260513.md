## iOS 27 Leaks: Customizable Camera App, Weather and Safari Improvements

**Summary:** According to MacRumors, Apple's upcoming iOS 27 will allow users to set third-party camera apps as default, with improvements coming to the Weather app and Safari browser.

---

According to MacRumors, Apple plans to introduce several long-awaited features in the upcoming iOS 27.

The most notable change is the ability to customize the default camera app. Users will be able to choose a third-party camera app as their system default, meaning that launching the camera from the lock screen or Control Center will open the selected app instead of Apple's native camera.

The Weather app will receive more detailed meteorological data and an improved interface design. Safari will also see performance optimizations and new features, including improved tab management and reading mode.

iOS 27 will further refine the Liquid Glass design language, the new interface style Apple introduced at WWDC last year.
