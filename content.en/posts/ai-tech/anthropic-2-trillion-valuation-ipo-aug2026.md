## Core Summary

Anthropic is preparing for its initial public offering (IPO), with investor expectations valuing the company at $2 trillion—potentially breaking the record previously held by SpaceX. According to the Financial Times, Anthropic CFO Krishna Rao is leading early investor meetings, though formal valuation discussions have not yet taken place. This staggering valuation marks a new phase of capital exuberance in the AI industry.

## Event Details

### The Logic Behind the Skyrocketing Valuation

As a major competitor to OpenAI, Anthropic has rapidly risen through its Claude series of large language models. What does a $2 trillion valuation mean? This figure is equivalent to:
- The GDP of the United Kingdom, the world's fifth-largest economy
- Exceeding the current market capitalization of tech giants like Microsoft and Apple
- Nearly 10 times Anthropic's 2024 valuation

The core investment thesis centers on Anthropic being a leader in the "AI safety" track. Its Constitutional AI approach has gained favor with enterprise clients. As governments worldwide tighten AI regulation, Anthropic's "responsible AI" positioning has become a key differentiator.

### CFO Leading Investor Roadshow

According to CNBC, Anthropic CFO Krishna Rao is spearheading early investor meetings. Notably, formal valuation discussions have not yet occurred, suggesting final pricing may still have upside potential. Market rumors indicate that multiple sovereign wealth funds and hedge funds have expressed interest in subscribing.

### The Record Race with SpaceX

SpaceX's 2024 IPO set a historical record, but Anthropic may surpass it. MarketWatch analysis points out that capital markets are currently far more enthusiastic about AI infrastructure than the aerospace sector—this is the fundamental reason for the valuation difference.

## Panoramic Perspective

The phenomenon of Anthropic's $2 trillion valuation reflects deeper transformations in the AI industry. First, it marks the transition of AI from the "technology experimentation" phase to the "infrastructure investment" phase, similar to the cloud computing wave of the early 2000s. Second, the ultra-high valuation reflects market premium for the "AI safety" track—in a context of tightening regulation, companies that can provide explainable, controllable AI solutions are gaining capital favor.

However, this valuation also raises bubble concerns. A $2 trillion valuation means Anthropic needs to achieve compound annual growth rates of over 30% for the next 5-10 years—a tremendous challenge for any company. Historical experience shows that excessively high valuations often lead to corporate strategic myopia, prioritizing short-term performance over long-term technology accumulation.

From an industry landscape perspective, Anthropic's IPO will reshape the AI competitive landscape. If successfully listed, it will gain ample funding for computing power expansion and talent acquisition, further widening the gap with smaller AI companies. This could accelerate the "oligopolization" trend in the AI industry, forming a market dominated by a few giants.

Anthropic's IPO is not just a capital feast for one company, but a landmark event marking the development stage of the entire AI industry. The $2 trillion valuation is both the market's vote for AI's future and a prediction of the regulatory environment and competitive landscape. While investors chase AI dividends, they must also remain vigilant about risks from valuation bubbles and technological route uncertainties.
