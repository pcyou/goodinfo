## Core Summary

Electronic Arts (EA) has introduced AI-generated commentary from the late renowned commentator John Motson in its latest football game FC 27. This move has sparked widespread discussion about digital legacy, voice copyright, and AI ethics, while also demonstrating the application potential of generative AI in the entertainment industry.

## Event Details

EA Sports announced that through a licensing agreement with John Motson's family, FC 27 will use AI technology to recreate the iconic commentary style of this legendary football commentator. John Motson, who passed away in 2023, commented on over 1,000 football matches for the BBC, becoming famous for his distinctive sheepskin hat image and passionate commentary style.

The technology was trained on extensive historical recording data, capable of generating commentary content matching Motson's style based on real-time match situations. EA emphasized that all usage has received formal family authorization, with portions of proceeds donated to charities supported by Motson.

## Panoramic Perspective

This case represents the delicate balance between AI technology's role in digital legacy preservation and commercial application. On one hand, AI enables continuation of deceased celebrities' voices and styles, providing emotional connections for fans; on the other hand, it raises discussions about ethical boundaries of "digital resurrection." Who has the right to decide how deceased persons' digital images are used? Is family authorization sufficient? These questions lack clear legal frameworks.

From an industry perspective, competition in the sports gaming market is intense, with differentiated content being key to attracting users. By introducing legendary commentators, EA not only enhances game immersion but also creates marketing topics. However, this approach may face divided reactions from player communities: older fans may welcome this tribute method, while some users may feel uncomfortable about AI replacing real people.

Additionally, this provides reference paths for other entertainment companies. Music, film, and other industries similarly possess extensive classic IP and deceased artist resources, with AI technology potentially becoming a new tool for activating these assets. However, resulting copyright disputes and ethical controversies require the industry to establish norms in advance.

## Multiple Viewpoints

**EA Position**: EA Sports General Manager stated this move pays tribute to John Motson's legendary career, aiming to provide players with more authentic gaming experiences. The company promised strict compliance with licensing agreements and respect for the Motson family's wishes.

**Family Response**: The Motson family issued a statement supporting the project, considering it an appropriate way to commemorate John's legacy. They emphasized that the authorization process involved thorough communication, ensuring usage methods aligned with John's values during his lifetime.

**Player Community**: Social media reactions were polarized. Some fans praised this as an innovative and touching tribute method, while other users expressed concerns about AI replacing human commentators, believing this might set a bad precedent.

**Industry Observation**: Legal experts pointed out that countries vary in their protection levels for posthumous personality rights, and such cases may drive related legislative improvements. Meanwhile, labor unions have begun focusing on AI's impact on employment for creative workers like voice actors.
