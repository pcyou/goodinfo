## Anthropic Signs SpaceX Colossus 1 Data Center Deal Ahead of June IPO

AI company Anthropic announced it will use all compute capacity at SpaceX's Colossus 1 data center -- a major infrastructure move ahead of its planned June initial public offering (IPO).

### Deal Details

Anthropic's Claude AI models require massive computational power. This agreement with SpaceX ensures the company will have sufficient resources before going public.

Colossus 1 is SpaceX's large-scale data center focused on providing computing power for AI training and inference. The partnership highlights the intensifying competition for AI infrastructure across the tech industry.

### IPO Prospects

Anthropic's IPO is expected to be one of the biggest tech listings of the year. Market sentiment remains optimistic about the commercial potential of its Claude AI platform.

The data center deal is viewed as a strategic pre-IPO move, demonstrating to investors the company's commitment to securing its compute supply chain.

> AI infrastructure is becoming a core competitive advantage in the tech sector, with major companies racing to build capacity.
