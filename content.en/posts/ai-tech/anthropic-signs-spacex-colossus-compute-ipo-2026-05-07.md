## Anthropic Signs SpaceX Colossus 1 for Compute Capacity Ahead of June IPO

Anthropic, the maker of Claude AI, has signed a deal to use all compute capacity at SpaceX's Colossus 1 data center, marking one of the largest AI infrastructure agreements to date. The move comes as Anthropic prepares for a highly anticipated initial public offering expected in June. The partnership with SpaceX's massive AI supercluster underscores the intensifying competition for computational resources among leading AI companies. Colossus 1, powered by NVIDIA GPUs, is one of the world's largest AI training clusters. Analysts view the deal as a strategic positioning move ahead of Anthropic's IPO, signaling strong infrastructure readiness and growth potential. The AI company has been racing to close the gap with OpenAI and Google DeepMind in the rapidly evolving AI race.
