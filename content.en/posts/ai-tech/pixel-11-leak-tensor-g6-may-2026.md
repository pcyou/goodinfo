## Pixel 11 Specs Leak: Tensor G6 Details Exposed

According to 9to5Google, the latest specifications for Google upcoming Pixel 11 smartphone have been leaked.

The leak reveals that the Pixel 11 will be powered by the new Tensor G6 processor. Notably, the chip GPU architecture is not an entirely new design but rather an improved version of a GPU technology that first debuted approximately five years ago.

On the camera front, the Pixel 11 is expected to feature an entirely new camera hardware system, potentially continuing Google lead in computational photography. Specific upgrades include enhanced sensors and optical components.

Additionally, more configuration details for the Pixel 11 are emerging, including screen specifications, battery capacity, and possible design changes. Google is expected to officially unveil the phone this fall.

The performance of Tensor G6 will be a key factor in determining whether the Pixel 11 can stand out in the fiercely competitive flagship smartphone market.
