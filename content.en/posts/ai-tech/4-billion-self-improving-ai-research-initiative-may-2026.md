Leading AI researchers have joined a $4 billion initiative focused on developing self-improving artificial intelligence systems, the New York Times reports.

Self-improving AI remains one of the most controversial directions in AI research. Supporters argue it could lead to breakthrough advances, while critics warn of potential safety risks. The scale of investment is rare in AI research history.
