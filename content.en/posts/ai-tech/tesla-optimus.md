## 📰 Full Story

Tesla's **Optimus** humanoid robot has completed its first autonomous assembly task at the Fremont factory.

### Core Capabilities

- **Autonomous Navigation**: Moves independently in complex factory environments with millimeter-level obstacle avoidance
- **Precision Operations**: 11 degrees of freedom in each hand, capable of screw tightening and harness insertion
- **Visual Recognition**: Based on Tesla's proprietary vision system, real-time identification of parts and workstations
- **Continuous Learning**: Skill library expands daily through imitation learning and reinforcement learning

### Commercialization Timeline

Musk stated that Optimus is expected to begin external sales in 2027, initially targeting industrial scenarios.
