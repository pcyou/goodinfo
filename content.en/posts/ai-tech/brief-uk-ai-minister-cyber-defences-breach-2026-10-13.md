**Brief**

The UK's first minister for artificial intelligence, Kanishka Narayan, said on 12 October that Britain's cyber defences are "likely" to be breached, and announced that the government will deploy advanced AI tools to proactively detect threats and strengthen national cybersecurity.

Narayan framed AI-powered defence systems as essential infrastructure rather than optional tooling, especially against state-sponsored hackers and ransomware crews that have grown faster than traditional defences.
