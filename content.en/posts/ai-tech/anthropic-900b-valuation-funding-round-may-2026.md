## 📰 Body

Anthropic, the AI safety research company behind the Claude language model series, is reportedly negotiating a new funding round at a potential valuation exceeding $900 billion, according to multiple sources briefed on the matter. If completed, this would make Anthropic one of the most valuable private technology companies in the world, second only to a select few such as SpaceX.

### Record-Breaking AI Investment Wave

Sources told TechCrunch that the funding round "could happen within two weeks," suggesting negotiations have entered their final stages. A deal at a $900 billion-plus valuation would significantly surpass Anthropic's previous fundraising valuations, signaling sustained investor enthusiasm for AI infrastructure companies.

In recent years, funding volumes in the AI sector have repeatedly hit new highs. As large language models rapidly find commercial applications, investors are racing to back companies that could become core infrastructure for the next generation of technology platforms. Anthropic, with its Claude model series offering advantages in safety, long-context processing, and enterprise-grade capabilities, has emerged as one of OpenAI's primary competitors.

### Safety Alignment: Anthropic's Core Competency

Since its founding, Anthropic has made AI safety alignment its core mission. The company's Constitutional AI framework and training methods based on human feedback have been regarded as significant breakthroughs in large model safety research.

Previously, internal Anthropic documents were leaked, showcasing the evolutionary direction and capability boundaries of its flagship model, Opus 4.7. The document garnered over 900 upvotes on Hacker News, with 511 comments sparking heated discussion around AI model self-awareness and transparency issues — reflecting widespread industry attention to Anthropic's technical roadmap.

### Industry Landscape: A Two-Horse Race

The AI large model market has currently formed a "two-horse race" dynamic: OpenAI holds the lead in consumer markets with its GPT series and ChatGPT, while Anthropic has won over institutional clients with its Claude series and enterprise safety capabilities. The two companies are competing across valuation, technical approaches, and application scenarios.

Analysts note that if Anthropic successfully completes this funding round, it will gain ample capital to expand computing infrastructure, attract top AI research talent, and accelerate product commercialization — all critical for maintaining its position in an intensely competitive market.
