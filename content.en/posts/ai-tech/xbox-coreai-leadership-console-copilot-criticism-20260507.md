According to Forbes, Xbox's newly formed CoreAI leadership team has delivered unexpectedly sharp criticism of the existing Console Copilot feature.

The team argues that Console Copilot's design philosophy and user experience have fundamental flaws that fail to meet gamers' actual needs. This stance has drawn significant attention within Microsoft.

The CoreAI team advocates rethinking game AI design from a player-experience perspective rather than pure technology demonstration. This position contrasts sharply with Microsoft's previous AI strategy.

Analysts see this as reflecting the gaming industry's more pragmatic attitude toward AI applications.
