## Discord Major Service Disruption

Messaging platform Discord has suffered a widespread service disruption, with its API experiencing significant failures. Many users reported being unable to access servers or send messages.

Discord has acknowledged the issue and is working on a fix. The outage affected users globally, with numerous third-party applications and bots that rely on the Discord API also impacted.

This marks another notable service interruption for Discord in recent months, sparking discussion about the platform's infrastructure reliability.
