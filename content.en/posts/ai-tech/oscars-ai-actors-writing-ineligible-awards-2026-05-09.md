## [Flash] Oscars Announce AI Actors and Writing Cannot Win Awards

The Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences has issued new award eligibility rules, explicitly stating that AI-generated performances and writing are not eligible for Oscars. The policy aims to protect the central role of human creators in cinematic art.
