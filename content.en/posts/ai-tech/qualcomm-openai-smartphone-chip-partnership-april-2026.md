# Qualcomm Surges 13% on Report of OpenAI Partnership for Smartphone AI Chip

April 27, 2026 — Shares of Qualcomm surged more than 13% in pre-market trading following reports from CNBC and Reuters that the semiconductor giant is partnering with OpenAI to develop AI processor chips specifically designed for smartphones.

## Partnership Details

According to the reports, the collaboration will combine Qualcomm's hardware expertise in mobile processors with OpenAI's software capabilities in artificial intelligence, aiming to deliver powerful on-device AI inference capabilities for next-generation smartphones. Sources familiar with the matter said the custom chip will be designed to run OpenAI's large language models directly on smartphones, reducing reliance on cloud computing and thereby improving response speeds, lowering latency, and enhancing user privacy.

Notably, this partnership announcement coincides with OpenAI's restructuring of its relationship with Microsoft on the same day. Under the amended agreement, OpenAI gained the freedom to deploy products across cloud platforms, which may provide greater operational flexibility for its collaboration with Qualcomm on edge-device chip development.

## Market Reaction

Qualcomm's stock price climbed sharply following the news. Investing.com reported that pre-market gains reached as high as 13% before settling around 7%. The market reaction reflects strong investor optimism about Qualcomm's new opportunities in the AI smartphone chip segment.

Analysts noted that as AI capabilities become a core differentiator in smartphone competition, chipmakers are accelerating the integration of dedicated AI processing power. If confirmed, this move would give Qualcomm a significant advantage in the mobile AI chip race, directly challenging Apple's A-series chips and MediaTek's Dimensity lineup.

## Industry Context

The smartphone industry is currently at a critical juncture in its AI transformation. Major manufacturers are increasingly positioning on-device AI capabilities as a central selling point for flagship products. Features such as real-time translation, intelligent image processing, and personalized assistants all require robust local AI computing power. As one of the world's largest smartphone chip suppliers, Qualcomm's partnership with OpenAI would significantly enhance its competitiveness in the high-end mobile processor market.

The collaboration also reflects a broader industry trend: AI infrastructure is expanding from the cloud to the edge. With advances in model optimization and chip performance, running large-scale AI models directly on smartphones is transitioning from a technical vision to a commercial reality.
