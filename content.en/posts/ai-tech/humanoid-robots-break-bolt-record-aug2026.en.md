## Core Summary

Humanoid robots have broken Usain Bolt's world record in the 100-meter sprint, a milestone achievement marking a significant breakthrough in robotic athletic capability. Bolt's record stands at 9.58 seconds, and the robots successfully surpassed this time in recent tests, demonstrating the tremendous potential of advanced humanoid robot technology in athletic performance.

## Event Details

### Significance of the Technical Breakthrough

This achievement represents a major advance in robotics engineering. Modern bipedal robots must overcome complex challenges in balance control, power transmission, and real-time adjustment. The ability to surpass the human world record in short-distance sprinting indicates breakthroughs in:

- **Dynamic balance control**: Ability to maintain stability at high speeds
- **Power output efficiency**: Converting electrical energy to mechanical energy at new heights
- **Real-time decision systems**: Millisecond-level movement adjustments and posture control
