Google's May 2026 system update for Pixel 10 devices now prevents users from installing older Android versions.

The change is driven by security concerns — older systems may contain unpatched vulnerabilities. But for users who enjoy customization and flashing, the restriction has sparked debate about user control.

Google has been gradually tightening control over Android devices, including requiring apps to pass Play Protect verification. This update further limits users' ability to fully control their devices.
