Apple released iOS 26.5 on May 13, officially rolling out end-to-end encrypted RCS messaging for both iPhone and Android users.

## The Update

RCS end-to-end encrypted messaging begins rolling out today across iPhone and Android platforms, significantly enhancing cross-platform messaging security.

## Why It Matters

This marks the first time Apple has enabled encrypted messaging interoperability between iOS and Android, representing a notable step forward in cross-platform communication standards.
