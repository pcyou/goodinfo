The U.S. government has announced a $2 billion investment in IBM and other quantum computing companies to accelerate the development of quantum technologies. This marks a major federal push in the quantum sector, aimed at moving quantum computing from laboratory research to commercial applications. Quantum computing is viewed as a core battleground in next-generation technology competition, with potential far-reaching implications for cryptography, drug discovery, and artificial intelligence.
