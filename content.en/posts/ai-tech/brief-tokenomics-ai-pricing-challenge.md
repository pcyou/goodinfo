## Core Summary

The BBC recently published an in-depth analysis exploring the challenges of pricing AI services. The article highlights that buyers of AI services struggle to control costs, while sellers are uncertain about how much to charge. This "pricing puzzle" is becoming a key bottleneck constraining the scaled development of the AI industry, reflecting the sector's lag in business model innovation.
