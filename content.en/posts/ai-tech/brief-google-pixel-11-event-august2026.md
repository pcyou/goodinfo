# [Brief] Google Announces August 12 Event for Pixel 11 and Pixel Watch 5

Google has confirmed it will hold its annual hardware launch event on August 12, 2026, where the Pixel 11 smartphone series and Pixel Watch 5 smartwatch will be officially unveiled. According to 9to5Google, the event will also showcase Google's latest advances in AI and wearable technology.

Pixel 11 is expected to feature Google's latest Tensor G6 chip with significant improvements in AI processing and energy efficiency.
