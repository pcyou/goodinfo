## Amazon, Coinbase, and Stripe Build Payment Rails for AI Agents

Amazon (AWS) has announced a partnership with Coinbase and Stripe to build payment infrastructure for AI agents. This collaboration marks a significant shift from AI agents as experimental tools to autonomous economic participants.

According to CoinDesk, AWS's "AI wallet" will allow AI agents to purchase API services, web content, and other online services. Future versions will support more complex transaction scenarios such as hotel bookings.

This move aligns with CoinDesk's reporting that "DeFi is going mainstream." eToro CEO Yoni Assia stated at Consensus that DeFi and its underlying technology have already proven themselves at scale, and AI agents will become mainstream adopters of DeFi.

BNB Chain founder CZ also proposed that BNB Chain is the ideal payment rail for automated transactions between AI agents, hinting at a potential revival of Binance.US to give American users access to global crypto liquidity.

Currently, 20 banks and tech giants are queued to issue stablecoins through Anchorage Digital. Since the passage of the GENIUS Act, Anchorage has won every single large stablecoin issuance mandate.
