## Valve's Steam Machine Ships June 29 for $1,049

Valve has officially confirmed that its next-generation Steam Machine console will begin shipping on June 29, 2026, with a retail price of $1,049. The device runs SteamOS, a Linux-based operating system, and is designed to bring full PC gaming capabilities to the living room.

According to Ars Technica, the Steam Machine features a custom AMD processor and graphics card capable of running most AAA titles at 4K resolution. Unlike traditional closed consoles, the device offers full PC functionality — users can install any software, modify system settings, or even dual-boot alternative operating systems.

However, consumers should be prepared for limited initial availability. Valve has warned that supply chain constraints and production ramp-up may restrict first-month shipments, and recommends that interested buyers register on the official website to improve their chances of securing a unit.

The Steam Machine represents a significant milestone in Valve's "PC gaming in the living room" strategy, following the success of the Steam Deck handheld. If well-received, it could challenge the dominance of Sony PlayStation and Microsoft Xbox by offering an open alternative to closed console ecosystems.
