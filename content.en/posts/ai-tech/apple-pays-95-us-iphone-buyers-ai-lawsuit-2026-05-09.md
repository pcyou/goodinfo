## [Flash] Apple to Pay Up to $95 to Some US iPhone Buyers Over AI Lawsuit

Apple will pay up to $95 to some US iPhone buyers to settle a lawsuit filed last year. Plaintiffs alleged that Apple's advertising of Apple Intelligence misled consumers. This is another case of tech companies facing legal challenges over AI marketing claims.
