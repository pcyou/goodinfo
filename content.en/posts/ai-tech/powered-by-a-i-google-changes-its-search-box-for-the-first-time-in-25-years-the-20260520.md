## Google Changes Its Search Box for the First Time in 25 Years: AI Reshapes Search

The New York Times reports that Google is undertaking its biggest overhaul of the search box in 25 years, with AI technology set to fundamentally change how people use search.

The core of this transformation: search will no longer just return a list of links, but directly understand and answer user queries. Google is leveraging its latest AI models to enable the search box to handle more complex queries and provide structured answer summaries.

This shift marks the search industry's paradigm transition from "information retrieval" to "intelligent Q&A." For websites dependent on search traffic, this could mean a significant restructuring of their traffic patterns.
