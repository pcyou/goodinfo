## Sam Altman Takes the Stand in Musk's Lawsuit Against OpenAI

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman testified in Elon Musk's lawsuit against the company, according to CNBC. The case centers on OpenAI's transition to for-profit status and the commercial disputes between the two parties. It is one of the most closely watched legal battles in the tech industry.
