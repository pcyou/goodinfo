[Brief] BBC News reported on September 8 that Rene Haas, chief executive of UK chip design giant Arm Holding, has publicly stated that artificial intelligence will find a cure for cancer within our lifetime. Speaking at a technology summit in London, Haas pointed out that AI has demonstrated unprecedented capabilities in medical image analysis, drug target discovery, and large-scale genomic data processing, significantly accelerating cancer research and new drug development. He believes that AI-driven precision medicine will be one of the most transformative forces in healthcare over the next decade.
