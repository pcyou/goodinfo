## Cloudflare Kicks Off Stablecoin Wallet Rollout for AI Agents to Pay for APIs

**[Core Summary]** According to The Block, internet infrastructure giant Cloudflare has officially launched its stablecoin wallet deployment plan, allowing AI agents to use cryptocurrency to pay for APIs and online content. This innovative move marks the deep integration of Web3 payments with the AI economy, opening entirely new scenarios for machine-to-machine (M2M) payments.

### Event Details

Cloudflare announced that users can now claim Cloudflare wallet handles, while funding and payment features will arrive in subsequent phases. The wallet is built on mainstream stablecoins, aiming to provide seamless on-chain payment experiences for AI agents.

This initiative stems from Cloudflare's forward-looking layout for the AI agent economy. As AI agents increasingly call external APIs, purchase computing resources, and access paid content, traditional credit card payment models face challenges: AI agents cannot hold credit cards, and transaction fees for micro-payments are prohibitively high. Stablecoin wallets provide an elegant solution to this problem.

### Panoramic Perspective

Cloudflare's move represents an important step in the migration of internet payment infrastructure toward Web3. When AI agents become the internet's primary "users," payment systems must adapt to machine needs: automation, programmability, and support for micro-transactions. Stablecoins' instant settlement, low fees, and global accessibility perfectly match these requirements.

At a deeper level, this signals the formation of a monetization pathway for the "AI economy." AI agents are not just content consumers but may also become content producers. Through stablecoin wallets, AI agents can autonomously complete value exchanges, forming a closed-loop machine economy ecosystem. This poses potential challenges to traditional payment gateways and bank clearing systems.

From a technical architecture perspective, Cloudflare integrates stablecoin functionality into its edge computing network, ensuring low-latency and high-availability payment processing. This "Payment-as-a-Service" model may become a new growth engine for Cloudflare.

### Multiple Perspectives

**Crypto Industry View**: The industry broadly welcomes this development, seeing it as a landmark in stablecoins' transformation from speculative tools to practical payment media. Large-scale AI agent adoption will bring massive transaction demand to stablecoins.

**Traditional Payment Industry**: Payment giants like Visa and Mastercard have expressed support for multi-stablecoin coexistence strategies, showing traditional players are also actively positioning in this space.

**Regulatory Focus**: Policymakers are concerned about compliance issues with AI agents using crypto payments, particularly how anti-money laundering (AML) and Know Your Customer (KYC) requirements can be implemented in automated environments.
