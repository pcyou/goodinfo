Amazon AWS, Coinbase, and Stripe announced a partnership to develop payment infrastructure for AI agents. The rails would enable AI systems to autonomously execute micropayment transactions, providing underlying financial support for the AI agent economy. This marks the first time major tech companies have collaborated so deeply in the payments space.
