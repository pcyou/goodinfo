## Core Summary

Huawei has been found guilty of stealing US trade secrets in a federal court case, while being acquitted of more serious racketeering charges. This verdict marks another significant legal milestone in US-China tech competition and could have far-reaching implications for the global telecommunications equipment supply chain.

## Event Details

A US jury determined that Chinese tech giant Huawei was guilty of stealing robotic testing technology from American carrier T-Mobile. The case dates back to 2013, when Huawei engineers were accused of improperly obtaining technical details of "Tappy," a robot used by T-Mobile to test phone durability.

However, the jury acquitted Huawei on the more severe racketeering charge, which could have resulted in harsher criminal penalties and substantially higher fines.

## Panoramic Perspective

This verdict reflects the complex stance of the US judicial system when handling cases involving multinational tech corporations. On one hand, the court affirmed the importance of intellectual property protection by finding Huawei guilty of trade secret theft. On the other hand, refusing to elevate the case to organized crime-level racketeering charges demonstrates judicial caution regarding evidentiary standards.

From an industry impact perspective, this ruling may accelerate the fragmentation trend in the global telecom equipment market. The US and its allies may use this as further justification to restrict Huawei's participation in 5G network construction, while developing countries may continue choosing Huawei equipment based on cost considerations. This divergence will pose challenges to the standardization process of global communication infrastructure.

Furthermore, the verdict may prompt Chinese tech enterprises to place greater emphasis on overseas compliance systems, strengthening internal controls for intellectual property protection to avoid similar legal risks.

## Multiple Viewpoints

**US Position**: The Department of Justice stated that the verdict proves its determination to protect innovation achievements of US companies. Prosecutors emphasized that trade secret theft will be legally sanctioned regardless of corporate background.

**Huawei Response**: Huawei welcomed the acquittal on racketeering charges, viewing it as proof of the company's lawful operations. Regarding the trade secrets conviction, Huawei indicated it would continue pursuing legal avenues to defend its interests.

**Industry Observation**: Telecom analysts suggest this verdict could set precedent for similar cases, meaning other Chinese tech firms operating in the US will face stricter compliance scrutiny. It may also accelerate the "de-risking" process of global tech supply chains.

**International Reaction**: Some EU member states said they would closely monitor subsequent developments, assessing impacts on European telecom markets. Certain countries may reference this case's legal reasoning when formulating security standards for 5G suppliers.
