## Core Summary

Meta has suspended an internal program that tracked employees' computer usage to collect AI training data, after only two months of operation. The decision came following strong concerns raised by employees and privacy advocates.

## Overview

According to the BBC, Meta had begun tracking employees' computer activity records as a source for generating AI training data. The practice quickly triggered internal backlash, with workers concerned about privacy invasion and lack of transparency about how data was being used.

A Meta spokesperson said the company is reassessing the program's implementation and pledged to establish stronger privacy protections before resuming. The incident highlights the growing tension in the tech industry between AI data collection and personal privacy protection.
