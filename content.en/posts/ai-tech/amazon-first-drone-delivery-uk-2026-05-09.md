## [Flash] Amazon Delivers First Parcels by Drone in UK

Amazon has completed its first drone parcel deliveries in the UK. The company says it plans to gradually expand the service as demand for ultra-fast deliveries grows. Drone delivery is viewed as the future of logistics, with potential to significantly reduce delivery times and carbon emissions.
