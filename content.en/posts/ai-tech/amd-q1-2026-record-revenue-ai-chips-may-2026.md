## AMD Reports Record Q1 2026 Revenue

AMD reported record Q1 2026 revenue driven by surging demand for AI accelerators and data center processors. The company's MI series chips are gaining traction with large-scale customers as AMD continues to challenge Nvidia's market dominance.

CEO Lisa Su expressed confidence in long-term AI growth and announced plans for next-generation AI chip development. Analysts note AMD is steadily gaining market share in the AI chip space.
