**【Brief】**

Microsoft president Brad Smith publicly stated on October 12 that the artificial intelligence industry is currently less regulated than a carton of milk, and called on governments worldwide to establish unified and binding international rules to prevent regulatory arbitrage and a race to the bottom. His remarks reflect how major tech firms, against a backdrop of lagging legislation, hope that unified rules can reduce the uncertainty of global deployment.
