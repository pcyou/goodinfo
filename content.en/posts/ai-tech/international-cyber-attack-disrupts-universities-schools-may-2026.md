## Cyber Attack Targets Education Sector

An international cyber attack on Friday simultaneously disrupted IT systems at universities and schools across the United States and Europe, forcing teaching interruptions and paralyzing administrative operations.

### Scope of Impact

According to the BBC, the attack affected educational institutions in multiple countries, from research universities to local school districts. Many schools' email systems, online learning platforms, and administrative networks were forced offline.

In the United States, a large number of universities and school districts reported system outages. Educational institutions in several European countries faced similar problems, with the attack showing coordinated, cross-national characteristics.

### Attack Characteristics

Cybersecurity experts believe the attack was highly organized and coordinated, possibly carried out by a state-sponsored hacker group. The methods included ransomware encryption and distributed denial-of-service attacks.

### Response

Affected institutions are urgently restoring systems while notifying students and staff about security measures. Cybersecurity agencies in multiple countries have launched investigations to trace the attack's origin.

Education industry experts note that this incident highlights the weaknesses in cybersecurity protections within the education sector, calling for increased investment in educational cybersecurity.
