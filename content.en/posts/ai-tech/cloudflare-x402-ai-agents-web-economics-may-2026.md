## Cloudflare Proposes x402 Protocol to Address AI Agents Breaking Web Economics

CoinDesk reports that as AI agents increasingly access internet content, traditional web economic models are facing challenges. Cloudflare proposes the x402 protocol as a solution.

### The Problem

AI agents are consuming web resources at an unprecedented scale, often without compensating content creators. This imbalance threatens the sustainability of the open web.

### x402 Solution

The x402 protocol aims to establish a payment mechanism between AI agents and content creators, enabling micropayments when AI systems scrape and use web content.

The protocol marks a redesign of payment infrastructure for the AI era, potentially creating a new economic model for web content in the age of autonomous agents.
