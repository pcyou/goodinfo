## [Brief] Intel Appoints New Foundry EVP, Invests in Diamond Wafer Technology

Intel announced on June 22 the appointment of Seok-Hee Lee as Executive Vice President of Intel Foundry, reporting directly to CEO Lip-Bu Tan. Lee will lead advanced packaging, systems integration, and back-end technology development. Previously, Lee served as CEO of both SK On and SK Hynix. Separately, CEO Lip-Bu Tan revealed Intel has invested in a diamond wafer company, viewing synthetic diamond as a superior thermal conductivity material for chip cooling as advanced processes approach physical limits.
