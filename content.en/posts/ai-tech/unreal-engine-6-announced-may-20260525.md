## [Brief] Epic Games Unveils Unreal Engine 6 with Rocket League Demo

Epic Games officially announced Unreal Engine 6, accompanied by a Rocket League demo built on the new engine. This marks UE6's first public appearance, showcasing significant improvements in graphics rendering, physics simulation, and real-time lighting. According to Gematsu, the demo footage shows visual quality well beyond UE5, generating significant interest in the game development community.
