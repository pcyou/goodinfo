## Hut 8 Secures Massive AI Data Center Lease

Bitcoin mining company Hut 8 shares jumped over 30% after signing a long-term hyperscale AI data center lease built to NVIDIA specifications in Texas, with a total potential value of $9.8 billion. This marks Hut 8's significant pivot from crypto mining to AI infrastructure.
