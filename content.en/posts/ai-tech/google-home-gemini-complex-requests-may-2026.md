Google Home's Gemini AI assistant has been upgraded to handle more complex, multi-step requests. This update significantly improves smart home control capabilities.

Users can now execute more complex scene setups via voice commands, such as "When I leave home, turn off all lights, lower the temperature, and activate security mode." Gemini's new capabilities allow it to understand context and perform multiple operations in sequence.

This upgrade is an important step for Google in competing with Amazon Alexa and Apple HomeKit in the smart home space. Gemini's language understanding capabilities give it an advantage in complex command processing.
