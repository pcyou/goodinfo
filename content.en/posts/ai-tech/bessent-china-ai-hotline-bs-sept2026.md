## Core Summary

The U.S. and China have reportedly set up an AI hotline to exchange views on frontier models and regulatory standards. However, Treasury Secretary Bessent publicly stated that the technical details of the mechanism are 'BS', reflecting that the substantive differences between the two sides on AI issues remain hard to bridge.

## Event Details

According to disclosures, the proposed hotline would cover frontier model capability assessment, compute export controls, and AI ethics standards. However, Bessent, in recent congressional hearings and media interviews, argued that the relevant technical details are superficial and lack executability, and that the U.S. side will not be bound by such a mechanism.

Bessent's remarks continue the Trump administration's overall coolness toward the previous administration's multilateral dialogue mechanisms, while not ruling out maintaining some communication with China on strategic stability. Bilateral interaction on AI is expected to continue in a state where form outweighs substance.
