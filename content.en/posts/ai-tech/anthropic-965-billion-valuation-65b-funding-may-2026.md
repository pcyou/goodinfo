## Anthropic Reaches $965B Valuation After Raising $65 Billion

AI safety research company Anthropic has reached a valuation of $965 billion in its latest funding round, raising $65 billion in what stands as the largest single financing event in artificial intelligence history.

According to Reuters, the round was led by multiple Wall Street institutions, reflecting sustained investor enthusiasm for AI infrastructure. Anthropic's Claude large language model has been rapidly expanding in the enterprise market, positioning itself as OpenAI's most formidable competitor.

The funding will primarily go toward scaling compute infrastructure, advancing AI safety research, and accelerating Claude's deployment across enterprise applications. Anthropic has previously signed a partnership with SpaceX's Colossus supercomputing cluster to secure adequate computing capacity.

Wall Street analysts note that this valuation places Anthropic ahead of numerous legacy technology giants, making it one of the most valuable private tech companies in the United States. Market appetite for AI investments shows no signs of cooling, with big tech capital expenditure competition far from over.

Meanwhile, Anthropic is also accelerating its Agent ecosystem development. Claude's capabilities in autonomous task execution and multi-step reasoning continue to attract enterprise clients.
