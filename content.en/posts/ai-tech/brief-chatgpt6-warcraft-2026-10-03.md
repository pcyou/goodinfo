According to multiple tech outlets, OpenAI's latest model ChatGPT-6 Astra has cleared the World of Warcraft Classic orc starting zone 'blind' using only in-game text instructions within 40 minutes and without a single death, setting a new complex-task benchmark previously held by several research institutions. The agent did not rely on screenshot overlay or pre-set APIs but instead parsed the game's raw text stream directly, demonstrating strong learning and decision-making capabilities in complex virtual environments.

It is worth noting that this test is the first time the Astra agent has demonstrated autonomous planning for long-horizon game objectives, prioritizing quests, gear assignments and skill rotations without explicit prompting. The breakthrough is widely seen as an important marker of AI agents evolving from 'executable tools' to 'autonomous decision-making systems,' providing a fresh benchmark for game AI, reinforcement learning and human-computer interaction research. OpenAI says it will continue to optimize agent stability on long-horizon, multi-step tasks and will gradually open related capabilities to the developer community in the coming months.
