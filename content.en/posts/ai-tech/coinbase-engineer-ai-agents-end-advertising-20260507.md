## AI Agents Disrupt Ad Economy

Coinbase engineer Erik Reppel, creator of the x402 protocol, said the web economy depends heavily on advertising revenue from human users. AI agents bypass the ad ecosystem entirely, potentially upending the internet's dominant business model.
