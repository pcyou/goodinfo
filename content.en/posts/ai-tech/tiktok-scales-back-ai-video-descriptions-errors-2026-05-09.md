## [Flash] TikTok Scales Back AI Video Descriptions After Absurd Errors

TikTok is scaling back its AI-generated video descriptions feature after users widely shared and mocked the feature's bizarre and nonsensical outputs. The incident highlights ongoing reliability challenges in AI content generation for real-world applications.
