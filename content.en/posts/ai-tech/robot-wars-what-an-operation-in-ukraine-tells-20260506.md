## Robot wars - what an operation in Ukraine tells us about the battlefield of the near future

Ukrainian President Zelensky recently revealed that Ukrainian forces have successfully carried out a territorial recapture operation using only robots and drones, sparking global discussion about the future of unmanned warfare.

BBC analysis notes this operation demonstrates several key trends: autonomous drone swarms, ground-air robot coordination, and AI-powered target recognition.

Military experts say the Ukrainian battlefield has become the world first large-scale testing ground for unmanned combat. These combat experiences will be studied by militaries worldwide and shape future weapons development.
