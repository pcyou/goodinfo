## Executive Summary

Apple released iOS 27.2 on September 16, adding deep language support for Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan), Cantonese, Thai, Vietnamese and other Asian languages, with localized optimizations for regional accents and expression patterns. The update signals Apple's strategic push on AI localization.

## Key Details

Siri AI's recognition accuracy for Asian languages improved by roughly 25% over the previous version, with notable gains in noisy environments and mixed-language conversations. The on-device knowledge base was also expanded to handle regional queries on weather, maps, dining, and more. The update applies to iPhone 15 and later models.
