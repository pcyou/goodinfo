## Overview

AI safety and research company Anthropic is nearing a 1.5 billion dollar joint venture with several major Wall Street firms, according to people familiar with the matter.

## Deal Details

The deal would represent one of the largest investments in AI infrastructure by the financial sector to date. The joint venture is expected to focus on developing AI systems tailored for financial services applications.

## Industry Impact

Anthropic, known for its Claude AI assistant and focus on AI safety, has attracted significant attention from institutional investors. The partnership underscores the growing convergence of Wall Street capital and artificial intelligence technology as financial firms race to adopt AI across trading, risk management, and customer service.
