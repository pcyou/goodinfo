### NASA Webb Telescope Detects Biosignatures in Exoplanet Atmosphere

NASA's James Webb Space Telescope has detected dimethyl sulfide (DMS) in the atmosphere of exoplanet K2-18b for the first time. On Earth, this substance is produced exclusively by biological activity.
