## Supreme Court and the Midterm Elections

According to The Independent, US Supreme Court Chief Justice John Roberts has made several key rulings favoring the Trump administration's positions ahead of the midterm elections.

Analysts note that these decisions could significantly impact the upcoming midterm elections, providing legal support for Republican campaigns.

Supreme Court rulings remain a central topic of political debate in the United States, with ongoing discussions about the court's impartiality.
