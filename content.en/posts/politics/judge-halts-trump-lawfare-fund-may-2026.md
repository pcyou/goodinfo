## Judge Temporarily Halts Trump Administration 'Lawfare' Fund

A federal judge has temporarily blocked the Trump administration's "anti-weaponization" fund, described by critics as a "lawfare" fund, after a prosecutor involved in the Jan. 6 investigation filed a lawsuit.

The fund, established by the Trump administration's Department of Justice, was intended to investigate and respond to the "political weaponization" of the federal justice system. Critics argue the fund itself is politically motivated and could be used to target Trump's political opponents.

The ruling reflects ongoing battles within the U.S. justice system over the Trump administration's judicial policies.
