## [Flash] Amazon Drone Delivers First Parcels in UK

Amazon has completed its first commercial drone deliveries in the UK. The company plans to gradually expand the ultra-fast delivery service as demand grows. While currently limited in scope, the milestone marks drone logistics entering practical deployment.
