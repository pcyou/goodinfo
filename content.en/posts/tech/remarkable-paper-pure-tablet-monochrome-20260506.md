## reMarkable's new Paper Pure tablet goes back to basics with monochrome screen

reMarkable has launched the Paper Pure, a new e-ink tablet featuring a monochrome display that returns to basic digital note-taking. The device focuses on a pure writing and reading experience, stripping away complex features like color displays.

reMarkable is known for its focused e-ink writing devices, and this new product further strengthens its position in the digital notebook market.
