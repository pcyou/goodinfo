## Leaks Suggest Seven New Features Coming to iPhone 17

On May 5, 2026, a well-known Apple leaker revealed seven potential new features for the upcoming iPhone 17, though industry analysts caution that early-stage leaks frequently prove unreliable.

### Rumored Features

The leaks suggest the iPhone 17 may feature a thinner chassis, an improved front-facing camera system, faster charging capabilities, and new display technology. Some features may be exclusive to Pro models.

### Analyst Advice

Tech industry analysts note that months remain before the iPhone 17 launch, and Apple's development roadmap can change significantly during this period. Early leak accuracy rates are historically low.
