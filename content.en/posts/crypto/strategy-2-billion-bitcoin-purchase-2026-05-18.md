## Strategy Makes $2 Billion Bitcoin Purchase

Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) has made a massive bitcoin purchase worth approximately $2 billion last week, according to CoinDesk.

The move further solidifies Strategy's position as the largest publicly traded bitcoin holder. The company's continued accumulation comes as bitcoin recently dipped below $77,000.
