## [Brief] Coinbase's Base Blockchain Resumes After Two-Hour Outage

Coinbase's Base, one of the largest Ethereum layer-2 networks, experienced a roughly two-hour outage during which transaction processing was temporarily halted. The network has since resumed normal operations. Coinbase has not yet disclosed the detailed cause of the incident. The outage has raised questions about network reliability among users of the fast-growing platform.
