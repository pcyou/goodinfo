## Core Summary

The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) has announced it will hold a meeting this week to vote on proposing a new regulatory framework called "Reg Crypto," aimed at providing clear compliance pathways for certain types of digital asset offerings. This marks the SEC's first major crypto rulemaking process since its establishment, signaling a new phase in U.S. crypto asset regulation.

## Event Details

According to CoinDesk reporting, the SEC plans to formally introduce the Reg Crypto rule proposal at its public meeting this week. The rule aims to provide exemption from registration or streamlined registration procedures for digital asset offerings that meet specific conditions, while clarifying issuers' disclosure obligations and investor protection requirements.

This move is seen as the SEC's formal response to the growing market influence of the crypto industry. In recent years, as the market capitalization of crypto assets like Bitcoin and Ethereum has soared, along with continuous innovations in stablecoins and decentralized finance (DeFi), existing securities regulations have faced numerous applicability challenges. The proposal of Reg Crypto means the SEC is attempting to find balance between encouraging innovation and protecting investors.

Market observers point out that if Reg Crypto is passed, it will provide clearer legal certainty for crypto projects, potentially attracting more traditional financial institutions and institutional investors into the sector.

## Panoramic Perspective

The proposal of Reg Crypto reflects the evolving trend of global crypto regulatory landscapes. Over the past few years, U.S. regulation of crypto assets has relied primarily on enforcement actions and interpretations of existing securities laws, lacking specialized rule frameworks. This "regulation through enforcement" approach has drawn widespread industry criticism for creating excessive legal uncertainty and hindering compliant innovation.

From an international comparison perspective, the European Union has already passed the Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA), providing a unified regulatory framework for crypto assets. Financial centers like Singapore and Hong Kong have also established relatively clear crypto regulatory systems. If the U.S. launches Reg Crypto, it will help maintain its leading position in global crypto regulatory competition.

However, the specific details of Reg Crypto still face numerous controversies. Critics worry that overly lenient exemption conditions could provide opportunities for fraudulent projects; while overly strict compliance requirements could stifle innovation. The SEC needs to find the appropriate balance between the two.

Additionally, Reg Crypto's implementation will have spillover effects on global crypto markets. As the world's largest financial market, U.S. regulatory standards often serve as benchmarks for other countries. If Reg Crypto successfully lands, it may promote further coordination of global crypto regulatory standards.

## Multiple Perspectives

**SEC Commissioners**: Stated that Reg Crypto aims to provide clearer legal frameworks for market participants while protecting investors from fraud and manipulation.

**Crypto Industry Representatives**: Generally welcomed the SEC's rulemaking efforts but called for ensuring rules don't impose excessive compliance burdens, especially for small and medium-sized projects.

**Investor Protection Organizations**: Emphasized that any regulatory framework must prioritize investor protection, requiring strict information disclosure and anti-fraud measures.

**Traditional Financial Institutions**: Expressed anticipation for clearer regulatory environments, believing this will reduce legal and reputational risks of entering crypto markets.
