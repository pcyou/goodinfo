Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) reported an $8.2 billion loss in Q2, primarily because bitcoin prices at quarter-end were over 40% lower compared to Q2 2025. Despite the loss, the company continued to accumulate, increasing its bitcoin holdings by 11%.
