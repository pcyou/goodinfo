**[Summary]** Crypto prediction market platform Polymarket has filed applications with US regulators to offer regulated margin trading services, marking the platform's further push toward compliance with mainstream financial markets.

**[Event Details]** According to The Block, Polymarket is seeking approval to offer regulated margin trading within the United States. This means users may in the future be able to participate in prediction market contract trading through leverage. Polymarket has previously faced regulatory pressure over issues such as US user access restrictions, and this proactive application for margin trading credentials shows the platform is actively pursuing a compliance pathway.

**[Analysis]** Margin trading is common in traditional financial markets but remains a novel concept in the crypto and prediction market space. If approved, Polymarket's move would make it the first compliant platform to offer leveraged prediction market contracts in the US, potentially reshaping the competitive landscape of the industry. However, the high-risk nature of margin trading may also draw increased regulatory attention to retail investor protection.
