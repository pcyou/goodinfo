## Sequans Liquidates Bitcoin Holdings

Sequans sold 1,025 bitcoin, reducing its treasury reserves to 1,114 BTC. The company's Q1 revenue fell 24.8 percent to 6.1 million dollars, with proceeds used for debt redemption and share buybacks.
