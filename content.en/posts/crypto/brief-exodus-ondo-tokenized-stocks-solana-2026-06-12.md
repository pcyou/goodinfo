## Core Summary

Crypto wallet developer Exodus and tokenized finance platform Ondo have partnered to launch tokenized trading markets on the Solana blockchain, with over 200 traditional stocks and ETFs available initially. Users can trade these traditional financial products directly on-chain.

## Details

According to The Block, the partnership enables Exodus users to trade tokenized traditional financial assets directly within the wallet interface. Ondo provides the underlying tokenization infrastructure, converting traditional securities into digital tokens on Solana.
