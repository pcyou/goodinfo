## Core Summary

The European Central Bank (ECB) announced it has selected 36 payment providers to participate in a digital euro pilot project, including major financial institutions such as Deutsche Bank and Revolut. The pilot will begin in late 2027 and run for one year, testing the digital euro in online, offline, in-store, and e-commerce payments.

## Details

The ECB stated on July 14 that the pilot will test a beta version of the digital euro, with ECB and national central bank staff serving as the first users. This marks a substantive step for Europe in the central bank digital currency (CBDC) space. The pilot will cover multiple payment scenarios, aiming to assess the technical feasibility, user experience, and impact on existing payment systems of the digital euro.
