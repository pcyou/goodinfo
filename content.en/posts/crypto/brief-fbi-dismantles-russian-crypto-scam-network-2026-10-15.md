**[Core Summary]**
US federal prosecutors in Brooklyn, New York, working with the FBI, dismantled an investment scam network targeting Russian-speaking communities, seizing 112 servers and shutting down 241 related websites. The action is the latest major US enforcement operation against cross-border crypto fraud rings.

**[Event Details]**
- **Enforcement Scale**: The operation seized 112 servers, shut down 241 websites and brought federal charges against several core operators.
- **Victim Profile**: The network primarily targeted Russian-speaking users via social media and instant messaging, with victims spread across multiple regions.
- **Modus Operandi**: The ring lured victims with "high-yield crypto investment" pitches, paid early returns to build trust, then disappeared with funds.
- **Coordination**: The action was jointly carried out by federal prosecutors, the FBI and multiple local law enforcement agencies, showing continued strengthening of US cross-border enforcement against crypto fraud.

**[Panoramic Analysis]**
The action shows that crypto investment scams have evolved from early individual opportunistic cases toward organized, cross-border and technically sophisticated operations. Regulators are raising effectiveness through server seizures, domain takedowns and international cooperation; ordinary investors should be wary of projects that promise high returns, require rapid deposits, or restrict withdrawals.
