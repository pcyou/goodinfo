## Coinbase CLO Bullish on Stablecoin Legislation

Coinbase Chief Legal Officer Grewal said at the Consensus conference that stablecoin legislation "will pass this summer." He called for compromise on a stablecoin regulatory framework and urged industry participants to work constructively with regulators to advance the legislative process.
