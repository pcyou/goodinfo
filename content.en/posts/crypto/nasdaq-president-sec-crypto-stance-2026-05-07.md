## [Brief] Nasdaq President: SEC's New Crypto Stance Lets Markets "Build" Again

Nasdaq president Tal Cohen said the SEC's friendlier regulatory stance is giving crypto firms and exchanges room to experiment with tokenization and digital market infrastructure.

Cohen noted that the new regulatory environment allows market participants to resume innovation and building rather than stalling in regulatory uncertainty. This marks a significant shift in US crypto regulation from "enforcement-first" to "building-first."
