## Core Summary

Ethereum's core development team has officially confirmed the specific timeline for the Glamsterdam upgrade, marking the countdown to this highly anticipated network upgrade. The team also warned that some fake builders may drag down network performance and the upgrade process through low-quality protocol proposals, calling on the community to focus on substantive improvements that can truly advance the ecosystem.
