Nvidia's earnings beat and strong outlook boosted Bitcoin mining stocks amid the AI boom. An increasing number of miners are expanding into AI computing, leveraging their large-scale GPU infrastructure to provide AI inference and training services. The market views this transformation as enhancing miners' earnings diversification and reducing reliance on Bitcoin price volatility.
