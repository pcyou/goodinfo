## Core Summary

SoFi Technologies has partnered with Payward to connect its banking settlement network with Kraken's digital asset infrastructure. This collaboration marks a significant step toward integrating traditional banking systems with crypto exchanges, potentially improving efficiency and compliance in digital asset trading.

## Event Details

SoFi, a leading US fintech platform with a full banking license, will enable its customers to access Kraken's digital asset trading services more conveniently while enjoying bank-grade fund security. Kraken, as a major global crypto exchange, will expand its reach in compliant markets through this partnership.

## Panoramic Perspective

This partnership reflects the accelerating convergence of crypto and traditional finance. As regulatory frameworks become clearer, more traditional institutions are exploring collaboration models with crypto platforms, helping raise compliance standards while offering customers broader asset allocation choices.
