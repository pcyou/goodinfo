## Core Summary

Japanese financial giants SBI Holdings and Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group (SMFG) have announced a joint venture based on Solana blockchain technology to develop institutional-grade stablecoin payment infrastructure. This move marks deep integration of traditional financial institutions with public chain technology and could reshape cross-border payment dynamics in Asia.

## Event Details

SBI Holdings and SMFG have formally signed an agreement to establish a joint venture focused on Solana blockchain technology. The entity will work on building enterprise-oriented stablecoin payment solutions, leveraging Solana network's high throughput and low transaction cost advantages to provide more efficient underlying technical support for international trade and financial settlements.

According to the announcement, both parties will contribute resources and technical teams, with initial focus on developing compliant stablecoin issuance and clearing systems that meet Japan Financial Services Agency regulatory requirements. The platform is expected to enter pilot phase in the first half of 2027, with multinational corporations and financial institutions as首批服务对象.

## Panoramic Perspective

This collaboration represents a new stage in the convergence of traditional finance and blockchain technology. Previously, most banks maintained cautious attitudes toward public chains, preferring private or permissioned chain solutions. SBI and SMFG's direct selection of Solana, a high-performance public chain, demonstrates increasing recognition among mainstream financial institutions regarding public chain technology maturity.

Strategically, this move may trigger chain reactions in Asian financial markets. As the world's third-largest economy, Japan's leading financial institutions' demonstration effect may prompt banks in South Korea, Singapore, and other regions to follow similar strategies. Simultaneously, this could pose competitive pressure to existing cross-border payment networks like SWIFT, particularly in small and medium enterprise international trade settlement areas.

Furthermore, choosing Solana over Ethereum or other public chains reflects institutional users' pragmatic considerations regarding transaction speed and costs. Solana's capability of processing thousands of transactions per second, along with extremely low gas fees, provides clear advantages in high-frequency payment scenarios.

## Multiple Viewpoints

**SBI Position**: SBI Group Chairman Yoshitaka Kitao stated that blockchain technology will become a core component of next-generation financial infrastructure. Through cooperation with SMFG, SBI aims to establish a leading digital asset ecosystem in Japan.

**SMFG Response**: Sumitomo Mitsui Financial Group emphasized that this collaboration is a key step in its digital transformation strategy. The group will ensure all products strictly follow Japanese and international financial regulatory standards, providing customers with safe and reliable stablecoin services.

**Industry Analysis**: Blockchain research institutions pointed out that traditional financial institution entry may accelerate the standardization process of the stablecoin market. However, increased regulatory compliance costs may also limit innovation speed, requiring balance between security and efficiency.

**Regulatory Stance**: Japan's Financial Services Agency has previously released stablecoin regulatory frameworks, requiring issuers to hold sufficient reserve assets and undergo regular audits. Industry expectations suggest regulators will give more attention and support to projects led by such large financial institutions.
