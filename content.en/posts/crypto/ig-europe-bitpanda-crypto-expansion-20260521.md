UK trading platform IG Europe has partnered with crypto platform Bitpanda to offer cryptocurrency trading to its EU clients.

"Our clients want crypto exposure from a platform they trust. This partnership delivers it," said IG Europe's Managing Director. The move reflects growing traditional finance adoption of crypto services.

Bitpanda, headquartered in Austria, is one of Europe's leading crypto service providers with regulatory licenses across multiple EU member states.
