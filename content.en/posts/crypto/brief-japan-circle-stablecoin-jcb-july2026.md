## Core Summary

Japan's largest credit card network JCB has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with stablecoin issuer Circle to explore using USDC stablecoin for cross-border payments and merchant transactions. This marks an important step for Japan in integrating stablecoins into everyday commerce, potentially covering approximately 40 million merchants.

## Details

CoinDesk reported on July 14 that JCB and Circle will collaborate on research into how USDC can support cross-border payments and merchant transactions. As Japan accelerates efforts to bring stablecoins into everyday commerce, this partnership will provide Japanese merchants with more efficient and lower-cost payment solutions. The combination of JCB's extensive merchant network and Circle's technical expertise in stablecoins is expected to drive further adoption of stablecoins in the Asian market.
