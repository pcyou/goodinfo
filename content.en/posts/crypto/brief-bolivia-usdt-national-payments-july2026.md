## Core Summary

According to CoinDesk, Bolivia is considering adding Tether (USDT) to its national payments system. After the country lifted cryptocurrency restrictions in mid-2024, transaction volumes surged to $430 million. This move reflects Latin American nations' growing recognition of stablecoins' potential in cross-border payments and financial inclusion.
