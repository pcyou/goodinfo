## Core Summary

Strive purchased 1,110 bitcoin for $81.5 million, bringing total holdings to 21,356 BTC. This acquisition makes Strive the seventh-largest public company bitcoin holder, continuing the institutional accumulation strategy during market volatility.

## Market Context

Bitcoin has experienced significant price volatility recently, but institutional holdings continue to increase. Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) remains the largest corporate holder, with approximately 4% of the bitcoin supply cap, valued around $66 billion. Strive's purchase indicates that despite short-term market uncertainty, long-term bullish expectations remain strong.
