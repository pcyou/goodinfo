## [Brief] Pantera Capital: Tokenization Market in Early Stages

Pantera Capital stated in a new report that while the global tokenization market has reached $321 billion it remains in a very early development phase.

The firm compared the current stage to newspapers on the early internet suggesting massive growth potential ahead. The tokenization market saw 60 percent growth in 2025 showing continued institutional interest in blockchain-based asset digitization.

The report predicts tokenization will gradually cover traditional financial assets including treasury bonds real estate and private equity.
