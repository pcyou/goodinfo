## NYSE tokenization partners warn synthetic stock tokens could mislead retail

NYSE tokenization partners have warned that offshore synthetic stock tokens may not represent the underlying equity, with some projects using company names without approval and exploiting regulatory arbitrage.

As tokenized stock products proliferate, investors are urged to be cautious about the risk that these products may be disconnected from actual equity ownership.
