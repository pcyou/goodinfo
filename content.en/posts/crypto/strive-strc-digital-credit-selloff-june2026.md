## Core Summary

Digital credit markets experienced severe volatility Thursday. Strategy's preferred stocks STRC and SATA, designed to trade near 100 dollars par value, saw sharp declines. Strive CEO Matt Cole attributed the selloff to cascading forced liquidations from leveraged investors.

## Analysis

The event exposes systemic risks of leverage usage in crypto financial products. Forced liquidations can trigger price deviations far exceeding fundamental changes during short-term market volatility.
