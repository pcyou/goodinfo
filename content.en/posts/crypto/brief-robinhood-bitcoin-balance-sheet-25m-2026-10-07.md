**[Key Takeaways]**
The Block reported that Robinhood added approximately $25 million worth of Bitcoin to its corporate balance sheet, marking a strategic extension of its crypto business from a trading platform toward asset reserves.

**[Event Details]**
- **Scale**: The purchase totaled roughly $25 million in Bitcoin, the latest move in Robinhood's deepening crypto push.
- **Strategy**: Putting Bitcoin on the corporate balance sheet signals the company views crypto as a long-term strategic reserve rather than a short-term trading product.
- **Industry Trend**: Unlike bitcoin-native treasuries, Robinhood's move represents a traditional brokerage actively allocating crypto to its balance sheet, signaling accelerated convergence of crypto and mainstream finance.

**[Perspective]**
Robinhood's addition of Bitcoin to its corporate balance sheet reflects crypto assets being recognized as "stores of value" within the mainstream financial system. For the broader industry, traditional brokerages allocating bitcoin to their balance sheets marks another milestone in crypto's evolution from "alternative investment" toward "core asset." For the crypto market, sustained corporate buying provides long-term price support and reinforces institutional and retail conviction in long-term value; at the same time, regulatory uncertainty and the impact of crypto price volatility on corporate financials will be points to watch. The move may prompt peer brokerages to reassess their crypto strategies and accelerate overall industry deployment.
