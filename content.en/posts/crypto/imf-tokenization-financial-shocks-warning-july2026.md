## Core Summary

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) has released a report indicating that while tokenization technology could make financial services faster and cheaper, it also makes the financial system more susceptible to sudden shocks. This report has tempered enthusiasm around the tokenization trend and sparked in-depth discussion about financial stability.

## Event Details

According to CoinDesk, the International Monetary Fund conducted a comprehensive assessment of tokenization technology in its newly released report. The report acknowledges that tokenization can significantly improve financial transaction efficiency and reduce costs, but simultaneously warns that this technology may also amplify risk transmission within the financial system.

The IMF points out that the high liquidity and 24/7 trading characteristics of tokenized assets, while providing convenience for investors, also mean that market shocks can spread more quickly through the system. Additionally, the connection between tokenized assets and the traditional financial system may create new risk contagion channels.

The report specifically focuses on risks in key infrastructure such as stablecoins, decentralized finance (DeFi) protocols, and cross-chain bridges. The IMF recommends that regulators from various countries strengthen coordination and establish unified regulatory frameworks for tokenized assets.

## Panoramic Perspective

This IMF report reflects growing global regulatory attention to crypto assets and tokenization technology. As tokenization moves from concept to practical application, its potential systemic risks are becoming increasingly apparent.

From a technical perspective, tokenization indeed brings efficiency gains. Traditional financial transactions require multiple intermediaries and take days, while tokenization can enable near-instant settlement. However, this efficiency improvement also comes with new risks: smart contract vulnerabilities, oracle manipulation, and cross-chain asset security issues could all trigger chain reactions.

From a regulatory perspective, the IMF's call for a unified global regulatory framework is positive for long-term industry development. Clear regulatory rules can reduce uncertainty, attract more traditional financial institutions to participate, while protecting investor interests.

## Multiple Perspectives

**IMF Position**: The report emphasizes the need to balance innovation with stability. The IMF does not oppose tokenization technology but calls for establishing appropriate regulatory guardrails to prevent technological risks from evolving into systemic financial crises.

**Crypto Industry Reaction**: Some industry participants believe the IMF is overly cautious, arguing that tokenization risks are overstated. They point out that traditional financial systems also have risk transmission problems, and blockchain transparency actually aids risk management.

**Regulatory Attitude**: Regulators from various countries responded positively to the IMF report. Officials from the EU, US, Singapore, and other regions indicated they would consider adding special provisions for tokenized assets to existing frameworks.

**Academic Perspective**: Financial scholars note that the IMF report raises important questions, but specific impacts require more empirical research. They believe tokenization's impact on financial stability depends on specific implementation methods and regulatory environments.
