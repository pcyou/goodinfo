## Summary

According to The Block, Bitcoin ETF weekly trading volume has dropped to its lowest level since October 2024. Meanwhile, ether funds have continued to attract capital inflows over the past three weeks, nearly matching Bitcoin ETF inflows despite significantly lower assets under management. This trend indicates a notable shift in crypto market investor capital preferences.
