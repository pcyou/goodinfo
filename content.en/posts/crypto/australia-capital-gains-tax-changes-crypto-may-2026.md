## Australia Tax Reform to Impact Crypto Holders

The Australian government plans to replace a 50% capital gains tax discount on assets held for more than one year, according to the Australian Financial Review.

This change will directly affect crypto investors' tax burden. Crypto assets held for over a year currently benefit from the 50% CGT discount, which would be eliminated under the proposed policy.
