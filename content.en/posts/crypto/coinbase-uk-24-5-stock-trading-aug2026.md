## Core Summary

Cryptocurrency exchange Coinbase announced on August 6 the launch of 24/5 US stock trading for UK users. Coinbase UK CEO Keith Grose stated the launch is part of the exchange's broader strategy to bridge traditional and onchain finance. This move makes Coinbase the first crypto exchange to offer around-the-clock US stock trading, representing a major advancement in its "Everything Exchange" strategy.

## Event Details

According to The Block, Coinbase UK's new stock trading service features trading available 5 days a week (Monday through Friday), 24 hours a day, covering regular US market hours as well as pre-market and after-hours sessions. UK users can now directly buy and sell shares of US-listed companies through the Coinbase platform without opening a separate traditional brokerage account.

Coinbase UK CEO Keith Grose stated in an announcement that this launch is a key component of the company's "Everything Exchange" strategy. "Our goal is to bridge the gap between traditional and onchain finance," Grose said. "Users should be able to manage all their assets on a single platform, whether crypto or traditional stocks."

The backdrop for this move is the increasing convergence of crypto and traditional finance. In recent years, multiple crypto exchanges have begun offering traditional financial products, while traditional institutions have gradually embraced crypto assets. Coinbase's new US stock trading service represents the latest attempt by a crypto platform to transform into a comprehensive financial services provider.

Notably, Coinbase's choice of the UK as the launch market is deliberate. The UK's Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) recently introduced a new crypto regulatory framework, providing clearer compliance pathways for crypto companies. Coinbase has obtained relevant FCA licenses, enabling it to legally offer such comprehensive financial services in the UK.

## Panoramic Analysis

Coinbase's move has landmark significance for the convergence of crypto and traditional finance. First, it blurs the boundaries between crypto exchanges and traditional brokerages. When users can trade both Bitcoin and Apple stock on the same platform, the definition of "crypto exchange" needs re-examination. This may trigger regulatory reclassification and could spawn new forms of financial services.

Second, the 24/5 trading model challenges traditional market time constraints. US stock markets traditionally operate from 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM Eastern Time, while Coinbase's 24/5 offering means users can respond to market developments at any time. This flexibility may attract more retail investors but could also increase market volatility.

From a competitive standpoint, this move may prompt other crypto exchanges to follow suit. If Coinbase's "Everything Exchange" model succeeds, competitors like Binance and Kraken may be forced to launch similar services or lose market share. This would accelerate the convergence of crypto and traditional finance industries.

However, this model faces challenges. First is regulatory risk, as different countries have varying attitudes toward crypto platforms offering traditional financial services. Second is operational complexity, as managing both crypto assets and traditional stocks requires different custody, clearing, and compliance systems. Coinbase's ability to effectively integrate these systems will determine its strategy's success.

## Multiple Perspectives

**Coinbase Official Position**: The company emphasizes this is "user demand-driven." "Our users have been asking if they can trade stocks on Coinbase," Grose said. "We heard them and are committed to providing the most comprehensive asset management experience."

**Traditional Brokerage Response**: Some traditional brokerages are cautious. "Crypto platforms offering stock trading face numerous compliance and operational challenges," one UK brokerage executive said. "We doubt they can meet the same regulatory standards as traditional brokerages."

**Regulatory Perspective**: An FCA spokesperson said they would closely monitor the development of such comprehensive financial services. "Our goal is to protect consumers while promoting innovation," the spokesperson said. "Any company offering comprehensive financial services must comply with relevant regulations."

**User Feedback**: Early users have welcomed the move. "Being able to manage my crypto assets and stock investments on the same platform is very convenient," one Coinbase UK user said. "I hope to trade more asset classes in the future."
