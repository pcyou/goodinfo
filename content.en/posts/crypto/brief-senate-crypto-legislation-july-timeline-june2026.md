## [Brief] Senate Races to Pass Crypto Legislation in July as Timeline Narrows

A comprehensive cryptocurrency market structure bill faces a narrowing path to passage in the U.S. Senate. Pressure is mounting to advance the legislation next month, but turmoil surrounding a housing bill threatens the original timeline. The White House had previously set July 4 as a target date for passage. Industry observers say passing the bill before summer recess would provide long-awaited regulatory clarity for the U.S. crypto sector.
