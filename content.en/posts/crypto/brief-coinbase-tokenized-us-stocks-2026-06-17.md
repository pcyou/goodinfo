## Core Summary

Crypto exchange Coinbase has officially launched tokenized US stock trading. All tokenized shares are backed 1:1 by actual stocks, with holders entitled to the same dividend distributions as traditional shareholders. The move places Coinbase in competition with Robinhood, Kraken, and several traditional financial institutions in the tokenized assets space.
