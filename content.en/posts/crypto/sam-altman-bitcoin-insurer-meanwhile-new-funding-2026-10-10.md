**[Key Points]**

Meanwhile, the Bitcoin-denominated life insurer backed by OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, has closed a new funding round amid a marked increase in international demand for Bitcoin-denominated life-insurance products. The fresh capital will support the company's compliance build-out and distribution expansion in the US, Europe and Asia.

**[Details]**

- **Funding update**: Meanwhile has closed a new funding round; the exact amount was not disclosed, but the company's valuation rose materially versus its previous round.
- **Business model**: Meanwhile offers life-insurance products denominated in Bitcoin, with both policy value and claims paid out in Bitcoin.
- **Demand driver**: Sustained international demand for Bitcoin-native insurance against a backdrop of broader macro volatility is the main driver of the round.
- **Use of proceeds**: The fresh capital will be deployed primarily to expand compliance teams, broaden licensing coverage and build distribution channels in Asian markets.
- **Sector context**: The round fits the recent pattern of institutions accelerating into crypto-native financial services and points to a deeper integration of traditional insurance with crypto finance.
