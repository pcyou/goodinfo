Crypto exchange Binance announced on October 4 that new rules for Brazil-based crypto transfer flows will take effect on November 1, 2026. Binance said the changes respond to recent compliance guidance from Brazil's central bank, including stricter KYC identity verification, adjusted daily transfer limits, and tightened suspicious transaction monitoring.

Binance Brazil's country lead said in the announcement that the new rules are part of localizing Binance's global compliance framework, and pledged to continue communication with the Brazilian central bank and securities regulator for compliant operations.
