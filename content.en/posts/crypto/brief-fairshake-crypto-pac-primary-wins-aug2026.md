## [Brief] Crypto PAC Fairshake Scores Primary Wins in Michigan and Washington

**[Core Summary]** Fairshake, the largest Political Action Committee (PAC) in the US crypto industry, achieved important victories in the August 4 primaries, with its backed candidates successfully securing nominations in Michigan and Washington. This result demonstrates the crypto industry's growing influence in US politics, laying the foundation for advancing friendly regulatory policies in the future.

### Event Details

According to The Block, Fairshake PAC invested heavily in supporting pro-crypto candidates in this round of primaries, ultimately achieving victories in multiple key districts. The organization is funded by crypto giants such as Coinbase and Ripple, aiming to elect members of Congress who are open to the crypto industry.

The victories in Michigan and Washington are particularly critical. Michigan, as a swing state, holds its federal Senate seat crucial for congressional control; Washington is a tech industry hub, and its lawmakers wield significant influence over tech and crypto regulatory policy.

### Panoramic Perspective

Fairshake's success reflects the trend of the crypto industry moving from the margins to the mainstream of American politics. Over the past few years, the crypto industry has undergone a transformation from "wild growth" to "active political participation." Facing the tough stance of regulatory bodies like the SEC, the industry has realized that technology and markets alone cannot resolve regulatory issues—it must influence policy through political lobbying and electoral impact.

This trend could reshape the US crypto regulatory landscape. As more pro-crypto lawmakers enter Congress, the industry may push for clearer and more friendly regulatory frameworks. However, this also raises concerns about "money politics," with critics arguing that crypto giants influencing regulation through political donations could harm investor protection. In the future, the game between the crypto industry and regulators will become increasingly complex.
