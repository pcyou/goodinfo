## Key Facts

- **Product mechanism**: Ondo's tokenized stock product allows investors to hold digital tokens representing actual Anthropic shares. These tokens are issued and traded on blockchain but backed by real stocks held in custody at qualified financial institutions.
- **Regulatory compliance**: To ensure regulatory compliance, Ondo will partner with licensed brokers and custodians to guarantee proper custody and ownership records of underlying stocks. Token issuance and transfers will follow securities law requirements, open only to qualified investors with necessary identity verification and anti-money laundering procedures.
- **Strategic timing**: Launching on Anthropic's IPO day carries strategic significance. IPOs typically generate substantial market attention and trading activity, allowing maximum exposure and early adopter attraction. As a leading AI research company, Anthropic's listing itself draws significant attention, providing an ideal test scenario for Ondo's product.
- **Technical infrastructure**: Ondo plans to operate the token on its existing blockchain infrastructure, potentially using Ethereum or other compatible smart contract platforms. This leverages blockchain's transparency, programmability, and global accessibility advantages while automating processes like dividend distribution and corporate actions through smart contracts.

## Event Details

Blockchain finance platform Ondo Finance announced plans to launch tokenized products based on Anthropic stock on the same day the artificial intelligence startup conducts its initial public offering. This initiative aims to allow global investors to trade ownership certificates of traditional stocks through blockchain networks, representing another deep integration between traditional financial markets and cryptocurrency ecosystems.

Each token corresponds to one share of Anthropic stock, with holders enjoying corresponding economic rights. The company emphasized that through partnerships with licensed institutions and strict adherence to regulatory requirements, innovative products can be provided while maintaining compliance.

Industry background shows that tokenized stocks are not entirely new concepts, with multiple projects previously attempting to tokenize stocks of companies like Tesla and Apple. However, most such efforts were hindered or remained limited in scale due to regulatory uncertainty. ## Broader Perspective

Ondo's initiative reflects profound transformations occurring in the financial industry. Traditionally, stock trading has been constrained by geographical boundaries, trading hours, minimum investment thresholds, and intermediary fees. Blockchain technology has the potential to break these barriers, enabling truly globalized and around-the-clock trading markets. Tokenized stocks represent concrete practice of this vision—combining advantages of traditional assets (such as regulatory protection, corporate governance rights) with characteristics of digital assets (such as instant settlement, programmability, fractional ownership).

From a regulatory perspective, this development brings both opportunities and challenges. On one hand, tokenization can improve market efficiency, reduce transaction costs, and enhance financial inclusion; on the other hand, it raises new questions about investor protection, market manipulation risks, and cross-border enforcement coordination. Regulators need to find balance between encouraging innovation and preventing risks, which may involve updating existing securities regulations or developing new specialized frameworks.

For Anthropic, having its stock tokenized may expand its investor base, particularly attracting younger investors and technology enthusiasts who prefer digital asset management approaches. However, this may also bring increased stock price volatility risks, as cryptocurrency markets tend to be more speculative than traditional stock markets. The company needs to monitor how this dual trading environment affects its valuation and shareholder structure.

More broadly, this trend may reshape the entire asset management industry. If tokenized stocks gain regulatory recognition and market acceptance, we may see more traditional assets appearing in digital form, including real estate, private equity, and even artwork. This will create new investment opportunities but may also blur boundaries between traditional finance and crypto finance, requiring participants to possess cross-domain expertise.

**Ondo Finance**: Company spokespersons stated that tokenized stocks represent the next step in financial democratization. They emphasized that through partnerships with licensed institutions and strict regulatory compliance, innovative products can be offered while maintaining compliance. Ondo believes that as regulatory frameworks mature, tokenization will become an important component of mainstream financial products.

**Anthropic**: While Anthropic has not issued official statements on this matter, sources indicate the company remains open to potential benefits of increased stock liquidity and expanded investor base. However, they also expressed caution regarding market volatility and reputational risks.

**Regulatory Authorities**: The US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) has previously expressed concerns about tokenized securities, emphasizing that all security tokens must comply with federal securities laws. Observers believe Ondo's compliance approach may serve as a reference template for other projects, but regulators' final positions remain to be seen.

**Traditional Financial Institutions**: Some Wall Street firms view tokenization skeptically, worrying it may weaken existing intermediary roles and introduce new systemic risks. However, major banks are also exploring their own tokenization solutions, indicating industry divisions but also active preparation for this trend.

**Cryptocurrency Community**: Crypto-native investors generally welcome this development, viewing it as an important signal of traditional finance moving toward the blockchain world. However, some purists criticize this approach for still relying on centralized custody, failing to fully realize decentralized finance ideals.

**Academic Researchers**: Finance professors point out that tokenized stock success depends on multiple factors including regulatory clarity, technical reliability, market liquidity, and investor education. They believe that while long-term prospects are optimistic, short-term adoption speed may be constrained by these factors.
