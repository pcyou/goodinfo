## [Brief] Binance Adds Options on 1,000 US Stocks and ETFs as TradFi Perpetual Volume Hits Record

According to The Block, Binance, the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange, has announced the launch of options on over 1,000 US stocks and ETFs, while its traditional finance (TradFi) perpetual contracts have reached a new all-time high in monthly trading volume.

This move marks a deepening convergence between cryptocurrency platforms and traditional financial products. Users can now trade traditional financial assets directly on a crypto exchange, blurring the lines between crypto markets and traditional securities markets.

Analysts note that Binance's move aims to attract more institutional users and traditional investors by offering diversified asset classes to enhance platform competitiveness. However, this may also draw regulatory scrutiny, as crypto exchanges offering securities products involve complex compliance issues.
