## Core Summary

Leading global crypto market maker Wintermute has officially registered as a broker-dealer with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA). This move enables Wintermute to legally trade traditional stocks, options, and cryptocurrency ETFs, marking a significant step for crypto market makers into the traditional finance domain.

## Event Details

Wintermute USA, the company's New York-based subsidiary, has completed broker-dealer registration with both the SEC and FINRA. This license allows Wintermute to offer trading services for stocks, options, and crypto ETFs in the U.S. market.

Wintermute is one of the world's largest crypto market makers, with daily trading volumes exceeding billions of dollars. Obtaining this traditional securities trading license demonstrates the company is advancing its "everything exchange" strategy, deeply integrating crypto markets with traditional financial markets.

This move also reflects the growing trend of convergence between the crypto industry and traditional finance. An increasing number of crypto companies are pursuing compliant operations to gain recognition from institutional investors and regulators.

## Panoramic Perspective

Wintermute's registration marks crypto market makers officially entering the regulated traditional securities space. This trend could reshape the service model of the entire crypto industry. Previously, crypto market makers focused primarily on digital asset markets, but now they are becoming bridges connecting traditional finance and the crypto world.

From a regulatory perspective, the SEC's stance toward crypto companies is evolving. Allowing crypto-native companies like Wintermute to obtain broker-dealer licenses indicates regulators are exploring more flexible frameworks to adapt to the rapid evolution of financial markets.

For investors, this means the boundary between crypto assets and traditional securities will further blur. In the future, investors may seamlessly trade stocks, bonds, and crypto assets on the same platform, greatly enhancing market efficiency and liquidity.

## Multiple Perspectives

**Supporting Compliance**: Industry analysts believe Wintermute's registration is an important milestone in the crypto industry's maturation. Compliant operations can not only attract more institutional investors but also enhance the entire industry's credibility.

**Regulatory Concerns**: Some regulatory advocates worry that crypto companies entering traditional securities may introduce new risks. They call on the SEC to strengthen oversight of such hybrid business models.

**Increased Competition**: Other crypto market makers may be compelled to follow suit, seeking similar licenses to remain competitive. This will drive the entire industry toward greater standardization and transparency.
