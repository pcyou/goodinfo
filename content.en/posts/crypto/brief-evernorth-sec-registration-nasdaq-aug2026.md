## Core Summary

According to The Block, XRP-focused treasury company Evernorth announced that its S-4 registration statement filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) is now officially effective. The registration involves Evernorth's merger with Special Purpose Acquisition Company (SPAC) Armada, after which it will list on the Nasdaq exchange.

## Event Brief

Evernorth adopts a MicroStrategy-like treasury strategy, using XRP as its core reserve asset. This SPAC merger listing reflects the continuing trend of crypto treasury companies seeking traditional capital market access. The completion of SEC registration means key regulatory obstacles have been cleared, and the listing process enters its final stage.

## Panoramic Analysis

This event continues the wave of crypto treasury company listings that began in 2025. After MicroStrategy achieved tremendous success with its Bitcoin treasury strategy, other crypto assets are beginning to see similar treasury company models. Evernorth's choice of XRP as reserve assets reflects optimism about the Ripple ecosystem and cross-border payment market.

From a regulatory perspective, SEC approval of the registration indicates a more pragmatic approach toward crypto treasury companies. This provides precedent for more crypto enterprises entering traditional capital markets, helping promote further integration of crypto assets with traditional finance.
