## Core Summary

According to CoinShares' latest report, deposits of tokenized real-world assets (RWA) grew from $2.3 billion in Q2 2025 to $7.4 billion in Q2 2026, more than tripling. However, the broader DeFi market contracted 15% during the same period, indicating capital is shifting from speculative crypto assets toward tokenized assets backed by real value.

## Event Details

CoinShares' quarterly report shows RWA tokenization is becoming the crypto market's primary growth engine. Deposits of tokenized RWA into lending platforms and decentralized exchanges (DEXs) increased from $2.3 billion in Q2 2025 to $7.4 billion in Q2 2026.

This growth is primarily driven by institutional demand. Traditional financial institutions are tokenizing assets such as real estate, bonds, and commodities to achieve higher liquidity and lower transaction costs. Meanwhile, DeFi protocols are actively integrating RWA to provide users with more diversified investment options.

Notably, this growth occurred against a backdrop of broader DeFi market contraction. Total DeFi value locked (TVL) declined 15% during the same period, reflecting market caution toward high-risk speculative assets.

## Panoramic Perspective

The explosion of RWA tokenization marks the crypto industry's shift from speculation-driven to value-driven. Previously, the crypto market was primarily driven by speculative trading and meme coins, but now institutional investors are seeking investment opportunities backed by real assets.

From a technical perspective, RWA tokenization faces multiple challenges including legal compliance, asset custody, and price discovery. However, as regulatory frameworks become clearer and technical infrastructure matures, these obstacles are being gradually overcome.

For traditional finance, RWA tokenization represents both opportunity and challenge. On one hand, it can enhance asset liquidity and reduce transaction costs; on the other hand, it may disrupt existing financial intermediary models, forcing traditional institutions to rethink their business approaches.

## Multiple Perspectives

**Optimistic Outlook**: Industry analysts believe RWA tokenization is the crypto industry's "killer app." It bridges traditional finance and the crypto world, creating value for both.

**Cautious Stance**: Some observers note RWA tokenization remains in early stages, and mass adoption will take time. They emphasize that regulatory uncertainty and technical risks remain primary obstacles.

**Intensifying Competition**: As the RWA track heats up, more projects and enterprises are entering this space. This will drive innovation but may also lead to market fragmentation.
