## Core Summary

CoinDesk reported on September 13 that as the U.S. Senate returned from summer recess, the Crypto Clarity Act still lacks a clear schedule for advancement in the Senate. The bill has already passed the House, but the Senate remains significantly divided over core provisions on digital-asset classification, the allocation of regulatory authority and stablecoin reserve requirements. Several industry observers have described the bill as being in a Schrödinger-style state of simultaneous life and death, stressing that until the Senate majority leader signals that it will be placed on the priority agenda, the bill's ultimate fate remains highly uncertain.
