The Ethereum Foundation's privacy team has officially spun out as an independent for-profit company called EthSystems. The company will focus on developing technology and providing consulting services for institutional investors, helping them operate on the Ethereum network while maintaining transaction privacy and confidentiality. This move marks an important step for the Ethereum ecosystem in serving institutional clients.
