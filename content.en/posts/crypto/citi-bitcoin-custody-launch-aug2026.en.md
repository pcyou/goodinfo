## Core Summary

Citibank announced on August 18 that it plans to launch bitcoin custody services under its newly upgraded "Custody+" platform later in 2026, targeting institutional clients. This move marks a new milestone in Wall Street traditional financial giants' acceptance of crypto assets and is expected to drive more institutional capital into the digital asset market.

## Event Details

According to CoinDesk and The Block, Citibank plans to offer bitcoin custody services under its new Custody+ platform. This platform will integrate Citibank's existing custody business capabilities, providing institutional clients with secure, compliant digital asset storage solutions.

Citibank is one of the world's largest banks, managing trillions of dollars in client assets. This entry into bitcoin custody reflects growing institutional demand for crypto assets. In recent years, multiple Wall Street giants including Bank of New York Mellon and JPMorgan Chase have successively launched or announced plans for digital asset custody services.

Meanwhile, other financial institutions are accelerating their crypto market expansion. Metaplanet established a US bitcoin treasury company through a $135 million deal in Japan; Securitize brought Neuberger Berman's $230 billion fixed-income platform onchain with a new tokenized fund. These developments indicate that crypto assets are increasingly integrating into the traditional financial system.

## Panoramic Analysis

Citibank's entry into bitcoin custody has far-reaching industry implications. First, it marks an acceleration in the transformation of crypto assets from "alternative investments" to "mainstream asset class." When one of the world's largest banks formally offers bitcoin custody services, it signifies that crypto assets have gained substantive recognition from traditional finance.

Second, the launch of institutional-grade custody services will significantly lower barriers for large capital entering crypto markets. Many pension funds, endowments, and insurance companies have previously stayed on the sidelines regarding crypto assets due to lack of compliant custody solutions. Citibank's participation will provide these institutions with the necessary trust foundation.

From a regulatory perspective, traditional bank participation will also drive development of clearer regulatory frameworks. As more regulated financial institutions enter the crypto market, regulators will face pressure and motivation to establish clear rules, benefiting the industry's long-term healthy development.

However, this also brings new challenges. Entry of traditional financial giants may intensify market concentration, creating competitive pressure for small and medium-sized crypto enterprises. Meanwhile, balancing innovation with risk management will be a shared challenge for regulators and businesses.

## Multiple Perspectives

**Supportive View**: Crypto industry advocates see Citibank's participation as an important milestone for crypto assets going mainstream. They note that traditional financial institutions' compliance capabilities and risk management experience will bring higher security and stability to the crypto market.

**Cautious Stance**: Some market observers caution that traditional banks entering crypto also means potential systemic risk transmission. If the crypto market experiences significant volatility, it could affect the broader financial system through these large financial institutions.

**Competitive Perspective**: Industry analysts note that Citibank's move will intensify competition among financial institutions in crypto custody. Bank of New York Mellon, JPMorgan Chase, State Street and others are competing for this emerging market, and competition will drive service innovation and cost reduction.

**Regulatory Focus**: Regulators take a prudent approach to traditional banks entering the crypto market, emphasizing the need to ensure adequate risk management and consumer protection measures are in place.
