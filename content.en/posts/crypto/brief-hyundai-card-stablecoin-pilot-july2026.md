[Brief] South Korea's Hyundai Card has announced a partnership with blockchain platform Avalanche and stablecoin issuer Tether to successfully complete its first real-world stablecoin payment pilot project. According to The Block, the pilot validated the feasibility and efficiency of stablecoins in actual commercial transactions.

This collaboration marks a significant step forward in traditional financial institutions' adoption of digital assets. Analysts believe that the participation of large corporations and financial institutions will accelerate the transition of stablecoins from speculative instruments to practical payment tools. Blockchain-based stablecoin payments have the potential to reduce cross-border transaction costs, improve settlement efficiency, and lay the foundation for future digital financial infrastructure.
