## [Brief] Eliza Labs Founder Declares Native Token 'Dead,' Winds Down Foundation

**[Core Summary]** Eliza Labs founder Shaw Walters announced that the project's native token ELIZA is "dead" and will wind down the associated foundation. This move follows a settlement agreement between Eliza Labs and the US law firm Burwick Law, marking the end of this once highly-watched AI+crypto project.

### Event Details

According to The Block, Eliza Labs founder Walters announced on social media that the project's native token ELIZA has lost its raison d'être, and the foundation will cease operations. Previously, Eliza Labs faced a class-action lawsuit filed by Burwick Law alleging securities violations, and the parties ultimately reached a settlement.

Eliza Labs gained widespread attention in 2024-2025 for its open-source AI agent framework, which allows developers to build autonomously running AI agents. At its peak, the ELIZA token's market capitalization exceeded $500 million. However, mounting regulatory pressure and intensifying market competition ultimately made the project unsustainable.

### Panoramic Perspective

The demise of Eliza Labs reflects the deep challenges facing the AI+crypto track. On one hand, AI agent technology itself holds enormous potential for automating complex tasks; on the other hand, the model of combining AI with token economics faces dual tests of regulation and sustainability.

This event also reminds investors of the importance of maintaining rationality amid the AI boom. Many projects issued tokens by leveraging the AI concept but lacked clear business models and compliance frameworks. As global crypto regulation tightens, projects similar to Eliza Labs may face greater pressure. In the future, the integration of AI and blockchain may require more pragmatic paths rather than relying solely on token speculation.
