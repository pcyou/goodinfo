## Overview

Ripple and JPMorgan, in collaboration with Ondo Finance and Mastercard, have completed the first cross-border tokenized Treasury redemption on the XRP Ledger. The pilot processed the redemption of Ondo's OUSG tokenized Treasury fund in under five seconds.

## Impact

This breakthrough demonstrates the potential of blockchain technology for settling traditional financial assets across borders. The near-instant settlement time could revolutionize how Treasury securities are traded and redeemed internationally, bridging traditional finance with decentralized infrastructure.
