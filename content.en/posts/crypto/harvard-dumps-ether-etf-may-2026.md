Harvard's endowment has recently liquidated its ether ETF positions, while Abu Dhabi's sovereign wealth fund Mubadala continues to accumulate shares in BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust ETF, adding over $90 million to its already substantial position.

Institutional allocation strategies in crypto are showing clear divergence: academic institutions reducing ethereum exposure while Middle Eastern sovereign funds remain bullish on bitcoin.
