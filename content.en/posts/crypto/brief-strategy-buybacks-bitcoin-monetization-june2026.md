**[Core Summary]** According to CoinDesk, Michael Saylor's Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) has adopted a new capital management framework, authorizing up to $2 billion in share buybacks while creating a program that allows future bitcoin sales to support liquidity. The company also raised the STRC preferred stock dividend.

This marks a significant strategic shift. Strategy had previously maintained a strict "buy only, never sell" bitcoin policy. The introduction of a monetization mechanism signals a more flexible approach to balancing bitcoin holdings with shareholder returns.
