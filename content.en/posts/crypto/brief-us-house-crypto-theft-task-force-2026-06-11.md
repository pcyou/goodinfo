According to CoinDesk, a new bill has been introduced in the US House of Representatives to establish a cross-agency task force targeting crypto asset theft. The bill, pushed by Texas Republican Representative Lance Gooden, aims to strengthen inter-agency cooperation and improve efficiency in combating crypto crime.
