The US has imposed sanctions on multiple Iranian cryptocurrency exchanges, alleging they facilitate terror financing and money laundering. The sanctions are part of broader economic pressure on Iran. Cryptocurrencies have become a key channel for sanctions evasion due to their decentralized nature, prompting regulators worldwide to tighten monitoring.
