**[Core Summary]**
Crypto media reports say major shareholders are pressing Solana on-chain treasury company SkyAI to explain recent large-scale sales of SOL. SkyAI's disclosed SOL treasury value shrank by roughly 70% in under a month, and the use of around 400,000 SOL has not been clearly disclosed in public materials.

**[Event Details]**
- **Treasury Shrinkage**: SkyAI previously disclosed large SOL holdings as its on-chain treasury, but recent data shows the value of these holdings dropped about 70% in less than a month.
- **Fund Flows**: The specific use and flow of approximately 400,000 SOL have not been clearly disclosed in public materials, prompting shareholder and market questions.
- **Shareholder Reaction**: Some major shareholders are dissatisfied with the terms of the management buyout offer, arguing the bid does not reflect the true value of the SOL treasury.
- **Market Concern**: The incident again highlights the importance of disclosure quality and corporate governance for listed crypto treasury companies.

**[Panoramic Analysis]**
As crypto treasury companies rise rapidly, the quality of their information disclosure and corporate governance is facing stricter scrutiny. Lack of transparency in asset disposals and opaque capital operations can easily trigger market trust crises. Regulators may in future require such companies to establish stricter disclosure and audit standards for on-chain asset holdings, changes and major disposal events.
