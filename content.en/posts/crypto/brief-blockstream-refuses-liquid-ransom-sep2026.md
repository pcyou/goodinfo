## Event Summary

Bitcoin infrastructure company Blockstream has publicly refused to pay a ransom and demanded that attackers return approximately 600 BTC withdrawn following the Liquid network exploit. In its statement, Blockstream made clear that the company will not engage in any form of negotiation with the attackers and is pursuing asset recovery through technical tracing and legal channels.

According to information disclosed by Blockstream, the Liquid network has gradually restored normal transaction functions following the exploit, but peg-out functionality remains suspended pending further recovery and audit work. The company stated that it will continue to cooperate with law enforcement agencies and security research institutions to trace fund flows and evaluate further legal action. This incident once again highlights the security challenges faced by sidechains and cross-chain bridges within the Bitcoin ecosystem, and has become an important case for industry discussions on security standards for decentralized financial infrastructure.

Blockstream's firm stance has received support from parts of the crypto industry and is viewed as an exemplary response that clearly rejects ransomware attacks. At the same time, however, security researchers have pointed out that the rapid development of the Bitcoin ecosystem has made cross-chain bridges and sidechains high-value targets for hackers, and that related security defenses still require continuous upgrades.
