## Core Summary

The US Treasury announced new sanctions on Iranian cryptocurrency exchange BitBank on September 17, accusing the platform of using the previously sanctioned Hormuz Safe payment channel to transfer cryptocurrency funds collected since June 2026 to Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps. This sanctions action marks a further escalation of US pressure on Iran's cryptocurrency financial infrastructure and represents the first explicit accusation against a mainstream Iranian domestic crypto trading platform for directly providing funding channels for military organizations.

## Event Details

According to The Block's reporting, the US Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control detailed the legal basis and specific enforcement details of the sanctions in its latest announcement. The announcement shows that BitBank is accused of maintaining a systematic cooperative relationship with the already-sanctioned Hormuz Safe payment platform, which has cumulatively processed substantial cryptocurrency payments from third-party clients over the past two months.

Treasury officials emphasized in the announcement that cryptocurrency has become one of the important tools for Iran to evade traditional financial sanctions, and tracking the funding chain of the IRGC, which is listed by the US as a terrorist organization, is particularly critical. The sanctions against BitBank aim to cut off a key channel through which the IRGC obtains external funding via cryptocurrency and weaken its operational capabilities in the Middle East.

The specific sanctions measures include: prohibiting US citizens from engaging in any transactions with BitBank; freezing any assets the platform may hold within US jurisdiction; and prohibiting US financial institutions from providing any financial services to the platform. At the same time, any third-party entities with business relationships with BitBank also face secondary sanctions risk.

This sanctions action occurred against the backdrop of continued tension in US-Iran relations. Earlier this year, the US had already imposed sanctions on the Hormuz Safe payment platform, but that platform clearly continued to maintain operational channels through cooperation with BitBank. The Treasury's latest action indicates that the US is adopting a "full-chain" sanctions strategy, systematically dismantling Iran's domestic cryptocurrency financial infrastructure.

Notably, BitBank is not entirely an obscure platform. The platform has long served cryptocurrency trading needs within Iran, possessing a considerable user base and transaction volume locally. Being formally placed on the US sanctions list means that any entity globally with business associations with the platform needs to reassess its own compliance risk.

## Panoramic Perspective

The US Treasury's sanctions action carries significant geopolitical and financial regulatory implications. From a geopolitical perspective, cryptocurrency has become a new strategic tool in great power competition. Traditional financial sanctions rely primarily on controlling the SWIFT system and dollar clearing channels, but the decentralized nature of cryptocurrency makes it an alternative channel for some countries to evade sanctions. The US action against BitBank essentially sends a clear signal to the global crypto industry: any platform providing services to sanctioned entities will face full accountability under US law.

The deeper impact lies in how this sanctions action may reshape the global cryptocurrency industry's compliance landscape. For a long time, some cryptocurrency exchanges have adopted selective compliance strategies regarding geographic location and regulatory requirements, operating in lightly regulated areas to avoid compliance costs. The BitBank incident shows that the political risk of this "regulatory arbitrage" model is rising sharply. More exchanges may be compelled to strengthen due diligence on customer backgrounds and fund flows to avoid similar legal consequences.

From a technical perspective, the effectiveness of sanctions faces numerous challenges. On-chain cryptocurrency transfers have strong anonymity and cross-border liquidity. Even if BitBank is placed on the sanctions list, its potential users may still circumvent regulation through mixer services, cross-chain bridging, and other technical means. The US Treasury needs to work closely with blockchain analytics companies, identifying wallet addresses associated with BitBank through on-chain tracking technology, to truly achieve effective sanctions enforcement.

Furthermore, this incident also highlights the urgency of international regulatory coordination. Unilateral US sanctions measures have limited impact on entities operating within Iran. Genuine regulatory effectiveness requires coordinated cooperation from major economies including the EU, UK, and Japan. If parties cannot reach a basic consensus on cryptocurrency regulatory standards, similar sanctions actions will face the awkward situation of "policies on top, workarounds below."

## Multiple Perspectives

**US Treasury Position:** Treasury officials clearly stated that cryptocurrency must never become a tool for sanctioned entities to evade legal obligations. BitBank's actions directly support IRGC military activities and threaten US national security and Middle East stability. The Treasury will continue working with allies to systematically dismantle Iran's cryptocurrency financial infrastructure.

**Crypto Industry Reaction:** Some crypto industry practitioners expressed understanding of the sanctions, viewing this as a reasonable extension of compliance regulation. However, some voices also pointed out that sanctions overly dependent on off-chain identity verification may harm ordinary crypto users, particularly ordinary Iranian citizens who legitimately use cryptocurrency for personal cross-border payments.

**Iranian Government Attitude:** Iran's foreign ministry criticized US sanctions as typical "economic terrorism," pointing out that cryptocurrency as an emerging financial tool inherently has decentralized characteristics, and unilateral sanctions cannot block Iran's determination to develop an autonomous financial system. The Central Bank of Iran has stated multiple times that it will support the development of domestic cryptocurrency exchanges.

**Blockchain Analytics Company Views:** Chainalysis, Elliptic, and other professional blockchain analytics companies expressed support for the sanctions, arguing that the maturation of on-chain tracking technology makes sanctions enforcement possible. However, these companies also acknowledged that completely cutting off cryptocurrency flows to sanctioned entities still faces technical difficulties and requires continued investment in R&D resources.

**International Regulatory Body Stance:** The European Securities and Markets Authority and other international regulators are closely monitoring the situation. Some regulators believe the US action provides an important opportunity for global crypto regulatory coordination and may promote closer cooperation mechanisms among countries in anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing.
