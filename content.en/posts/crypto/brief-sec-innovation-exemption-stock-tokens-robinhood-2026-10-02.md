According to The Block's reporting, Robinhood's crypto chief stated at a recent industry conference that the SEC's innovation exemption framework, while providing an initial compliance on-ramp for tokenized securities, remains narrow in scope and conditions, posing a meaningful constraint on crypto platforms including Robinhood as they seek to bring traditional US equity tokenization products to the US market.

The remarks underscore that the US tokenized securities regulatory path is still at an early exploratory stage, with the industry broadly awaiting clearer specific rules from the SEC, CFTC, and market participants on the issuance, trading, and custody of tokenized equity products.
