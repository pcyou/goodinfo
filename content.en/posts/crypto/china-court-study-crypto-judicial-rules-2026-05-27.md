## [Brief] China Supreme Court Studies Crypto Judicial Rules

China's Supreme People's Court announced it will develop judicial guidance rules for crypto-related cases, responding to a growing number of legal disputes involving virtual assets in recent years, including fraud, money laundering, and illegal fundraising.
