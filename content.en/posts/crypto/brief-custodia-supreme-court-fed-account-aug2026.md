## Core Summary

The Blockchain Association has announced support for Custodia Bank's Supreme Court appeal over Federal Reserve master account access. The industry group says the case is about ensuring "lawful digital asset businesses can compete on equal footing." Custodia was previously denied Fed master account access, preventing it from offering traditional banking services.

## Event Details

According to The Block, the Blockchain Association has filed an amicus brief supporting Custodia Bank's Supreme Court appeal. Custodia is an institution aiming to provide full-service banking for the digital asset industry, but was denied Fed master account access.

A Fed master account is key for financial institutions to access the US payment system. Without this account, Custodia cannot provide customers with traditional banking services like check clearing and wire transfers. In its brief, the Blockchain Association argues that denying Custodia access was based on "discrimination against the digital asset industry" rather than legitimate regulatory considerations.

The association emphasized that this case concerns not just Custodia's fate, but whether the entire digital asset industry can obtain fair access to financial services. If legitimately operating digital asset businesses cannot access the same financial services as traditional enterprises, it will seriously hinder industry innovation and development.

## Panoramic Perspective

This case reflects the deep contradictions between the traditional financial system and the emerging digital asset industry. The Fed has discretionary authority over master account issuance, but ensuring this power isn't used to discriminate against specific industries is an important regulatory challenge.

From an industry perspective, if Custodia wins, it will pave the way for other digital asset businesses to obtain banking services, reducing industry dependence on traditional banks. Conversely, if the appeal fails, it may further strengthen traditional financial institutions' gatekeeper role in the digital asset space.

This case may also affect broader financial regulatory frameworks. The court's definition of Fed discretion will provide important precedent for future similar cases, potentially reshaping the power balance between financial institutions and regulators.
