## Core Summary

Cross-chain infrastructure project ZetaChain has launched a governance vote to shut down its standalone Layer 1 network and migrate the native ZETA token into the Solana ecosystem. The team also announced it will concentrate its main resources on its private AI application Anuma, with the proposal still requiring a second community vote to take effect. The decision marks another cross-chain infrastructure chain, once viewed with high market expectations, voluntarily folding into a mainstream high-performance chain ecosystem after years of independent operation, reflecting the crypto industry's entry into a new consolidation cycle centered on applications and ecosystem integration.

## Event Details

**Proposal Content and Timeline**: According to The Block and other industry media, the ZetaChain team recently initiated a first-round governance vote in the community. Its core content is to shut down the ZetaChain standalone Layer 1 network and migrate the native ZETA token to the Solana network at a defined ratio, making ZETA a token asset within the Solana ecosystem. The proposal also includes concentrating the team's main energy and development resources on its private AI application Anuma. The proposal has not yet been finally passed and requires a second confirmation vote after the first round, with the full process expected to take several weeks.

**ZetaChain Historical Positioning**: ZetaChain was founded around 2018 and has long been positioned as a cross-chain infrastructure project supporting asset and data flows between non-smart-contract chains such as Bitcoin and mainstream smart-contract chains. Its technical approach is to act as a universal message and value transfer layer between different blockchains through a standalone Layer 1 network, making it one of the early representative projects in the cross-chain track. However, the cross-chain track has seen significant competitive shifts in recent years, with projects such as LayerZero, Wormhole and Axelar entering via different technical paths, and frequent cross-chain bridge security incidents have placed broad commercial and ecosystem growth pressure on early cross-chain infrastructure projects.

**Anuma Transition Direction**: The team positions Anuma as a consumer-facing private AI application, a notable departure from ZetaChain's previous decentralized cross-chain positioning. From the official framing, Anuma places greater emphasis on end-to-end encryption, local inference and personal data protection to deliver consumer-grade AI experiences, seeking new growth space in the current AI application boom. Concentrating resources on Anuma means ZetaChain's previous decentralized narrative will be visibly weakened.

**Solana Ecosystem Effect**: If ZETA successfully migrates into Solana, it will enrich the diversity of token assets within the Solana ecosystem and bring new potential assets and liquidity to related Solana DeFi protocols. From historical experience, cross-asset migration typically involves multiple complex engineering issues such as token exchange ratios, migration window periods, cross-chain bridge schemes and token holder community communication; the specific plan for this migration still needs to be detailed by the ZetaChain team in subsequent governance procedures.

## Panoramic Perspective

ZetaChain's choice to shut down its independent chain and fully migrate to Solana is a landmark event in the current crypto industry's deep consolidation phase. Over the past few years, a large number of early Layer 1 projects emerged during financing booms and cross-chain narratives, but now, with mature high-performance chain ecosystems, a cooling cross-chain narrative and the rise of an AI application wave, more and more projects are choosing to abandon independent operation, attach themselves to mainstream ecosystems, or transform entirely into the application layer. Behind this phenomenon is the crypto industry's shift from underlying infrastructure competition to application-layer and user-experience competition. From an industry landscape perspective, high-performance chains such as Solana and Base are becoming the convergence hub for more projects and assets, with ecosystem concentration continuing to rise; on the other hand, the convergence of AI and crypto is giving rise to new application paradigms, and existing projects are pursuing second-growth curves through AI applications. From a risk perspective, cross-asset migration involves multiple layers such as token economic model reset, user community consensus rebuilding and regulatory compliance; whether ZetaChain's transformation can ultimately win recognition from the community and the market still requires observation of subsequent voting outcomes and execution details.

## Multi-Perspective Comparison

**Supporting View**: Some community members and developers believe that abandoning the standalone Layer 1, attaching to the high-performance Solana ecosystem and concentrating resources on AI applications is a pragmatic choice aligned with current industry trends. Solana's high throughput and low fees can deliver a better experience for existing ZetaChain users, and the AI application direction has a clear commercialization path.

**Opposing View**: Some early token holders and decentralization advocates have expressed disappointment, arguing that abandoning the independent chain goes against the decentralized promise made when ZetaChain was founded, and that the ratios, cross-chain schemes and community communication mechanisms in the migration process carry uncertainties that could lead to token value dilution and community fragmentation.

**Industry Observer View**: Multiple crypto research institutions note that ZetaChain's transformation is a microcosm of the collective predicament faced by early cross-chain infrastructure projects. With next-generation cross-chain protocols such as LayerZero now dominating the market, the cross-chain landscape has largely solidified, and subsequent resource consolidation and transformation will become the norm.

**Ecosystem Operator View**: Related Solana ecosystem developers have expressed welcome for ZETA's migration, arguing it will bring new asset liquidity and community attention to Solana, while emphasizing the need to ensure technical security and community communication transparency throughout the migration process.
