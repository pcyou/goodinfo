**[Summary]** North Carolina has passed a new bill formally recognizing the Commodity Futures Trading Commission's (CFTC) federal preemption over prediction market regulation, paving the way for compliant operation of prediction market platforms in the state.

**[Event Details]** According to The Block, North Carolina becomes the latest state to explicitly acknowledge CFTC's federal regulatory authority over prediction markets. This means prediction market platforms operating in the state will primarily be subject to federal rather than state-level gambling regulations. This legislative development is seen as an important step for the crypto prediction market industry's push for legitimacy in the US, especially as multiple states have taken restrictive stances toward prediction markets.

**[Analysis]** The question of prediction market regulatory jurisdiction has become a focal point of debate among US state legislatures. The CFTC traditionally oversees commodity futures and derivatives markets, but prediction markets occupy a legal gray area - whether they constitute gambling or financial derivatives remains contested between state and federal authorities. North Carolina's position may provide legislative reference for other states, but could also trigger further legal conflicts over state versus federal regulatory authority.
