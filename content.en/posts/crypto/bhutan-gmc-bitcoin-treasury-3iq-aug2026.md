## Summary

Bhutan's planned Gelephu Mindfulness City (GMC) project has announced it will delegate part of its bitcoin treasury to digital asset manager 3iQ for market-neutral strategy operations. GMC previously pledged to hold 10,000 BTC, and this shift marks the country's transition from simple long-term holding to active crypto asset management.

This strategic adjustment reflects that sovereign-level crypto asset holders are exploring more sophisticated asset management approaches, no longer content with passive holding but seeking asset appreciation while controlling risk.
