## Core Summary

Alfa Bank, one of Russia's major banks, has announced plans to launch a digital depository to provide cryptocurrency-related services for its clients. This move aligns with similar recent announcements from other large Russian banks, showing continued interest from the country's banking sector in digital assets, while also reflecting the gradual formation of Russia's crypto regulatory framework.

## Event Overview

According to TheBlock, Alfa Bank's digital depository will focus on providing compliant crypto asset custody and trading services for institutional and high-net-worth clients. This plan aligns with Russia's recently passed crypto asset legal framework, which allows banks to provide digital asset services under specific conditions.

Russian banking sector exploration of crypto services reflects dual drivers of market demand and regulatory easing. Despite facing international sanctions pressure, Russia is actively building domestic digital financial infrastructure.

## Panoramic Perspective

Russian banks' entry into the crypto sector has geopolitical context. Under Western financial sanctions, Russia seeks to establish alternative cross-border payment channels through digital assets. The decentralized nature of crypto technology makes it a potential tool for sanctions evasion, but also raises international concerns.
