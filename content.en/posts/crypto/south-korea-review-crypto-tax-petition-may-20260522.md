title: "[Brief] South Korea to Review Crypto Tax Plan After 50K-Signature Petition"
date: '2026-05-22T16:21:00+08:00'
categories: [crypto]
tags: ["South Korea", "crypto tax", "regulation"]
---

The South Korean government said it will review its cryptocurrency taxation plan after a national petition garnered over 50,000 signatures. The petition calls for canceling or delaying taxes on crypto trading gains. South Korea has been wavering on crypto regulation, and this review reflects domestic divisions over the tax framework for the digital asset industry.
