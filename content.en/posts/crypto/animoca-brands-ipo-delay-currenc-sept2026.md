## Core Summary

Hong Kong-based crypto and gaming venture firm Animoca Brands has delayed its IPO plans and suspended its months-long merger talks with Currenc, originally initiated late last year.

## Event Details

Animoca Brands stated that the company is currently reassessing its capital market approach, deciding to postpone its scheduled IPO timeline and suspend merger discussions with Currenc that had been ongoing for months. Under the preliminary understanding reached late last year, Animoca was to own 95% of the merged company, though the final transaction structure and timeline had not been determined.

## Comparison of Viewpoints

Some market observers see this postponement as a reflection of the broader volatile capital market environment for the crypto industry, with some companies choosing to stay on the sidelines until valuation windows improve. Others argue that Animoca, as a key player in the Web3 and gaming sectors, will not have its long-term development path fundamentally affected by short-term decisions.

## Full Picture

Animoca's decision to postpone its IPO and suspend the merger talks is a microcosm of current crypto industry capital market dynamics. Against the backdrop of bitcoin's price recovery and gradually clearer regulatory frameworks, leading industry companies are reassessing the timing and approach of entering public capital markets. While this move may delay capital operations in the near term, in the longer run it helps anchor industry valuations at more reasonable levels. Investors will continue to watch Animoca's next steps and the capital flow direction of the Web3 and gaming convergence track that it reflects.
