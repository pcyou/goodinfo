## [Brief] SEC to Propose Tokenized Stock Framework

According to Bloomberg, the US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) will propose a regulatory framework for tokenized stocks. As Wall Street's interest in tokenized securities deepens, this move is seen as a significant signal of convergence between crypto and traditional finance.
