## Ether's Biggest Corporate Holders Back Ethlabs Nonprofit

SharpLink and Bitmine, the two largest corporate holders of ether, along with Consensys CEO Joe Lubin, have jointly backed the formation of Ethlabs, a new nonprofit research organization. According to CoinDesk and The Block, Ethlabs will focus on advancing Ethereum's "next phase of growth," with emphasis on institutional adoption and core protocol development.

The formation of Ethlabs signals a shift in Ethereum's governance landscape, as corporate participants increasingly take on roles traditionally held by the Ethereum Foundation.
