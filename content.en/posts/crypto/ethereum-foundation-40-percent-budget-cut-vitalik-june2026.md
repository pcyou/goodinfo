## Core Summary

Ethereum co-founder Vitalik Buterin has announced that the Ethereum Foundation will slash its budget by 40% and cut 20% of its workforce. The restructuring comes alongside the departure of co-Executive Director Hsiao-Wei Wang, marking the most significant organizational overhaul in the Foundation's history.

## Event Details

The Ethereum Foundation confirmed the major restructuring on June 23. Vitalik Buterin stated the Foundation will reduce its budget by 40% to allocate resources more efficiently. On the same day, the Foundation confirmed a 20% workforce reduction and a reorganization into five core clusters.

Co-founder Hsiao-Wei Wang's resignation is the most notable personnel change. She was a core early team member who long oversaw Foundation operations and ecosystem coordination. Her departure further depletes the Foundation's senior leadership.

The restructuring comes after a challenging year for the Ethereum ecosystem, including network upgrade delays, increased Layer 2 competition, and market volatility. The Foundation had already faced concerns about stability following earlier personnel changes, which Consensys founder Joe Lubin described as "not a crisis" in early June.

Vitalik emphasized that the reset aims to refocus the Foundation on core Ethereum protocol research, security, and decentralization goals rather than overextending its scope.

## Analysis

The Foundation's restructuring reflects a broader industry shift from expansion to operational efficiency. After the 2021 bull market, many crypto organizations are reassessing resource allocation. The budget cut means the Ethereum ecosystem will rely more on external developers and community contributions rather than centralized funding.

From a governance perspective, this could accelerate Ethereum's move toward greater decentralization. A smaller Foundation means decision-making power disperses to individual core developer teams and community organizations, similar to the Linux Foundation model.

However, risks remain. A reduced Foundation may struggle to fund critical infrastructure projects, particularly in security auditing and protocol upgrades. The market will closely watch how the restructuring affects Ethereum's technical progress and ecosystem development speed.
