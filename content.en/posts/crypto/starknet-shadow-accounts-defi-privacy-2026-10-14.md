**[Core Summary]**

Ethereum Layer 2 network Starknet has launched a privacy feature called Shadow Accounts, allowing users to isolate their main wallets from DeFi activity at the account level, making it harder for external observers to directly correlate on-chain behavior between the two. The feature is implemented through account abstraction and zero-knowledge proof properties, but on-chain transactions themselves remain publicly visible.

**[Event Details]**

- **Feature Positioning**: Shadow Accounts allows users to create independent accounts for DeFi interactions outside their main wallets, using privacy routing mechanisms to obfuscate fund flows and operational correlations between them.
- **Technical Principle**: The feature relies on Starknet's account abstraction architecture and zero-knowledge proof properties, allowing users to switch flexibly between different accounts while preserving verifiability of on-chain transaction records.
- **Privacy Boundary**: Although Shadow Accounts can obfuscate the correlation between main wallets and DeFi accounts, specific on-chain transaction amounts, contract calls, and timestamps remain visible on-chain, meaning it is not fully anonymous.
- **Use Cases**: The privacy feature primarily targets professional users and institutions seeking to protect trading strategies in DeFi activities and avoid being tracked by front-running bots or market analysis tools.

**[Panorama]**

Starknet's privacy feature reflects Layer 2 networks entering a new competitive phase around user experience and feature differentiation. Against the backdrop of slow privacy technology development on Ethereum mainnet and high usage barriers for dedicated privacy chains, Layer 2 networks achieving limited privacy through zero-knowledge proofs and account abstraction have become a viable path. The design philosophy of Shadow Accounts sits between full transparency and full anonymity: it acknowledges that on-chain data cannot be fully hidden, but reduces the risk of malicious tracking and strategy leakage through obfuscation of account correlations. This approach maintains certain compatibility with regulatory requirements, as transactions themselves remain traceable for compliant investigations. However, the introduction of privacy features may trigger further regulatory discussion about compliance obligations for Layer 2 networks, particularly regarding the boundaries of anti-money laundering and suspicious transaction reporting. From a market perspective, the privacy feature may become an important selling point to attract professional DeFi users, but may also be abused as market manipulation tools, attracting regulatory attention.

**[Multiple Perspectives]**

- **Starknet Team Position**: Emphasizes that privacy is a fundamental user right, with Shadow Accounts designed to balance privacy protection and compliance needs.
- **DeFi Developer View**: Some developers believe account-level privacy helps reduce front-running transactions and sandwich attacks, improving DeFi market fairness.
- **Regulator Concerns**: Compliance agencies warn that privacy features may be abused for money laundering and terrorism financing, demanding strengthened AML oversight of Layer 2 networks.
