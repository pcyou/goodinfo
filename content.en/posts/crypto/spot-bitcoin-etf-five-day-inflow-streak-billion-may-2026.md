## Sustained Inflows

US spot Bitcoin ETFs have recorded net inflows for five consecutive trading days, totaling nearly $1.7 billion. The ETFs are on track for a sixth consecutive week of net inflows, which would be the longest weekly streak since July 2025.

This trend signals continued institutional demand for Bitcoin allocation and recovering market confidence in crypto assets. Analysts note that sustained ETF inflows provide significant price support for Bitcoin.
