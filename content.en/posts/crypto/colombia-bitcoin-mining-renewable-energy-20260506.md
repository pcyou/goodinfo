## Colombia wants to mine bitcoin with surplus renewable energy

Colombian President Gustavo Petro said the country's Caribbean coast could host bitcoin mining facilities powered by surplus renewable energy, following a path similar to El Salvador's.

This marks another Latin American nation formally embracing cryptocurrency mining, combining renewable energy infrastructure with bitcoin mining operations.
