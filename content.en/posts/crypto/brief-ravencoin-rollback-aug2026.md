## Core Summary

Cryptocurrency Ravencoin is considering rolling back four days of transactions after discovering a critical block validation flaw. The extreme measure highlights blockchain network vulnerability to underlying protocol issues.

## Event Overview

According to CoinDesk, the Ravencoin network discovered a critical flaw affecting block validation. To prevent potential fund losses from exploitation, the development team proposed rolling back the blockchain to a state before the flaw was discovered, effectively reverting all transactions from the past four days.
