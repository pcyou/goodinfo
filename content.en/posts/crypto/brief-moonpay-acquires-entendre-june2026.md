## MoonPay Acquires AI Accounting Startup Entendre

Crypto payments infrastructure company MoonPay has acquired artificial intelligence accounting startup Entendre to bolster its back-office financial operations capabilities. According to The Block, this marks at least the fifth acquisition MoonPay has completed in 2026, signaling aggressive expansion through M&A.

Entendre specializes in automating enterprise accounting workflows using AI. Its technology will be integrated into MoonPay Trade and MoonPay Institutional product lines.
