**[Core Summary]**

Cryptocurrency exchange Coinbase has published a priority list for Bitcoin's post-quantum migration, placing flexible signature scheme design as the top priority and urging the community to begin preparing signature upgrade paths and large-scale fund migration mechanisms to address potential quantum computing threats.

**[Event Details]**

- **Priority List**: Coinbase's technical document places flexible post-quantum signature algorithm design as the highest priority, emphasizing support for parallel transitions across multiple signature schemes to avoid ecosystem risks from single standard lock-in.
- **Core Concern**: Bitcoin's existing elliptic curve digital signature algorithm could theoretically be broken by sufficiently powerful quantum computers, with the industry broadly recognizing the need for a transition to quantum-resistant signature schemes.
- **Migration Challenge**: Coinbase noted that the most difficult part is not the technical signature algorithm replacement itself, but how to safely migrate trillions of dollars in on-chain assets without triggering market panic.
- **Community Call**: Coinbase recommended that Bitcoin core developers and ecosystem participants begin relevant standards discussions and testing early, avoiding rushed responses when quantum threats actually materialize.

**[Panorama]**

Coinbase's latest statement marks the beginning of mainstream crypto infrastructure moving post-quantum migration from theoretical discussion toward practical action planning. The Bitcoin network has relied on elliptic curve signatures to secure on-chain assets since its inception, and the rapid evolution of quantum computing places this foundational assumption under medium-to-long-term challenge. The complexity of the migration effort lies not only in the cryptographic algorithm replacement at the technical layer, but also in the difficulty of coordinating large-scale consensus within Bitcoin's decentralized governance ecosystem, and how to protect user assets from attacks during the upgrade window. Coinbase's prioritization of flexible design reflects a strategic preference for diversified signature scheme coexistence rather than betting on a single standard. In the long run, this issue may drive the Bitcoin ecosystem to face its first real cryptographic-layer hard fork discussion, producing demonstration effects for long-term security planning across other blockchain networks.

**[Multiple Perspectives]**

- **Coinbase Position**: Emphasizes early planning to avoid reactive action when quantum threats materialize, and recommends flexible multi-signature schemes to reduce single-point risk.
- **Bitcoin Core Developer View**: Some developers argue current quantum threats have not reached the level requiring immediate action, with priorities remaining on improving network throughput and reducing fees.
- **Academic Research View**: Quantum computing researchers believe quantum computers capable of breaking current cryptography may emerge by the end of this decade or early next, urging the industry to accelerate related standards development and standardization processes.
