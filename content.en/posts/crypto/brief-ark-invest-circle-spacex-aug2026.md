## Core Summary

Ark Invest, founded by "Woman Warren Buffett" Cathie Wood, made large-scale investment moves following Q2 earnings reports, purchasing $17 million worth of Circle shares and $20 million in SpaceX stock. This move demonstrates the investment firm's long-term optimism about crypto financial infrastructure and the space industry.

## Event Details

Ark Invest's investment decisions were based on in-depth analysis of both companies' fundamentals. Circle, as the issuer of the USDC stablecoin, reported Q2 revenue of $701 million, with USDC circulation increasing to $73.3 billion. Despite SpaceX's Q2 capital expenditure reaching $18.4 billion, which concerned investors, revenue growth remained strong.

Notably, Circle simultaneously announced that nine institutions including BlackRock and DTCC have become its Arc validators, further solidifying its position in the crypto financial infrastructure space.
