## Core Summary

Data analytics firm CryptoQuant points out that Bitcoin's sustained price recovery requires growth in USDT stablecoin supply as support.

## Event Details

According to CryptoQuant's latest research report, there is a close correlation between Bitcoin prices and market liquidity. The report data shows that when USDT supply increases, Bitcoin prices tend to rise; conversely, when USDT supply decreases, the market faces downward pressure.

Analysts explain that as the main trading pair and liquidity source in the cryptocurrency market, changes in USDT supply directly affect market purchasing power. Currently, USDT supply is in a relatively stable state, failing to provide sufficient incremental funds to push Bitcoin through key resistance levels.

The report also points out that institutional investors' demand for Bitcoin allocation is increasing, but retail investor participation remains low. This structural imbalance may lead to increased market volatility, requiring more stablecoin inflows to balance supply and demand.

## Panoramic Perspective

This analysis reveals the deep connection between the cryptocurrency market and the traditional financial system. As a bridge connecting traditional fiat currencies and crypto assets, the issuance mechanism and regulatory policies of stablecoins have systemic impacts on the entire market ecosystem.

From a monetary policy perspective, changes in USDT supply reflect market sentiment toward risk assets. When investors expect market rises, they exchange USDT in advance for buying; when market sentiment is pessimistic, USDT is redeemed for fiat currency, causing supply contraction. This positive feedback mechanism may amplify market volatility.

Regulators' attention to stablecoins is increasing, with many countries developing relevant regulatory frameworks. In the future, transparency and compliance of stablecoin issuance will become key factors affecting market confidence. If major stablecoin issuers face regulatory pressure, it may trigger chain reactions affecting liquidity across the entire crypto market.

Furthermore, the rise of decentralized stablecoins and other alternatives may change the current market landscape. A diversified stablecoin ecosystem helps reduce systemic risks from single issuers but also brings new complexities.

## Multiple Viewpoints

Bullish analysts believe that the current stable USDT supply is temporary, and incremental funds will gradually enter as market confidence recovers. They point out that historical data shows every bull cycle has been accompanied by significant growth in stablecoin supply.

Bearish views argue that relying on stablecoin supply growth to drive price increases is an unsustainable model. Critics point out that this model is vulnerable to regulatory intervention and issuer credit risks, lacking intrinsic value support.

Neutral parties suggest investors focus on broader market indicators, including on-chain data, institutional holding changes, and macroeconomic environment, rather than relying solely on stablecoin supply as a single indicator.
