[Brief] Grayscale has filed a Worldcoin ETF application with the US Securities and Exchange Commission. If approved, it would be the first exchange-traded fund based on a biometric identity protocol. Worldcoin, co-founded by OpenAI's Sam Altman, distributes tokens via iris scanning. The project has faced privacy concerns in multiple countries.
