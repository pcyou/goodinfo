## Core Summary

Blockchain project Harmony experienced a severe security attack when an attacker exploited a system vulnerability to mint approximately 4 billion ONE tokens during Asian trading hours, equivalent to 25% of the token's total circulating supply. This unprecedented attack caused the ONE token price to instantly plummet 26%, triggering market panic. The Harmony team has urgently coordinated with major exchanges to freeze related funds and is preparing a software fix.

## Incident Details

According to CoinDesk reports, the attack occurred during early Asian trading sessions when the attacker successfully exploited a smart contract vulnerability in the Harmony blockchain to mint a large quantity of ONE tokens without authorization. Upon discovering the anomaly, the Harmony team immediately released a statement indicating they are working closely with multiple cryptocurrency exchanges to freeze the flow of funds related to the attack.

Harmony's official statement noted: "We are fully addressing this security incident and have established emergency communication channels with major exchanges to ensure that tokens illegally minted by the attacker cannot be traded or transferred on exchanges. At the same time, our technical team is developing a software patch to seal the vulnerability and prevent similar incidents from occurring again."

Affected by this news, ONE token prices experienced violent fluctuations across multiple major exchanges, with declines reaching 26% in a short period. Trading data showed panic selling sentiment spreading, with trading volumes surging across multiple trading pairs.

## Panoramic Perspective

This Harmony security incident exposes the ongoing challenges blockchain projects face in smart contract security auditing. Although blockchain technology is known for decentralization and security, smart contract vulnerabilities remain one of the major risks facing the industry. The illegal minting of 4 billion tokens not only caused direct economic losses to the Harmony ecosystem but also seriously damaged investor confidence in the project.

From an industry perspective, this incident may trigger a re-examination of Layer 1 blockchain project security. Harmony, once a highly anticipated cross-chain project, has security vulnerabilities that could affect the stability of the entire DeFi ecosystem. Exchanges and DeFi protocols need to establish more comprehensive risk monitoring mechanisms to respond quickly when similar incidents occur.

Additionally, this incident highlights the importance of blockchain projects' emergency response capabilities. The Harmony team's ability to quickly cooperate with exchanges to freeze funds demonstrates certain crisis management capabilities, but pre-incident security precautions were clearly insufficient. In the future, projects need to invest more resources in security audits and bug bounty programs to prevent potential attacks.
