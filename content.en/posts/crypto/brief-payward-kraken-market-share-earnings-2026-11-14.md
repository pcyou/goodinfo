## Key Facts

- **Market share**: The share of global crypto spot trading rose to 6.5 percent in September, from 3.3 percent a year earlier.
- **Volume**: Quarterly transaction volume was $353 billion, down 30 percent year over year.
- **Profit**: Adjusted EBITDA fell to $60 million from $178.6 million a year earlier.
- **New measure**: Platform margin fell 12 percent to $487 million, a measure introduced this quarter to replace adjusted revenue, with a deduction for staking rewards paid out to customers.
- **Why the two figures differ**: Market share tracks the company's portion of global spot trading across its platforms in September, while quarterly volume includes spot, margin, equities, payments and certain derivatives.
- **Expansion strategy**: Over the past two years the company has used acquisitions to move beyond its core exchange business into derivatives, proprietary trading, tokenised assets and payments, with the aim of reducing reliance on transaction fees.

## Competing Views

**The bullish reading treats the share gain as improving competitive position.** In a period when industry-wide activity is weakening, roughly doubling the relative share suggests the product and liquidity continued to attract orders while competitors were under pressure, and that revenue from services beyond fees expanded as well.

**The cautious reading notes that this is a victory in share rather than in volume.** A thirty percent decline in total transaction value means the pie itself is shrinking, and a rising share is then partly a symptom of a deteriorating denominator. The sixty-six percent drop in adjusted profit far exceeds the fall in volume, which indicates that costs did not compress in step and that the scale economics are running in reverse.

**A third reading focuses on the change of accounting measure itself.** The company now reports platform margin in place of adjusted revenue, and that measure deducts staking rewards paid to customers. New measures tend to appear when a business needs to show the breadth of what it does, but they also make quarter-on-quarter comparison harder rather than easier.

## Panoramic Analysis

The value of this disclosure is that it lays out cleanly the narrative problem that has defined exchange business for two years: market share and market volume can move in opposite directions.

The first layer is that market share is a relative measure while transaction volume is absolute. When the two diverge, the common reading is that a winner is emerging as the industry consolidates. But when volume falls thirty percent and adjusted profit falls sixty-six percent while share doubles, the likelier explanation is that the denominator is collapsing faster than the numerator, meaning part of the share gain comes from shrinkage rather than from competitive success. The question worth asking is not how much share was gained but what happened to fee income per unit of volume and to the cost structure beneath it.

The second layer is that the transition of the revenue mix has reached the stage where it has to be demonstrated. Leading exchanges have spent two years using acquisitions to move income from pure transaction fees into derivatives, proprietary trading, payments and tokenised assets. The common feature of these businesses is lower correlation with market activity, alongside higher front-loaded investment, a heavier cost structure and greater regulatory sensitivity. The new platform margin measure is the accounting reflection of that transition.

The third layer concerns how staking rewards are deducted. Counting customer staking rewards on a net basis means giving up part of the return in order to retain assets. While rates are high and institutional money is searching for yield, that is a reasonable defensive move; under intensifying fee competition it is also a concession of margin.

Four indicators are worth tracking. The share of platform margin that comes from non-fee sources. Number of paying customers and average revenue per customer. Whether absolute spot transaction volume recovers as industry activity improves. And how the proportion of staking rewards deducted changes over subsequent quarters.
