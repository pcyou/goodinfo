[Brief] Bitcoin spot ETFs have recently returned to net capital outflows, while ether-related funds have extended their streak of net inflows, indicating diverging institutional sentiment toward the two largest crypto assets. According to CoinDesk, this shift in fund flows reflects cautious market sentiment toward Bitcoin's short-term trajectory and optimistic expectations for Ethereum's ecosystem development and technical upgrades.

Analysts note that the Bitcoin ETF outflows may be linked to escalating geopolitical tensions, with investors tending to reduce risk asset exposure amid rising uncertainty. Meanwhile, the continued inflows into ether funds may be driven by expectations of network upgrades and growing institutional interest in smart contract platforms.
