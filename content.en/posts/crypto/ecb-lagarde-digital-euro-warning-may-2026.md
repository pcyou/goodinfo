[Brief] European Central Bank President Christine Lagarde warned Friday that Europe should not simply replicate the US stablecoin model when launching the digital euro. She emphasized that Europe needs its own regulatory framework and monetary policy transmission mechanism, rather than relying on private-sector issuance.
