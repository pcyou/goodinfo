## Core Summary

According to CoinDesk, wallets linked to Trump Media and Technology Group now hold bitcoin equal to the amount previously disclosed as note collateral, raising speculation that all holdings may be pledged for loans.

## Event Details

CoinDesk tracking revealed that wallets associated with the Truth Social parent company recently moved approximately $165 million in bitcoin. After the transfer, these wallets hold bitcoin equal to the amount previously disclosed in filings as note collateral.

This discovery has sparked market speculation that Trump Media's bitcoin holdings may all be pledged as loan collateral rather than freely held assets. The company's upcoming 10-Q quarterly report will be key to verifying this speculation.

Using bitcoin as corporate asset collateral is not uncommon in the industry, but pledging all holdings is relatively rare and may indicate liquidity pressure or leveraged operations using bitcoin.
