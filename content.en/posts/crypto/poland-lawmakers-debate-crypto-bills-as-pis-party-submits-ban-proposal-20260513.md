## Poland Lawmakers Debate Crypto Bills as PiS Party Submits Ban Proposal

Polish lawmakers are debating cryptocurrency legislation while the PiS party has submitted a separate proposal to ban crypto, according to TheBlock. The development reflects divergent regulatory approaches across European nations.
