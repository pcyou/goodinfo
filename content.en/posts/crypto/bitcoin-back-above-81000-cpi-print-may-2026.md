## Bitcoin Rebounds Above $81,000 After CPI

CoinDesk reports that Bitcoin has climbed back above $81,000 following the hot US CPI inflation print. BNB and DOGE led gains among major cryptocurrencies. Despite the inflation beat, the crypto market rebounded, suggesting investors may have already priced in the inflation data and are now focusing on other positive signals.
