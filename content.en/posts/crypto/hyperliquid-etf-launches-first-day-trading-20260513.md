## First-Ever Hyperliquid ETF Launches with Solid First-Day Trading

**Summary:** The world's first Hyperliquid ETF has officially launched, with analysts calling its first-day trading performance "very solid," reflecting strong market interest in decentralized derivatives protocols.

---

According to reports, the world's first Hyperliquid ETF has officially launched and begun trading. Hyperliquid is a decentralized derivatives trading protocol with a substantial user base in the crypto space.

Analysts noted that the ETF's first-day trading performance was "very solid," showing growing interest from traditional investors in decentralized finance (DeFi) protocols.

As a decentralized perpetual contract trading platform, Hyperliquid has seen rapid growth in trading volume in recent years. The ETF launch provides traditional investors with a way to gain exposure to the Hyperliquid ecosystem without directly holding crypto tokens.

Industry observers believe that as more crypto-native protocols launch ETF products, the bridge between traditional finance and decentralized finance will continue to widen.
