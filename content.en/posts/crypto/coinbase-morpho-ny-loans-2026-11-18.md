Cryptocurrency exchange Coinbase announced a partnership with decentralized lending protocol Morpho to expand onchain loan services to users in New York State. This move marks a business breakthrough for America's largest cryptocurrency platform in regions with strict traditional financial regulation.

According to the announcement, users can access more flexible lending options through the Morpho protocol on the Coinbase platform. Morpho, as an emerging decentralized finance infrastructure, is known for its capital efficiency and risk management systems.

New York State has always been a challenging market for cryptocurrency businesses due to its strict BitLicense requirements. Coinbase's partnership demonstrates that innovative financial services under traditional regulatory frameworks remain achievable through compliant technology integration.
