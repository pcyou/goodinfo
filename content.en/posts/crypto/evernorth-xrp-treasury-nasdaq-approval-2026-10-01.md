## Evernorth Shareholders Approve $1 Billion XRP Treasury Deal, Clearing Path to Nasdaq Debut as XRP Institutionalization Accelerates

### Core Summary

According to The Block, shareholders of special purpose acquisition company Armada Acquisition Corp. II have formally approved the merger with XRP treasury company Evernorth. The milestone vote clears the key obstacle to Evernorth listing on Nasdaq. The company is expected to hold roughly 473 million XRP, making it one of the largest single-asset treasury platforms in the crypto industry since MicroStrategy. The deal also marks a new phase in the institutionalization of XRP.

### Event Details

According to The Block, Armada Acquisition Corp. II shareholders voted through the merger with Evernorth. The deal uses the SPAC reverse-merger structure, and upon completion Evernorth will list as an independent public company on the Nasdaq exchange, with the ticker symbol and exact listing date to be disclosed in further announcements following closing.

Evernorth is positioned as an institutional XRP treasury company. Its core strategy mirrors the way MicroStrategy has approached Bitcoin, systematically accumulating XRP through public market and over-the-counter transactions to build long-term holding and market-making capability. Upon closing, the company is expected to hold approximately 473 million XRP, a meaningful share of XRP's global circulating supply, making Evernorth one of the most influential institutional holders in the XRP ecosystem.

The merger also brought in support from multiple strategic investors and market makers. Industry sources say that after closing Evernorth will gain institutional-grade compliance framework, custody infrastructure, market-making network, and investor relations support, a meaningful shift from the operational mode of the SPAC shell stage. Evernorth also plans to deploy a portion of the proceeds into XRP ecosystem build-out, including DeFi protocols, market-making infrastructure, and cross-chain liquidity provision.

Notably, the Evernorth listing comes at a critical moment when U.S. regulators are clarifying their posture toward crypto treasury companies. Following the successful operation of spot Bitcoin exchange-traded products in the U.S. market, expectations for institutional pathways for other major coins such as XRP have risen significantly. The Evernorth listing will provide traditional equity market investors with their first public market vehicle for institutional-grade XRP exposure.

### Panoramic Analysis

The Evernorth listing is a pivotal moment in the institutionalization of XRP. Over the past several years, the XRP ecosystem has navigated a long legal dispute with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission and relistings at major exchanges, with relatively limited institutional access channels. As the first institutional treasury platform focused exclusively on XRP, Evernorth's listing will formally bring XRP into the U.S. traditional capital markets compliance framework and meaningfully broaden the entry path for institutional capital.

At a broader crypto asset institutionalization level, the deal reflects a fundamental shift in industry ecosystem over the past two years. MicroStrategy's success with Bitcoin triggered a wave of imitative innovation that extended from Bitcoin to Ethereum, Solana, XRP, and other major assets. Institutional treasury platforms not only give traditional equity investors indirect crypto exposure, they also provide a more stable long-term holding entity for crypto assets themselves, helping reduce volatility and lift the institutional-grade pricing efficiency of asset prices.

From the XRP ecosystem's own perspective, the listing will bring meaningful upgrades to liquidity structure through deeper market making and institutional custody standards. Institutional-grade compliant custody, audit disclosure, and market-making network will help attract pensions, family offices, and traditional asset managers to include XRP in diversified allocations. At the same time, Evernorth's pledged ecosystem build-out spending may provide new development momentum for DeFi, cross-chain, and real-world asset applications on the XRP Ledger.

Deeper implications run through the policy shaping of crypto treasury models by U.S. regulators. Regulators will observe Evernorth's actual performance on information disclosure, insider trading control, and market manipulation identification, and use that to assess whether more specific crypto treasury rules need to be introduced within the existing securities law framework. The process will not only affect the future of XRP-related products, it will also create a demonstration effect for institutionalization pathways across the broader crypto industry.

### Multi-Perspective Comparison

Industry voices supportive of the Evernorth listing argue that the deal will bring the strongest institutional infrastructure support the XRP ecosystem has ever seen, meaningfully improving liquidity, market-making depth, and institutional holding ratios. Supporters note that the MicroStrategy model has proven sustainable, that extending it to XRP is a natural next step, and that institutional capital entry will provide solid support for XRP's long-term value.

Cautious voices emphasize that as a single-asset treasury, Evernorth's stock performance will be highly correlated with XRP price, and investors are effectively taking on a composite exposure that layers listed company operational risk with XRP market risk. Cautious observers also point out that SPAC mergers commonly face valuation disputes, lock-up expiry pressure, and shallow initial market making, and that whether Evernorth can maintain healthy secondary market performance after closing remains to be seen in the months ahead.

Regulatory voices focus on how Evernorth will meet disclosure and market manipulation control standards comparable to traditional listed companies after listing. Regulators say they will closely track Evernorth's financial reporting, XRP holding change disclosures, and large shareholder selling pace to ensure investors receive a fair and transparent information environment. Some regulators are also evaluating whether to introduce additional capital adequacy and liquidity requirements for crypto treasury listed companies.

Within the XRP community, views are diverse. Supporters believe Evernorth will bring institutional capital and mainstream market recognition to XRP, meaningfully lifting XRP's long-term value. But some community members worry that the concentrated holding by institutional capital could create selling pressure at some point in the future, exacerbating market volatility. The community broadly expects Evernorth to maintain high transparency after listing and to channel part of its returns back into technical and application development across the XRP ecosystem.
