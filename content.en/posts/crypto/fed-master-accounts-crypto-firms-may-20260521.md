The Federal Reserve has proposed granting limited master account access to crypto firms that have long sought such privileges. This move is seen as a significant signal of shifting US regulatory stance toward the crypto industry. Master accounts allow institutions to directly use the Fed's payment clearing system, and gaining this access would substantially improve crypto firms' operational efficiency and fund liquidity. The proposal is currently in the public comment period.
