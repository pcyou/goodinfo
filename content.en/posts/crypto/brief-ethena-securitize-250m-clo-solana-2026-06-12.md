## Core Summary

Stablecoin protocol Ethena Labs announced it will allocate $250 million to Securitize's tokenized AAA collateralized loan obligation fund, deployed simultaneously on the Solana blockchain. This marks the entry of institutional-grade structured credit products into decentralized finance.

## Details

Securitize's STAC fund is one of its in-house institutional offerings, providing on-chain investors access to floating-rate structured credit. Ethena Labs' large allocation validates market demand for tokenized credit products and brings high-credit-rated traditional financial assets to the Solana ecosystem.
