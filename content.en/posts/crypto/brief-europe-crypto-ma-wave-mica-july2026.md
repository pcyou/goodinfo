## [Brief]

According to CoinDesk, as the EU's Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA) enters full implementation and the UK finalizes its crypto regulatory framework, stringent requirements could drive a new wave of mergers and acquisitions in the crypto industry. Analysts note that rising compliance costs will force smaller crypto firms to seek acquisition or establish closer partnerships with banks to remain competitive. This trend is expected to accelerate industry consolidation over the next 12 to 18 months.
