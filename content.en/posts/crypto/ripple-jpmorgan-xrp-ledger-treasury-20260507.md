## Ripple, JPMorgan Use XRP Ledger for Tokenized Treasury Transfer

Ripple and JPMorgan jointly executed a cross-border transfer of a tokenized Treasurys fund on the XRP Ledger, representing an example of crypto firms and Wall Street institutions exploring 24/7 settlement systems.
