## Core Summary

Ethereum Foundation research team has officially selected a post-quantum secure hash algorithm, preparing for potential threats that future quantum computers may pose to existing cryptographic systems. This technical decision will impact Ethereum network's long-term security and may serve as a reference standard for other blockchain projects.

## Event Details

After multiple rounds of evaluation and public discussion, the Ethereum Foundation cryptography research team announced selection of a SHA-3 variant as the core component of its post-quantum migration strategy. This algorithm can resist known quantum computing attack methods, including Grover's algorithm acceleration threat against hash functions.

The research team stated that while the timeline for practical quantum computers remains uncertain, proactive preparation is crucial. The new algorithm will be integrated into the Ethereum protocol through gradual upgrades, ensuring compatibility with existing systems while preparing for potential future hard forks.

## Panoramic Perspective

Quantum computing's potential threat to blockchain security has been a focus of academic and industry attention. Traditional public-key encryption systems like RSA and ECC will become vulnerable in the face of sufficiently powerful quantum computers, while hash functions, although relatively more resistant to quantum attacks, still need upgrading to specially designed post-quantum versions.

Ethereum's move demonstrates its forward-thinking approach as a leading smart contract platform. Compared to waiting passively until threats become imminent, proactive technical preparation can reduce future upgrade risks and costs. Simultaneously, this sends a signal to the entire industry: post-quantum security should not be viewed as a distant issue, but as a technical roadmap requiring immediate planning.

From an implementation difficulty perspective, hash algorithm replacement is simpler than consensus mechanism or virtual machine upgrades, as it doesn't involve state migration complexity. However, any changes to underlying cryptographic components require extremely careful testing and verification to avoid introducing new security vulnerabilities.

## Multiple Viewpoints

**Ethereum Foundation Position**: Chief researcher emphasized that this selection was based on rigorous academic evaluation and community feedback. The team will continue monitoring quantum computing progress, adjusting technical routes when necessary. The open and transparent decision-making process ensures solution credibility.

**Academic Evaluation**: Cryptography experts generally recognized the timeliness of this decision. Some scholars pointed out that other major blockchain projects should also accelerate post-quantum migration research to avoid creating security weaknesses.

**Industry Response**: Some developers expressed concerns about compatibility issues from upgrades, particularly for decentralized applications relying on specific hash functions. The Foundation promised to provide detailed migration guides and technical support.

**Competing Project Dynamics**: The Bitcoin community is also discussing similar issues, but due to its conservative technical evolution style, expected action pace will be slower. In contrast, Ethereum's flexible governance structure enables faster response to emerging threats.
