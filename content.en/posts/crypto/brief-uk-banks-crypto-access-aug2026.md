## Core Summary

UK lawmakers expressed strong dissatisfaction with banks refusing banking services to compliant crypto firms, calling it "one of the single biggest barriers to growth." A parliamentary committee has formally demanded major banks explain their crypto client policies.

## Event Overview

According to The Block and CoinDesk, a UK parliamentary committee recently questioned executives from major banks about why they refuse basic banking services to crypto companies registered with the Financial Conduct Authority. Lawmakers noted that many compliant crypto firms have been forced to relocate operations overseas due to lack of bank accounts, threatening the UK's position as a fintech hub.
