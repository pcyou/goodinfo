## [Brief] Ethereum Foundation Cuts and Departures Not a Crisis, Founder Joe Lubin Says

According to CoinDesk, Consensys founder Joe Lubin addressed market concerns over recent Ethereum Foundation layoffs and personnel changes. Lubin stated that these changes are not a crisis but a natural evolution of the foundation's role. He argued that the Ethereum Foundation should narrow its scope and focus more on stewarding the network's core technology and values rather than expanding its operational footprint. The comments aim to reassure the market about the stability of the Ethereum ecosystem.
