## O'Leary Warns Tokenization Needs Regulatory Framework

"Shark Tank" investor Kevin O'Leary said Wall Street's current tokenization boom lacks a clear crypto regulatory framework, and institutional investors still view it as too risky. He noted that widespread institutional adoption cannot happen until the US establishes clear cryptocurrency compliance standards.
