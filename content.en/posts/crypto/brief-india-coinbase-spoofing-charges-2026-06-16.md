## Core Summary

India's Directorate of Enforcement has filed charges against Chirag Tomar and seven associates for an alleged $20 million spoofing scheme on the Coinbase cryptocurrency exchange. The group allegedly created fake buy and sell orders to artificially influence prices and profit from the manipulation.
