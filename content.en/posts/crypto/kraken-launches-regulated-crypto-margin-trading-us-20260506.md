## Kraken Launches Regulated Margin Trading

Crypto exchange Kraken announced the launch of regulated spot margin trading in the United States, following its recent acquisition of Bitnomial. The move marks a significant step in Kraken's compliance strategy, providing US investors with more institutional-grade crypto trading tools.
