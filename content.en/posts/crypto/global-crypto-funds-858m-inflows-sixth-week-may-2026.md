## Crypto Fund Inflows Continue for Sixth Week

Global crypto investment products recorded $858 million in inflows for the sixth consecutive week, according to CoinShares. US products and Bitcoin ETFs led the inflows.

Progress on the Clarity Act has boosted sentiment, with institutional investors continuing to add positions. This marks the longest streak of positive weekly inflows since the beginning of the year.
