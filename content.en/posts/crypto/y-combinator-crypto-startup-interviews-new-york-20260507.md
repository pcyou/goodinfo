Prominent startup incubator Y Combinator has held crypto startup interviews in New York for the first time, signaling the crypto industry's expansion from Silicon Valley to financial centers.

Previously, Y Combinator's crypto-related interviews were primarily held in the San Francisco Bay Area. The choice of New York reflects Wall Street's growing interest in crypto technology.

Startups interviewed span DeFi, stablecoin infrastructure, tokenization, and other sectors. This trend suggests accelerating convergence between traditional finance and crypto technology.
