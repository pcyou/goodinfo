Tokenized real-world assets (RWA) platform Ondo Finance announced Tuesday the unexpected death of founder Nathan Allman. Longtime president Ian De Bode will take over management. Ondo Finance is one of the largest RWA platforms in crypto, managing billions in tokenized assets.
