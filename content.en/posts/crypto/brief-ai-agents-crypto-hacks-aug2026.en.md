## Core Summary

According to TheBlock, multiple crypto industry leaders have warned that as AI agent technology rapidly develops, current billion-dollar scale crypto asset hacks could soon look trivial. This perspective has sparked deep discussion about security risks at the intersection of AI and blockchain.

## Key Details

Industry experts point out that AI agents possess capabilities to autonomously execute complex transactions, identify smart contract vulnerabilities, and even coordinate multi-step attacks. Unlike traditional hackers requiring manual programming and execution, AI agents can scan targets 24/7, automatically discovering and exploiting security weaknesses.

However, the industry has also identified three major barriers to large-scale AI agent adoption: first, trust issues — whether users are willing to entrust asset control to AI systems; second, AI hallucination risks — erroneous decisions could lead to massive losses; third, unclear legal liability — responsibility attribution is difficult to define when AI agents cause losses.

## Panoramic Analysis

This warning reveals deep risks at the AI-crypto asset intersection. The crypto industry has already suffered billions in hacker attack losses, but if AI agents are maliciously exploited, attack scale and frequency could grow exponentially. This requires the industry to establish stronger security protection mechanisms and regulatory frameworks while pursuing technological innovation.
