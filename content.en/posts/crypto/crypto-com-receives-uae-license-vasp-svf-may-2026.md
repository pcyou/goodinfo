## Crypto.com Gets UAE Regulatory License

Crypto.com's UAE entity became the first VASP to receive a UAE central bank SVF license tied to Dubai government crypto payment services.

This marks another milestone in the UAE's efforts to position itself as a leading crypto hub in the Middle East.
