## Core Summary

Bitcoin broke through the $70,000 milestone during New York trading on August 19, reaching its highest price since June 2026. Ethereum gained approximately 10% in tandem, indicating broad market recovery. The rally was driven by two main factors: the US Treasury's accelerated bond buyback program injecting liquidity, and the SEC advancing a crypto regulatory framework proposal that markets interpreted as a signal of clearer regulatory environment.

## Event Details

According to real-time data from CoinDesk and The Block, Bitcoin surged rapidly on the evening of August 19, breaking through the $70,000 psychological resistance level from the $68,000 range, touching near $70,250 at its peak. This marks Bitcoin's return above the $70,000 threshold after nearly two months.

**US Treasury Buyback Program Boosts Markets**

The US Treasury announced this week it will accelerate its bond buyback operations, planning to increase purchases of short-term Treasuries over the coming months. Markets interpreted this as a signal of liquidity injection into the financial system, benefiting risk assets broadly. The Wall Street Journal reported that Treasury yields fell noticeably after the announcement, with the 10-year yield dropping below 4%, as capital flowed into stocks and cryptocurrencies.

**SEC Crypto Regulatory Proposal Signals Positive Direction**

Simultaneously, the Securities and Exchange Commission is advancing a crypto asset regulatory framework proposal. While specific details remain undisclosed, the market broadly views this as providing clearer regulatory guidance for crypto assets, reducing industry uncertainty. The Block reported that the SEC's proposal could clear obstacles for institutional investors entering the crypto market.

**Ethereum Rises in Tandem**

Ethereum showed particularly strong performance in this rally, gaining approximately 10% in a single day, indicating renewed activity in decentralized finance and NFT markets. Analysts noted Ethereum's rise was also supported by Layer 2 network expansion and improved staking yields.

## Panoramic Perspective

Bitcoin breaking $70,000 represents more than a price milestone—it reflects a fundamental identity shift in the crypto market from "speculative asset" to "mainstream financial asset." The US Treasury's buyback operations, while originally intended to manage Treasury market liquidity, objectively provided liquidity support for risk assets including crypto. This linkage between traditional monetary policy tools and the crypto market indicates blurring boundaries between the two.

From a regulatory standpoint, the SEC advancing its crypto framework proposal marks a strategic shift from "enforcement-first" to "rule-making" approach. Over the past two years, the SEC primarily regulated crypto through enforcement actions, creating significant policy uncertainty. The pivot to proactive rulemaking, while specific provisions remain to be seen, represents a directional change sufficient to boost market confidence.

Notably, this rally differs in logic from the post-January 2024 Bitcoin spot ETF approval surge. That was driven by incremental capital inflows causing valuation reassessment, while this round is primarily sentiment recovery driven by liquidity and regulatory expectations. This means future trajectory will depend heavily on macroeconomic conditions and actual regulatory policy implementation.

## Multiple Perspectives

**Optimistic View**

Grayscale Investments believes Bitcoin's break above $70,000 signals structural market improvement. The firm noted in its latest research that institutional participation is increasing, with enhanced custody infrastructure lowering entry barriers. Grayscale projects that if the SEC's regulatory proposal lands within the year, Bitcoin could test $80,000 by end of 2026.

**Cautious View**

JPMorgan analysts take a more cautious stance. The bank warned in client briefings that Bitcoin's rally lacks fundamental support, being primarily liquidity and sentiment-driven. JPMorgan noted that Bitcoin's volatility remains significantly higher than traditional assets, and prices could reverse quickly if liquidity conditions tighten or regulatory policy shifts.

**Regulatory Voice**

The SEC Chair stated in a public speech that regulators are working to balance innovation protection with investor protection. She emphasized that crypto markets need clear rule frameworks while warning that enforcement against fraud and market manipulation would not be relaxed. Markets interpreted this as a "moderate regulation" signal.

## Conclusion

Bitcoin breaking $70,000 marks the crypto market entering a new phase. Deepening linkage between traditional financial policy tools and crypto markets, along with gradually clarifying regulatory frameworks, are reshaping crypto asset pricing logic. However, high volatility characteristics persist, and investors should remain alert to risks from liquidity environment changes.
