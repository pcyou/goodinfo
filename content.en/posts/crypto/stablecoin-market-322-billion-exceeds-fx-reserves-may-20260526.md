The total stablecoin market value has reached $322 billion, exceeding the official foreign exchange reserves of 95 nations worldwide. This marks the continued expansion of the crypto ecosystem's "shadow banking" scale, with user-held dollar and fiat assets increasingly operating outside traditional banking channels.
