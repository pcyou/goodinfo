## [Flash] Senate Banking Committee Sets Date for Sweeping Crypto Legislation Vote

The US Senate Banking Committee has announced it will hold a markup hearing vote on comprehensive crypto market structure legislation. This marks the committee's second attempt to advance the bill, signaling a critical phase in the development of US crypto regulatory framework. Markets expect the legislation to have significant implications for the cryptocurrency industry once passed.
