## Core Summary

Metaplanet CEO publicly denied recent market rumors about the company selling $320 million worth of bitcoin. The CEO reaffirmed that the company still holds 43,000 BTC and emphasized its continued long-term holding and accumulation strategy. This clarification aims to stabilize investor confidence and eliminate market concerns.

## Event Details

According to CoinDesk, Metaplanet's CEO recently publicly responded to market rumors, clearly stating the company has not sold any bitcoin holdings. Previously, rumors circulated that the company had liquidated approximately $320 million worth of bitcoin, triggering investor concerns.

In a statement, the CEO emphasized that the company currently still holds 43,000 bitcoin, consistent with previously disclosed holdings. He stated the company will continue executing its bitcoin accumulation strategy, maintaining it as a core asset allocation.

Metaplanet is one of Japan's earliest listed companies to adopt the "bitcoin treasury strategy," following MicroStrategy's model of using bitcoin as a primary reserve asset. This clarification is crucial for maintaining market confidence, especially in the current volatile crypto market environment.

## Panoramic Perspective

Metaplanet's bitcoin strategy reflects the shift in Japanese corporate attitudes toward digital assets. As Asia's first enterprise tolarge-scale adopt the bitcoin treasury model, its movements serve as a bellwether for the entire Asian crypto market.

From an investor perspective, listed companies including bitcoin on balance sheets brings both potential returns and increased financial risk. Balancingreturns with risk in volatility management is an ongoing challenge for such enterprises.

This incident also highlights the characteristics of information dissemination in crypto markets. Unverified rumors can quickly trigger market volatility, underscoring the importance of transparency and timely information disclosure.
