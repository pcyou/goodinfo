## [Brief] Invesco Files for Tokenized Fund Targeting Stablecoin Reserve Market

Invesco, the global asset manager with $2.5 trillion in assets under management, has filed for a tokenized fund. The fund will invest primarily in U.S. Treasuries, repurchase agreements and cash equivalents, maintaining a stable $1 net asset value. The target market is stablecoin issuers' reserve management needs, marking a deepening push by traditional finance giants into blockchain assets.
