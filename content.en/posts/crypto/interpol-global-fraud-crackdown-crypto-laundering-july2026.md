## Core Summary

INTERPOL has coordinated a massive global anti-fraud operation with law enforcement agencies from multiple countries, successfully arresting over 5,800 criminal suspects. The operation uncovered a crypto money laundering network using cross-chain swap technology to hide funds, with associated wallets processing up to $122.5 million in illegal funds over 10 months.

## Event Overview

According to TheBlock, this global law enforcement action targeted various fraud crimes including investment scams, phishing, and business fraud. Law enforcement discovered that criminal organizations are increasingly using cryptocurrencies for fund transfers and laundering, particularly through cross-chain swap technology to move assets between different blockchains, increasing tracking difficulty.

The uncovered money laundering network used specialized cross-chain swap protocols, processing $122.5 million over 10 months. This discovery highlights the scale and complexity of crypto money laundering activities, and demonstrates the importance of international law enforcement cooperation in combating such crimes.

## Panoramic Perspective

This operation reflects that global law enforcement agencies are intensifying efforts against crypto crimes. The exposure of cross-chain money laundering technology is prompting regulators and technology companies to accelerate development of tracking tools. In the future, anti-money laundering compliance requirements may further expand to decentralized finance sectors.
