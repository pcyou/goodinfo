## Overview

AI safety company Anthropic has issued a public warning regarding unauthorized exposure of its stock in trading markets. Meanwhile, token markets trading on Anthropic equity imply a valuation approaching the trillion-dollar threshold.

## Token Markets Price in Massive Valuation

Tokens linked to Anthropic equity have been trading actively in crypto and prediction markets. Market analysts note that the prices imply extremely high expectations for the company's future earnings, with the implied overall valuation nearing the one-trillion-dollar mark.

## Anthropic's Warning

Anthropic has officially stated that it has not authorized any platform to issue or trade tokens representing company equity. The company cautioned investors that participating in such unauthorized trading could expose them to legal risks and financial losses.

## Industry Impact

This episode highlights the trend of AI company valuation bubble. As the AI boom continues, market expectations for top AI companies keep climbing, but whether actual business models and profitability can support such high valuations remains highly questionable.

Analysts note that the high valuations in token markets reflect speculative sentiment rather than a rational assessment of company fundamentals.
