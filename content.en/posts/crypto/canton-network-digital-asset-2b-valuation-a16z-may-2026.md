## Digital Asset Seeks Major Funding Round

Canton Network's Digital Asset is targeting a $2 billion valuation in a new funding round led by a16z crypto, according to Bloomberg.

Sources familiar with the matter told Bloomberg the fundraiser is expected to close in the coming weeks and the final amount might vary. Digital Asset is a major player in enterprise-grade blockchain infrastructure.
