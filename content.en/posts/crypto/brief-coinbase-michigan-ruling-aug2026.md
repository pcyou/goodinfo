## Core Summary

A Michigan judge has denied cryptocurrency exchange Coinbase's request to block state enforcement of a sports event contract. The ruling goes against the exchange's attempt to avoid state-level regulation of prediction markets, setting an important precedent for crypto regulation in the United States.

## Event Details

The judge, referred to as ruling on the "Applesauce" case, rejected Coinbase's legal arguments that federal law should preempt state enforcement actions. The decision means Michigan authorities can proceed with enforcement of their sports event contract regulations against the crypto exchange.

This ruling is significant for the broader debate over prediction market regulation in the United States, as it reinforces state-level authority over crypto activities even when federal frameworks may be ambiguous.
