## Coinbase CLO Bullish on Stablecoin Legislation

Coinbase Chief Legal Officer Paul Grewal said at the Consensus conference that the Clarity Act will pass this summer. He backed the Tillis-Alsobrooks stablecoin compromise and urged banks to accept the deal framework.
