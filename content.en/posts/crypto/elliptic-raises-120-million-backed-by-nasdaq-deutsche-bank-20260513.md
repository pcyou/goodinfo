## Elliptic Raises $120 Million Backed by Nasdaq, Deutsche Bank

Blockchain analytics firm Elliptic has closed a $120 million Series D funding round backed by Nasdaq and Deutsche Bank, according to TheBlock. The company leverages AI technology to enhance crypto security and compliance, serving exchanges and financial institutions.
