## Core Summary

Crypto brokerage firm Alpaca has raised $135 million to develop infrastructure for tokenized stocks. The funding round reflects growing institutional confidence in bringing traditional securities to blockchain-based systems.

## Event Details

According to CoinDesk, Alpaca closed a $135 million funding round dedicated to building tokenized stock infrastructure. The company aims to enable traditional equities to be traded on blockchain networks, reducing settlement times and increasing market accessibility.

The investment signals that tokenization of real-world assets continues to attract significant capital, with traditional financial instruments increasingly moving on-chain.
