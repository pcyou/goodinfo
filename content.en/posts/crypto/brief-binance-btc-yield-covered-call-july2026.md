Binance has launched a new product called "BTC Yield," designed exclusively for users who already hold bitcoin. The product employs a covered call strategy to provide users with additional income sources.

According to CoinDesk, the BTC Yield product targets existing bitcoin holders. It uses a covered call strategy, allowing users to earn additional returns while holding their bitcoin positions.

This launch reflects the growing maturity of the cryptocurrency market, where users are seeking more diversified income sources beyond simple holding strategies. Covered call strategies are widely used in traditional finance, and Binance's introduction of this approach to crypto provides bitcoin holders with new value realization methods.

The introduction of yield products marks an important evolution toward more mature financial products in the crypto space. Institutional and conservative investors often focus on stable returns rather than pure capital appreciation, and the expansion of yield products helps attract more traditional capital into the crypto market.
