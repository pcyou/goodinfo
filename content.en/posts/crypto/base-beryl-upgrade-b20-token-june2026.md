## Core Summary

Base has announced its Beryl upgrade will go live on mainnet on June 25, 2026. The upgrade introduces the B20 native token standard and reduces withdrawal wait times from seven days to five.

## Analysis

As Coinbase's core Ethereum ecosystem product, this upgrade signals accelerating technical iteration to capture L2 market share. The B20 standard launch may trigger a token standard competition among L2 networks.
