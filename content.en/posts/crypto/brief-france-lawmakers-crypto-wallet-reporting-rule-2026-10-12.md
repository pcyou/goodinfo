**[Brief]**

The French legislature passed a supportive vote on October 12 requiring mandatory reporting for crypto wallet holders with assets exceeding €100,000 (approximately $112,000). Failure to declare qualifying assets could result in fines of up to €10,000. The rule marks a further tightening of crypto asset transparency regulation in a major EU member state.
