**[Summary]**

A U.S. federal judge denied on October 16 a joint motion by Gemini Trust and the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) to vacate a prior $5 million settlement, underscoring the difficulty of unwinding regulatory consent decrees in the crypto sector.

**[Event Details]**

- **Background**: Gemini had previously reached a $5 million settlement with the CFTC over allegations of derivatives-related rule violations; Gemini and the CFTC subsequently filed a joint motion to vacate the agreement.
- **Court ruling**: The federal judge ruled that Gemini and the CFTC had not provided sufficient grounds to overturn the original settlement, denying the joint motion and leaving the original consent decree in force.
- **Industry impact**: The ruling raises the bar for crypto firms seeking to unwind settlements with U.S. regulators, suggesting regulators are tightening execution of consent decrees.
- **Next steps**: Gemini may pursue further appeal or negotiate amendments with the CFTC, but in the near term the original settlement will continue to serve as a key compliance benchmark.
