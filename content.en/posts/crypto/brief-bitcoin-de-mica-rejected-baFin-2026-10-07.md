**[Core Summary]** According to Cointelegraph, the German Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin) officially rejected cryptocurrency exchange Bitcoin.de's MiCA compliance application. The company has suspended trading services since the start of regulatory review, and the rejection means its trading business is unlikely to resume in the short term, producing clear impact on the German local crypto market.

**[Event Details]**
- **Regulatory Action**: BaFin officially rejected Bitcoin.de's MiCA compliance application after completing reviews of anti-money laundering, internal governance, and client fund segregation.
- **Direct Impact**: Bitcoin.de will continue to suspend new trading business; existing user assets are temporarily unaffected.
- **Industry Impact**: Germany is one of the largest cryptocurrency markets in Europe; tighter regulation will affect the compliance rhythm of multiple German local exchanges and service providers.
- **Follow-up**: Bitcoin.de stated it will adjust business structure based on feedback and reapply, but a short-term relaunch looks unlikely.
