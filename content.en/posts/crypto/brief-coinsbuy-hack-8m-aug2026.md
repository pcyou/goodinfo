## Core Summary

Cryptocurrency exchange Coinsbuy suffered a coordinated two-blockchain attack, losing approximately $8 million in assets. Security researchers say the attackers simultaneously targeted two different blockchains, demonstrating highly sophisticated attack techniques.

## Event Details

According to CoinDesk, Coinsbuy exchange confirmed a security incident on August 10, with attackers exploiting smart contract vulnerabilities to launch simultaneous attacks on two different blockchains. Preliminary investigations show losses of approximately $8 million.

Security teams are tracking fund flows and working with blockchain analytics firms to attempt to freeze stolen assets. Coinsbuy has suspended deposit and withdrawal services on the affected blockchains and stated it will fully compensate affected users.

## Panoramic Perspective

This security incident once again highlights the security challenges facing cryptocurrency exchanges. Unlike single-blockchain attacks, coordinated dual attacks require attackers to possess cross-chain technical capabilities and complex vulnerability exploitation knowledge.

From an industry security perspective, exchanges need to strengthen smart contract audits and multi-chain security protections. As cross-chain bridges and multi-layer chain architectures become more prevalent, the attack surface is expanding, and security protection strategies need to be upgraded accordingly.
