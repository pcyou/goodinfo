**[Core Summary]**

Tracking data shows institutional Ether ETFs have recorded net outflows for nine consecutive trading days with the cumulative outflow reaching a notable level for the year, while Solana funds — which had posted 14 straight weeks of net inflows — registered their first weekly net outflow in the same window. Bitcoin ETFs also booked weekly outflows in the same period, pointing to a broad-based softening in institutional positioning across major crypto assets.

**[Event Details]**

- **Ether ETFs**: Nine consecutive trading days of net outflows, with the cumulative figure at a notable year-to-date level, reflecting a marked cooling of institutional sentiment.
- **Solana funds**: The previously unbroken streak of 14 consecutive weeks of net inflows has been broken, with the most recent week posting the first net outflow in months.
- **Bitcoin ETFs**: Logged weekly net outflows over the most recent trading week, moving in the same direction as Ether and Solana products.
- **Market observation**: The rare synchronized softening of the three major crypto ETFs is being read by some analysts as a combination of short-term institutional profit-taking and a broader decline in risk appetite.
- **What's next**: Next week's fund size and holdings reports will be the key signal for whether fund flows are stabilizing, and will clarify the direction of any institutional rotation.
