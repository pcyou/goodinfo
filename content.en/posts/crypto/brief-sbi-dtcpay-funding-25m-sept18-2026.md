## Core Summary

Japanese financial giant SBI Group has led Singapore-headquartered stablecoin payments firm dtcpay's $25 million Series A financing. The new capital will primarily be deployed to expand Asia-Pacific business, strengthen cross-border payment infrastructure, and promote compliant integration of stablecoins into traditional payment scenarios. As a major player in Southeast Asia's stablecoin payments space, dtcpay's access to traditional finance capital will further accelerate compliance across the industry.
