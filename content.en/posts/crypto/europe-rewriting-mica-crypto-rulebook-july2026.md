# Europe Rewrites Landmark Crypto Rulebook MiCA as July 1 Deadline Passes

## Core Summary

The European Union has officially reached the hard deadline for full implementation of its Markets in Crypto-Assets regulation (MiCA) on July 1, but simultaneously, EU lawmakers have begun major revisions to the regulation. Revisions include relaxing compliance requirements for smaller crypto firms and adjusting the regulatory framework for decentralized finance (DeFi) protocols, reflecting ongoing tension between regulatory ideals and industry reality.

## Event Details

According to joint reporting from CoinDesk and The Block, MiCA was originally scheduled to require all crypto service providers operating in the EU to obtain full licenses by July 1. However, facing strong industry pushback and technical implementation challenges, the European Commission has proposed multiple revision packages.

Key revision directions include:
- Establishing "sandbox" exemption periods for smaller crypto companies below certain transaction volume thresholds
- Clarifying conditions under which DeFi protocols may be exempt from MiCA regulation
- Simplifying cross-border crypto service license mutual recognition procedures
- Adjusting reserve requirements for stablecoin issuers

The European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA) stated the revisions aim to "ensure the regulatory framework protects consumers and financial stability without stifling innovation."

## Panoramic Perspective

MiCA's revision journey is a microcosm of global crypto regulatory evolution. As the world's first comprehensive crypto asset regulatory framework, MiCA has been regarded as an industry standardization benchmark since its passage in 2023. However, problems exposed during implementation: excessive compliance costs, insufficient understanding of decentralized technology, and conflicts with existing financial regulations have forced lawmakers to continuously adjust.

This revision trend sends several important signals: first, regulators are shifting from "one-size-fits-all" to more refined layered regulation; second, the regulatory boundaries of decentralized finance are being gradually clarified; third, the EU is striving to balance consumer protection with maintaining competitiveness.

For the crypto industry, MiCA's revisions are both opportunity and challenge. More flexible regulatory frameworks will reduce compliance costs and encourage innovation, but constantly changing rules also increase business uncertainty. In the long run, a mature, stable, and predictable regulatory environment is the cornerstone of healthy industry development.

## Multiple Perspectives

**EU regulatory officials** say revisions are to "make regulations better adapt to rapidly changing technological realities," emphasizing this is a "living document" rather than rigid doctrine.

**Crypto industry associations** welcome the revision direction but call for more substantial relaxation, especially exemptions for DeFi protocols and open-source developers.

**Traditional financial institutions** worry that excessive regulatory relaxation may create space for illicit activities, undermining the consumer protection system MiCA established.

**Legal experts** note that MiCA's revision process itself proves the effectiveness of the "regulatory sandbox" approach: implement first, observe problems, then adjust, which is more pragmatic than attempting to craft perfect rules in one shot.
