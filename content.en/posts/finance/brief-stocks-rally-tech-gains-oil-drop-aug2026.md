## Core Summary

US stocks rallied sharply on August 4, with the Dow Jones Industrial Average surging over 500 points in a single session. Strong performance from major tech stocks combined with falling oil prices drove the market rebound. Meanwhile, joint US-Japan forex intervention to support the yen provided additional market sentiment support. Investor expectations for a soft economic landing have heated up once again.
