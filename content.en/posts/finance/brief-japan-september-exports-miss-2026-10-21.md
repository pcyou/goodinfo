### Key Facts

Japanese exports rose only 16.7% in September, well short of expectations, while imports increased 18.9%, also missing the 19.6% consensus. External demand and domestic consumption are both losing momentum.

### Wider Perspective

Slower exports alongside faster imports points to a narrowing trade surplus, a warning sign for an economy that relies heavily on external demand for growth. With currency effects and softer growth across major Asian economies adding pressure, the yen and fourth-quarter order recovery will be the key indicators to watch.
