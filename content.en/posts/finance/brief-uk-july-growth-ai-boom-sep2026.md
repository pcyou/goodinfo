## Event Summary

Official UK Office for National Statistics data showed the economy expanded 0.4% month-on-month in July 2026, significantly higher than the zero growth widely expected by analysts. AI-related investment and services sector performance were cited as the main drivers of the monthly growth.

This unexpectedly positive reading provided some support for the UK economy, whose growth outlook has come under pressure recently. The services sector continued to expand against the backdrop of accelerating AI application deployment, and was the main contributor to the monthly growth. Market observers noted that corporate capital spending on AI infrastructure, model training, and compute-related capacity has increased significantly, with demand expansion in related industry chains spreading to broader economic activity.

However, some economists cautioned that a strong single-month reading does not necessarily imply a trend recovery. The UK economy still faces multiple structural challenges, including chronically weak productivity, limited public fiscal space, and external demand uncertainty. Whether the growth dividend brought by AI can continue to transmit to the broader economy will still need to be observed through employment, wage, and investment data in the coming months.
