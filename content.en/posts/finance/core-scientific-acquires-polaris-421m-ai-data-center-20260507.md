Core Scientific has announced the acquisition of Bitcoin miner Polaris for $421 million, aiming to expand its AI data center operations in Oklahoma.

The deal reflects the accelerating trend of Bitcoin mining companies transitioning to AI data centers. As AI computing demand surges, miners' power infrastructure and cooling systems have become valuable AI computing resources.

Polaris operates large-scale data center facilities in Oklahoma. Post-acquisition, Core Scientific will gain additional AI computing capacity. This merger continues the industry wave of miners transforming into AI infrastructure providers.
