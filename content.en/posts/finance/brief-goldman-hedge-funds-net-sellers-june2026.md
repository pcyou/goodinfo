## [Brief] Goldman Sachs: Hedge Funds Net Sellers of Global Stocks

Goldman Sachs' prime brokerage data shows that last week, hedge funds became net sellers of global stocks for the first time in five weeks, with the largest selling in North American and European markets. The short-selling to long-buying ratio stood at 1.3 to 1, with net selling across all major regions except developed Asia. The shift may reflect institutional caution regarding current valuations and geopolitical risks.
