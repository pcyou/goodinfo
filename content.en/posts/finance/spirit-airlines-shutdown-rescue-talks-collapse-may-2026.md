## Spirit Airlines Shuts Down After Rescue Talks Collapse

According to reports from BBC and The Wall Street Journal, US low-cost carrier Spirit Airlines announced on May 1 that it is ceasing operations, following the collapse of negotiations with the Trump administration over a $500 million rescue package. The decision makes Spirit the latest casualty in a turbulent period for the US airline industry.

Spirit Airlines has long been one of America's largest ultra-low-cost carriers, known for its highly competitive fares and stripped-down service model. However, the company has faced mounting financial pressures in recent years, including a sharp decline in passenger traffic during the pandemic, rising fuel costs, and intense competition from other carriers.

As previously reported by The Wall Street Journal, the Trump administration had considered providing Spirit with a $500 million emergency loan guarantee to keep the airline operating and protect tens of thousands of jobs. But negotiations stalled over several points of disagreement, including the government's strict conditions on the use of rescue funds and ongoing labor disputes between the airline's management and its unions.

The shutdown will directly affect tens of thousands of employees and a large number of passengers. The company operated approximately 170 aircraft across hundreds of routes in the United States and Latin America. Industry analysts expect that after Spirit's exit, some of its routes will be absorbed by major competitors including Delta, United, and Southwest Airlines.

The shutdown has also reignited debate about concentration in the US airline industry. Critics note that the past decade has seen massive consolidation, with the four largest carriers now controlling roughly 80% of the domestic market, raising concerns that reduced consumer choice could lead to higher fares.

A Department of Transportation spokesperson said the government is closely monitoring the impact of Spirit's shutdown on the market and has committed to ensuring that affected passengers' rights are protected, including refund arrangements for ticket holders.
