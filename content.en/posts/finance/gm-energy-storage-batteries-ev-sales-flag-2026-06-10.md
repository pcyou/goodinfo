**[Brief]** General Motors (GM) has announced plans to develop new energy storage battery technology, responding to slowing electric vehicle sales growth. According to The New York Times, GM is exploring new battery chemistry to serve AI data center and grid-scale energy storage markets.

This strategic shift comes as global EV demand growth shows signs of deceleration. GM's move reflects a broader industry trend where battery technology is transitioning from "automotive-exclusive" to "multi-scenario" applications.

The rapid expansion of AI data centers has created massive power demands, making energy storage batteries critical infrastructure for stable operations. With its deep expertise in battery technology, GM aims to establish itself in this emerging market.

Industry analysts view battery technology diversification as inevitable. Energy storage for AI infrastructure is approaching an inflection point — as large language model and AI inference demands grow, efficient storage solutions will become a core procurement need for tech companies.
