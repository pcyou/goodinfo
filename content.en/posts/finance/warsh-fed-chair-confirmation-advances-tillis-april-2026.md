## 📰 Key GOP Senator Ends Block, Clearing Way for Warsh as Fed Chair

According to Politico, CNBC, and Bloomberg, North Carolina Republican Senator Thom Tillis announced on April 26 that he will vote to confirm Kevin Warsh as Federal Reserve chair, removing the final political obstacle to Trump's nominee for the nation's top banking position.

Tillis had been the primary holdout in the Senate against Warsh's nomination. According to The New York Times, his change of position came after the US Justice Department dropped its criminal investigation into current Fed Chair Jerome Powell. This move is seen as a response to pressure from the Trump administration and simultaneously eliminated divisions within the Republican Party over Warsh's confirmation.

CNBC reported that with Tillis ending his block, Warsh's confirmation process is now set to advance in the Senate. As a former Fed governor and Wall Street veteran, Warsh is known for his hawkish stance on monetary policy. Market participants widely believe that Warsh's appointment could signal a shift in the Fed's interest rate policy.

The Associated Press noted that the Justice Department's decision to drop the probe into Powell was the key turning point in this political standoff. The investigation into Powell had previously raised concerns about the Fed's independence, and that threat has now been removed.

Bloomberg analyzed that Warsh's confirmation will have profound implications for US monetary policy. As a former Morgan Stanley director, Warsh has deep connections and extensive experience in the financial world, but his hawkish leanings on interest rate policy have also introduced uncertainty into markets.

The Senate is expected to complete the voting process on Warsh's nomination in the coming weeks. If confirmed, Warsh would become one of the youngest Fed chairs in history and will play a pivotal role in shaping the direction of US economic and monetary policy.
