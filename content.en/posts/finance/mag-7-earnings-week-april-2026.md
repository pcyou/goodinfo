## 📰 Busiest Week of Earnings Season: Five Tech Giants Set to Report

This week marks the most critical period of the 2026 earnings season, with five of the "Magnificent Seven" tech giants—Meta Platforms, Apple, Amazon, and others—scheduled to report quarterly results. Wall Street analysts and investors are closely watching these reports to determine whether massive artificial intelligence investments are translating into meaningful revenue growth.

### AI Investment Returns in Focus

Over the past year, capital expenditure on AI infrastructure by major tech companies has surged dramatically. Industry estimates suggest that AI-related capex from leading tech firms exceeded $200 billion in the first quarter of 2026 alone. Markets remain divided on whether such scale of investment will deliver corresponding returns.

Analysts expect Meta and Google (Alphabet) to report strong growth in their AI advertising and cloud services businesses, while Apple may face pressure from slowing hardware sales. Amazon's AWS cloud business performance will also be a key focus area.

### Expected Market Reactions

Currently, the stock performance of tech giants has been mixed. As earnings season progresses, investors will be closely monitoring several key metrics:

- **AI Revenue Growth**: Revenue growth across each company's AI business lines
- **Capital Expenditure Guidance**: Plans for AI infrastructure investment in the coming quarter
- **Margin Impact**: How massive AI spending is affecting overall profit margins
- **User Growth**: The effect of AI features on platform user engagement

### Berkshire Hathaway Annual Meeting

Meanwhile, Berkshire Hathaway's annual shareholder meeting is also taking place this week. This is the first annual meeting since Warren Buffett's departure, and the new management team will face shareholder questions on stock buyback strategies and technology investment directions for the first time.

The earnings data and Berkshire meeting this week are expected to have a significant impact on the subsequent trajectory of US equity markets.
