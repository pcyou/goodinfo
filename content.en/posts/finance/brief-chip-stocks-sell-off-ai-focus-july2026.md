Global chip stocks faced significant selling pressure on Monday as investors adopted a more cautious stance amid the AI investment boom. Market focus is shifting from AI hype to actual profitability and valuation rationality.

According to CNBC, semiconductor stocks declined broadly as investors reassess the true value of AI-related investments. After months of substantial gains driven by AI enthusiasm, the market is now scrutinizing actual earnings performance and valuation levels.

Analysts note the market is transitioning from "AI concept" to "AI profitability." Investors are no longer satisfied with AI narratives alone—they demand to see actual revenue growth and profit contributions. This shift is creating adjustment pressure for high-valuation chip stocks.

The selloff reflects a recalibration of expectations around AI investment returns. The market may be moving from valuation expansion to earnings verification, which has significant implications for the entire AI supply chain.
