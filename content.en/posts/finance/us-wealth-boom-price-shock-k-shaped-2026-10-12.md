**【Brief】**

A Reuters deep analysis on October 12 showed that the assets of wealthy American households continued to swell over the past year, while lower- and middle-income families endured a sustained price shock, making the K-shaped divergence increasingly pronounced. The report argues that this structural contradiction could meaningfully shape the upcoming midterm elections and the Federal Reserve's policy path.
