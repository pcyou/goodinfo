## Gas Price Impact Shows K-Shaped Divide

The New York Times reported that recent gasoline price surges are hitting lower-income Americans the hardest. Research shows a "K-shaped" consumption divide: higher-income households are barely affected, while lower-income families face significantly higher transportation and living costs.
