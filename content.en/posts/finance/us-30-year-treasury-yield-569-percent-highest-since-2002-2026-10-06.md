**[Brief]** The US 30-year Treasury yield touched 5.69% intraday on October 5, the highest since 2002. The 10-year yield climbed in tandem to 4.78%, reflecting mounting concerns over long-term fiscal deficits and inflation stickiness. Analysts note that sustained high yields would put further pressure on mortgage and corporate funding costs.
