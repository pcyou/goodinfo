## S&P 500 Closes at Record High as May Trading Opens with Strong Gains

U.S. stocks ended April's final trading session with strong gains, as the S&P 500 index closed at a record high, kicking off May trading on a positive note. According to CNBC, cooling oil prices and Apple's stock surge were the primary drivers behind the market rally.

### Market Data

At the close, major indices performed as follows:
- **Dow Jones Industrial Average**: 49,499.27, down 0.31% (-152.87)
- **S&P 500**: 7,230.12, up 0.29% (+21.11), a new record close
- **Nasdaq Composite**: 25,114.44, up 0.89% (+222.13)
- **Russell 2000**: 2,812.82, up 0.46% (+12.92)

### Driving Factors

Analysts point to several factors behind the market's strength: declining international oil prices eased inflation expectations, while technology stocks — particularly Apple — delivered strong performances. Apple's recent earnings report revealed that the iPhone 17 was its "most popular model ever," boosting investor confidence.

CNBC's reporting noted that investor sentiment improved significantly in early May following April's volatility. Wall Street traders are climbing the "wall of worry," with fast-money trades making a strong comeback.

### Outlook

Despite the record highs, analysts caution that investors should remain vigilant about geopolitical risks, particularly developments surrounding the Iran situation and trade policy changes. The Federal Reserve's interest rate trajectory will continue to be a key focus for market participants.
