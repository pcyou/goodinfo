## Trump's Latest Tariff Setback Looms Over China Trade Talks

President Trump's tariff agenda suffered another legal setback as a court ruling limits the scope of executive tariff authority. The decision casts uncertainty over upcoming US-China trade negotiations, potentially reshaping the balance of power in trade policy.

Analysts say the ruling could fundamentally alter the landscape of US trade policy decision-making, weakening the executive branch's ability to act unilaterally on trade matters. For upcoming China-US economic negotiations, this legal variable may affect both sides' strategies and expectations.

Markets reacted cautiously. While the ruling does not directly affect existing tariffs, it raises questions about the sustainability of future tariff policy. Investors are closely watching whether the US government will appeal the ruling and how this legal challenge will impact the timeline for upcoming trade talks.
