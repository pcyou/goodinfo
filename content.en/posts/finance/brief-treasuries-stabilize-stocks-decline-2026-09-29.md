## [Brief] Treasuries Stabilize After Selloff as US Stocks Continue to Decline

### Core Summary

Bloomberg market wrap shows that the US Treasury market has stabilised after a string of selloffs, while the major US equity benchmarks continue to come under pressure. Mixed crosscurrents have left market sentiment cautious and the divergence between the two markets captures how investors are simultaneously pricing the rate path and the earnings outlook.

### Event Details

According to Bloomberg's market wrap, the US Treasury market has stabilised this week following the recent run of selling pressure. Long-end yields have eased back a touch from the previous session's highs, but the overall level remains in a multi-year high range. In parallel, the major US equity benchmarks continue to come under pressure, with the three main indices trading in a clearly weak range in early-session trade. The combined picture reflects investors' dual concerns about the rate path and corporate earnings.

The report notes that market sentiment has stayed cautious partly because Treasury yields remain at historically elevated levels and partly because corporate earnings expectations are still being revised lower. Traditional safe-haven assets such as gold have continued to see fresh inflows, signalling that defensive portfolio positioning against systemic risk is still rising on the institutional side. Over the near term, market focus is set to remain on three threads: the rate trajectory, geopolitical risk and the earnings outlook.
