## Core Summary

The Federal Reserve is expected to announce its first rate hike in three years today. Of greater note, new Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's remarks at the press conference may matter more for markets than the rate decision itself. Wall Street is approaching the decision with caution, and Bitcoin has already fallen near $76,000, reflecting risk assets' advance pricing of the Fed's tightening stance.

## Event Details

### First Rate Hike in Three Years

Reuters and other mainstream media reported that the Fed is expected to announce a 25 basis point rate hike at its September 16 meeting, lifting the federal funds rate target range to 4.75%-5.00%. This will be the Fed's first rate hike since 2023, marking a fundamental shift in US monetary policy from an easing cycle to a tightening cycle.

### The Critical Role of Chair Warsh

Newly appointed Fed Chair Kevin Warsh will hold his first press conference after this meeting. Warsh has repeatedly hinted in congressional testimony that the start of a hiking cycle is a necessary measure to address the 'last mile' of inflation. The market is highly focused on Warsh's statements about 'the pace of future hikes,' 'inflation tolerance,' and 'the possibility of an economic soft landing,' viewing their guidance value as far exceeding any single rate decision.

### Market Reaction in Advance

Wall Street's major indices posted modest declines ahead of the meeting, while Bitcoin fell near $76,000, reflecting the market's advance pricing of the Fed's tightening stance. Investors are reassessing the relative probability of 'soft landing' versus 'secondary inflation' narratives, and Warsh's remarks will be the key variable for short-term market direction.

## Panoramic Analysis

**First, Chair Warsh's 'communication skills' have become a core variable in Fed policy.** Since Powell became Fed Chair, 'forward guidance' has been an important component of Fed monetary policy. As the new Chair, Warsh's language art at press conferences and his ability to guide market expectations will directly affect the transmission efficiency of Fed policy. The market is scrutinizing every statement of Warsh with a 'microscope,' and any subtle language changes may trigger sharp market reactions.

**Second, the 'Warsh trade' may become a new market paradigm.** The Greenspan era had the 'Greenspan put,' the Bernanke era had the 'Bernanke put,' and the Powell era had the 'Powell put.' After Warsh takes office, the market may form a new paradigm of the 'Warsh trade,' that is, a trading strategy based on Warsh's personal statements. The formation of this paradigm will profoundly affect the short-term volatility patterns of global asset prices.

**Third, the start of the hiking cycle is putting systemic pressure on cryptocurrency.** Bitcoin's drop to around $76,000 ahead of the meeting reflects the cryptocurrency market's sensitivity to Fed tightening. Historical experience shows that the start of a hiking cycle typically puts pressure on risk assets for 2-6 months. However, if Warsh signals a 'dovish' stance at the press conference, cryptocurrency could see a rapid rebound.

**Fourth, global monetary policy coordination faces new challenges.** After the Fed's hike, the European Central Bank, the Bank of England, and the Bank of Japan will face new tests in their policy choices. If the Fed continues to tighten while other major economies remain accommodative, the dollar may strengthen further, putting pressure on emerging market capital flows. This is one of the most important variables for global financial stability in the second half of 2026.

## Multiple Perspectives

**Fed Position**: Chair Warsh has emphasized in multiple public appearances that curbing inflation is the Fed's top priority and that further tightening measures will be taken 'without hesitation' if necessary. At the same time, he stressed that policy decisions will be 'data-dependent' and will not pre-commit to a path.

**Wall Street Position**: Major investment banks such as JPMorgan and Goldman Sachs widely expect the Fed to hike by 25 basis points, but are divided on the future pace. Some believe the hiking cycle will continue into early 2027, while others think this hike could be a 'one-time' operation.

**Trump's Position**: Trump has repeatedly publicly pressured the Fed to cut rates and criticized Warsh as 'too hawkish.' But market analysis suggests Trump's pressure is unlikely to change Warsh's policy stance, as Fed independence is the core of its credibility.

**Emerging Market Position**: Central banks in emerging market countries such as Brazil, Mexico, and India have already 'responded in advance,' offsetting the impact of Fed tightening through rate hikes or exchange market interventions. But some highly indebted countries such as Turkey and Argentina still face severe challenges.

**Cryptocurrency Community Position**: Some crypto advocates argue that the impact of the hiking cycle on Bitcoin is 'short-term volatility,' and that Bitcoin's value as 'digital gold' will not be fundamentally affected in the long term. But short-term traders remain 'highly vigilant' about Fed policy.
