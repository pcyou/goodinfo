## US Inflation Jumps to 3.8% as Energy Costs Surge from Iran War

US inflation has risen to 3.8%, driven by surging energy costs linked to the Iran conflict, according to BBC. The figure exceeded market expectations and adds fresh pressure on Federal Reserve monetary policy debates. Energy price increases are weighing on both consumer spending and business costs.
