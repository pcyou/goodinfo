## [Brief] Micron Beats Estimates, Memory Stocks Surge

Micron Technology reported quarterly revenue and profit that exceeded Wall Street expectations. Shares of SanDisk, Western Digital and other memory chip makers rallied in response. Analysts say Micron's performance "justifies elevated valuations" in the storage sector, with optimism sustained by AI-driven demand for memory capacity.
