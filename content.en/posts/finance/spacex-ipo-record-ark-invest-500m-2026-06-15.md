## Core Summary

SpaceX completed its initial public offering on June 15, setting the record for the largest aerospace IPO in history. In premarket trading on its debut day, shares surged approximately 6%, reflecting strong market enthusiasm. ARK Invest, led by renowned investor Cathie Wood, purchased over $500 million worth of SpaceX shares on the first trading day, becoming one of the largest institutional investors in the offering. Analysts note this investment scale reflects strong institutional confidence in the long-term growth prospects of the commercial aerospace industry.

## Event Details

According to CNBC and CoinDesk, SpaceX's IPO attracted massive participation from both institutional and retail investors. The company's valuation received strong market recognition during its roadshow, with final pricing exceeding the initial expected range. In premarket trading, shares quickly climbed, gaining 6%, demonstrating optimistic market sentiment about the company's future.

ARK Invest's CEO Cathie Wood made a major move on the IPO day. Data shows that multiple ARK funds collectively purchased over $500 million in SpaceX shares. These purchases were likely funded by selling other positions. ARK Invest is also one of the loudest Bitcoin bulls, with a million-dollar price target. The simultaneous heavy positioning in aerospace and crypto assets reflects the fund's concentrated strategy in disruptive technology sectors.

Bernstein noted in a research report that SpaceX's listing could provide significant momentum for prediction markets. With the World Cup approaching, prediction market volumes are expected to reach record levels, and SpaceX as a popular underlying asset will further activate market liquidity.

## Panorama Analysis

SpaceX's record-breaking IPO marks the commercial aerospace industry's formal entry into mainstream capital markets. Over the past two decades, SpaceX fundamentally transformed launch cost structures through reusable rocket technology, bringing the cost per kilogram to orbit from tens of thousands of dollars to thousands. This technological breakthrough not only lowered barriers to space exploration but also spawned entirely new business models including satellite internet, space tourism, and on-orbit services.

From an investment perspective, ARK Invest's major purchase sends an important signal. Cathie Wood, as a representative of growth investing, often serves as a bellwether. The $500 million position indicates that institutional investors now view commercial aerospace as a long-term track on par with artificial intelligence and clean energy. SpaceX's Starlink satellite internet already covers dozens of countries globally, and the Starship program's success will further expand the possibilities for space transportation.

Notably, SpaceX's listing has a multiplier effect across the entire aerospace supply chain. Upstream rocket component suppliers and satellite manufacturers, downstream satellite communications operators and ground equipment manufacturers, will all benefit from the capitalization of the aerospace industry. Additionally, the financialization of the space economy is becoming increasingly apparent, with new financial instruments emerging from space insurance to satellite asset securitization.
