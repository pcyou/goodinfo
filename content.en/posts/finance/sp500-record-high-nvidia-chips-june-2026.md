The S&P 500 index reached an all-time high on June 2, driven by a surge in AI chip stocks led by Nvidia. Despite geopolitical tensions and elevated energy prices from Middle East conflicts, the tech sector's strength propelled the broader market higher. CNBC live coverage showed Nvidia leading chip stocks significantly higher. Analysts note that massive AI infrastructure spending by tech giants continues to fuel semiconductor demand, keeping investor optimism intact. The rally suggests strong confidence in AI commercialization, though some warn that risk factors including energy costs may be underpriced in the current market.
