Whirlpool warned Wednesday that the Iran war and tariff rulings are causing a "recession-level" sales slump. Shares fell as much as 20%, reflecting how geopolitical conflict is spreading economic impact from energy to consumer manufacturing. Analysts say more manufacturers could face similar pressure if the war continues.
