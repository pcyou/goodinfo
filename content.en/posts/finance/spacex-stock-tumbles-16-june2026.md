## SpaceX Stock Tumbles 16.4%, Erasing Most IPO Gains

SpaceX shares plunged 16.4% in Monday trading, marking the company's worst single-day decline since its historic $75 billion IPO on June 12. The selloff erased most of the gains the stock had accumulated since going public on NASDAQ.

According to CNBC and Yahoo Finance, the stock came under heavy selling pressure throughout the session, extending a three-day losing streak that has brought the cumulative decline to nearly 20%. The drop coincided with a broader weakness in technology stocks, with the S&P 500 closing lower on the day.

Market analysts attribute the selloff to a combination of factors: profit-taking after the initial IPO euphoria, concerns about the company's lofty valuation, and a general rotation away from high-growth technology names in the current interest rate environment.

The decline is being closely watched as a bellwether for the broader IPO market. If SpaceX, despite its dominant position in commercial launch and satellite internet, cannot sustain post-listing momentum, it may signal a more cautious environment for other large-scale technology offerings.
