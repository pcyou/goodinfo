## Sony Announces $3 Billion Buyback Amid Memory Price Pressure


Sony Group announced a $3 billion share buyback program. Its semiconductor division faces pressure from declining memory chip prices. Despite slowing PlayStation growth, Sony maintains its double-digit profit growth forecast.
