Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell issued a rare warning that the Fed's credibility is at risk, suggesting that political pressure is threatening the central bank's independence. His reference to "stress tests" was interpreted as a response to executive interference in monetary policy. The statement has drawn market attention as it may signal unprecedented external pressure on the Fed's rate-setting process.
