**[Key Takeaways]**
CNBC reported that after the S&P 500 index hit a fresh record high, US stock futures were little changed during Asian trading hours as the market entered a wait-and-see mode ahead of key inflation and jobs data later this week.

**[Event Details]**
- **Index Action**: The S&P 500 set a fresh all-time high, primarily driven by the technology and AI sectors, with investors maintaining an optimistic view on fourth-quarter earnings.
- **Futures Action**: US stock futures traded with limited movement in the pre-market session, indicating that both bulls and bears remained cautious near the record high.
- **Focus**: Upcoming US CPI and employment data will shape market views on the Federal Reserve's rate-cut trajectory and determine whether the index can extend its rally.

**[Perspective]**
The S&P 500's continued push to fresh highs reflects a dual bet on the AI wave and US economic resilience. On one hand, hyperscaler capex and AI compute deployment continue to anchor the index; on the other hand, a faster-than-expected cooling in inflation would reinforce rate-cut expectations and provide liquidity support to risk assets. However, an index trading at record levels also magnifies valuation pressure — any surprise in key economic data could quickly flip the tape from "momentum grinding higher" to "profit-taking". Investors should closely monitor this week's data window to guard against the pullback risk from elevated volatility.
