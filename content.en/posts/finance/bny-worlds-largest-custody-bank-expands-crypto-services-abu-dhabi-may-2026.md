## Overview

BNY Mellon, the Wall Street giant overseeing $59 trillion in client assets, has announced a partnership with Finstreet and the ADI Foundation to expand digital asset infrastructure services in Abu Dhabi.

## Significance

As the world's largest custody bank, BNY Mellon's move into crypto services in the Middle East represents a major milestone in the institutional adoption of digital assets. The partnership will provide institutional clients with digital asset custody and settlement capabilities in one of the fastest-growing crypto hubs globally.
