# Key GOP Senator Tillis Drops Blockade, Clearing Path for Warsh's Fed Chair Nomination

WASHINGTON — North Carolina Republican Senator Thom Tillis announced on Sunday that he will support President Trump's nominee for Federal Reserve Chair, Kevin Warsh, ending his previous hold on the nomination. The decision marks a critical turning point in what is expected to be a smooth confirmation process for Warsh.

## Tillis's Reversal

In a statement, Tillis said that after multiple in-depth conversations with Warsh, he was convinced that Warsh would "lead the Federal Reserve with independence and professionalism." Tillis had been among the key Republicans in the Senate expressing reservations about the nomination, and his support all but ensures Warsh's confirmation.

"I am prepared to move forward with the confirmation of Kevin Warsh," Tillis said Sunday afternoon. "He has sufficiently articulated his monetary policy framework and his commitment to the Fed's independence."

## Warsh's Background and Policy Stance

Kevin Warsh, 53, previously served as a Federal Reserve Governor from 2006 to 2011, making him the only current nominee with prior Fed experience. After leaving the Fed, he became an investment banker at Morgan Stanley, accumulating extensive financial market expertise.

Warsh is viewed as a relatively hawkish monetary policymaker. He has repeatedly emphasized the importance of controlling inflation in public speeches and has criticized the Fed's previous quantitative easing programs. Market observers widely believe that if confirmed, Warsh could steer monetary policy in a more restrictive direction.

## Market Reaction

Following the announcement, US Treasury yields rose modestly, reflecting market expectations of tighter monetary policy. The US dollar also strengthened in after-hours trading.

Wall Street analysts generally believe Warsh's confirmation would bring a more market-friendly policy orientation to the Fed. "Warsh combines insider knowledge of the Fed's operations with Wall Street experience — he may be the most ideal Fed Chair candidate the Trump administration could find," said a senior economist.

## Confirmation Timeline

The Senate Banking Committee is expected to hold Warsh's confirmation hearing next week. If all goes smoothly, a full Senate vote could take place in mid-May. With Republicans holding a narrow majority in the Senate, Warsh's confirmation prospects are widely viewed as favorable.

The nomination carries symbolic significance for the Trump administration. Warsh would become the first new Fed Chair since Jerome Powell's appointment in 2018, and his policy direction will profoundly influence the US economy for years to come.
