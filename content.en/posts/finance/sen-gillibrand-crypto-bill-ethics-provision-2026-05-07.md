## [BREAKING] Sen. Gillibrand: No Crypto Bill Without Ethics Provision

Senator Kirsten Gillibrand warned that sweeping cryptocurrency market structure legislation will not pass without an ethics provision banning officials from having industry ties, citing concerns about political connections to memecoins.
