## Core Summary

Starbucks publicly denied on October 12 market speculation that it was in acquisition talks with fast-casual chain Chipotle, making clear that the company will continue to focus on its core coffee business and is not considering any large cross-category acquisition in the short term.

## Event Details

According to Fox Business, the rumor first surfaced on social media and financial blogs before being picked up by some financial outlets, sparking market chatter about a possible merger between the two food-and-beverage giants. A Starbucks spokesperson said in a statement that the company is not in any form of acquisition talks with Chipotle and that the rumor does not reflect reality. Reuters, citing people familiar with the matter, said recent internal discussions at Starbucks have focused on store-level operating efficiency, digital experience improvements, and loyalty-program expansion rather than cross-category acquisitions. Bloomberg added that Chipotle has not made any official comment on the rumor, though its share price moved noticeably during the speculation.
