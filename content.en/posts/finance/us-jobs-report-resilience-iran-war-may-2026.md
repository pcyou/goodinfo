## Article

On May 2, 2026, Bloomberg analysis indicates that the upcoming US nonfarm payrolls report is expected to show continued labor market resilience despite the uncertainty brought by the Iran war. This forecast carries significant implications for assessing the war's impact on the US economy.

### Labor Market Resilience

Bloomberg economists argue that despite geopolitical tensions and the Iran war creating uncertainty, the fundamentals of the US job market remain strong. New job additions are expected to exceed market expectations, with the unemployment rate remaining at low levels.

### War's Economic Impact

The direct impact of the Iran war on the US economy is mainly reflected in rising energy prices and supply chain disruptions. However, the resilience of the labor market suggests that overall economic activity has not been severely impacted. The expansion of defense-related industries has partially offset slowdowns in other sectors.

### Market Reaction

Wall Street's expectations for employment data lean optimistic. Investors believe that strong jobs data will support consumer spending, thereby driving economic growth. Meanwhile, the market is closely monitoring the Federal Reserve's response to employment and inflation data to assess future monetary policy direction.

### Outlook

Analysts note that employment data in the coming months will be a key indicator for assessing the Iran war's impact on the US economy. If the labor market continues to show resilience, it will strengthen market confidence in a soft economic landing.
