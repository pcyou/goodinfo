## Core Summary

SpaceX is advancing toward a record $75 billion IPO at a $135 per share target price, with its Bitcoin corporate treasury strategy drawing investor scrutiny, according to Barron's and CoinDesk reports.

## Event Details

SpaceX has selected its investment banking team with a target valuation of approximately $2 trillion. The company plans to raise $75 billion through the IPO to fund Starship development, Starlink global expansion, and preparations for upcoming Mars missions.

Notably, SpaceX's financial disclosures mention a Bitcoin treasury strategy, drawing significant attention from traditional investors. Some institutional investors have expressed concerns about the role of crypto assets in the company's balance sheet.

## Perspective & Analysis

SpaceX's IPO is not just a capital market event—it marks a pivotal moment in the space economy's transition from government-led to market-driven operations. If successful, SpaceX would become the world's most valuable aerospace company, potentially surpassing Boeing and Lockheed Martin combined.

From an industry perspective, the IPO could trigger a wave of space tech company listings. Blue Origin, Rocket Lab, and other competitors may accelerate their capital market strategies. The $75 billion raise also reflects long-term investor confidence in the space economy—from satellite internet to lunar base construction.

However, the Bitcoin treasury strategy warrants careful consideration. While it reflects Musk's consistent innovative financial thinking, it may deter traditional institutional investors. Finding the balance between innovation and stability will be SpaceX's primary challenge post-IPO.

## Multiple Viewpoints

**Bullish camp** argues that SpaceX's dominant position (Starlink has over 10 million users, Falcon rockets have exceptional success rates) gives it strong profitability potential.

**Cautious camp** warns that the valuation already prices in significant growth expectations, and any technical setbacks or regulatory hurdles could trigger substantial stock volatility.

**Crypto industry** has welcomed SpaceX's Bitcoin strategy broadly, with Bitwise CIO stating that mainstream corporate Bitcoin adoption is an irreversible trend.
