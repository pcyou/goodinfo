## Brief

According to the Associated Press, US Treasury Secretary Bessent has described recent discussions with Beijing as productive, and global equities have broadly advanced ahead of an upcoming meeting between Trump and Xi Jinping. Major Wall Street indices opened higher, with markets sending cautious optimistic signals on the outlook for US-China economic relations. Analysts have noted that Bessent's remarks and the imminent Trump-Xi meeting will have a significant impact on US-China trade dynamics, tariff policy, and supply-chain coordination in the period ahead. Investors are closely watching further signals and statements ahead of the meeting, and the interaction between market sentiment and policy expectations will continue to shape the short-term trajectory of global financial markets.
