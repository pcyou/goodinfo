## Core Summary

After the Bank of Japan's rate hike, Japanese government bond yields and the yen both pulled back, while the Nikkei 225 closed higher against the trend. The market reaction suggests investors interpret the BOJ's tightening pace and forward policy path as relatively gentle, with no clear liquidity tightening and Asia-Pacific equities broadly stable.
