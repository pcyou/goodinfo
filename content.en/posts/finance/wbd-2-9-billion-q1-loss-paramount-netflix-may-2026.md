## [Brief] Warner Bros Discovery Reports 2.9 Billion Dollar Q1 Loss on M&A Charges

According to Deadline, Warner Bros. Discovery (WBD) reported a first-quarter loss of $2.9 billion, primarily driven by M&A-related charges including a termination fee Paramount paid to Netflix.

The loss reflects one-time financial impacts from media industry consolidation rather than ongoing core business deterioration.
