**[Core Summary]**
Yahoo Finance reported that, driven by the simultaneous rise in 10-year and 30-year US Treasury yields, the US 30-year fixed mortgage rate climbed to its highest level in nearly three years, further raising the burden on purchasers and exerting sustained pressure on housing-market activity.

**[Event Details]**
- **Rate Level**: The US 30-year fixed mortgage rate touched its highest level in nearly three years, moving in step with long-end Treasury yields.
- **Market Transmission**: Sustained increases in long-end Treasury yields are directly pushing up mortgage pricing, with market expectations of Federal Reserve rate cuts being pushed further back.
- **Scope of Impact**: Monthly mortgage payments have risen notably, putting sustained pressure on housing market transaction activity.
