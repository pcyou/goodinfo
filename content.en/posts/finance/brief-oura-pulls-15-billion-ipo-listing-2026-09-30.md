## [Brief] Oura Pulls $15 Billion Stock Market Listing Days After Announcement

### Core Summary

Wearable health device maker Oura has withdrawn its planned U.S. stock market listing just days after the announcement, which had targeted a $15 billion valuation. The decision comes amid rising valuation pressure on consumer hardware companies in the public market.

### Event Details

According to reports, Oura's planned listing had been targeting the U.S. capital market and was one of the largest proposed IPOs by a consumer hardware company this year. However, market conditions shifted rapidly after the formal announcement, and the company ultimately decided to withdraw the plan.

Oura primarily makes smart rings and other wearable health monitoring products, and has seen significant user growth and revenue expansion over the past several years. Yet in an environment where the public market has become more cautious about consumer hardware valuations, the $15 billion valuation expectation faced notable pressure. The withdrawal reflects the public market's heightened sensitivity to hardware company valuations and the narrowing room for negotiation between issuer expectations and market acceptance.
