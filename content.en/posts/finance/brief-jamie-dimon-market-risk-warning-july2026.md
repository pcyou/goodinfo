[Brief] JPMorgan Chase CEO Jamie Dimon warned that financial markets are significantly underestimating current risks, including geopolitical tensions, persistent inflation, and growing fiscal deficits. He stated he would not recommend buying stocks or US Treasurys at current valuations. The comments come as US-Iran conflict escalates and new 50% tariffs on Canada add to market uncertainty.
