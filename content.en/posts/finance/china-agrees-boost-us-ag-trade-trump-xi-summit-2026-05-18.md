## Summary

China has agreed to increase purchases of US agricultural products, including beef and poultry, following the Trump-Xi summit. The deal represents a meaningful step toward easing bilateral trade tensions.

## Details

According to the Associated Press, the agreement was reached during the recent summit between President Trump and President Xi. Under the terms, China will expand imports of American beef, poultry, and other agricultural goods.

Agricultural trade has been a flashpoint in US-China relations since the trade war erupted. American farmers and agricultural exporters have long lobbied for restored access to the Chinese market, which was severely disrupted by retaliatory tariffs.

The deal is seen as a positive signal for broader trade negotiations. However, analysts caution that deeper structural disagreements -- including technology transfer requirements, intellectual property protections, and state subsidies -- remain unresolved.

Market reaction was muted, with investors focused more on the ongoing Iran conflict and its impact on oil prices and global inflation expectations.
