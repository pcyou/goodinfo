A Robinhood executive has said that overseas demand for US equities is continuing to grow.

As Robinhood expands its services across multiple international markets, the company has observed strong interest in US stock trading from users in Europe and Asia. This trend reflects sustained global investor confidence in US equity markets.

Robinhood's international expansion strategy is a key component of its growth plan, aiming to replicate its US success model globally.
