**【Brief】**

A leading UK think tank warned on October 12 that Chancellor Rachel Reeves may need to raise around 35 billion pounds in the upcoming autumn budget to honour the government's spending pledges. The report has drawn close market attention to the scale of potential tax increases and public borrowing, testing whether the government's fiscal discipline promises can hold.
