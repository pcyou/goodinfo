Japanese and South Korean stock markets both hit record highs as investors buy into expectations of a possible turning point in the Iran conflict. Meanwhile, international oil prices continue to climb due to fragility around the Strait of Hormuz. Despite geopolitical risks, Asian markets are showing strong growth momentum, with technology and manufacturing sectors leading gains.
