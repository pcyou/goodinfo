## Spirit Airlines Completes Refund Process

US low-cost carrier Spirit Airlines has ceased operations after a rescue deal fell through. The company recently announced it has nearly completed refund processing for all affected customers.

### Bankruptcy Background

Spirit Airlines had previously reached a rescue agreement with Frontier Airlines, but the deal ultimately failed to close, forcing Spirit to shut down. As one of the major low-cost carriers in the US, its bankruptcy sent shockwaves through the industry.

### Customer Support

Frontier Airlines announced discounted rescue fare plans following Spirit shutdown, to support affected travelers. This move provided alternative travel options for a large number of stranded passengers.

### Political Response

Senator Warren previously expressed strong concern over the Spirit Airlines collapse, criticizing the adverse impact of airline industry consolidation on consumers.

### Industry Impact

The closure of Spirit Airlines is the latest case in the US airline industry consolidation trend. Analysts believe the competitive landscape of the low-cost airline market is undergoing significant changes.
