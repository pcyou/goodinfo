## Oil Prices Fall Sharply on Iran Deal News

International oil prices fell sharply on news of a potential Iran-US agreement. Brent crude dropped significantly as markets priced in reduced Middle East supply risks. Analysts warn prices could rebound quickly if negotiations collapse.
