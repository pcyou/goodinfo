[Brief] China reported record-breaking export and import figures for April, with the trade surplus widening significantly. Despite high U.S. tariffs and elevated energy costs from the Iran conflict, Chinese exports maintained strong growth while imports also rose, reflecting recovering domestic demand and supply chain resilience. Analysts note the data could strengthen China's position in ongoing trade negotiations.
