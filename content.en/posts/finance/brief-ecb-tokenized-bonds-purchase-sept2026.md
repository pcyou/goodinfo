## Summary

The European Central Bank is reportedly planning to use its own funds to buy tokenized bonds, CoinDesk reported, a move seen as a major breakthrough for traditional central banks in blockchain asset allocation. If implemented, the plan would mark a new phase in the substantive participation of major global central banks in the tokenized asset space.

## Details

According to CoinDesk, the European Central Bank is formulating a plan to use its own funds to buy tokenized bonds. The report noted that this plan involves the ECB's substantive allocation to tokenized assets and is seen as an important step for traditional central banks into the blockchain asset space. Tokenized bonds are financial instruments that digitize traditional bond assets through blockchain technology, with potential advantages such as improved transaction efficiency, reduced settlement costs, and enhanced transparency.

If implemented, the ECB's plan will have an important demonstration effect on the development of the global tokenized asset market and may encourage other major central banks to accelerate their attention to and allocation of tokenized assets. Market analysts noted that substantive participation by central banks will help enhance the liquidity, transparency, and regulatory compliance of the tokenized asset market, which is of great significance for innovation and stability in global financial markets.

## Perspectives

Supporters argue that the ECB's plan will help promote the maturity and development of the tokenized asset market and provide an important demonstration for traditional financial institutions to participate in the blockchain asset space. Critics have expressed concerns about the risks and challenges that may arise from central banks' direct participation in tokenized asset allocation, calling for enhanced risk assessment and regulatory coordination in the process.
