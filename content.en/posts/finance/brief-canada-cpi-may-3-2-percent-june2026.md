## [Brief] Canada May CPI Accelerates to 3.2%

Statistics Canada reported on June 22 that Canada's May Consumer Price Index (CPI) accelerated to 3.2% year-over-year, above market expectations. The data reflects the impact of rising energy prices and supply chain cost pass-through on consumer prices. The inflation rebound may influence the Bank of Canada's future interest rate decisions.
