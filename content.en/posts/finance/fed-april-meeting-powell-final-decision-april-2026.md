## Key Event

The Federal Reserve's April monetary policy meeting officially commenced on April 29, 2026. Markets widely expect the Fed to maintain current interest rates unchanged, but this meeting has drawn extraordinary attention as it may be the final rate decision of Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell's tenure.

## Rate Expectations

Both Reuters and CNBC reported that analysts broadly expect the Fed to hold the federal funds rate at its current level. Market focus has shifted from "will they cut rates" to "what comes after the Powell era."

Fox Business noted that with Powell's term nearing its end, former Fed Governor Kevin Warsh has emerged as a leading candidate for the next chair, and markets are closely watching his potential policy leanings.

## Powell's Final Chapter

PBS reported that Powell will hold a press conference following the meeting, widely viewed as his final public policy address as Fed Chair. An ongoing investigation by the Inspector General's office adds further uncertainty to the proceedings.

The New York Times analysis noted that Powell has achieved significant results in taming inflation and engineering a soft landing, but his successor's policy direction will have profound implications for markets.

## Macro Context

The U.S. economy currently faces multiple challenges:

- **Inflation pressures**: While overall inflation has retreated from its peak, core inflation remains above the Fed's 2% target
- **Labor market**: The jobs market remains resilient, though signs of slowdown have emerged in select sectors
- **International trade**: Tariff policy uncertainty continues to weigh on business confidence

## Market Outlook

Investopedia noted that this meeting's statement and Powell's remarks will provide critical clues about the future policy path. Against the backdrop of major tech earnings releases, the Fed's language will have a direct impact on market sentiment.
