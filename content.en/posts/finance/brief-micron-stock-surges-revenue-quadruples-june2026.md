## [Brief] Micron Stock Jumps 10% as Memory Crunch Drives Revenue Quadrupling

Micron Technology shares jumped roughly 10% in trading, driven by a severe supply-demand imbalance in the global memory chip market. Soaring chip prices have pushed quarterly revenue up approximately fourfold year-on-year, according to CNBC.

Analysts note that robust demand for high-bandwidth memory from AI training and inference workloads is the core driver of this price surge. Massive data centre expansion by major technology companies has further tightened memory chip supply.
