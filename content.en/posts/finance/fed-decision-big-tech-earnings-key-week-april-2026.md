## Pivotal Week: Fed Decision and Tech Earnings Double Test

On April 29, 2026, Wall Street entered what markets widely consider one of the most critical trading weeks of the year. S&P 500 index futures remained flat on Tuesday as investors adopted a cautious stance amid the dual uncertainty of the Federal Reserve's rate decision and quarterly earnings from major technology companies.

### Powell's Final Fed Meeting

According to NPR, this will be Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell's final Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meeting before his term expires. The interest rate decision and policy statement from this meeting will receive heightened market scrutiny.

Markets broadly expect the Fed to maintain current interest rates. Chicago Fed President Austan Goolsbee has previously noted that ongoing geopolitical uncertainty in the Strait of Hormuz is a significant factor in keeping rates steady. Energy price volatility has already spilled over into broader economic sectors, and concerns about stagflation risks persist.

### Big Tech Earnings Season

This week, several major technology companies will report quarterly results. These earnings are viewed as a critical test of the current AI-driven market rally. According to CNBC, traders have shifted to predominantly long positions in the Nasdaq 100, but analysts warn that if tech giants fail to demonstrate compelling returns on AI investments, the market could face pullback pressure.

The Economic Times notes that Wall Street has experienced a significant rally since early April, but the sustainability of this rebound hinges on whether tech companies can prove their massive AI infrastructure spending is translating into substantive revenue growth.

### Oil Prices and Geopolitical Factors

Meanwhile, international oil prices rose on Tuesday. According to the Wall Street Journal, the increase reflects market concerns about ongoing tensions in the Strait of Hormuz. The US naval blockade of Iran shows no signs of easing, with the Trump administration reportedly instructing aides to prepare for an extended blockade. Persistently high energy prices pose an additional challenge to the Fed's monetary policy decisions.

Bloomberg notes that this week's market faces a triple test: tech earnings, the Fed decision, and geopolitical risk. Unexpected developments on any front could trigger significant market volatility.

### Market Outlook

Analysts generally agree that this week's market performance will set the tone for the coming weeks. If tech earnings are strong and the Fed signals a dovish stance, the rally could continue; conversely, if earnings disappoint or the Fed takes a hawkish tone, compounded by geopolitical risks, the market could face a significant correction.
