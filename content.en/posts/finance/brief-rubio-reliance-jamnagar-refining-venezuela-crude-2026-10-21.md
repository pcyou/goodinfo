### Key Facts

US Secretary of State Rubio visited Reliance Industries' Jamnagar refinery, saying its extraordinary refining capacity could help India diversify energy supplies, and hinting at greater offtake of Venezuelan crude.

### Wider Perspective

Jamnagar is among the world's largest single-site private refineries, and its heavy-crude processing capacity is exactly what makes it valuable under sanctions. By tying energy trade to diplomatic goals, Washington is steering sanctioned barrels toward new buyers beyond traditional Asian markets. For India this secures discounted crude and diversifies supply risk; for the US it opens a sanctioned outlet. A deeper energy-diplomacy alignment would give both sides new leverage in future rounds of geopolitical bargaining.
