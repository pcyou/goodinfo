## Major Bank Expands Crypto Business

Bloomberg reported that Morgan Stanley is piloting crypto trading on its E*Trade platform at just 50 basis points in fees. The move comes shortly after the firm launched a bitcoin ETF, signaling continued expansion into digital assets by traditional financial institutions.
