## [Brief] Mercedes and Porsche Announce New Cost-Cutting Plans

Mercedes-Benz and Porsche have finalized new cost-cutting plans amid mounting tariff pressures, shifting market demand, and global trade friction. Both German luxury automakers face fierce competition from Chinese electric vehicle brands, while US tariff policies further squeeze profit margins. The measures are expected to cover supply chain optimization, production efficiency improvements, and adjustments to certain non-core business lines.
