## Core Summary

Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh is set to deliver his first major public address at the Jackson Hole Global Central Bank Symposium this Friday. With U.S. inflation hovering near a three-year high, markets are highly sensitive to any signals regarding the Fed's next move on interest rates. Multiple Wall Street institutions expect Warsh's wording to directly impact the dollar and U.S. equity valuations.

## Event Details

According to NPR, this Jackson Hole symposium carries significant weight for Warsh — it marks his first opportunity to articulate his policy vision before global central bank peers and financial market participants. CNBC reports that the core topics of the speech will center on the inflation outlook and interest rate path.

Investing.com analysis suggests Warsh faces a "high-stakes market test": he must reassure markets about inflation concerns without adopting such hawkish rhetoric that economic expectations deteriorate sharply. Fox Business reports that Warsh's policy leanings favor inflation control, but markets await whether he will hint at potential rate hikes in his speech.

## Panoramic Perspective

The Jackson Hole symposium has historically served as a key stage for Fed chairs to signal policy direction. Former Chair Powell once hinted at rate cuts here, while Bernanke inadvertently triggered the "taper tantrum." Warsh's debut is particularly notable given that he has only been in office for a few months and has yet to navigate a full economic cycle — markets are still building confidence in his policy credibility.

From a broader macro perspective, central banks worldwide face a rare synchronized dilemma: post-pandemic supply chain disruptions have pushed up inflation, while geopolitical conflicts and trade protectionism constrain economic growth. Under this "stagflation" shadow, every word from Warsh will be scrutinized by markets. A hawkish signal could strengthen the dollar and trigger capital outflows from emerging markets; a dovish tilt could accelerate inflation expectations becoming unanchored.

## Multiple Perspectives

**Hawkish Expectation Camp**: Some Wall Street analysts believe Warsh's consistent inflation hawkishness means he will emphasize "price stability first" in his speech, potentially hinting at another rate hike this year.

**Market Stability Camp**: Other analysts note that Warsh understands the market impact of his first speech and may choose moderate wording to avoid unnecessary volatility, saving substantive policy adjustments for subsequent FOMC meetings.

**Economy-First Camp**: Some economists urge Warsh to pay more attention to signs of labor market cooling rather than focusing solely on inflation data, avoiding excessive tightening that could stifle economic recovery.
