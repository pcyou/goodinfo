## [Brief] Air India's New CEO Faces Dual Challenges

According to BBC, Air India's new CEO Gebremariam has officially taken office, facing two core challenges: the aftermath of last year's deadly crash and mounting financial losses.

Last year's Air India crash caused significant casualties, deeply impacting the company's reputation and operations. The new CEO must restore public trust while driving comprehensive reform of the company's safety management system.

Financially, Air India has long been loss-making. Despite previous government bailouts, intense market competition and rising operating costs continue to pressure the company. Analysts believe the new CEO must take decisive measures in fleet optimization, route restructuring, and cost control to reverse the company's financial difficulties.
