Wall Street asset manager Franklin Templeton has partnered with crypto infrastructure company MoonPay to integrate its BENJI tokenized fund with MoonPay's payment network. The partnership allows eligible institutional clients to seamlessly swap stablecoins for yield-generating tokenized funds around the clock, without leaving the blockchain environment. Franklin Templeton described the deal as "the foundation for a broader strategic relationship" between the two companies. This marks another step in traditional finance's integration with crypto infrastructure.
