## What Happened

Former New York City mayor Rudy Giuliani has been hospitalized in critical but stable condition, his spokesman confirmed on Sunday evening.

Giuliani was rushed to a hospital after his health rapidly deteriorated. Medical staff are closely monitoring his condition.

## Background

Giuliani, 82, served as a key advisor to Donald Trump during his presidency. He has faced multiple health challenges in recent years.

He has previously disclosed that he is being treated for prostate cancer. His health has been a subject of ongoing public attention.

## Reactions

News of his hospitalization prompted expressions of concern and well-wishes from political figures and public figures across the spectrum.

Giuliani served as mayor of New York City for eight years and gained widespread recognition for his leadership during the 9/11 attacks.
