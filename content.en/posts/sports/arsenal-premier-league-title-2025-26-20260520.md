## Arsenal Clinch First Premier League Title Since 2004

Arsenal have won the 2025-26 Premier League title, ending a 22-year wait for the club's first top-flight championship since the famous "Invincibles" season of 2003-04.

**How It Happened:**

Manchester City's unexpected draw against Bournemouth was enough to confirm Arsenal as champions. The Gunners' consistent performances throughout the season ultimately saw them edge past their rivals.

**Key Factors:**

- **Set-Piece Mastery**: Arsenal's set-piece play has been a major weapon this season, providing crucial goals
- **Arteta's Persistence**: Manager Mikel Arteta faced years of criticism before finally delivering the ultimate prize
- **Style Meets Steel**: The team evolved from pursuing attractive football to embracing a more pragmatic, efficient approach

This is Arsenal's 14th English top-flight title. Arteta, who took over in 2019, has gone from being labeled "perennial runners-up" to finally achieving the breakthrough.
