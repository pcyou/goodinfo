## 2026 Pulitzer Prize Winners Announced

The 2026 Pulitzer Prize winners have been announced, with The New York Times receiving three awards. This marks another significant achievement for the newspaper in journalism.

The Minnesota Star Tribune won the Breaking News Pulitzer Prize for its coverage of the fatal shooting of UnitedHealth CEO Brian Thompson in New York. The paper's reporters provided rapid and comprehensive coverage following the incident.

The Pulitzer Prize is the highest honor in American journalism, recognizing outstanding achievements in news reporting, literature, and music composition.
