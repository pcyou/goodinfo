## Latest Development

President Trump announced that the US Navy will begin escorting vessels through the Strait of Hormuz starting Monday, in what has been dubbed Project Freedom.

Iran responded with threats of retaliation, further escalating tensions in the Gulf region.

## Background

The Strait of Hormuz is one of the world most critical oil transit chokepoints. Recent Middle East conflict has severely threatened passage through the strait.

US Central Command has issued an official statement supporting the effort to reopen the strait. Several ships have been advised to reroute to avoid danger zones.

## Market Impact

International oil prices have shown volatility in response to the announcement. Market participants are closely monitoring the potential impact on global energy supplies.

Asian stocks rose amid the Gulf proposals and relatively stable oil prices, though analysts warn that further escalation could disrupt markets.

## Iran Response

Iran had previously submitted a 14-point peace proposal to the US, but Trump said the latest terms are unacceptable. The outlook for negotiations remains unclear.
