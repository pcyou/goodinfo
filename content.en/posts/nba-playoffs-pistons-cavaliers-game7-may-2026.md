## Pistons Complete Historic Comeback

The Detroit Pistons defeated the Orlando Magic 116-94 in Game 7 on May 4, completing a rare comeback from a 3-to-1 deficit in NBA playoff history.

Tobias Harris delivered an outstanding performance, leading his team to dominate throughout the game and securing victory with a 22-point margin. This marks the Pistons' first major playoff series comeback in many years.

## Cavaliers Also Advance Through Game 7

On the same day, the Cleveland Cavaliers defeated the Toronto Raptors in another Game 7 showdown to advance to the next round. The Cavaliers will face the Pistons in the Eastern Conference semifinals.

Two Game 7 matchups on the same day delivered an exciting basketball feast for NBA fans.
