## Met Gala 2026 Highlights

The 2026 Met Gala was held on May 4 at the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York. As one of fashion's most prestigious annual events, this year's red carpet drew numerous celebrities and designers.

Vogue magazine provided comprehensive coverage of the red carpet looks, showcasing each guest's carefully curated ensemble. This year's Met Gala maintained its reputation as a premier fashion spectacle.

The Met Gala annually raises funds for the museum's Costume Institute while providing a creative showcase for designers and celebrities alike.
