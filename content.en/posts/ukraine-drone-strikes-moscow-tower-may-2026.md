## Ukrainian Drone Strikes Central Moscow

A Ukrainian drone has hit an upmarket high-rise building in central Moscow, according to BBC reports. The attack comes as the Kremlin prepares to hold a scaled-back military parade on Saturday for Victory Day commemorations.

### A Strike Before Victory Day

May 9 marks Russia Victory Day, celebrating the end of World War II. Each year, Moscow Red Square hosts a grand military parade. However, this year the parade will be reduced in scale, likely due to current security concerns and the ongoing conflict with Ukraine.

The drone strike, coming just days before Victory Day celebrations, is seen as a direct blow to the heart of Russia. The targeting of an affluent residential district in central Moscow demonstrates the growing range of Ukrainian long-range strike capabilities.

### Escalating Russia-Ukraine Conflict

This attack is the latest development in the escalating Russia-Ukraine conflict. Ukraine has previously used drones to target Russian oil infrastructure, including tankers belonging to the shadow fleet used to evade sanctions. Russia, meanwhile, continues to strike multiple locations across Ukraine.

With Victory Day approaching, confrontation on both military and diplomatic fronts is expected to intensify further.
