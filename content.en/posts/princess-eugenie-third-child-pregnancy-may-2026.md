## Overview

The British royal family has welcomed happy news. Princess Eugenie, 34, and her husband Jack Brooksbank have announced they are expecting their third child. Buckingham Palace and multiple British media outlets have confirmed the announcement, which marks Eugenie's first major personal statement since returning to social media.

## Details

Princess Eugenie is the second daughter of Prince Andrew, Duke of York, and Sarah Ferguson, and niece of King Charles III. She married Jack Brooksbank in 2018, and the couple already has two children.

The pregnancy announcement has drawn widespread media attention across Britain. Multiple outlets including the Associated Press, BBC, and USA Today have covered the story. Notably, this is Eugenie's first major personal announcement since returning to social media following controversies surrounding her father, Prince Andrew.

## Reactions

Royal observers note that Eugenie's pregnancy brings positive public attention to the royal family. Amid the various challenges the monarchy has faced in recent years, this family joy helps to reshape the royal family's public image.

Buckingham Palace confirmed the news in the customary manner but did not disclose a due date or further details. The British public and media have broadly expressed congratulations.
