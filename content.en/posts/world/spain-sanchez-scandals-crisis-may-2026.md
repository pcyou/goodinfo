## [Brief] Spain's PM Sánchez Faces Greatest Political Crisis

Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez is facing the most severe political crisis of his eight years in office, with multiple corruption scandals threatening his political survival.

According to the BBC, Sánchez is resisting growing calls to resign, saying he will "fight to the end." However, voices within his own Socialist Workers' Party are also calling for a leadership change.

Analysts suggest that if Sánchez cannot effectively manage the crisis, Spain could face early elections.
