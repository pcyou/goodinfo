## Core Summary

According to Axios, the speaker lineup for the Republican 2026 Midterm Convention has been finalized. The convention will be held in Dallas, Texas, and has been dubbed "Trump-a-palooza" by insiders. President Trump and Vice President Vance will both deliver keynote addresses.

## Event Details

The report indicates the convention aims to energize the Republican base ahead of the 2026 midterm elections. Multiple governors, senators, and House leaders will also attend. The Republican National Committee stated the convention will focus on economic policy and national security.

The Democratic side has not yet commented. Political analysts note the high-profile lineup demonstrates the Republican Party's determination to win the midterms.
