[Brief] AP News reported on September 8 that according to newly disclosed information, UAE intelligence officials had warned Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu days before the October 7, 2023 Hamas attack on Israel, with warnings involving a major upcoming Hamas operation. The report noted that the UAE side delivered relevant intelligence through multiple channels, but Israel failed to take effective preventive measures. After the disclosure, domestic political pressure on Israel rose significantly, with the opposition calling for an independent investigation committee to hold relevant parties accountable. Multiple Israeli political analysts believe the disclosure may further affect the Netanyahu government's position and have a profound impact on the Middle East peace process. The incident also highlights the key role of intelligence sharing in preventing major conflicts.
