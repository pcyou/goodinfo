## Core Summary

Cambridge University Professor Jason Arday has formally resigned after facing serious plagiarism allegations. Arday was once a star scholar in the university's diversity research field, but investigations found systematic plagiarism issues in multiple academic works, including large sections of text copied without proper attribution.

## Event Details

The scandal was initially exposed by media, after which Cambridge University launched an independent investigation. The investigation revealed serious academic misconduct in multiple published papers by Arday. Following the release of investigation results, Arday chose to resign voluntarily.

This incident has caused widespread shock in British academia, sparking discussions about academic publishing review mechanisms and university internal oversight systems. Multiple universities have begun reviewing their academic integrity safeguards measures.
