Louisiana Senator Bill Cassidy is standing firm amid pressure from Trump's "revenge campaign" against disloyal Republicans. Saturday's Senate primary will serve as a key test of Trump's political influence.

Politico reports that while Trump is attempting to punish Republican senators through primary challengers, Cassidy maintains a solid support base in Louisiana.
