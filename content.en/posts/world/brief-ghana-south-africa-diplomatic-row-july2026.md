# [Brief] Ghana Halts Visit by South African President Amid Row Over Anti-Migrant Protests

Ghana has formally suspended South African President Cyril Ramaphosa's planned state visit. The decision stems from recent anti-immigrant protests in South Africa, with Ghana expressing serious concern over the safety of its diaspora community.

Recent protests in South Africa have targeted immigrants from other African nations, with some turning violent against foreign businesses and residents. Ghana has a large diaspora community in South Africa.
