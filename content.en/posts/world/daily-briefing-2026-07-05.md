## 3️⃣ Ukraine Strikes St. Petersburg Oil Hub, Russian Energy Lifeline Severely Damaged

**Importance Rating: 8.5/10**

In the early hours of July 4, Ukrainian forces launched a precision strike on a major oil terminal near St. Petersburg, Russia, causing multiple oil storage facilities to catch fire. The Primorsk oil terminal is one of Russia's largest oil export hubs on the Baltic coast, with an annual throughput of over 50 million tons, primarily serving the European market. Ukrainian military spokespersons stated that the operation used domestically produced long-range cruise missiles, precisely hitting the target's core area. Satellite images show that at least three large oil storage tanks were hit and caught fire, with flames burning for hours before being brought under control.

**Panoramic Perspective**: This strike marks a significant enhancement in Ukrainian forces' long-range strike capabilities and raises international concerns about energy security in the Black Sea region. Energy infrastructure becoming a target for strategic strikes means the conflict has entered a new phase, with both sides weaponizing energy as a means of confrontation.

## 4️⃣ Russian Forces Announce Full Control of Kostiantynivka, Major Turning Point in Russia-Ukraine War

**Importance Rating: 8.0/10**

The Russian Ministry of Defense announced on July 4 that Russian forces have gained full control of the strategic eastern Ukrainian city of Kostiantynivka. This fortified stronghold in the Donetsk region fell after months of intense fighting, marking a major turning point in the Russia-Ukraine war. Military analysts point out that the city was one of the core nodes of the Ukrainian army's "fortress belt" in the Donetsk region, connecting multiple important supply lines. Its loss will have a cascading effect on Ukrainian deployments across the entire eastern front.

**Panoramic Perspective**: The fall of Kostiantynivka means a major gap has appeared in Ukraine's defensive line on the eastern front. Russian forces can now advance toward larger cities such as Kramatorsk and Sloviansk, which will further alter the battlefield situation and may also affect future peace negotiation leverage.

## 5️⃣ Peru Election Results Announced, Keiko Fujimori Wins by Narrow Margin

**Importance Rating: 7.5/10**

Peru's electoral court officially announced that right-wing candidate Keiko Fujimori won the second round of the presidential election held on June 7. She defeated left-wing candidate Roberto Sánchez with 50.135% of the vote, with a margin of less than 50,000 votes. This is the 51-year-old Fujimori's fourth attempt at the Peruvian presidency, having previously run in 2011, 2016, and 2021, each time losing by narrow margins. Her victory, along with the election of Abelardo de la Espriella as Colombia's president, together mark an important trend of political shift to the right in the Latin American political landscape.

**Panoramic Perspective**: Fujimori's victory reflects the pendulum effect in Peruvian politics. After experiencing economic difficulties under left-wing governance, voters are turning to the right seeking change. This trend, combined with Colombia's election results, constitutes a regional phenomenon of political shift to the right in Latin America, reflecting the region's reassessment of populism and economic management capabilities.
