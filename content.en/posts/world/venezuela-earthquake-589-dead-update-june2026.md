## Summary

The death toll from Venezuela's devastating earthquake sequence has risen to at least 589, with many still missing. International rescue teams from multiple countries have arrived in the disaster zone. The rare "doublet" earthquake — two strong quakes in rapid succession — caused unprecedented destruction.

## Details

The earthquake sequence began earlier this week in northwestern Venezuela. The death toll has climbed from an initial 75 to 235 and now to 589, with rescue operations hampered by collapsed buildings and destroyed roads.

International aid is accelerating, with rescue teams from Colombia, Brazil, Mexico and other nations arriving with specialized equipment. The US has also announced emergency humanitarian assistance.

BBC reported the story of a mother who died shielding her daughter. Her husband wrote on social media: "You gave your own life for our daughter."

## Analysis

The severity of this disaster is compounded by Venezuela's prolonged economic crisis, which has eroded building standards and infrastructure resilience. The rare "doublet" earthquake pattern has drawn intense scientific interest, as it challenges conventional seismic risk models.
