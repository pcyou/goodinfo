## Coast Guard in 'Crisis' as Government Shutdown Halts May Pay, Cuts Power

The US Coast Guard is facing an escalating operational crisis. According to CBS News, the ongoing Department of Homeland Security (DHS) government shutdown will result in halted paychecks for May, power cuts at overseas mission bases, and significant impairment of the service's global operational capabilities.

### Pay Interruption Affects Tens of Thousands

The Coast Guard is one of the five branches of the US armed forces, with over 40,000 active-duty personnel and tens of thousands of reservists and civilian employees. The pay interruption caused by the government shutdown will directly affect the livelihoods of these personnel and their families, potentially triggering mass attrition and declining morale.

Unlike other military branches, the Coast Guard does not automatically receive appropriated funding guarantees during a government shutdown. This makes Coast Guard personnel among the most severely affected federal employee groups.

### Overseas Mission Capabilities Impaired

Reports indicate that the shutdown has not only affected pay distribution but also significantly impacted the basic operations of Coast Guard overseas facilities. Power supplies at multiple overseas mission bases have been cut, directly affecting the daily operations and mission execution capabilities at these facilities.

The Coast Guard deploys various cutters and aircraft globally, executing multiple missions including maritime search and rescue, law enforcement, drug interdiction, immigration enforcement, and national security operations. The deterioration of infrastructure threatens to degrade these critical mission capabilities.

### Growing Mission Pressure

In the current international environment, the Coast Guard faces increasing mission demands. From Arctic region patrols to Caribbean drug interdiction operations, from homeland port security to deep-sea search and rescue missions, the Coast Guard's operational scope continues to expand.

However, the resource constraints caused by the government shutdown are eroding the service's readiness. Analysts warn that if the shutdown persists, the Coast Guard may face irreversible capability losses, including the departure of key personnel and delays in equipment maintenance.

### Political Stalemate Continues

The DHS government shutdown stems from an ongoing congressional deadlock over budget issues. Despite all parties recognizing the negative impact of the shutdown on national security, political divisions have made reaching an agreement difficult.

Coast Guard leadership has issued urgent appeals to Congress to resolve the budget impasse and restore normal funding. However, as of now, no clear solution has emerged.
