## Core Summary

A dangerous heatwave is set to sweep across multiple US states ahead of the Independence Day long weekend. The National Weather Service has issued excessive heat warnings for large areas, with temperatures expected to break historical records in several locations.

## Brief

According to BBC, the heatwave will affect large swaths of the US Midwest and East, with temperatures expected to exceed 38°C in some areas. The combination of high temperature and humidity will produce dangerous heat indices, posing serious health threats to the elderly, children, and outdoor workers.

Multiple cities have activated cooling centers and urged residents to check on elderly neighbors. Utility companies warn that surging air conditioning demand could overload local power grids.
