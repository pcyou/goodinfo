## Core Summary

Iran launched a missile strike on a US military base in Jordan, killing two American service members and leaving one missing. This marks the first time US troops have been killed by direct Iranian military action since the escalation of US-Iran tensions, pushing the conflict into a dangerous new phase.

## Event Details

According to CBS News, Iran struck a US military installation in Jordan on July 18, resulting in the deaths of two American soldiers, with a third currently listed as missing. This represents the first confirmed US fatalities from direct Iranian military action since the current round of hostilities began.

Axios reported that the targeted base is located in northern Jordan and serves as a critical forward deployment node for US forces in the Middle East. The missile impact triggered a massive explosion, and rescue operations remain ongoing. The identity of the missing soldier has not been released, and the military is conducting an intensive search.

The Jordanian government has not yet issued an official statement. As a US ally, Jordan has historically been careful to avoid being drawn into direct US-Iran confrontation, but this attack on its territory places Amman under unprecedented diplomatic pressure.

US Central Command stated it is conducting a comprehensive assessment of the incident and reserves the right to take further military action. The White House has not yet announced the President's specific response plan, but multiple members of Congress have called for a "devastating" counterstrike against Iran.

## Panoramic Analysis

This event fundamentally alters the nature of the US-Iran conflict. Previously, the confrontation followed an asymmetric pattern of "US strikes, Iran absorbs" - the US military conducted multiple nights of airstrikes on Iranian targets, while Iran's retaliation primarily targeted US allies rather than US forces themselves. Now that Iran has directly attacked a US base and caused casualties, Tehran has clearly decided to break that tacit understanding.

From a military strategy perspective, Iran's choice of the Jordan base carries multiple considerations. First, the Jordan base is relatively close to Iran's western border, giving US forces limited warning and interception time. Second, targeting US forces rather than Israel or Gulf states sends a signal of "proportional retaliation" - you strike my homeland, I can strike your forward positions. Third, causing US casualties can dramatically increase domestic anti-war pressure in America, forcing Washington to reassess the cost of military operations.

However, this strategy carries extreme risk. US military casualties have a "nuclear-level" effect on American domestic politics - members of Congress and public opinion will strongly demand retaliatory action. Historical experience shows that US military deaths often lead to further escalation rather than de-escalation. After Iran's 2020 missile attack on Al-Asad Air Base in Iraq, both sides ultimately chose restraint, but that was in the context of Iran having shot down a Ukrainian passenger plane, creating enormous international pressure. The current situation offers significantly less room for maneuver.

Regarding regional dynamics, Jordan's involvement will have profound implications. Amman has long played a delicate balancing role between Washington and Tehran, maintaining its military alliance with the US while hosting a large Palestinian refugee population. This attack may force Jordan to de facto join US military operations, disrupting the fragile balance of power in the Middle East. Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and other Gulf states will face similar pressure - if Iran can strike US forces in Jordan, then US troops stationed in other Arab countries are equally vulnerable.

## Multiple Perspectives

**US Position**: American military and political circles widely characterize this attack as an "unacceptable provocation." Multiple senators are demanding a "overwhelming" military response against Iran, including strikes on IRGC command centers. The White House faces a dilemma - no response damages deterrence credibility, while an excessive response risks full-scale war.

**Iranian Position**: Tehran defines this operation as a legitimate exercise of "self-defense rights." Iranian state media emphasizes that US bases are "springboards for aggression against Iran" and striking them complies with international law. Iran simultaneously warns that if the US continues airstrikes on Iranian territory, it will expand the scope of attacks.

**Jordanian Position**: Amman remains silent but faces enormous internal pressure. Anti-American sentiment and pro-Western positions coexist within Jordan, and the government must balance maintaining national sovereignty with preserving the US-Jordan military partnership.

**International Community**: The UN Secretary-General has called for maximum restraint from both sides. The EU High Representative for Foreign Affairs expressed "deep concern" and urged all parties to resolve disputes through diplomatic channels. Russia and China have called on the US to cease military adventurism.
