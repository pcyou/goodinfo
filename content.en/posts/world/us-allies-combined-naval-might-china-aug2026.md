## Core Summary

The U.S. and its allies have temporarily set aside internal frictions to demonstrate combined naval power against China, marking a significant shift in Asia-Pacific security dynamics. This rare joint military display is seen as a direct response to regional security challenges, highlighting the coordination consistency of Western allies on Indo-Pacific strategy.

## Event Details

According to the Wall Street Journal, the U.S. and multiple allies are coordinating a large-scale joint naval operation aimed at demonstrating military deterrence capabilities against China. Notably, participating nations had previously shown clear divisions on issues such as trade and defense burden-sharing, but this operation indicates high consensus on addressing regional security challenges.

The joint operation involves naval forces from multiple countries, including aircraft carrier strike groups, submarines, and maritime patrol aircraft. This is one of the largest and most multilateral joint naval displays in the Asia-Pacific region in recent years. Analysts note that both the timing and scale of this operation were carefully planned to send a clear message to all parties in the region.

U.S. Defense Department officials stated that this joint operation is an important part of "maintaining the rules-based international order," emphasizing that participating countries are committed to ensuring freedom of navigation and regional stability. Meanwhile, senior military officials from multiple countries called in a joint statement for resolving disputes through diplomatic channels to avoid miscalculation and escalation.

## Panoramic Perspective

This combined naval demonstration by the U.S. and its allies marks a new phase in the Indo-Pacific security architecture. From a strategic perspective, this operation has multiple implications: first, it demonstrates the coordination capabilities of Western allies when facing common security challenges, forming a unified position despite internal frictions; second, the large-scale display of naval power itself serves as effective deterrence against potential adversaries, helping maintain regional balance of power.

However, this strategy also faces significant risks. Concentrated military displays may be interpreted as provocative, triggering regional arms races and escalation of tensions. For China, such joint military operations may reinforce its perception of "being contained," potentially leading to harder countermeasures. Finding the balance between demonstrating resolve and avoiding conflict will be a test of diplomatic wisdom for all parties.

In the long term, this joint operation may accelerate the reshaping of the Asia-Pacific security landscape. The strengthening of traditional alliances and establishment of new security cooperation mechanisms will profoundly affect regional order for decades to come. At the same time, this also provides new opportunities for multilateral diplomacy—against the backdrop of military deterrence, all parties may be more motivated to resolve differences through negotiation.

## Multiple Perspectives

**U.S. Official Position**: The U.S. Defense Department emphasized that this operation aims to "maintain the rules-based international order" and ensure freedom of navigation and regional stability. U.S. officials stated this is not hostile action against any specific country, but a demonstration of allies' determination to maintain regional peace.

**Participating Countries' Attitudes**: Multiple participating nations called in joint statements for resolving disputes through diplomatic channels, reflecting their complex mindset of wanting to show unity while avoiding loss of control. Some countries, while publicly supporting the joint operation, also emphasized the importance of dialogue.

**Regional Security Experts**: Analysts point out that the effectiveness of this joint operation depends on whether it can translate into effective diplomatic outcomes. Pure military displays cannot resolve fundamental differences—only combining military deterrence with diplomatic efforts can truly achieve regional stability.
