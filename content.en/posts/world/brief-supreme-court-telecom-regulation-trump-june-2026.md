The US Supreme Court ruled in favor of the Trump administration's efforts to strengthen federal oversight of telecom companies, allowing the FCC to levy fines against mobile carriers. The decision expands the Federal Communications Commission's enforcement authority and could significantly impact compliance costs and operational models across the telecommunications industry.
