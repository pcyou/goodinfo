## NATO Condemns Russian Drone Crossing Into Romania

NATO has formally condemned a Russian drone that crossed into Romanian airspace and struck a residential block, calling the act "reckless." The incident injured two people, and Romania's government has announced it will convene an emergency meeting to discuss its response.

This marks another instance of the Russia-Ukraine conflict spilling over into NATO member territory. Romania, as a key NATO eastern flank member, has its territorial security closely monitored by the alliance. Analysts suggest the incident could further escalate tensions between NATO and Russia.
