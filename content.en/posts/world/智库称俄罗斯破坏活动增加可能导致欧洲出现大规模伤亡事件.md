## Core Summary

A think tank has warned that Europe may face an increased risk of major security threat events as Russian sabotage activities rise.

## Event Details

According to the latest research report, multiple European countries have reported suspicious activities targeting critical infrastructure in recent months. These incidents include unusual disruptions to power facilities, communication networks, and transportation hubs. Analysts point out that the patterns of these actions are similar to known operational methods of Russian intelligence agencies.

The report emphasizes that although no incidents causing significant casualties have occurred so far, the frequency and complexity of sabotage activities are increasing. Experts warn that more serious security threat events may occur in the future if effective measures are not taken.

EU countries are strengthening coordination, sharing intelligence information, and enhancing the protection capabilities of key facilities. NATO has also stated that it will closely monitor this trend and work with member states to develop response strategies.

## Panoramic Perspective

This warning reflects the severe challenges facing Europe's current security environment. The use of hybrid warfare methods poses new tests to traditional military defense. The combination of cyber attacks, physical destruction, and information warfare constitutes a multi-layered security threat.

From a historical perspective, similar security threats appeared during the Cold War, but the current technological environment makes sabotage activities more widespread and covert. The interconnectivity of critical infrastructure means that a single node failure may trigger a chain reaction, affecting the normal operation of the entire region.

The international community needs to reassess the framework of security cooperation. Traditional military alliance mechanisms may not be sufficient to address such asymmetric threats, requiring more flexible intelligence sharing and rapid response mechanisms. At the same time, resilience building for civilian infrastructure should also become an important part of national security strategy.

Furthermore, this trend highlights the importance of energy security and supply chain stability. Europe's dependence on new infrastructure during the energy transition makes it more vulnerable to potential attack targets.

## Multiple Viewpoints

Security experts generally believe that this warning has a realistic basis. Several former intelligence officials pointed out that Russia does have the capability and historical record to carry out such operations. However, some analysts believe that some incidents may be exaggerated to promote political agendas for increased defense budgets.

An EU Commission spokesperson stated that the EU is assessing related risks and will take necessary measures to protect member states' critical infrastructure. Some Eastern European countries have called for strengthening NATO's military presence on the eastern flank to deter potential sabotage activities.

On the other hand, Russian officials deny involvement in any sabotage activities and claim these accusations are baseless slander. The Russian side expressed willingness to resolve security concerns through dialogue but demanded that the West stop hostile actions.
