## Core Summary

British hygiene brand Dettol has apologised after an advertisement in China sparked widespread backlash. The ad featured a man looking for a partner who is not tainted by other men, drawing accusations of sexism.

## Event Details

According to BBC News, the ad spread rapidly on social media before triggering strong backlash. Dettol China team issued a statement acknowledging the ad was inappropriate and insensitive.

## Panoramic Analysis

This incident reflects the cultural sensitivity challenges facing multinational brands in the Chinese market. Chinese consumers attention to gender equality issues has risen significantly in recent years.
