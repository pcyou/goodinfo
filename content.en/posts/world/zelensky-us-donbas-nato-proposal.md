## Key Facts

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky revealed that the United States has proposed a new peace plan. The core of this proposal is that Ukraine agrees to renounce its territorial sovereignty claims over the Donbas region (including Donetsk and Luhansk) in exchange for NATO accepting Ukraine as a full member. This proposal marks a major adjustment in Western countries' approach to resolving the Ukrainian conflict and has drawn widespread international attention.

## Event Details

According to The Independent, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky disclosed in his latest statement that the US government has put forward a new peace proposal. The essence of this plan is: Ukraine agrees to give up its territorial sovereignty claims to the Donbas region in exchange for NATO admitting Ukraine as a full member.

This proposal is considered one of the most substantive peace initiatives proposed by Western countries since the outbreak of the conflict. Analysts point out that the plan attempts to find a balance between ensuring Ukraine's security and satisfying some of Russia's strategic demands.

Zelensky stated that the Ukrainian government is seriously evaluating this proposal but emphasized that any final decision must undergo national discussion and parliamentary approval. He also noted that the Ukrainian public holds strong positions on territorial integrity issues, making the negotiation process challenging.

Currently, Russia has not yet made an official response to this proposal. Kremlin spokespersons stated that Moscow will continue to adhere to its established position, namely that Ukraine must recognize the "new realities" of the Donbas region and Crimea.

## Competing Views

**Supportive Perspective**: Some European diplomats believe this proposal provides a viable path to ending the prolonged conflict. They argue that NATO membership would provide Ukraine with strong security guarantees, and while territorial concessions are painful, they may bring lasting peace. Some analysts also emphasize that Ukraine's accession to NATO would enable its economic reconstruction to receive more international support.

**Opposing Perspective**: Ukrainian nationalist groups and some parliamentarians strongly oppose this proposal, viewing it as a betrayal of Ukrainian sovereignty. They point out that millions of Ukrainian citizens live in the Donbas region, and abandoning the area means abandoning these compatriots. Additionally, critics worry this precedent could be emulated by other countries, undermining fundamental principles of international law.

**Neutral Perspective**: International relations scholars caution that successful implementation of such proposals requires complex execution mechanisms and international oversight. They note that historical experience shows territorial-for-security agreements often face long-term implementation difficulties. The key lies in establishing credible security guarantee mechanisms to ensure Ukraine does not face renewed security threats after making concessions.

## Panoramic Analysis

The emergence of this proposal reflects that the Ukrainian conflict has entered a new phase. After nearly two years of intense confrontation, all parties have realized that military means cannot achieve decisive victory, making diplomatic resolution inevitable. However, how to balance safeguarding national sovereignty with maintaining regional stability remains an extremely complex challenge.

From a geopolitical perspective, Ukraine joining NATO would fundamentally alter Europe's security architecture. This not only means NATO expansion reaching new heights but may also trigger strong reactions from Russia. Historically, NATO eastward expansion has been a sensitive issue in Russia-Europe relations, and this proposal may further exacerbate strategic mutual suspicion between both sides.

From an international law perspective, the practice of exchanging territorial concessions for security guarantees is controversial. The UN Charter clearly stipulates that respecting the sovereignty and territorial integrity of all countries is a fundamental principle of international relations. Any arrangement violating this principle could set a dangerous precedent, affecting the stability of global order.

From a humanitarian perspective, residents of the Donbas region, regardless of their political stance, are the biggest victims of this conflict. Any solution must fully consider the wishes and well-being of local populations, avoiding the creation of new humanitarian crises. The international community should strengthen attention to civilian protection, ensuring the peace process does not come at the expense of ordinary people's interests.

In the future, the ultimate fate of this proposal will depend on the outcome of multi-party maneuvering. The Ukrainian government's decisions, Russia's response, divisions within NATO, and the attitude of the international community will all influence the development of events. Regardless, achieving lasting peace requires sincerity and wisdom from all parties, not simple zero-sum games.
