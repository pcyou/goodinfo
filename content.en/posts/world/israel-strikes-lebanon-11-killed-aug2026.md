## Core Summary

Israeli military forces conducted airstrikes on southern Lebanon on August 15, killing at least 11 people and injuring several others. Lebanese authorities reported that the strikes targeted multiple locations, causing significant casualties and property damage. This represents one of the most serious military operations in the region recently, sparking deep international concern about further escalation. The United Nations and multiple governments have called on all parties to exercise restraint and prevent further deterioration of the situation.

## Event Details

According to BBC reporting, Israeli forces launched new airstrikes on southern Lebanon on August 15 local time, killing at least 11 people and injuring several others. A spokesperson for Lebanon's Health Ministry stated that casualties included civilians, with some injuries being critical.

Lebanese security sources revealed that Israeli warplanes entered Lebanese airspace from the Mediterranean and carried out precision strikes on multiple targets in the south. The airstrikes damaged numerous buildings and disrupted power and communications infrastructure in some areas. Rescue workers are searching through rubble for survivors, and the death toll may rise further.

The Israeli military has not yet issued an official statement on the airstrikes, but previously indicated it would take decisive action against any security threats from southern Lebanon. Israeli officials stated the strikes targeted Hezbollah military facilities in the region, aimed at eliminating threats to northern Israel.

The Lebanese government strongly condemned the airstrikes, calling them a serious violation of Lebanese sovereignty. Lebanon's president issued a statement expressing deep condolences to victims' families and calling for international intervention to halt Israeli military operations.

The United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon stated it is closely monitoring developments and calling on all parties to exercise maximum restraint to avoid actions that could escalate the situation. A UN Secretary-General spokesperson expressed concern over casualties from the strikes and called for resolving differences through dialogue and diplomacy.

These airstrikes occurred against the backdrop of ongoing tensions in the Middle East. Recent confrontations between Israel and Iran, evolving situations in Syria, and other regional flashpoints have kept the entire Middle East in a highly sensitive state. The international community fears that any new military action could trigger chain reactions leading to broader conflict.
