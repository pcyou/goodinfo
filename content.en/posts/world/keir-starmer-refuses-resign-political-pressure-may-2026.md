## 📰 Article

### Starmer Holds Firm

British Prime Minister Keir Starmer has stated unequivocally that he will not resign despite mounting political pressure, The New York Times reports. The newspaper notes that Britain's electorate is "splintering," raising questions about whether the country's political system can withstand the strain. The Labour Party faces multiple challenges as local elections approach.
