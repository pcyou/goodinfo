## [Flash] Indonesia Mount Dukono Erupts; 3 Hikers Killed Despite Climbing Ban

Indonesia's Mount Dukono erupted, sending huge plumes of ash into the sky. Authorities had issued a climbing ban, but several hikers ignored the warning. The eruption killed 3 people and injured others. Indonesia sits on the Pacific Ring of Fire with over 130 active volcanoes.
