## 2 Dead, 3 Injured After Shooting at Carrollton Koreatown Shopping Center

CBS News reports that a shooting at a Koreatown shopping center in Carrollton, Texas, left 2 people dead and 3 injured.

Police say the suspect is in custody. The incident occurred in a suburban Dallas area with a significant Asian-American business community. The motive remains under investigation.

Authorities responded to the scene and secured the area. Victims were transported to local hospitals for treatment.
