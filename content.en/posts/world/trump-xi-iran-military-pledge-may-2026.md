## Trump Says Xi Pledged Not to Supply Military Equipment to Iran

US President Donald Trump stated during his Beijing summit that Chinese President Xi Jinping has pledged not to provide military equipment to Iran. The announcement marks a significant diplomatic development amid escalating US-Iran tensions in the Strait of Hormuz.

Trump said China made clear it would not supply Iran with equipment or technology that could be used for military purposes, a commitment seen as crucial for de-escalating the regional crisis.

## Core Issues at the US-China Summit

Iran was a central topic during the bilateral talks. Trump and Xi discussed Iranian military assistance, freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz, and regional stability in the Middle East.

Analysts note that China's neutral stance on Iran is critical to easing tensions. Beijing has consistently called for dialogue and opposed using the strait as a venue for military confrontation.

## The Strait of Hormuz Crisis

The Strait of Hormuz is one of the world's most important oil transit routes, with roughly a third of seaborne oil passing through daily. Recent weeks have seen military confrontations between US and Iranian forces, raising global concerns about energy supply security.

If China can play a mediating role by preventing Iran from receiving additional military support, the risk of conflict escalation would be significantly reduced. The international community is hopeful that the two major powers can find common ground on the Iran issue.
