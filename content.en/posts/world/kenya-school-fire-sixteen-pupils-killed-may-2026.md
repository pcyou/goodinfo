A severe fire at a school in Kenya has killed at least sixteen students on May 28, local police confirmed. Search-and-rescue operations are ongoing, and the casualty toll is expected to rise.

The fire broke out in the school's dormitory area and spread rapidly. The tragedy has reignited concerns about fire safety in East African schools, many of which operate with aging infrastructure and lack basic fire suppression equipment.
