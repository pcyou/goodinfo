# [Brief] Three Tankers Hit in Latest Attacks in the Strait of Hormuz

British military authorities have confirmed that three tankers were hit in the latest attacks in the Strait of Hormuz. This marks another major security incident in the strategic waterway following a previous single-tanker attack.

The Strait of Hormuz is the world's most important oil transit route, with approximately 21 million barrels of crude oil passing through daily. The incident immediately triggered oil price fluctuations, and maritime insurers have raised war risk premiums for the region.
