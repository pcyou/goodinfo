## King Charles III Addresses U.S. Congress

On April 28, 2026, King Charles III delivered a speech at a joint session of the U.S. Congress, calling for enhanced cooperation between the United Kingdom and the United States to address increasingly pressing global challenges. The speech was a centerpiece of the King and Queen Camilla's state visit to the United States, which is being held to commemorate the 250th anniversary of American independence.

### Core Message

"The challenges we face are too great for any one nation to bear alone," King Charles told lawmakers from both chambers of Congress. In his address, he emphasized the need for cooperation in areas including climate change, international security, and global public health, and called on both nations to continue upholding the rules-based international order.

The New York Times noted that Charles's speech was carefully crafted to express steadfast support for the "special relationship" between the UK and US, while also signaling different positions from the Trump administration on certain global issues. Analysts suggested that this delicate balance reflects Britain's cautious stance between maintaining traditional alliance ties and preserving diplomatic independence.

### State Visit Itinerary

The state visit by King Charles III and Queen Camilla spans multiple locations, including Washington, D.C., New York City, and Virginia. Prior to the Congressional address, the royal couple met with President Trump and attended a White House state dinner.

USA Today reported that at the state dinner, both Melania Trump and Queen Camilla chose pink-toned gowns, becoming a focal point for media coverage. The New York Times also published a detailed feature on the state dinner menu.

### Political Significance

The visit comes at a time when UK-US relations face multiple challenges. While the two countries maintain close ties in traditional security and trade areas, there are certain differences in their positions on climate change, international trade, and global governance.

The Guardian noted that Charles's speech struck a balance between ceremonial humor and solemnity, but also contained subtle diplomatic undertones. His call for "stronger cooperation" was seen as a recalibration of the UK-US relationship in the current international context.

### Reactions

Lawmakers from both parties in Congress gave broadly positive responses to Charles's address. Several cross-party lawmakers stated after the speech that the UK-US partnership is essential for addressing 21st-century global challenges.

Analysts believe that the state visit is not only a celebration of America's 250th anniversary of independence, but also an important diplomatic activity for both nations to reaffirm their bilateral relationship in a new era.

### What's Next

King Charles III and Queen Camilla will continue their visit in New York and Virginia. The trip is widely viewed as a significant milestone in UK-US relations in the new period.
