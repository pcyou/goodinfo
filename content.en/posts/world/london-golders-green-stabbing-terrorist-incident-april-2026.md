## 📰 London Police Declare Terrorist Incident After Golders Green Stabbing Attack

Two Jewish men were stabbed in Golders Green, north London, on April 29, 2026, in an attack the Metropolitan Police have declared a terrorist incident.

A 45-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of attempted murder. CCTV footage showed a man lunging at another man, and police forensic officers in protective suits were deployed to the scene to conduct a large-scale evidence collection operation.

Golders Green is home to one of London's largest Jewish communities, with multiple synagogues and Jewish community facilities. The attack has caused significant concern and distress within the local community.

The Metropolitan Police said the investigation is ongoing. While the motive has not been confirmed, the incident has been classified as terrorism based on its nature and the targeted individuals. Police have increased patrols in the area to reassure the community.

The attack comes against a backdrop of rising antisemitism concerns in the UK. In recent years, the security of Jewish communities in London and other British cities has been a focal point for government policy.

Local Jewish community leaders have condemned the attack and called for enhanced security measures. Police have advised community members to remain vigilant and report any suspicious activity.
