## [Brief] Saudi Led Coalition Dismisses Misleading Houthi Claim of Riyadh Attack Warns Against Regional Escalation

Al Jazeera and Reuters report that the spokesperson for the Saudi-led coalition has dismissed the Houthi claim of an attack on the Saudi capital Riyadh as false information and warned the group against creating regional tensions through misleading propaganda. The spokesperson stressed that the coalition will continue to monitor Houthi activity and take necessary countermeasures.

The reports noted that the Houthis had earlier claimed to have carried out missile and drone strikes against Saudi Aramco facilities and publicly said the strikes had been executed. The news triggered market concerns over the stability of Middle East energy supply, and international oil prices saw a short-term rise after the news was released. Saudi officials denied the attacks and said the relevant threats had been successfully intercepted by air defenses.

Market analysts cited in the report argued that the release of false attack information together with short-term oil price moves once again highlights the Middle East energy market's high sensitivity to geopolitical news. The episode will push energy market participants to continue strengthening real-time monitoring of Middle East events and to use supply chain diversification and inventory adjustments to reduce the impact of short-term price swings.
