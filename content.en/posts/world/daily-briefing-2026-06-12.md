## 4. Scientists Map Underground Fungal Network Spanning 68 Quadrillion Kilometers

**Impact Score: 7.5/10**

A newly published study reveals that the total length of mycorrhizal fungal networks beneath Earth's surface exceeds 68 quadrillion kilometers, far exceeding previous scientific estimates. The research produced the world's first global map of mycorrhizal fungal distribution, revealing the critical role these microscopic organisms play in maintaining ecosystem health. Mycorrhizal fungi form symbiotic relationships with plant roots, helping them absorb water and nutrients while storing significant carbon. Scientists say this discovery improves understanding of Earth's carbon cycle and offers new scientific basis for addressing climate change.

## 5. Tornadoes Strike Chicago Area; Homes Collapsed in Streator

**Impact Score: 7.5/10**

The Chicago area was hit by severe tornadoes on the evening of June 11 through early June 12. The city of Streator, Illinois, was the hardest hit, with multiple homes completely collapsed and streets covered in debris. Rescue teams have arrived on scene, with casualty counts still being determined. Meteorological authorities issued extreme weather alerts for the region. This is one of the most severe convective weather events to hit the U.S. Midwest this year, underscoring the growing trend of extreme weather.
