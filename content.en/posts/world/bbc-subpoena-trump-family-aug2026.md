## Core Summary

According to recent British media reports, BBC's legal team is pushing to subpoena members of Trump's family, requiring them to provide testimony regarding intentions related to the speech delivered outside the U.S. Capitol on January 6, 2021. BBC lawyers argue that Trump family members "have personal knowledge" of his intentions in the speech, and this legal action represents an escalation in the legal investigation surrounding the event.

## Event Details

According to BBC News, this legal action stems from a documentary produced by BBC's investigative program Panorama about the January 6, 2021 event. The documentary involved an in-depth investigation into the events, which sparked related legal disputes.

BBC's legal team argued in filed documents that Trump's family members have first-hand knowledge of the planning process and true intentions behind the former president's speech outside the Capitol. Lawyers believe these testimonies are crucial for reconstructing the full picture of the event.

This legal action involves multiple layers of complexity. First, it touches on the boundary between media investigative rights and personal privacy rights. Second, given the involvement of the former president's family members, the case is highly politically sensitive. Legal experts point out that cross-border subpoenas face different procedural requirements in the British and American legal systems, and the entire process could take months.

Trump's side has not yet issued a formal response. His legal team has previously taken a hardline stance in similar cases, questioning the motivations and legitimacy of investigative bodies.
