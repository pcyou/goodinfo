## Core Summary

Social democratic candidate Mamdani's endorsed candidates achieved a sweeping victory in New York City's Democratic primary. Most notably, Brad Lander unseated incumbent Democratic Representative Dan Goldman in a race that laid bare the city's deep divisions over the Israel-Gaza conflict.

## Event Details

According to BBC reporting, Mamdani-backed candidates won decisively across multiple New York City Democratic primaries. Brad Lander successfully challenged and defeated incumbent Congressman Dan Goldman.

The central dispute in this election focused on the Israel-Gaza war issue. Goldman was viewed as a moderate Democrat with firm pro-Israel positions, while Lander gained support from social democratic and progressive factions with positions more critical of Israeli policy.

Election results show New York City voters' divisions on foreign policy issues are deepening, particularly among younger voters and progressive communities where support for traditional pro-Israel positions is weakening.
