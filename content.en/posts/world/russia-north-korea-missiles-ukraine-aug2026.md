## Core Summary

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky accused Russia of using ballistic missiles supplied by North Korea to strike Ukrainian territory for the first time, killing at least six people and injuring 19 others. Zelensky warned that North Korean military assistance is expanding, with up to 50,000 North Korean soldiers potentially being sent to fight alongside Russian forces, posing a serious threat to European and global security.

## Event Details

According to BBC reports, Ukraine and Russia continued striking each other overnight. Zelensky stated that strikes in Zaporizhzhia involved ballistic missiles from Russia's ally North Korea, killing at least six and injuring at least 19. Meanwhile, Ukraine hit Wildberries warehouses in Voronezh in southern Russia. The company, known as Russia's Amazon, has been repeatedly targeted in recent weeks.

The attacks come a day after at least 13 people were killed in Ukrainian drone strikes on the Russian republic of Tatarstan, more than 1,100 km from the Ukrainian border.

In a video address, Zelensky said "for the first time" Russia "cannot wage war without reinforcements from North Korea." He added: "The more North Korean strikes there are here in Ukraine, in Europe, the more their missiles and soldiers are used, the more they correct their shortcomings and blind spots," posing a danger to the rest of the world.

Zelensky also hailed a successful air strike on a Russian petrochemical plant in western Siberia as a "very significant achievement" by the Ukrainian military, promising "more Ukrainian deep strikes, which should demonstrate to Russia and the Russians that peace is necessary."

## Panoramic Perspective

This incident marks a significant escalation in the internationalization of the Russia-Ukraine conflict, with profound geopolitical implications:

**First, North Korean military involvement is altering the conflict's balance of power.** North Korea's provision of ballistic missiles to Russia means this war, originally viewed as a regional conflict, is drawing in more external participants. North Korea exchanges military assistance for Russian technology, resources, and political support, forming a new type of strategic alliance.

**Second, real-world testing of North Korean missile technology raises regional security concerns.** North Korea has long been suspected of covertly proliferating missile technology to the Middle East and other regions. If North Korean missiles obtain combat data from the Ukrainian battlefield and use it to improve their weapons systems, it will pose a greater threat to South Korea, Japan, and US allies in the Asia-Pacific region.

**Third, the conflict's "globalization" trend is accelerating.** From North Korean soldiers and missiles to continued Western military aid to Ukraine, this conflict has evolved into a proxy battlefield for multi-party power competition. This internationalization trend makes conflict resolution more complex, as any peace agreement must coordinate the demands of multiple stakeholders.

From a strategic perspective, Zelensky's public disclosure of North Korean military assistance serves both to sustain international support for Ukraine and to pressure North Korea by warning of consequences for its military involvement. This strategy reflects Ukraine's proactive posture in information and diplomatic warfare.
