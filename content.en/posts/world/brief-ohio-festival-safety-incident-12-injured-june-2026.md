## [Brief] Ohio Festival Safety Incident; At Least 12 Injured, Suspects at Large

According to NPR, a safety incident occurred at a music festival in Toledo, Ohio, leaving at least 12 people injured. Police stated that a search for suspects is ongoing and the exact circumstances of the incident are still under investigation. Local medical facilities have activated emergency protocols to treat the injured. The event has raised concerns about safety management at large public gatherings.
