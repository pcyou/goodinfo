According to Politico, US President Trump has directly pressured Senate Majority Leader Thune, requesting the Senate cancel recess arrangements and maintain continuous session status to ensure the SAVE America Act can be voted on during the current session.

The SAVE America Act is an electoral reform bill promoted by the Trump administration, containing multiple controversial provisions including voter ID verification and restrictions on mail-in voting. Supporters believe the bill is crucial for maintaining electoral integrity, while critics accuse it of creating barriers to voting in future elections.

Trump's request for the Senate to maintain continuous session is unusual in US politics, typically employed only when the legislative agenda is extremely urgent. This move indicates the White House views the bill's passage as the highest priority for the current legislative cycle.
