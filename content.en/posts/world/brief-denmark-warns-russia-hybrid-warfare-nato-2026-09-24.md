## Core Summary

The Guardian reports that Danish intelligence services have issued a public warning that Russia is expected to significantly escalate hybrid warfare operations against NATO allies over the coming months. According to the report, the expected hybrid operations span cyber intrusions, disinformation campaigns, disruption of critical infrastructure, and systematic pressure on political and social stability. The public assessment puts European security under renewed pressure and re-energizes internal NATO debate over how to counter such operations.

## Event Details

**Core of the intelligence assessment**: Citing the latest public assessment released by Danish intelligence, the report states that Russia is preparing more intensive hybrid warfare operations against NATO member states in the coming months. The expected operations are no longer confined to traditional military confrontation but combine cyber intrusions, critical-infrastructure disruption, manipulation of public opinion, and targeted pressure on specific political figures, keeping targeted countries under continuous strain without crossing clear military thresholds.

**Europe's response dilemma**: The report analyzes that although European governments have markedly improved their awareness of hybrid threats in recent years, response options remain constrained by multiple factors. On one hand, hybrid operations are deliberately kept below the threshold that would trigger military retaliation, blurring the applicability of NATO's collective defense clause. On the other hand, while public attribution and exposure help raise public vigilance, they can also compromise intelligence sources and provoke more targeted retaliation. Analysts cited in the report note that EU and NATO member states still have visible disagreements on how to coordinate a unified response and how to share responsibility.

**Indirect impact on the Ukraine situation**: The report notes that the Danish warning comes amid the continuing Russia-Ukraine conflict and repeated NATO internal debates over aid to Ukraine. Some observers read the expected escalation of hybrid operations as Russia's attempt to apply greater non-military pressure on European willingness to support Ukraine while testing the resilience of NATO's internal coordination mechanisms. The report argues that the security hardening of energy, communications, and financial infrastructure across Europe will be an important indicator of hybrid-warfare response capability over the coming period.
