## Key Facts

US President Donald Trump arrived in Shenzhen, China on November 18, 2026, to attend the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Leaders' Informal Meeting. Chinese President Xi Jinping broke diplomatic protocol by personally welcoming Trump at the airport, a rare gesture that international observers interpret as a positive signal of potential thawing in US-China relations. Trump is also scheduled to hold highly anticipated trilateral talks with Russian President Vladimir Putin.

## Event Details

According to reports from NBC News and AP News, when Trump's special aircraft landed at Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport, President Xi was already waiting on the tarmac. This high-level reception is extremely rare in recent high-level US-China interactions, with the last similar scene dating back to Trump's first visit to China in 2017.

In his welcome remarks, Xi emphasized: "The Asia-Pacific region needs an open trade environment and multilateral cooperation mechanisms." He simultaneously criticized Western countries' protectionist policies and unilateral sanctions, calling on all nations to jointly maintain global economic stability. Russian President Putin also attended related events and condemned Western sanctions.

The core agenda of this APEC summit includes regional economic integration, digital economy development, climate change response, and supply chain resilience building. As the world's two largest economies, the interaction between US and Chinese leaders has drawn significant attention. Analysts point out that the Trump administration has shown some flexibility in its trade policy toward China recently, which may create conditions for both sides to reach consensus in certain areas.

Notably, after meeting with Xi, Trump will also hold bilateral talks with Putin. This arrangement of US-China-Russia trilateral interaction is relatively rare in international diplomatic history, reflecting the complexity of the current global power structure.

## Competing Views

**US Position**: White House spokesperson stated that President Trump's visit aims to advance US economic interests and ensure a fair trade environment. Officials emphasized that the US will not make concessions on core security interests but is willing to engage in pragmatic cooperation with China in economic and technological fields.

**Chinese Position**: Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson pointed out that China has always advocated resolving differences through dialogue and consultation, opposing unilateralism and protectionism. President Xi's personal welcome reflects China's importance and sincerity in developing healthy and stable US-China relations.

**International Observations**: The UK's Economist magazine commented that while Trump's diplomatic style remains unpredictable, his pragmatic tendencies may provide a window of opportunity for easing US-China tensions. However, the article also warned that the sustainability of personalized diplomacy is questionable, and institutional frictions cannot be completely eliminated through leaders' personal relationships.

**Russian Perspective**: The Kremlin stated that President Putin looks forward to in-depth exchanges with Trump on issues such as the Ukraine crisis, energy security, and global governance. Russia hopes to use this opportunity to improve relations with the West while consolidating its strategic partnership with China.

**Regional Reactions**: US allies such as Japan, South Korea, and Australia have expressed cautious concern about the US-China rapprochement. These countries worry about being excluded from great power consultations and are also concerned that US-China compromises may harm their security interests. Southeast Asian countries generally welcome dialogue and cooperation among major powers, believing it contributes to regional stability.

## Panoramic Analysis

Trump's visit to China and his interactions with Xi and Putin carry multiple geopolitical implications. First, it marks a possible important adjustment in US foreign policy. Since Trump's second term, his administration has shown a more pragmatic attitude in handling great power relations, no longer using ideological confrontation as the sole consideration. This shift may stem from the dual drivers of domestic economic pressure and changes in the international situation.

Secondly, the APEC summit has become an important stage for showcasing a multipolar world order. The simultaneous attendance of leaders from the US, China, and Russia symbolizes that the traditional Western-dominated international system is evolving in a more diversified direction. Especially against the backdrop of rising influence of Global South countries, coordination and cooperation among major powers has become increasingly important.

From an economic perspective, the Asia-Pacific region remains the main engine of global economic growth. APEC member economies account for over 60% of global GDP and nearly 50% of trade volume. As the two largest economies in the region, the cooperative relationship between the US and China directly affects prosperity and stability at both regional and global levels. Trump's emphasis on open trade contrasts sharply with the protectionist policies he pursued during his first term, which may signal a major shift in US trade policy.

However, deep structural contradictions still exist. Sensitive issues such as technological competition, the Taiwan question, and South China Sea disputes have not disappeared due to this high-level interaction. Observers warn that superficial diplomatic etiquette should not obscure substantive strategic differences. The long-term trajectory of US-China relations still depends on whether both sides can find a balance point on core interest issues.

Additionally, the Russian factor makes the situation more complex. Putin is using this opportunity to strengthen strategic coordination with China while attempting to divide US-European relations. The outcome of this triangular game will have profound implications for the future international order.
