## Core Summary

Russia continued its overnight attacks near Kyiv after President Volodymyr Zelensky warned of Ukraine's dwindling supplies of missile interceptors, killing three people including a child. The incident highlights the ongoing challenge to Ukraine's air defense capabilities and the continued impact of the conflict on civilians.

## Event Details

According to BBC reports, Russia carried out missile attacks on areas near Kyiv overnight. Zelensky had previously publicly warned that Ukraine's stock of air defense interceptor missiles was being rapidly depleted and urgently needed more support from international allies.

The attacks killed three people, including a child. Local rescue personnel conducted search and rescue operations in the rubble, trying to find possible survivors.

This incident occurred against the backdrop of continued international attention on the direction of the Ukraine conflict. Zelensky has repeatedly called on Western allies to accelerate the provision of air defense systems, but the actual pace of delivery still fails to meet frontline needs.
