## Russia ignores Ukraine ceasefire and attacks kindergarten

Ukrainian President Zelensky said Russia has "spurned the ceasefire" and launched attacks including one on a kindergarten. Kyiv is deciding on further action to take.

Zelensky previously announced that Ukrainian forces captured territory using only robots and drones, demonstrating the future potential of unmanned warfare.
