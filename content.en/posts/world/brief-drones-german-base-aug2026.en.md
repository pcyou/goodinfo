According to BBC reports, German security authorities confirmed on August 9 that unidentified drones were detected over a military base days after the Leipzig bombing incident. The origin and purpose of the drones remain unclear, and Germany's Bundeswehr and intelligence agencies have jointly launched an investigation.

A German Defense Ministry spokesperson stated that security measures have been strengthened at all military facilities and called on the public to provide relevant information. The investigation into the Leipzig bombing is ongoing, and authorities are assessing whether there is any connection between the two incidents.

This drone incident has heightened concerns among European countries about the security of critical infrastructure. Recently, several NATO member states have reported similar security incidents, raising alertness to potential threats.
