### 2. PHP Remote Code Execution Flaws Spark Internet-Wide Panic as Government Sites Disconnect

Two severe vulnerabilities in PHP, the language running most of the web, let attackers take over servers with no password. Cloudflare CEO Matthew Prince said the network saw thousands of exploit attempts even before the vulnerability was publicly disclosed. AWS logged exploitation four days before the patch shipped, and attacks are now being broadly exploited across the internet. Government websites disconnected entirely, and the PHP Foundation admitted a structural failure delayed the coordinated disclosure.

**Source**: The Decoder, Decrypt, Wired, Cointelegraph, Hacker News

**Panorama**: This is the first time AI has made a mass-scale contribution to offensive security. The flaw was discovered independently by OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol and Google's Gemini 4 Argon, with security firm Wiz reproducing the same finding. The real cost is not these two CVEs but a replicable supply-chain failure: when 70 percent of the internet runs on a single point maintained by a handful of volunteers, AI that accelerates discovery also compresses defenders' reaction time.
