## US Fires on Two Iran-Flagged Tankers, Escalating Middle East Tensions

The US military has struck two Iran-flagged tankers, further escalating tensions in the Middle East. Secretary of State Rubio said the US expects Iran to respond to the peace deal proposal.

The military action comes against the backdrop of a stalemate in US-Iran negotiations over the nuclear agreement and regional security. Rubio told reporters Washington hopes Iran will provide a clear answer on the peace deal offer.

### Incident Details

According to CBS News, the US military fired on two Iranian tankers near the Persian Gulf. Washington alleged the vessels violated international sanctions, but Iran has not yet issued an official response.

### Regional Impact

The incident further heightens tensions across the Middle East. Analysts warned that direct military confrontation between Washington and Tehran could trigger a chain reaction, affecting regional energy supplies and shipping security.

The international community has called for restraint and diplomatic resolution. The UN Secretary-General urged all parties to avoid further escalation.
