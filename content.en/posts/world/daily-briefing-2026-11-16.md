### 1. Zelenskyy Reveals US Proposal: Territory for NATO Membership

### 2. SpaceX Successfully Catches Starship with 'Chopsticks'

SpaceX achieved a major milestone by successfully catching the returning Starship spacecraft mid-air using the launch tower's mechanical arms (nicknamed "chopsticks"). This technological breakthrough represents a key step toward fully reusable rocket systems, potentially dramatically reducing space transportation costs. Elon Musk stated that this achievement lays the groundwork for future Mars missions and demonstrates commercial aerospace leadership in innovation.

### 3. Investors Sue Intel After 29% Stock Plunge

Intel faces a class-action lawsuit from investors following a 29% single-day stock drop. The suit alleges that company executives failed to disclose financial difficulties and strategic missteps, causing significant shareholder losses. Intel's recent earnings showed continued decline in its data center business and lagging behind NVIDIA and AMD in the AI chip market. This incident has sparked broad discussion about governance transparency and disclosure obligations among tech giants.

**Category**: Tech/Finance | **Impact**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

### 4. Yemeni Forces Retreat Leaves Houthis Controlling Key Red Sea Waterway

Saudi-backed Yemeni government forces announced their withdrawal from the Bab al-Mandab Strait to defend the interim capital Aden. This decision allows Houthi rebels to regain control of the strategic waterway connecting the Red Sea and Gulf of Aden, potentially escalating regional tensions and affecting global shipping security. The strait handles approximately 10% of global maritime trade, making its control changes significant for the world economy.

**Category**: World Affairs | **Impact**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

### 5. Widow of Assassinated Haitian President Registers for Election

Martine Moïse, widow of assassinated former Haitian President Jovenel Moïse, has officially registered for the presidential election. Her candidacy is seen as an important step in Haiti's political reconstruction, but the country faces severe political and economic crises, with gang violence rampant and basic public services paralyzed. Holding elections itself presents a huge challenge.
