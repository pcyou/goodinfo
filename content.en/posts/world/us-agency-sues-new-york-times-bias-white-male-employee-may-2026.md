## US Agency Sues New York Times, Accusing It of Bias Against White Male Employee

A US federal agency has filed a lawsuit against The New York Times, alleging systematic bias against white male employees in hiring and promotion decisions, The Washington Post reports.

This is one of the few employment discrimination lawsuits against a major news organization in recent years. The New York Times has not yet responded to the suit. The case could spark broader discussions about diversity and fair hiring practices in the journalism industry.
