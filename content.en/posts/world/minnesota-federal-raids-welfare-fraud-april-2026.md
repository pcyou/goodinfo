## 📰 Federal Agents Raid 22 Minnesota Sites in Social Welfare Fraud Investigation

April 28, 2026 — NBC News reports that federal agents conducted large-scale raids on 22 sites across Minnesota on Tuesday, as part of an investigation into alleged social welfare fraud.

### The Raid Operation

According to NBC News, the raids targeted multiple locations, including business centers, offices, and a daycare facility called Quality Learning Center. Federal agents entered the premises with search warrants, seizing documents and electronic devices.

Local media reported that agents forced their way into a Minneapolis daycare center, causing unease among neighbors. The area had recently experienced violent incidents, making the raid operation particularly notable.

### Political Context

The investigation has drawn political reactions. Senator Mullin from Missouri called out Minnesota Governor Walz, stating that he has "zero credibility on this issue."

Meanwhile, federal official Patel responded to a viral tweet from Walz, retorting: "Come again?"

### Investigation Scope

According to FOX 9 Minneapolis-St. Paul, Quality Learning Center was among the businesses raided by FBI agents on Tuesday. Investigators are pursuing allegations of welfare fraud, with the specific amounts involved and the number of suspects yet to be disclosed.

### Community Reaction

The raids have sparked widespread discussion in Minnesota's local communities. On one hand, some voices support the federal government's crackdown on fraud, arguing it helps protect taxpayer funds and those who genuinely need assistance. On the other hand, some have expressed concern about the scale and manner of the raids, arguing that overly aggressive tactics may cause unnecessary disruption to innocent residents and businesses.

At present, the FBI has not released detailed information about the investigation, including the amounts involved, the number of suspects, or potential prosecution plans. The case remains under active investigation.
