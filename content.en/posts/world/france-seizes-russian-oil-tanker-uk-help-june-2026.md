French President Emmanuel Macron announced that the French Navy, with support from the UK and other allies, has seized a sanctioned Russian oil tanker. This is the latest Western action to enforce oil sanctions against Russia, demonstrating Europe's continued strengthening of economic sanctions. The operation highlights coordinated international efforts to restrict Russian energy exports.
