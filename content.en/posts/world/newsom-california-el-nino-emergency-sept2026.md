## Core Summary

The San Francisco Chronicle reported that California Governor Newsom formally declared a statewide state of emergency in anticipation of an incoming strong El Niño climate event. The El Niño is expected to bring extreme weather including heavy rainfall, strong winds, massive waves, and flooding to the California coast, posing severe tests to California residents' safety of life and property, critical infrastructure, economic and social operations, as well as agricultural and marine industries. Following the declaration of a state of emergency, California governments at all levels will activate broader emergency response mechanisms, coordinating disaster preparedness resource deployment, critical facility reinforcement, public evacuation and sheltering, and post-disaster recovery planning.
