## Massie Falls in Kentucky GOP Primary

Trump-backed candidate Ed Gallrein has defeated incumbent Representative Thomas Massie in Kentucky's Republican House primary. Massie was a well-known "rebel" within the Republican Party who frequently opposed Trump's agenda.

**Election Results:**

- Navy veteran Gallrein defeated long-serving Representative Massie in a heated primary
- This marks another victory in Trump's "revenge" campaign against Republicans who opposed him
- Meanwhile, Andy Barr won the Kentucky Senate Republican primary to compete for McConnell's vacated seat

**Political Impact:**

Massie's defeat demonstrates Trump's enduring influence within the Republican Party. His "revenge spree" has stunned Senate Republicans, with Texas facing similar challenges. By backing primary challengers to punish disloyal Republicans, Trump is reshaping the party's power structure.
