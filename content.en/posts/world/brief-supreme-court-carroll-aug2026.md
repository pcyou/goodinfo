## Core Summary

The US Supreme Court on Monday again declined to hear Trump's appeal of the $5 million civil fraud judgment, upholding the lower court ruling. The case involves writer E. Jean Carroll's lawsuit alleging sexual abuse and defamation by Trump.

## Event Details

According to Reuters, the Supreme Court on Monday denied the certiorari petition filed by Trump's team, meaning the lower court's $5 million damages award remains in effect. This marks the third failed attempt by Trump's side to overturn the ruling through judicial channels.

Previously, Trump's team had appealed multiple times on grounds including alleged jury instruction errors and improper evidence admission, but all were rejected by courts at various levels. The Supreme Court's refusal to hear the case is seen as final confirmation of the legal conclusions.

## Panoramic Analysis

The Supreme Court's repeated rejections reflect consistent recognition of the case's legal conclusions across the judicial system. From a legal perspective, this means Trump's team has largely exhausted possibilities for reversal through judicial channels.

The political implications of this case continue to develop. As a sitting president facing a civil damages judgment, it holds landmark significance both in legal precedent and public opinion.

## Multiple Perspectives

**Legal experts** note that the Supreme Court's denial typically indicates the justices found no significant legal error in the lower court's ruling, representing an important victory for the plaintiff.

**Trump's team** stated they will continue seeking relief through other legal avenues, but available options have been substantially reduced.

**Plaintiff's attorneys** called the result a "demonstration of judicial independence and the rule of law," emphasizing that no one is above the law.
