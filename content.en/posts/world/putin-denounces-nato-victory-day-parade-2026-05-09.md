## Overview

Russian President Vladimir Putin delivered a speech at the Victory Day parade on Moscow's Red Square, sharply condemning NATO's expansion policy. This year's parade was scaled back compared to previous years.

## Parade Highlights

- The parade was smaller than in previous years, reflecting Russia's current military and economic situation
- Putin reiterated Russia's position on the Ukraine conflict
- The Red Square parade remains one of Russia's most important annual commemorative events

## NATO Condemnation

In his address, Putin accused NATO of continued eastward expansion, arguing it threatens Russia's security interests. He reaffirmed Russia's need to take all necessary measures to protect its security.

This statement is seen as continued pressure from Russia on Western nations and signals that Russia's position on Ukraine is unlikely to change in the near term.

## Geopolitical Impact

- Russia-West relations continue to deteriorate
- Little prospect of peace in the Ukraine conflict short-term
- Long-term uncertainty for European security architecture
