## 📰 Full Story

US envoy **Witkoff** and **Kushner** departed for Pakistan today to participate in mediation peace talks on Iran's nuclear issue.

### Background

These talks represent the latest development in recent Middle East diplomacy. Pakistan, as an intermediary, provides a neutral platform for dialogue between all parties.

### Key Issues

- **Nuclear Issue**: The future trajectory of Iran's nuclear program and non-proliferation
- **Regional Security**: Stability and security mechanisms in the Middle East
- **Economic Sanctions**: The phased lifting of economic sanctions on Iran

### International Reaction

The international community is closely watching this round of negotiations, with representatives from multiple countries also participating in related discussions in Pakistan.
