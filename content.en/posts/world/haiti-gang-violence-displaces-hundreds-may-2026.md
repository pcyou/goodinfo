Escalating gang violence in Port-au-Prince has displaced hundreds of residents, Al Jazeera reports. Haiti has long faced a security crisis, with armed gangs controlling large parts of the capital while government forces struggle to maintain order.

The international community has repeatedly called for strengthened peacekeeping efforts, but no effective intervention has emerged. Humanitarian organizations warn that the situation is deteriorating further.
