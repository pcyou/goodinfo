## Operation Overview

Spanish law enforcement agencies have successfully seized a record quantity of cocaine in the Atlantic Ocean in one of the largest drug interdiction operations in the country history.

## Operation Details

The operation was led by the Spanish Civil Guard which intercepted a vessel suspected of drug trafficking along an Atlantic shipping route. Boarding teams discovered large quantities of packaged cocaine aboard the ship.

## International Cooperation

The seizure was supported by intelligence from multiple European law enforcement agencies demonstrating the growing effectiveness of international anti-drug collaboration.

## Trafficking Trends

In recent years the Atlantic route has become a primary channel for South American drugs entering Europe. As a gateway nation Spain plays a critical role in combating transnational organized crime.
