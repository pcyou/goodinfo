## 摘要

密西西比州遭遇龙卷风群袭击，超过1000栋建筑受损。《纽约时报》报道，这场罕见的龙卷风群给当地社区造成严重破坏。救援人员正在评估损失并展开搜救工作。气象部门警告，恶劣天气可能持续影响该地区。

## Summary

A tornado cluster in Mississippi has damaged more than 1,000 buildings, according to The New York Times. Rescue workers are assessing damage and conducting search operations. Weather authorities warn that severe conditions may continue to affect the region.
