## Iran Accuses US of 'Reckless Military Adventure' After Hormuz Strait Exchange

Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi has strongly condemned US military actions in the Strait of Hormuz, calling them a "reckless military adventure." Araghchi stated that the US launches attacks every time a diplomatic solution appears within reach.

### The Hormuz Strait Incident

The exchange of fire occurred in the Strait of Hormuz, the world's most critical oil transit chokepoint. Approximately one-fifth of global oil shipments pass through the strait daily, making any military incident a significant concern for energy markets worldwide.

### Oil Prices Spike

International oil prices surged following reports of the clash. Markets are concerned that US-Iran tensions could escalate further, threatening global energy supply security. Analysts note that Hormuz Strait tensions have been a key driver of recent oil price volatility.

### Diplomacy vs. Military Posturing

President Trump stated that the US-Iran ceasefire remains in effect, but Iran's hardline rhetoric signals a severe deficit of mutual trust. The incident underscores the fragility of the Middle East geopolitical landscape and the precarious balance between diplomatic efforts and military confrontation.

### International Response

The international community has expressed deep concern over the military friction in the Hormuz Strait. Nations worldwide have called for restraint to prevent further escalation. The UN Secretary-General's spokesperson emphasized that peaceful resolution remains the only sustainable path forward.
