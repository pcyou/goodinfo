## Suicide Car Bomb Attack on Train in Pakistan Kills at Least 24

A devastating terrorist attack struck Pakistan as a passenger train was targeted by a suicide car bomb, killing at least 24 people and injuring dozens more.

## The Attack

The explosion occurred while the train was traveling along a key route. According to reports from the scene, a vehicle laden with explosives approached or drove toward the train before detonating. The blast caused significant damage to several train carriages.

Emergency responders have rushed to the scene, and the wounded have been transported to nearby hospitals. Local medical facilities have activated emergency protocols as the death toll may rise further.

## Context

Parts of Pakistan have long faced security threats, with militant groups repeatedly targeting transportation infrastructure. The scale and casualties of this attack underscore the ongoing severity of the security situation.

No group has yet claimed responsibility for the attack. Pakistani authorities have launched a full investigation.
