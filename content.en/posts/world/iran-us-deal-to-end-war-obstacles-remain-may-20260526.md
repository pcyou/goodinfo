## Iran-US Framework Deal Takes Shape, But Tehran Says Obstacles Remain

Reports indicate that Iran and the United States have reached preliminary agreement on a framework to end their conflict, though Iranian officials subsequently stated that significant obstacles remain before a final deal can be concluded.

### Negotiation Progress

According to reports, both sides have made breakthrough progress on several key issues, including military coordination and regional security arrangements. However, Iranian officials emphasized that deal details still require further negotiation and are far from a final stage.

While the Trump administration has actively promoted progress on the Iran issue, Tehran's statements suggest that negotiations still face numerous challenges.

### International Reactions

The international community has expressed cautious optimism about the ceasefire prospect. The UN Secretary-General urged both sides to seize the current opportunity and advance the peace process. European leaders have also voiced support for resolving disputes through diplomatic channels.

However, Israel's position on Iran remains hardline. Netanyahu announced plans to intensify military strikes against Hezbollah in Lebanon, signaling Israel's independent approach to regional security.
