## Today's Top 5 Global Stories

### 1. Europe Wildfire Crisis: Over 300,000 Evacuated

The wildfire crisis in southern Europe has continued to worsen over the past 24 hours. Cumulative evacuations in southwestern France and eastern Spain have now exceeded 300,000, with pine forests and camping areas around Arcachon Bay in France's Gironde department being extensively burned. The EU Civil Protection Mechanism has been fully activated, with Italy, Greece, and Portugal dispatching firefighting aircraft and ground teams to assist. Meteorological departments warn that high temperatures will persist, with fire risk levels at "extreme."

**Impact Assessment**: This is Europe's largest natural disaster this summer, causing severe impacts on tourism and agriculture. The insurance industry is expected to face billions of euros in claims.

### 2. DRC Ebola Outbreak: Death Toll Exceeds 1,300

The Ebola outbreak in eastern Democratic Republic of Congo has deteriorated sharply, with cumulative deaths now exceeding 1,300. The WHO warns the virus is spreading at an alarming rate. The outbreak is concentrated in North Kivu and Ituri provinces, where ongoing armed conflict has created enormous difficulties for containment. Some communities remain skeptical of vaccination, further complicating control efforts.

**Impact Assessment**: The crisis exposes structural shortcomings in the global public health system's ability to respond to outbreaks in conflict zones, with international response speed being questioned.

### 3. Berlin Pride Safety Incident: Vehicle Strikes Crowd

A serious security incident occurred at a Pride parade in central Berlin, Germany, when a vehicle drove into the crowd, injuring multiple people. Berlin police have cordoned off the scene and launched a large-scale manhunt. The motive for the incident has not yet been disclosed. This is another serious security incident at a major public event in Berlin in recent years, once again sparking discussion about security standards for open-air street events in Europe.

**Impact Assessment**: Expected to push EU countries to reassess security measures for street events, including more physical barriers and vehicle interception systems.

### 4. Middle East Situation: Houthis Attack Saudi Arabia as US-Iran "Quiet Night" Emerges

Yemen's Houthi rebels fired missiles at Saudi Arabia's Jizan province in retaliation for Saudi airstrikes on the port of Hodeidah. The US military disabled a second tanker attempting to break the Iran blockade in the Gulf of Oman. Meanwhile, a "quiet night" emerged between the US and Iran—the first pause after 13 consecutive nights of strikes—with an Omani delegation arriving in Tehran to begin diplomatic mediation. War and diplomacy are proceeding in parallel, with the situation's direction remaining uncertain.

**Impact Assessment**: The ongoing Hormuz Strait blockade continues to affect global energy supplies, increasing oil price volatility. Whether the diplomatic mediation window can translate into substantive peace progress remains in doubt.

### 5. India Student Movement: Education Minister Resigns as Modi Government Faces Challenge

India has seen its largest student protest movement in years. Demonstrations led by the "Cockroach Janta Party" (CJP), which originated from exam paper leak scandals, have rapidly spread into a nationwide youth movement. The Education Minister announced his resignation under sustained protest pressure. The CJP subsequently suspended protests but stated it would continue to monitor the government's reform commitments.

**Impact Assessment**: Reveals Indian youth's deep anxiety about the job market and examination system, demonstrating a new form of decentralized youth political participation in the social media era.
