### Key Facts

France is keeping curfews in several cities while offering interest-free loans of up to 50,000 euros to businesses affected by recent unrest. Prime Minister Sebastien Lecornu will meet trade unions and business groups to discuss security for further protests.

### Wider Perspective

Pairing curfews with interest-free loans reflects a local-government balancing act between public order and economic recovery: restricting night movement narrows the space for confrontation, while accessible funding helps affected merchants reopen before disruption turns into lasting economic damage. The prime minister's direct engagement with unions and employers shows the issue has shifted from the streets to a negotiating framework, since repeated student-led protests point to structural strains in education and employment that administrative measures alone cannot resolve. A stable combination of relief and dialogue keeps the situation manageable; without substantive policy responses on education and jobs, curfews will only postpone the underlying conflict.
