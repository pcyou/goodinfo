Gunfire erupted in the Philippine Senate building on May 13 as police attempted to arrest a senator, triggering a lockdown. All personnel were ordered to remain in safe areas.

The incident shocked Philippine politics. The targeted senator faces multiple charges, and armed resistance was reported during the arrest attempt. Heavy police reinforcements were deployed to the area following the exchange.

Casualties remain unconfirmed. The presidential palace has not issued an official statement. Analysts warn the incident could intensify political tensions within the Philippines.
