**Core Summary**

Trump reposted Dr. El-Sayed's "TrumpCare" universal healthcare proposal on social media, aiming to provide affordable medical coverage for all Americans.

**Event Details**

Former US President Trump shared a universal healthcare plan proposal by physician and political commentator Dr. El-Sayed on his social media platform. The so-called "TrumpCare" plan promises comprehensive health insurance coverage for all US citizens.

This move sparked widespread discussion, as the Trump administration had previously attempted to repeal the Affordable Care Act. Analysts suggest this may indicate subtle shifts in Republican attitudes toward healthcare policy.

Source: Google News / Washington Post

**Panorama Analysis**

Trump's attention to the universal healthcare proposal reflects the ongoing controversy surrounding US healthcare system reform. Although the specific details of "TrumpCare" remain unclear, this move may signal a shift in the Republican Party's stance on healthcare policy. With US healthcare costs remaining high and tens of millions lacking health insurance, this issue has become a core topic of bipartisan competition. Any substantive reform will face strong resistance from insurance companies, pharmaceutical firms, and healthcare institutions, while also needing to consider fiscal sustainability.

**Multiple Perspectives**

Supporters argue that universal healthcare is a basic human right and that the government has a responsibility to ensure all citizens have access to affordable medical services. Opponents worry that a government-led healthcare system could lead to inefficiency and increased fiscal burden. Healthcare industry experts recommend gradual reforms that expand coverage while maintaining market competition. Polls show that most Americans support some form of healthcare reform, but there are clear divisions on specific approaches.
