## Core Summary

According to BBC reports, Portuguese football superstar Cristiano Ronaldo posted a photo on social media showing their hands together wearing wedding rings, officially announcing his marriage to long-time partner Georgina Rodríguez. The couple has been together for nearly a decade and has multiple children. The wedding announcement quickly became a trending topic on social media worldwide.

## Event Details

Ronaldo posted the heartwarming photo on his personal social media account—showing two hands clasped together, both wearing elegant wedding rings. Although the photo did not reveal the specific location or format of the wedding, multiple media outlets speculate the ceremony may have taken place recently in private.

Georgina Rodríguez began dating Ronaldo around 2016 and has been by the football star's side ever since. The couple has a daughter together, and Georgina also cares for Ronaldo's three previous children. She has grown from an ordinary fashion store clerk to a social media celebrity with tens of millions of followers and a commercial brand spokesperson.
