# [Brief] AI Workers Drive San Francisco Home Prices to Record High

## Core Summary

BBC reports that San Francisco's median home price has climbed to $1.7 million, setting a new record high. The influx of wealthy AI workers has become the primary driver of this surge, pushing housing costs in the tech hub to levels unreachable for ordinary residents.

## Event Details

According to BBC, the median cost of a home in San Francisco has reached $1.7 million, a record high according to the latest figures. Wealthy AI workers are the main force behind this price surge, driving housing costs in the tech city to heights that ordinary residents cannot afford. Local residents are facing an increasingly severe housing affordability crisis, with some middle-class families forced to relocate.
