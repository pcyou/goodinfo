## Core Summary

The Washington Post reports that US President Trump welcomed Chinese President Xi Jinping at a Washington airport with an unusually personal ceremony, and the two leaders used their meeting to agree on an extension of their trade truce. Structural differences on artificial intelligence safety and cross-strait issues, however, remain unresolved.

## Event Details

**The airport ceremony**: According to the report, Trump personally greeting Xi at the airport is a highly unusual arrangement in the history of US-China relations. A guard of honor was deployed and the two leaders shook hands at the bottom of the aircraft stairs before jointly reviewing the honor guard. The arrangement is widely read as a clear signal that the US side wants to send goodwill to China and create a constructive atmosphere for the broader summit.

**Trade truce extension**: The report cites White House remarks indicating that the most concrete deliverable of the meeting is the agreement by both sides to extend the trade truce, which was otherwise close to expiration. The extension buys both trade teams additional time to work through tariffs, market access, and critical supply-chain issues.

**Unresolved structural differences**: The report stresses that the two leaders continue to hold clear structural differences on artificial intelligence safety, technology export controls, and Taiwan and South China Sea security. Xi reiterated China's principled position on those topics, while Trump emphasized that US concerns about fair competition and allied security will not be shelved. The report argues that the meeting's largest value is in setting the tone for the next phase of the bilateral relationship rather than resolving all disputes in the short term.
