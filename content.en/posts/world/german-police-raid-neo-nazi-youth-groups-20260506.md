## German police raid neo-Nazi criminal youth groups

German police have conducted large-scale searches across 12 states targeting far-right neo-Nazi criminal youth groups. Prosecutors say the raids focused mainly on eastern and southern regions of the country, targeting an organized criminal network.

This is one of the largest operations against far-right extremism in Germany in recent years.
