## Core Summary

Syria's transitional government issued a formal statement on August 22, strongly condemning recent Israeli military strikes in areas surrounding Damascus. The Syrian Foreign Ministry described Israel's military operations as "a blatant violation of international law and UN Security Council Resolution 338," representing a serious infringement on Syria's sovereignty and territorial integrity. The statement noted that the latest airstrike occurred just tens of kilometers from Damascus, causing infrastructure damage but no reported casualties. This is the latest in a series of Israeli strikes on Syrian targets since the regime change.

## Event Details

### Airstrike Sequence

According to BBC reports, Syrian military sources revealed that the Israeli Air Force conducted a precision strike on a military facility southwest of Damascus on the evening of August 21. Witnesses reported hearing at least three intense explosions followed by thick smoke rising. Syria's air defense systems attempted to intercept incoming missiles but were unsuccessful.

In its statement, the Syrian Foreign Ministry detailed a series of recent Israeli military operations: in addition to the Damascus area strikes, Israel also hit an airbase in Aleppo province near the Turkish border on August 18. Syrian officials described these attacks as "clearly intended to escalate" and aimed at undermining Syria's stabilization process.

### Israel's Position

The Israeli military has not issued official comments on the latest airstrikes, but Israeli officials have previously stated that their operations in Syria aim to "prevent Iran and its proxies from transferring weapons to Hezbollah in Lebanon." Israeli security sources told local media that recent targets included "facilities suspected of storing precision-guided weapons."

An unnamed official from the Israeli Prime Minister's Office stated that Israel "reserves the right to take defensive action when necessary" and emphasized that "as long as Iranian forces remain in Syria, Israel will not cease operations."

### International Reactions

The UN Special Envoy for the Middle East issued a statement expressing "serious concern" over "continuing military operations in Syria" and calling on all parties to "exercise maximum restraint and avoid further escalation." Turkey's Foreign Ministry strongly protested Israeli strikes near its border, calling them a "serious threat" to regional security.

Russian Foreign Ministry spokesperson stated that Israel's actions "exacerbate already tense situations in the Middle East" and called on the international community to "adopt a responsible approach." The Arab League Secretary-General described Israel's behavior as "aggression" and demanded an emergency Security Council meeting.

## Panoramic Perspective

The evolution of the Syria situation is at a critical historical juncture. Since the fall of the Assad regime, Syria has entered a transitional period filled with uncertainty. Israel has exploited this power vacuum to intensify strikes on Syrian targets, attempting to establish a "strategic buffer zone" to prevent Iran from transferring weapons to Hezbollah through Syria.

From a geopolitical perspective, Israel's military operations reflect its deep anxiety about the Middle East order in the "post-Assad era." Although the new Syrian government has maintained some distance from Iran, Israel still distrusts any Syrian regime and insists on maintaining military pressure. This "preemptive" strategy may achieve short-term tactical objectives, but in the long run could inflame regional tensions and plant hidden dangers for future peace processes.

Notably, Turkey's role in this situation is becoming increasingly prominent. Israeli strikes on targets near the Turkish border directly touch Ankara's strategic red lines. As a NATO member and regional power, Turkey's response will significantly impact developments. If Turkish-Israeli relations further deteriorate as a result, the Middle East geopolitical landscape will face deeper restructuring.

## Multiple Perspectives

**The Syrian government's position** is the most hardline, characterizing Israeli operations as "acts of aggression" and reserving "the right to respond by all legitimate means." Syria's Foreign Minister emphasized that the new government is committed to national reconstruction and stability but "will never remain silent in the face of sovereignty violations."

**Israel's security establishment** generally believes the current period represents a "strategic window" to strike at Iranian influence in Syria. A former Israeli military intelligence director stated: "Syria is in a weakened state, and we must use this opportunity to degrade Iran's military infrastructure."

**International observers** have expressed different views. The International Crisis Group's Syria Project Director noted: "Continued military strikes may be counterproductive, inflaming anti-Israeli sentiment within Syria and potentially pushing the new government toward Iran." The analyst suggested that regional security dialogue mechanisms should be established through diplomatic channels rather than relying on military means.
