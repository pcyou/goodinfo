## Core Summary

Jason Arday, a former lecturer at the University of Cambridge and the institution's youngest Black professor, has been found dead at age 41. Arday was highly regarded in the fields of education and sociology. His family stated they were "in shock" to have lost him, adding that a "campaign of misinformation" had been too much to bear.

## Brief Details

According to BBC reports, Arday had previously resigned from Cambridge University. His family did not disclose the specific cause of death in the obituary but emphasized that he "left an indelible mark on academia." Arday's research focused on race, educational inequality, and social mobility, and his work provided important academic reference for many minority scholars and students.

Cambridge colleagues and academic peers expressed condolences, calling Arday "an outstanding scholar and mentor" whose passing is a significant loss to British academia.
