## Israeli Forces Intercept Gaza-Bound Flotilla Near Cyprus

Israeli forces have intercepted an international aid flotilla heading to Gaza near Cyprus, according to BBC reports. The flotilla, consisting of multiple international activists, aimed to break Israel's naval blockade of the Gaza Strip and deliver humanitarian supplies.

Activists said the Israeli military boarded the vessels in international waters. The interception has renewed international concern over the humanitarian crisis in Gaza, particularly as the ongoing Iran war continues to escalate tensions across the Middle East.

Israeli officials stated the flotilla violated naval blockade regulations and the boarding was a "legitimate security procedure." However, international human rights organizations expressed concern, arguing that restricting humanitarian aid to Gaza during a crisis violates international law.

The incident underscores the complex situation in the Middle East. Meanwhile, the Trump administration has warned Iran that "time is running out," but peace negotiations remain stalled as regional conflict intensifies.
