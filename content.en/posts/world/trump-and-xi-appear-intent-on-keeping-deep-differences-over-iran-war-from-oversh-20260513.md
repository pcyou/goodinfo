## Trump and Xi Seek to Keep Iran Differences from Overshadowing China Talks

Trump and Xi appeared intent on keeping their deep differences over the Iran war from overshadowing broader bilateral talks, according to Reuters. Both leaders signaled willingness to pursue economic and trade consensus despite divergent positions on the Iran conflict.
