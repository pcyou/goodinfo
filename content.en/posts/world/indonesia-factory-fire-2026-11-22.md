## Core Summary

A serious fire broke out at a confectionary factory in Indonesia on November 22, 2026, confirming at least three deaths with 36 people still missing. Rescue teams continue searching for survivors at the scene, and the cause of the incident remains under investigation.

## Event Details

The fire occurred at a candy manufacturing facility in Indonesia. According to local media reports, the flames spread rapidly, trapping multiple workers inside. As of now, rescue teams have confirmed three fatalities, with 36 workers still unaccounted for.

Fire departments and emergency response services have rushed to the scene to conduct search and rescue operations. The complex structure of the factory building, combined with thick smoke and high temperatures caused by the fire, has made rescue efforts particularly challenging. Authorities have established a specialized investigation team to thoroughly examine the cause of the accident.

## Global Perspective

Industrial safety accidents remain a severe challenge in developing countries. This Indonesian factory fire once again highlights the importance of safe production management in the manufacturing sector. Many small and medium-sized factories, in their rapid expansion, often neglect the improvement of fire safety facilities and the implementation of employee safety training.

From a broader perspective, this incident reflects differences in labor safety standards within global supply chains. While pursuing cost efficiency, multinational enterprises should strengthen safety audits and supervision of supplier factories. Local governments also need to improve relevant laws and regulations, increase penalties for non-compliant enterprises, and ensure that workers' basic safety rights are protected.

Furthermore, as industrialization progresses, how to balance economic development with safe production has become an important issue that governments and businesses worldwide must address. Establishing stricter safety production standards, strengthening daily supervision and inspections, and improving emergency response capabilities are key measures to prevent similar tragedies from recurring.

## Multiple Viewpoints

Local union representatives have called on the government to conduct a comprehensive review of factory safety conditions and demand that companies bear appropriate compensation responsibilities. They pointed out that many factories have hazards such as insufficient fire fighting equipment and blocked safety exits, issues that have long remained unresolved.

Safety production experts emphasized that preventing such accidents requires joint efforts from multiple parties. Enterprises should regularly conduct fire safety drills to ensure employees are familiar with evacuation routes; government regulatory agencies should increase law enforcement efforts, ordering rectification or closure of enterprises that do not meet safety standards.

Family representatives expressed concern about the progress of rescue operations, hoping that authorities would mobilize more resources to accelerate search efforts. They also called on all sectors of society to pay attention to affected families and provide necessary psychological support and financial assistance.
