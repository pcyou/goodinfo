## Core Summary

A commercial building fire in a north Indian city has killed 15 people. Witnesses described people jumping from windows to escape. Local authorities have ordered a full investigation.

## Event Details

According to BBC News, the fire broke out in the early hours of the morning. Fire services responded quickly, with rescue teams breaking through walls to reach trapped occupants.

## Panoramic Analysis

Building fires are frequent in India, reflecting serious lag in safety regulation during urbanization. Thousands die from fires annually, with commercial buildings accounting for the highest proportion.
