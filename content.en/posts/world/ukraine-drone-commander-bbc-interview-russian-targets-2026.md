## Ukraine's Unmanned Warfare Commander Reveals Battle Strategy

Robert Brovdi, commander of Ukraine's Unmanned Systems Forces, has given a rare interview to the BBC, offering insight into Ukraine's latest drone warfare strategy and operational results.

### One-Third of All Battlefield Targets Destroyed by Drone Unit

Brovdi revealed that his unit now accounts for approximately one-third of all targets destroyed on the Ukrainian battlefield. This figure underscores the central role that unmanned systems have come to play in Ukraine's military operations and their growing significance in modern warfare.

### Three Strategic Targets: Oil, Military Forces, and Morale

In the interview, Brovdi outlined his unit's three primary strategic objectives: Russia's oil infrastructure, military forces, and troop morale.

Regarding oil infrastructure, Ukraine's drone forces have repeatedly struck refineries and petroleum storage facilities deep within Russian territory, aiming to undermine Russia's war economy and energy export revenues. Brovdi noted that these operations have already inflicted material damage to Russia's energy supply chain.

On military targets, the drone unit continues to conduct precision strikes against Russian positions, logistics lines, and military equipment. Brovdi said that advances in drone technology have enabled Ukraine to achieve greater battlefield effects at lower cost and with fewer casualties.

On the psychological dimension, Brovdi emphasized that the persistent drone threat not only inflicts physical damage but also has a profound impact on the psychological state of Russian soldiers. "When they know a drone could appear overhead at any moment, that uncertainty itself is a powerful weapon," he said.

### Evolution of Drone Tactics

Brovdi discussed the evolution of Ukraine's drone tactics during the interview. From initially using commercially modified drones to developing dedicated military unmanned systems, Ukraine's drone warfare capabilities have improved significantly over the past year.

He highlighted enhanced long-range strike capabilities that enable Ukraine to conduct precision attacks on targets deep within Russian territory. These operations have not only degraded Russia's war capacity but also demonstrated to the international community Ukraine's determination to continue resisting.

### Looking Ahead

When asked about the future role of drone technology in the conflict, Brovdi said that unmanned systems will continue to occupy a central position in Ukraine's defense strategy. He stressed that technological innovation and tactical adaptability are key factors in maintaining Ukraine's combat effectiveness.

Analysts note that Brovdi's rare public appearance is itself a signal, indicating Ukraine's desire to garner greater international support and cooperation in the unmanned warfare domain.
