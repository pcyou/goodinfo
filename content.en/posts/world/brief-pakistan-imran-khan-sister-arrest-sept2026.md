## Brief

According to the BBC, Aleema Khanum, sister of Pakistan's former Prime Minister Imran Khan, has been arrested on allegations of mobilizing her brother's supporters to launch nationwide protests while he faces ongoing legal troubles. Following the arrest, Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) supporters held small demonstrations in multiple locations demanding her immediate release. Pakistan's domestic political tensions have escalated again, with the government and judiciary insisting the arrest was carried out strictly in accordance with legal procedures and denying any political motivation. The incident once again highlights the political divisions surrounding the former Prime Minister's judicial cases and has drawn further international attention to the country's rule of law and political stability.
