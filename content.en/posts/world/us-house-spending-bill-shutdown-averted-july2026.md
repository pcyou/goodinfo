## Core Summary

According to The Guardian, the US House of Representatives has passed a critical spending bill to prevent a federal government shutdown before midterm elections. This legislative achievement temporarily resolves the government closure crisis triggered by bipartisan budget disagreements, though the Senate must still complete its review process.

## Event Details

### Legislative Progress

The House passed the comprehensive appropriations bill by majority vote, providing operating funds for multiple federal departments through the end of the fiscal year. The bill's passage marks a last-minute compromise between the two parties, avoiding a government shutdown that could have affected millions of federal employees and public service programs.

### Political Background

With midterm elections approaching, avoiding a government shutdown has become a shared political priority for both parties. Polls show widespread voter dissatisfaction with Congress's practice of causing government closures over budget disputes. The bill's passage is seen as a pragmatic choice by congressional leadership under electoral pressure.

### Senate Prospects

Although the House has approved the bill, the Senate must still conduct review and voting. The Senate Majority Leader stated they will advance the legislative process as quickly as possible, but some senators remain reserved about specific spending provisions in the bill.

## Panoramic Perspective

The passage of this spending bill reflects deep contradictions in American fiscal politics. The two parties remain sharply divided on defense spending, social welfare, and border security. Short-term compromises have not resolved fundamental budgetary structural issues. Long-term fiscal challenges such as the federal debt ceiling negotiations and social security reform remain unresolved.

From an economic impact perspective, the elimination of the government shutdown threat provides a degree of certainty for markets. However, analysts note that frequent budget crises are undermining international investors' confidence in America's fiscal management capabilities. Credit rating agencies may factor political gridlock into sovereign rating considerations.

For federal employees, although this shutdown was avoided, the repeated crises in recent years have caused severe personnel losses and morale problems. Multiple federal agencies report recruitment difficulties and rising vacancy rates in critical positions.

## Multiple Perspectives

**Democratic Position**: House Democratic leaders emphasized that the bill protects funding for critical social programs while ensuring reasonable growth in the defense budget. They criticized Republicans for attempting to advance controversial policies through spending bill riders.

**Republican Position**: Republican spokespersons stated the bill reflects fiscal discipline principles, cutting unnecessary bureaucratic spending while meeting core border security needs. They accused Democrats of trying to avoid fiscal reform through increased spending.

**White House Stance**: The White House expressed support for the bill, stating it balances national security needs with domestic priorities. The President stated they will sign the bill into law.

**Market Reaction**: Wall Street reacted positively to the resolution of the shutdown crisis, with major stock indices rising slightly. However, analysts warn that the next round of budget negotiations may be more difficult.

**Public Opinion**: A recent Pew Research Center poll shows that 67 percent of American voters lack confidence in Congress's ability to handle the budget, believing both parties place political interests above national interests.
