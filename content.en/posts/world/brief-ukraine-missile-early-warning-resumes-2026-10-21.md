### Key Facts

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said an early-warning system for Russian missile attacks has resumed, following talks with US counterparts including a call with Secretary of State Marco Rubio.

### Wider Perspective

Early-warning systems determine how much time civilians have to take cover, and their availability often reflects system health more clearly than interception rates do. With Russian ballistic strikes on city infrastructure continuing, restoring the warning chain is both a humanitarian fix and a sign of deeper dependence on shared intelligence and warning data. How that data is sourced and shared will become a decisive variable in future urban protection efforts.
