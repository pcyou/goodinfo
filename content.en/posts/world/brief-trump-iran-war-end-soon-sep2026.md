## Core Summary

Fox News reported on September 13 that U.S. President Donald Trump predicted the war with Iran will end \"very soon,\" likely after the November midterm elections. The same round of coverage noted Saudi Arabia facing growing threats from Iran and its allies, and Iraqi security forces seizing a drone-launching platform used to strike a Saudi oil pipeline. Analysts noted that Trump's latest comments coincide closely with the recent series of strikes on Middle East energy infrastructure.
