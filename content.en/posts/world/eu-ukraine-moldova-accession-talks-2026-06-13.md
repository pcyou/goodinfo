## Core Summary

EU member states agreed on June 12 to formally launch accession negotiations with Ukraine and Moldova. This historic decision marks a crucial step in the EU's eastern enlargement and provides a clear European integration perspective for both nations facing geopolitical pressure.

## Event Details

According to Al Jazeera, EU representatives at the General Affairs Council in Luxembourg formally approved the decision to open accession talks. Ukraine and Moldova were granted candidate status in June 2022 and have been pushing for negotiations to begin as soon as possible.

Analysts note that the US focus on Middle East diplomacy has created space for the EU to demonstrate autonomy on enlargement. The move signals that European continental unification will not stall due to external pressures.

Moldova's accession process is also closely watched. The small former Soviet republic between Romania and Ukraine has made significant progress on its pro-European path but faces resistance from domestic pro-Russian forces.

## Analysis

EU enlargement has always been a key lever in geopolitical competition. Launching negotiations with two countries simultaneously is not only recognition of their reform achievements but also an important declaration of the EU's strategic autonomy. Each previous wave of enlargement profoundly reshaped Europe's political map — the 2004 "big bang" enlargement brought former Warsaw Pact nations into the Western camp, and this decision could become another milestone in post-Cold War European order.

For Ukraine, the prospect of EU membership provides an institutional anchor for nation-building amid ongoing security threats. For Moldova, it offers a final answer to its long-standing geopolitical oscillation between East and West.

However, accession negotiations typically take years. Both countries must still make substantial progress in judicial reform, anti-corruption, and market economy standards.
