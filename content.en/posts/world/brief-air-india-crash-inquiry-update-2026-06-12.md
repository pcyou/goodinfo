## Core Summary

Indian officials say more time is needed to complete the investigation into the Air India crash that killed 260 people a year ago. A statement said "significant progress" has been made, but the complexity of the case requires investigators to continue thorough analysis of all evidence.

## Details

The crash occurred in June 2025, killing all 260 people on board — one of India's deadliest aviation disasters in recent years. Investigators are conducting comprehensive analysis of flight data recorders, cockpit voice recordings, and aircraft maintenance records.
