## Core Summary

BBC reports that Chinese authorities have intensified censorship and control of public mourning activities during the former premier's funeral. According to reports, social media platforms have removed large amounts of mourning-related content, and offline memorial activities have been restricted. This incident has once again drawn international attention to freedom of expression and public expression rights in China.

## Event Details

### Report Content

According to BBC reporting, Chinese authorities implemented multiple measures to control public mourning activities during the former premier's funeral. Social media posts, comments, and images related to mourning were heavily censored and removed. Memorial activities in some cities also faced restrictions.

### Background Analysis

The former premier played an important role during China's reform and opening-up period and enjoys considerable prestige among the public. His passing has triggered widespread social attention and mourning sentiment. Authorities' control of mourning activities reflects the government's sensitivity to public emotional expression and social mobilization.

### International Reaction

This incident has attracted attention from international media and human rights organizations. Multiple international news outlets have reported on it, suggesting this reflects ongoing restrictions on freedom of expression in China.

## Panoramic Perspective

This incident reflects the tension between public emotional expression and government control in Chinese society. As a key driver of reform and opening-up, the former premier holds special symbolic significance among the public. Public mourning activities represent not only personal remembrance but may also carry emotional attachment to the direction of social development.

From a governance perspective, government control of mourning activities reflects a cautious attitude toward the gathering and expression of social sentiment. In the information age, social media provides platforms for public emotional expression but also increases the complexity of information dissemination and social mobilization.

This incident also highlights challenges China faces during digital transformation: how to provide reasonable channels for public emotional expression while maintaining social stability. This is not only China's challenge but a common governance dilemma many countries face in the digital age.
