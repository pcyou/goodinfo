## Summary

Spain has threatened retaliation if Italy does not lift border controls targeting migrants arriving from the Ceuta direction. The dispute follows a mass crossing of migrants from Morocco into the Spanish enclave of Ceuta, sparking intense debate within Europe over migration responsibility sharing. Italy has stated it will not accept "ultimatums or impositions."

## Details

According to BBC reporting, Italy tightened border checks on arrivals from the Ceuta direction following last week's mass crossing event. Ceuta's local leader says thousands of migrants remain stranded and estimates that approximately 100 deaths may have occurred during the crossing of roughly 78,000 migrants from Morocco.

The dispute reflects deep divisions within the EU on migration policy. Southern European countries bear the brunt of migration pressure from Africa and the Middle East, while Northern European nations tend toward stricter border controls. The European Commission has called for restraint and announced a unified migration response plan for next week.
