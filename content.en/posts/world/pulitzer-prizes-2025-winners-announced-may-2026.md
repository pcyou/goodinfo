# 2026 Pulitzer Prizes Announced: AP, Washington Post, Reuters Among Winners

> Summary: The 110th Pulitzer Prize winners were announced on May 4, 2026. The Associated Press, Washington Post, Reuters, and Minnesota Star Tribune were honored for outstanding journalism in 2025. New York Times contributor Saher Alghorra also received an individual award.

---

## Key Winners

Columbia University announced the 2026 Pulitzer Prize winners on May 4. This year's honorees reflect the excellence of American journalism in a complex social environment.

**The Associated Press (AP)** was recognized once again for its outstanding international news coverage. As the world's largest news agency, the AP continued to deliver high-quality global reporting to the public throughout 2025.

**The Washington Post** earned recognition for its in-depth investigative reporting. The paper's investigative work in 2025 generated widespread public attention and policy discussions across multiple topics.

**Reuters** was honored for its professionalism and impartiality in international news coverage. The British heritage news organization has a long-standing reputation for excellence in global journalism.

**The Minnesota Star Tribune** won for its comprehensive coverage of the Annunciation shooting. The paper's sustained reporting on this tragedy demonstrated the critical role that local newspapers play in covering major events.

## Individual Honors

Saher Alghorra, a contributor to The New York Times, received an individual award in recognition of outstanding contributions to journalism.

## The Significance of the Pulitzer Prize

The Pulitzer Prize, often called the highest honor in American journalism, has been a benchmark for journalistic excellence since its establishment in 1917. In an increasingly complex information landscape, the award serves not only as recognition for recipients but also as a reaffirmation of press freedom and journalistic professionalism.

## Arts and Letters Categories

In addition to journalism awards, this year's Pulitzer Prizes also covered literature, drama, and music. Daniel Kraus's *Angel Down* and Bess Wohl's *Liberation* were among the arts category winners.
