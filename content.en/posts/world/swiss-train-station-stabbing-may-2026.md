## Swiss Train Station Stabbing Leaves Three Injured

A stabbing incident at a Swiss train station has left at least three people injured, BBC reported. The attacker has been arrested by police, and casualty details are being confirmed.

Police have secured the scene and launched an investigation. The motive has not been disclosed. Swiss authorities said security patrols at public transport facilities will be strengthened.
