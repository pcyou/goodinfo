UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer has said he will not resign despite heavy losses for the Labour Party in local elections, with Reform UK making significant gains.

The local election results dealt a blow to Labour's standing, with Reform UK capitalizing on voter dissatisfaction. Despite the losses, Starmer insisted he remains committed to leading the government and delivering on Labour's agenda.

The results have intensified pressure on Starmer's leadership, with critics calling for a change in direction. However, the Prime Minister maintained that the government would continue its work regardless of the election setbacks.
