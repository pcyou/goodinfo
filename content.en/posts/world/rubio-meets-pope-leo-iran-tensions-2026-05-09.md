## [Flash] Rubio Meets Pope Leo Amid US-Vatican Tensions Over Iran War

US Secretary of State Rubio met with Pope Leo. As the first American pope, Leo has drawn criticism from the Trump administration for his firm opposition to the Iran war and hardline immigration policies. The meeting is seen as an effort to ease tensions between Washington and the Vatican.
