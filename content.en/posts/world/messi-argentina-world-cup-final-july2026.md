## Core Summary

In the 2026 World Cup semifinal, Argentina scored twice late in the match led by Lionel Messi to secure their place in the final. This will be Argentina's second consecutive World Cup final appearance, with the 38-year-old Messi poised to potentially lift the trophy again and make new history.

## Event Details

According to The Washington Post, in this thrilling semifinal match, Argentina scored two goals in the closing stages to seal the victory. Messi demonstrated masterful ball control and passing ability throughout the match. Although he didn't score directly, his organization and leadership were key to the team's success.

ESPN reported that Argentina's opponent will be either Spain or England. In the other semifinal, Spain defeated France 2-0, with Mbappé completely contained. England advanced narrowly led by Bellingham.

Messi said in a post-match interview: "We still have one final match to play, and this is our dream." The 38-year-old star is already a legendary figure in World Cup history, and if he wins again, he will cement his status as one of the greatest players of all time.

The Argentine Football Association issued a statement celebrating the team's advancement, and fans celebrated in the streets of Buenos Aires. This marks Argentina's return to the World Cup final after winning the 2022 Qatar World Cup.

For the global football industry, the World Cup final's commercial value is enormous. Broadcasting rights, sponsorship fees, ticket revenue, and more will reach new highs.
