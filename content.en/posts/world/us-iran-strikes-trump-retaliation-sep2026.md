## US and Iran Exchange Strikes for First Time as Trump Vows Retaliation

**[Core Summary]** The United States and Iran engaged in direct military confrontation for the first time on September 1, 2026, escalating decades of tension into open conflict. President Trump immediately vowed severe retaliation, raising fears of a broader Middle East war.

According to multiple international media reports, both nations carried out military strikes against each other in what represents the first direct military engagement since diplomatic relations were severed in 1979. The confrontation comes amid mounting tensions over Iran's nuclear program, regional proxy conflicts, and escalating economic sanctions.

### Background

US-Iran relations have deteriorated sharply in recent months. The United States has intensified economic sanctions while increasing military presence in the region. Iran, in turn, has accelerated uranium enrichment activities and repeatedly warned of decisive response to any military provocation.

The Trump administration quickly issued statements following the incident, with the President himself addressing the nation through both social media and press conferences, emphasizing that America "will not tolerate threats to allies and interests" and promising to take all necessary measures to protect US personnel and facilities.

### Comprehensive Analysis

The direct US-Iran military confrontation carries implications far beyond bilateral relations, posing profound impacts on global energy security, geopolitical dynamics, and international order.

From an energy market perspective, the Middle East region accounts for approximately one-third of global oil trade routes. The Strait of Hormuz, the world's most critical oil shipping chokepoint, sees roughly 20 million barrels of crude oil pass through daily. Should escalation disrupt this vital waterway, international oil prices could surge 30% to 50% in the short term, further inflating global inflationary pressures and challenging central bank monetary policies worldwide.

From a geopolitical standpoint, direct US-Iran conflict risks triggering chain reactions. Regional actors including Israel and Saudi Arabia face pressure to choose sides, while Russia and China—as Iran's key partners—will see their responses directly influence the conflict's trajectory and scale. The United Nations Security Council's mediation capacity will also face serious tests.

At a deeper level, this event may accelerate global multipolarization processes. US military action in the Middle East will further test its ability to allocate strategic resources globally, particularly under simultaneous pressure from the ongoing Ukraine crisis and Indo-Pacific strategic commitments.

### Multiple Perspectives

**US Position**: The Trump administration has framed the military action as a "defensive response" aimed at deterring Iran's "provocative behavior." White House spokespeople stated that America "does not seek conflict but will never shy away from defending its interests." Republican lawmakers have largely supported the President's tough stance, viewing it as necessary for maintaining regional stability.

**Iran's Position**: Iran's Supreme Leader Ayatollah Khamenei addressed the nation via state television, characterizing Iran's military action as a legitimate response to America's "long history of aggression." Iran's Foreign Ministry summoned the Swiss embassy representative (which handles US interests in Tehran) to deliver a formal protest note. Nationalist sentiment has surged domestically, with pro-government rallies erupting in multiple cities.

**International Response**: UN Secretary-General Guterres has called on both sides to "immediately cease military action and return to the negotiating table." The EU's High Representative for Foreign Affairs expressed "deep concern" and urged maximum restraint from all parties. China's Foreign Ministry spokesperson called on "relevant parties to remain calm and exercise restraint to avoid further escalation." Russian President Putin criticized America's "adventurist policy" for pushing the region toward war.

**Market Reaction**: International oil prices jumped sharply following news of the strikes, with Brent crude futures surging over 5%. Gold strengthened as a safe-haven asset, while global stock markets declined to varying degrees.
