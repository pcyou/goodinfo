AP News reports that three Australian women with alleged links to the Islamic State face serious criminal charges after returning from Syria. Two of the women are charged with slavery offences and crimes against humanity related to alleged IS activities. A third woman was charged with being a member of a terrorist organization.

The case is the latest in Australia's handling of IS-linked returnees, sparking debate over the balance between national security and human rights.
