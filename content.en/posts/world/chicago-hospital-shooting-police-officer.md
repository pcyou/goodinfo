# Chicago Police Officer Killed, Another Wounded in Hospital Shooting

A shooting incident at Swedish Covenant Hospital in Chicago on the evening of April 25, 2026, has left one police officer dead and another critically wounded, city officials confirmed. The suspect has been taken into custody.

## The Incident

According to the Chicago Police Department's initial briefing, two officers were shot upon arriving at the hospital. One officer was pronounced dead at the scene, while the second sustained critical injuries and was rushed to a nearby facility for emergency surgery.

The Chicago Police Department released a statement on social media: "It is with heavy hearts that we announce the line-of-duty death of one of our officers. Our thoughts are with the officer's family and fellow officers during this difficult time." The identities of the officers have not yet been publicly released, pending notification of their families.

## Suspect in Custody

CNN reports that the suspect involved in the shooting was apprehended shortly after the incident and is currently being interrogated by investigators. Police have not yet disclosed the suspect's identity, motive, or whether there was any prior connection to the officers involved.

Authorities stated that the investigation is ongoing and that there is no indication at this time that the shooting is related to terrorism. The hospital area has been cordoned off, and forensic teams are processing the scene for evidence.

## Community Response

In the aftermath of the shooting, Chicago's mayor and several local officials expressed their condolences. The mayor's office issued a statement: "Our city mourns tonight with the loss of a brave officer who dedicated their life to protecting others. We ask all Chicagoans to keep the officer's family in your thoughts and prayers."

Swedish Covenant Hospital, a major medical facility on Chicago's North Side, has enhanced its security protocols in response to the incident. Hospital administration confirmed full cooperation with the ongoing police investigation.

## Chicago's Crime Context

Chicago has faced persistent challenges with gun violence in recent years, despite various city initiatives aimed at increasing police presence and strengthening community policing programs. This incident, involving officers shot in the line of duty, underscores the significant risks that law enforcement personnel face on a daily basis.

The Chicago police union released a statement calling for increased attention to officer safety and urging city government to allocate additional resources to law enforcement.
