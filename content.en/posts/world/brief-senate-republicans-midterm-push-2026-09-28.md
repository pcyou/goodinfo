## [Brief] Senate Republicans Make Final Legislative Push Ahead of US Midterms

The Washington Post reports that Senate Republicans are mounting a final legislative sprint ahead of the US midterm elections, packing multiple items onto the Senate floor as the chamber abandons an early exit from Washington. The fast pace reflects Republican leadership's reading of the political stakes of the midterms, with several key bills moving into the deliberation phase.

Capitol Hill sources cited in the report say GOP leaders want to complete several core legislative priorities before the end of the current Congress and use the dense agenda to showcase governing results to voters. Democrats have criticized the GOP for rushing legislation on multiple files and for skipping the bipartisan consultation that regular order would require. The final shape and timing of the key bills remain to be seen.
