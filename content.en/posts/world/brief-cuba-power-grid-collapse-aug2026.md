## Core Summary

Cuba's national power grid suffered another nationwide blackout on August 3, 2026 (Beijing Time), less than a day after power was restored. This marks the fourth nationwide blackout in Cuba this year, with citizens enduring persistent power shortages amid sweltering heat. The United States has warned American citizens in Cuba to prepare for emergencies, while Havana residents have flocked to the seaside boulevard to seek relief and sleep overnight.
