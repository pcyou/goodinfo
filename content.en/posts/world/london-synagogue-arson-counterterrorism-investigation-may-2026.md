## UK Counterterrorism Police Probe Arson Attack at Former East London Synagogue

Counterterrorism police in the UK are investigating a suspected arson attack on a former synagogue in east London, as the national threat level remains at severe.

### The Incident

CCTV footage shows that the fire at the building on Nelson Street in Tower Hamlets was started deliberately. The fire caused minor damage to the gates of the synagogue, which holds cultural significance for London Jewish community. The building has been disused since 2020, and a Muslim group had recently been attempting to purchase it.

### Counterterrorism Investigation

UK counterterrorism police are examining whether this arson is part of a broader arson campaign linked to Iran. The UK raised its national terror threat level to severe last week, meaning an attack is highly likely.

### Community Response

London Jewish community has strongly condemned the incident. Local lawmakers have called for enhanced security measures at religious sites to ensure community members can practice their faith safely.
