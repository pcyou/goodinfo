## Vice-President JD Vance Hosts White House Press Briefing

US Vice-President JD Vance hosted a White House press briefing, stating that Trump is "locked and loaded" to restart military operations if necessary.

Vance made strong statements on the Iran situation, emphasizing that both diplomatic and military options remain on the table. The fact that the Vice-President rather than the President himself hosted the briefing has drawn attention to the current decision-making dynamics.
