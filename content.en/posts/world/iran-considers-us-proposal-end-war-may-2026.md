## Iran Considers U.S. Proposal to End War as Diplomatic Momentum Builds

The White House says it is nearing a 14-point memorandum of understanding with Iran that could end weeks of military escalation between the two countries, according to officials familiar with the negotiations.

Reports indicate that talks have entered a critical phase, with the White House expressing confidence that an agreement could be reached in the near term. Iranian officials confirmed they are seriously considering the U.S. ceasefire proposal.

### Market Reaction

Following the news, international oil prices fell sharply, with both Brent crude and WTI futures posting significant declines. Global stock markets rallied broadly as investors grew optimistic about the prospect of conflict de-escalation.

Analysts noted that the U.S.-Iran military standoff had been driving a risk premium in energy markets. A ceasefire agreement would likely push oil prices lower further, providing a tailwind for global economic recovery.

### Diplomatic Context

The current conflict stems from escalating tensions in the Middle East in recent weeks. After multiple rounds of diplomatic mediation and pressure from the international community, both sides have gradually shown willingness to engage in dialogue.

President Trump has expressed cautious optimism about reaching a peace deal but warned that significant differences remain to be resolved.

> Markets widely expect that if a ceasefire is finalized, it will further stabilize the geopolitical landscape in the Middle East and reduce volatility in global energy markets.
