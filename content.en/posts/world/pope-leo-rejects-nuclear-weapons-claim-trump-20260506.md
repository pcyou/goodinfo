## Pope Leo rejects claim he supports nuclear weapons after Trump tirade

Pope Leo XIV has publicly rejected claims that he supports nuclear weapons, responding to criticism from US President Donald Trump. Trump lashed out at the Pope on social media, accusing him of hypocrisy.

The dispute comes as US Secretary of State Rubio plans a visit to the Vatican, with the intersection of US-Iran relations and the Vatican's stance drawing international attention.
