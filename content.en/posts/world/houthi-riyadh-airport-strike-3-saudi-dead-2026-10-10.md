**[Core Summary]**

The Saudi-led coalition said Yemen's Houthi movement struck a civilian airport in Riyadh on Friday, killing three Saudi nationals and injuring several others. The strike ranks among the deadliest Houthi attacks on Saudi territory in recent months and marks an escalation in the two-year campaign of cross-border disruption.

**[Event Details]**

- **Target**: A civilian airport in Riyadh serving international civil aviation; the strike layered onto renewed Middle East tensions
- **Casualties**: Three Saudi nationals killed; multiple airport staff and travelers injured; brief disruption to airport facilities and aviation services
- **Claim**: Houthi official media said the movement carried out the strike, framing it as action against Saudi military and civilian infrastructure
- **Backdrop**: The Houthis have repeatedly hit Saudi targets since the Gaza war began, coordinating strategically with Iran since late 2024
- **Reaction**: The Saudi-led coalition quickly condemned the attack as a violation of international law and reserved the right to respond militarily

If Riyadh concludes that current protection is insufficient, Saudi-U.S. relations may enter a new round of adjustment with broader implications for the regional balance of power.
