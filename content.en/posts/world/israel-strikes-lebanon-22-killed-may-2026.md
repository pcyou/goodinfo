Lebanon's health ministry reported that a series of Israeli airstrikes struck southern Beirut on May 13, killing 22 people, among them eight children.

## Details of the Attack

The airstrikes hit multiple locations across southern Lebanon, representing one of the most severe military escalations in the region in recent weeks. The Israeli military said the operations targeted Hezbollah infrastructure, though specific targets have not been officially confirmed.

## International Response

The UN Secretary-General expressed grave concern and urged all parties to exercise restraint. Several Arab nations issued formal statements condemning the civilian death toll from the strikes.

## Regional Context

Middle East tensions have remained elevated throughout the Iran conflict period. While direct Israel-Hezbollah confrontations had shown signs of de-escalation, sporadic clashes have persisted. The intensity of this particular strike suggests the potential for a renewed escalation cycle in the region.
