## Summary

Nepali mountaineer Nirmal Purja has died in an avalanche while climbing in Pakistan, his company announced. He was renowned for setting the record of climbing all 14 peaks above 8,000 meters in just 6 months and 6 days.

## Details

BBC News reported that Purja was caught in an avalanche in northern Pakistan during a climbing expedition. The specific location and circumstances are still under investigation.

Purja became world-famous in 2019 when he completed all 14 eight-thousanders in record time, dramatically surpassing the previous best. His achievement made him an iconic figure in Nepali mountaineering and inspired countless climbers worldwide.

Northern Pakistan is home to some of the world's highest peaks, including K2, and remains a major destination for mountaineers despite the significant risks posed by avalanches, extreme weather, and altitude.
