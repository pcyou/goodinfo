Ghana plans to evacuate about 300 of its citizens from South Africa following escalating anti-immigrant violence, the BBC reports. South Africa has seen repeated waves of attacks on foreign nationals, raising international concerns about xenophobia.

The evacuation reflects the severity of migration tensions in South Africa. Several governments have urged their citizens to remain vigilant and consider temporarily leaving high-risk areas.
