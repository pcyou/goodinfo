# Ukraine Accuses Israel of Accepting Stolen Grain from Russia, Diplomatic Crisis Erupts

> Ukraine and Israel engage in a heated diplomatic exchange over a cargo ship allegedly carrying grain looted from occupied Ukrainian territories, with Zelenskyy warning of sanctions.

---

A major diplomatic dispute erupted on Tuesday between Ukraine and Israel after Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy publicly accused Israel of allowing a ship carrying allegedly stolen Ukrainian grain to dock at the port of Haifa, warning of "serious consequences" if such transactions continue.

## The Incident

According to Reuters, Ukraine alleges that a vessel affiliated with Russia's "shadow fleet" transported tens of thousands of tonnes of wheat stolen from occupied areas in southern Ukraine to the Israeli port of Haifa. Zelenskyy said in a statement on Tuesday: **"Israel's purchase of grain stolen from occupied Ukrainian territory is not legitimate**, and we must respond to such actions."

The Ukrainian Foreign Ministry subsequently **summoned the Israeli ambassador**, accusing Israel of showing a "lack of appropriate response" to the issue. Zelenskyy also made clear that Ukraine would consider imposing sanctions on relevant companies if Israel continued to allow such transactions.

## Israel's Response

Israel has firmly denied the accusations. The Israeli Foreign Ministry stated that the ship had not entered any Russian-occupied Ukrainian ports and accused Ukraine of "failing to provide sufficient evidence" to substantiate its claims.

According to Al-Monitor, the Israeli government emphasized that the wheat aboard the vessel came from legitimate third-party traders and had no direct connection to the Russian military. An Israeli Foreign Ministry spokesperson said: "We will not accept these baseless accusations without evidence."

## Diplomatic Implications

The dispute has drawn significant international attention. Ukraine and Israel have long maintained aligned positions against Russia, with Israel repeatedly voicing support for Ukraine in international forums. However, this grain dispute has created a **rare fissure** in bilateral relations.

Analysts note that if the issue is not properly resolved, it could not only damage diplomatic ties between the two nations but also have ripple effects on other countries' grain import policies. The European Union has previously stated it is "ready" to sanction Israel over the matter, further complicating the situation.

## Broader Context

Since the outbreak of the Russia-Ukraine conflict in 2022, Ukraine's southern grain-producing regions have been a focal point of Russian military operations. It is estimated that Russia has transported **millions of tonnes of grain** from occupied Ukrainian territory, with these commodities flowing into international markets through various channels.

The United Nations and the European Union have repeatedly condemned Russia's seizure of Ukrainian grain, but such transactions have continued. This incident once again highlights the difficulties in tracking and preventing illegal grain trade within the global food supply chain.
