## Core Summary

Israel carried out new airstrikes on multiple targets in Lebanon during a critical window of US-Iran peace negotiations. In an unusual move, President Trump publicly criticized Israeli PM Netanyahu at the G7 summit, saying he needed to be "more responsible with respect to Lebanon" — breaking longstanding US diplomatic restraint on Israeli military operations.

## Event Details

**Israeli Military Action**: The IDF confirmed strikes on military targets in southern Lebanon, claiming the facilities were used to plan attacks on northern Israel. Lebanon's Health Ministry reported at least three injuries and damage to civilian infrastructure. This is the largest Israeli operation in Lebanon since the recent ceasefire.

**Trump's Criticism**: Speaking at the summit, Trump said Netanyahu needed to be "more responsible with respect to Lebanon" — his first direct public criticism of Israel's military operations. The White House later stated the US supports Israel's right to self-defense while emphasizing all military operations should consider their impact on regional stability.

**Israeli Response**: Israel has not formally responded to Trump's comments. Military sources told local media operations were based on "real-time security intelligence" independent of external political factors. The Defense Minister emphasized Israel will not tolerate security threats on its northern border.

**Lebanon Situation**: Lebanon protested through UN channels. The Prime Minister called for international intervention. UNIFIL said it was assessing the situation and urged maximum restraint from all parties.

## Analytical Perspective

Trump's public criticism of Netanyahu marks a significant moment in US-Israel relations. The US has traditionally maintained diplomatic restraint regarding Israeli military operations even when disagreements existed privately.

This shift connects directly to the US-Iran negotiation context. Ongoing Israeli military operations in Lebanon could undermine the negotiation atmosphere and give Iranian hardliners pretext to obstruct the deal. Trump needs a stable Middle East environment to showcase diplomatic achievements.

The move also reflects shifting domestic politics — with Middle East issues carrying less electoral weight, the president has greater policy latitude. However the rift may lead Israel to pursue more autonomous military operations and reduce dependence on the US.

## Diverse Viewpoints

**US Position**: The White House attempted to balance support for self-defense with criticism of excessive force, but the public tone exceeded traditional diplomatic bounds.

**Israeli Position**: Israel emphasized the independence and necessity of military operations, signaling it will not alter security policy under external pressure.

**European View**: France called on all parties to respect ceasefire agreements, suggesting European concern about Israeli operations.

**Regional Analysis**: Al Jazeera noted Israel's actions could inflame internal Lebanese tensions and provide Hezbollah with justification for remobilization.
