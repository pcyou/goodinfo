---
title: At Least 13 Injured in Shooting at Oklahoma Campground Party
date: 2026-05-05T05:52:33+08:00
tags: [us, crime, shooting]
category: world
---

A shooting at a social gathering at an Oklahoma campground has left at least 13 people injured. Local law enforcement has secured the area and launched an investigation, with no suspect details released yet.

The incident occurred during a weekend evening party where dozens of people had gathered at the campsite. After gunfire erupted, attendees fled the scene and multiple injured individuals were transported to nearby hospitals. The ages and severity of injuries have not been fully confirmed.

Police have cordoned off the area and are investigating. Early reports suggest the shooting may be linked to a dispute among attendees, but the exact motive remains under review. No fatalities have been reported at this time.

Gun violence in Oklahoma has been a persistent concern in recent years. The state ranks among the highest in the United States for firearm-related mortality, and gun control legislation remains a contentious issue in the state legislature.

The local community has organized blood drives to support the medical needs of those wounded. Authorities are urging anyone with information to contact law enforcement immediately.
