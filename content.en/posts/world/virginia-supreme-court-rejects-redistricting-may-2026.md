## [BREAKING] Virginia Supreme Court Strikes Down Redistricting Plan

The Virginia Supreme Court ruled on Friday to reject a new congressional redistricting plan led by Democrats. The decision is seen as a major blow to Democratic efforts to counter Republican district strategies.

The court found issues with the redistricting plan that may violate relevant legal provisions. Previously, the FBI had raided the office of the Democratic lawmaker who led the redistricting effort, raising questions about the plan's legality.

This ruling could have significant implications for the upcoming midterm elections, potentially maintaining Republican advantages in Virginia's districts.
