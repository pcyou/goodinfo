**Brief**

A Saudi minister publicly pledged on 12 October that Riyadh would deliver a "serious response" to two Houthi strikes on the kingdom's international airport within 48 hours, an escalation that killed at least 13 people and exposed the vulnerability of civilian aviation and energy supply chains under sustained conflict.

The statement points to a possible new round of diplomatic or military moves by Saudi Arabia, with downstream effects on regional energy markets and Red Sea shipping security likely to follow.
