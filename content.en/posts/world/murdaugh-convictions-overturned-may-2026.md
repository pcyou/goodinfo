South Carolina's Supreme Court overturned Alex Murdaugh's murder convictions on May 13, ordering a new trial. Murdaugh was convicted in 2023 of murdering his wife Maggie and son Paul, a case that captivated national attention.

The court's ruling centered on evidentiary disputes. Murdaugh was once a prominent South Carolina attorney before facing numerous financial fraud and insurance scam charges.

Prosecutors said they will respond to the retrial order, but no date has been set for the new trial.
