# WHCD Shooting Suspect Charged with Attempted Assassination of President

> April 28, 2026 | Source: BBC, Fox News

Cole Thomas Allen, 31, the suspect in Saturday's White House Correspondents' Association Dinner shooting, appeared in federal court on Monday and was formally charged with attempted assassination of the President of the United States. The charge elevates the case to a more serious federal crime level.

## Prosecution's Case

According to investigators, the California man entered the White House Correspondents' Dinner venue armed with a shotgun and knives on Saturday night with the intention of "killing as many high-level officials as possible." Prosecutors said the suspect originally planned to target multiple senior members of the Trump administration.

Following the incident, the Secret Service responded rapidly, and President Trump was urgently evacuated to a secure location. One security officer was injured in the exchange of fire, and the suspect was subsequently taken into custody.

## First Court Appearance

Allen made his initial appearance in federal court on Monday. The Department of Justice is building its case against the California man. Reports indicate the suspect had posted extremist statements targeting the government on social media prior to the incident.

According to BBC, investigators discovered additional weapons and ammunition at the suspect's residence, along with a document allegedly containing a list of potential targets.

## Security Review

The incident has prompted a comprehensive review of security measures for large-scale events in Washington, D.C. The White House Correspondents' Dinner, a major annual gathering of American political figures and media, typically hosts the President, members of Congress, and thousands of journalists.

The Secret Service said it will conduct a full assessment of security deployment before and after the incident, as well as launch an in-depth investigation into the suspect's background.

## Broader Impact

This shooting is the latest in a series of political violence incidents in the United States in recent years. Analysts note that the frequency of political violence has become a severe challenge facing American society.
