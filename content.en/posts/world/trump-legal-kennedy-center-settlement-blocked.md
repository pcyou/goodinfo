Trump faces two significant legal rulings. According to Axios, a judge has ordered that his name must be removed from the Kennedy Center.

Separately, another court has temporarily blocked payouts from Trump's $1.776 billion "anti-weaponization" settlement, which had drawn widespread controversy.

Both rulings reflect ongoing legal challenges for Trump. Analysts suggest these decisions could further impact his political standing.
