## Key Summary

On the evening of July 19 local time, New York State and New Jersey were hit by intense rainfall, causing severe urban flooding in multiple areas. The downpour led to subway stations flooding in New York, with some streets experiencing knee-deep water, forcing pedestrians to wade through floodwaters.

## Event Details

According to the BBC, the sudden downpour caused significant disruption to the New York metropolitan area's transportation system. Rainwater cascaded like waterfalls into subway entrances, forcing multiple stations to temporarily close. Parts of New Jersey were similarly affected, with local governments issuing flood warnings and advising residents to avoid going outside.

Meteorological departments stated that the heavy rainfall was triggered by a stalled low-pressure system, with precipitation exceeding 100 millimeters in a short period. Continued rainfall is expected over the next 24 hours.
