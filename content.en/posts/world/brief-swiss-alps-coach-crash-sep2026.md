## Event Summary

A serious traffic accident involving a tourist coach occurred in the Swiss Alps. A coach carrying 48 Dutch passengers overturned on a mountain road, leaving multiple people dead, with multiple injured taken to surrounding hospitals for treatment. The accident occurred on a relatively narrow mountain road, and weather and road conditions at the time are being further verified.

After the accident, Swiss police and rescue services quickly activated emergency response procedures, with multiple mountain rescue teams and medical helicopters dispatched to the scene. As the accident involves cross-border tourists, relevant Swiss authorities have established contact channels with the Dutch diplomatic mission in Switzerland to assist in coordinating with the families of the injured and related parties. The specific road section where the accident occurred, the vehicle operator and the routine inspection situation prior to the accident are currently being investigated by the Swiss traffic safety regulatory authority, with preliminary results expected in the coming days.
