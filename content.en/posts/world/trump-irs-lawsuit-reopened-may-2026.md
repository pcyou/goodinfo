## [Brief] Judge Reopens Trump's $10 Billion Lawsuit Against IRS

A federal judge has reopened former President Donald Trump's $10 billion lawsuit against the Internal Revenue Service, the New York Times reports.

Trump had alleged the IRS targeted him politically during audits and sought massive damages. The case had been shelved but the judge has now decided to revive it.

Analysts say the case carries more political than legal significance and could further politicize America's tax agency.
