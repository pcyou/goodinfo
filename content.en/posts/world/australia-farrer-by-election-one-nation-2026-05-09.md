[Brief] The Farrer by-election in New South Wales is being viewed as a critical litmus test for the right-wing populist One Nation party. A victory would give the party its first-ever representative in Australia's federal parliament, signaling a potential shift in the country's political landscape. The outcome is being closely watched as an indicator of growing support for populist movements.
