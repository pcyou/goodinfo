## [Brief] Eiffel Tower Chief Resigns After Female Staff Replaced During Religious Visit

### Core Summary

BBC reports that the chief executive of the company that operates the Eiffel Tower has announced his resignation. The move follows controversy over the temporary replacement of female staff with male staff during a religious visit earlier this month, a practice the company has acknowledged as unacceptable.

### Event Details

According to the report, when hosting a religious visit at the beginning of this month, the Eiffel Tower operator temporarily replaced the female staff members handling the reception with male staff. The practice triggered widespread criticism after the incident became public and was seen as touching on gender discrimination and workplace equality.

The operating company acknowledged in an internal statement that the practice was unacceptable and announced the resignation of the chief executive to take managerial responsibility. The incident has also drawn renewed attention in France to how tourism operators and public venue managers balance multicultural reception with workplace equality.
