**[Key Points]**

Ukrainian drones struck a major oil storage and transit hub in Russia's Rostov region on Saturday, triggering a large blaze at a site located relatively close to the rear behind the front lines. Russian emergency services have deployed firefighting teams; the impact on regional energy infrastructure is still being assessed.

**[Details]**

- **Scale of the strike**: A Ukrainian drone group launched multiple waves against the target hub, triggering a large blaze.
- **Site profile**: The struck site is one of the major oil storage and transit hubs in the Rostov region and has significant node value for southern Russian energy supply chains.
- **Emergency response**: Russia's Ministry of Emergency Situations has deployed firefighting crews; local authorities have activated regional emergency protocols.
- **Strategic context**: The strike comes at a moment when transatlantic coordination on Russia policy is again under strain; the value of energy infrastructure as an extended battlefield objective is rising once more.
- **What to watch**: Markets are watching for any impact on the pace of Russian diesel and refined-product exports; the operational status of related refineries and pipelines is expected to come out over the coming days.
