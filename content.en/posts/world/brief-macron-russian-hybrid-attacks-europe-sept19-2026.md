## Core Summary

French President Emmanuel Macron has publicly warned that Russian hybrid attacks against multiple European countries are significantly intensifying, with related operations encompassing cyberattacks, disinformation campaigns, and critical infrastructure disruption. Macron called on EU member states to strengthen intelligence sharing and coordinated response capabilities to address this increasingly severe security challenge.

## Event Details

According to BBC World, President Macron made these remarks while attending a European security-related conference in Paris. He noted that over the past few months, Russian hybrid attacks against European countries have visibly escalated in both frequency and complexity, with targets spanning the government institutions, critical infrastructure, and public opinion spaces of multiple EU member states.

Macron specifically cited three main forms of threat: first, cyberattacks against governments and enterprises; second, disinformation spread through social media platforms; and third, disruption of critical infrastructure such as energy and transportation. He emphasized that these threats often work in combination to create comprehensive strategic pressure that no single country can handle alone.

French government officials said Paris is in discussions with several EU partners on building a closer security coordination mechanism, covering real-time intelligence sharing, joint cybersecurity exercises, and rapid response mechanisms for disinformation. Some EU diplomats revealed that related discussions are expected to produce a concrete cooperation framework in the coming months.

This statement comes against the backdrop of continuing tension in the European security landscape. Several European countries have recently reported Russian-linked cyber intrusions and disinformation operations, while the EU is engaged in wide-ranging discussions on how to more effectively respond to this non-traditional security threat.
