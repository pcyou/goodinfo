## Core Summary

Moscow was struck on June 18 by the largest drone attack since Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine. Nearly 200 Ukrainian drones targeted the southeastern region of the capital, igniting fires at a refinery and a shopping centre. The unprecedented scale of this assault marks a significant escalation in Ukraine's long-range strike capabilities.

## Event Details

According to BBC reporting, the Russian capital endured its most intense drone bombardment since the full-scale war began in February 2022. Almost 200 Ukrainian drones were directed at the Moscow region, with many striking industrial and commercial zones to the southeast.

Following the attack, a refinery and a shopping centre in southeastern Moscow caught fire. Emergency response teams were dispatched to contain the blazes. Russia's defence ministry claimed to have intercepted the majority of incoming drones, but ground imagery showed significant damage to multiple facilities.

This represents the largest strike in a recent series of Ukrainian attacks on deep targets within Russian territory. Ukraine has previously used drones to target refineries, fuel depots, and military installations inside Russia, aiming to degrade the Russian war economy and logistics chain.

## Panoramic Analysis

This large-scale drone attack marks a new phase of technological confrontation in the Russia-Ukraine war. Despite lacking advanced fighter jets or hypersonic weapons, Ukraine has achieved strategic deterrence against the enemy's capital region through self-developed, iteratively improved low-cost drone systems. This asymmetric warfare model of "low-cost weapons against high-value targets" is redefining the economics of modern conflict.

From a strategic perspective, Ukraine's decision to extend strikes to the Moscow metropolitan area serves multiple purposes: forcing Russia to redirect more air defence resources from the Ukrainian front line, demonstrating the real cost of war to Russian civilians, and increasing leverage in international negotiations by proving its capacity for continued escalation.

However, this strategy carries risks. Attacking a densely populated metropolitan area like Moscow could cause significant civilian casualties, potentially generating negative international reactions. Balancing military effectiveness with humanitarian considerations remains an ongoing challenge for Ukrainian decision-makers.

## Multiple Perspectives

**Ukrainian position**: Kyiv defines this operation as a legitimate response to Russia's continued bombing of Ukrainian cities, emphasising that targets are military and energy infrastructure rather than civilian.

**Russian position**: Moscow condemns this as a "terrorist act," claims air defence systems intercepted most drones, and hints at retaliatory measures.

**Western analysis**: Most Western military analysts believe the attack demonstrates Ukraine's rapid progress in drone technology but note that strikes on major cities may raise international concerns about conflict escalation. NATO members are expected to continue supplying defensive weapons while remaining cautious about the expansion of strike ranges to the capital region.
