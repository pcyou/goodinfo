## 📰 Israel Airstrikes Kill 41 in Lebanon in 24 Hours as Conflict Escalates

On May 2, 2026, Israel launched airstrikes across multiple locations in Lebanon, killing at least 41 people within a 24-hour period. According to Lebanon's Health Ministry, at least 10 people were killed in Saturday's strikes alone in southern regions, including four women and a child.

The cumulative death toll from Israeli strikes on Lebanon since March 2 has now risen to 2,659, with 8,183 others injured. The Israeli military also demolished parts of a Catholic convent in a border village, further escalating tensions.

The strikes came just two weeks after Israel declared a "ceasefire" and are widely viewed as a serious violation of the agreement. Hezbollah accused Israel of deliberately escalating the conflict, stating that the strikes targeted civilian infrastructure and residential areas.

The international community has expressed grave concern. The UN Secretary-General called on all parties to immediately cease hostilities and strictly adhere to the ceasefire agreement. Several diplomats warned that continued military operations could trigger a wider regional conflict.

Meanwhile, the aftermath of the Iran war continues to unfold. U.S. President Donald Trump sent a letter to Congress on Friday declaring that "hostilities" with Iran "have terminated," an effort to sidestep the statutory 60-day deadline requiring congressional authorization for military action. However, Trump also said he was "not satisfied" with Iran's latest peace proposal, stating it included demands he "can't agree to."

A Washington Post-ABC News-Ipsos poll revealed that 61% of Americans believe Trump's use of military force against Iran was a mistake, reflecting growing public dissatisfaction with Middle East military operations.

Iran submitted its latest negotiating proposal to the United States through mediators in Pakistan. Separately, 14 Iranian soldiers were killed while defusing unexploded ordnance in the northwestern Zanjan province, underscoring the ongoing dangers left by the conflict.

The escalation in Lebanon, intertwined with the complex Iran situation, makes the prospects for lasting peace in the Middle East increasingly uncertain. Analysts note that with ceasefire agreements repeatedly violated, achieving durable peace faces significant obstacles.
