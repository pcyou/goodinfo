## [Brief] Spain Sees Mass Protests After Housing Bill Rejected, With Tens of Thousands Demanding Action on Housing Crisis

Al Jazeera and the BBC report that Spain has seen mass protests in multiple cities after the parliamentary defeat of the housing bill, with tens of thousands taking to the streets over housing costs and affordability. Protesters criticized the government for acting too slowly on capping rent increases, expanding social housing and cracking down on speculative purchases, and called for more targeted policy responses.

The reports noted that the protests have taken place simultaneously in Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia and other major cities, with participants including youth groups, tenant associations, union representatives and left-wing political organizations. Police and organizers said the protests were largely peaceful, though brief confrontations with police occurred in some cities. Organizers warned that if the core issues are not addressed, even larger nationwide action will be mobilized in the future.

Citing Spanish political sources, the reports noted that the bill's defeat has once again exposed divisions within the governing coalition and has triggered discussion over whether the government will move toward a snap election. Some opposition lawmakers have called on the prime minister to resign and hold a national election, while some coalition members have stressed that they will continue to push for coordination on housing policy so as to avoid pushing the country into heightened political instability.
