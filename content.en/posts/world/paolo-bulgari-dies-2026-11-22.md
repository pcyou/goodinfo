## Core Summary

Paolo Bulgari, a prominent member of the famous Italian jewellery brand Bulgari's family and a businessman, passed away on November 22, 2026, at the age of 89. In a statement to Italian media, his family remembered him as "the greatest jeweller of our time."

## Event Details

Paolo Bulgari was an important member of the legendary Italian jewellery brand Bulgari's family. He was hailed by his family as "the greatest jeweller of our time" in a statement released to Italian media. Paolo played a key role in the development of the Bulgari brand, helping transform this Rome-originated jewellery store into a globally renowned luxury brand.

The Bulgari brand was founded by Sotirio Bulgari in Rome in 1884. Through the efforts of several generations, it has become one of the world's top jewellery and watch manufacturers. During his career, Paolo Bulgari was dedicated to promoting the brand's international expansion and made significant contributions to design innovation and craftsmanship heritage.

## Global Perspective

Paolo Bulgari's passing marks the end of an era in Italy's luxury goods industry. As a core figure of the family business, he witnessed Bulgari's entire journey from a small Roman jewellery shop to a global luxury brand. His departure is not only a loss for the family but also a significant loss for the entire jewellery industry.

In the global luxury goods market, Italian brands occupy an important position with their exquisite craftsmanship and unique design styles. As one of the leading brands, Bulgari represents the highest standard of Italian manufacturing. Paolo Bulgari's business vision and insistence on quality laid a solid foundation for the brand, enabling it to maintain a leading position in fierce international competition.

From a broader perspective, family businesses play a special role in the luxury goods industry. Unlike large multinational corporations, family businesses often maintain longer-term strategic perspectives and respect for traditional craftsmanship. Paolo Bulgari's career embodied these values, and his success provides valuable lessons for other family businesses.

## Multiple Viewpoints

Fashion industry figures expressed condolences for Paolo Bulgari's passing. Several renowned designers and fashion critics praised his contributions to jewellery design, believing that he perfectly combined traditional Italian craftsmanship with modern aesthetics.

Business analysts pointed out that Paolo Bulgari's leadership style emphasized quality first and brand heritage, a philosophy that appears particularly precious in today's rapidly changing luxury goods market. His passing may prompt the Bulgari brand to reassess its future development strategy.

Italy's cultural community also expressed regret over the passing of this jewellery master. They believe that Paolo Bulgari was not only a successful businessman but also an important guardian of Italian cultural heritage, whose works embody the essence of Italian design.
