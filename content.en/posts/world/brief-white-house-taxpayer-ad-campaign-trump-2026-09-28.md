## [Brief] White House Expands Taxpayer Funded Ad Campaign Boosting Trump

The Washington Post reports that the White House has significantly expanded a taxpayer-funded video campaign, with new content depicting President Trump in a strongman-style pose. The expansion has triggered a fresh round of controversy over the boundaries of executive resources and public communications, prompting some lawmakers and press-freedom advocates to call for closer oversight of government communications spending.

The White House has framed the campaign as a routine effort to communicate policy achievements and priorities to the public. Critics counter that using taxpayer funds to produce and distribute overtly political content with a strong personal emphasis goes well beyond the traditional scope of government information activities and raises serious questions about the use of public resources.
