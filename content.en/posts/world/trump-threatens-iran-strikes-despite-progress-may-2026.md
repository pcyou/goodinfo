## Mixed Signals

CBS News reports that Trump has threatened military strikes against Iran while stating that negotiations have made great progress, adding that it is too soon for new direct talks.

These contradictory signals have drawn attention. On one hand, US officials say both sides are close to a ceasefire memo; on the other, Trump's tough rhetoric adds uncertainty. Analysts view this as a pressure tactic aimed at pushing Iran toward a more favorable response.
