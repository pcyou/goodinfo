## Bulgaria Wins Eurovision 2026

At the grand final of the 70th Eurovision Song Contest, Bulgarian singer Dara claimed victory with her song "Bangaranga," securing the title for Bulgaria.

**Contest Highlights**

Dara's performance, blending ethnic elements with modern pop, stood out among entries from across Europe. "Bangaranga" captivated both juries and audiences with its distinctive melody and energetic stage presence. This marks one of Bulgaria's best-ever results at the competition, sparking celebrations across the country.
