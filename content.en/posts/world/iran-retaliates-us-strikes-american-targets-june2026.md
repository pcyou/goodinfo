## Summary

Iran has accused the United States of violating a prior agreement and launched retaliatory strikes against targets linked to American military forces on June 27. The move escalates the US-Iran conflict from a one-sided strike to a bilateral military confrontation, pushing the Middle East to the brink of full-scale war. The international community has urgently called for restraint, but the situation continues to deteriorate.

## Details

According to the latest BBC reporting, Iran accused the US of breaching a prior understanding on Strait of Hormuz navigation security and announced strikes against "targets associated with American military forces." Iranian officials called it a "legitimate self-defense response" to the earlier US strikes on Iranian military installations.

Details of the retaliatory action remain partially classified, but multiple sources confirmed that Iranian strikes targeted certain logistics facilities and allied bases used by US forces in the region. US Central Command is assessing damage and has not released casualty figures.

The White House has not issued a formal response, but an unnamed US official told reporters that the military has taken "precautionary measures" to protect personnel and assets in the region. President Trump has cancelled his domestic schedule and returned to the White House for an emergency National Security Council meeting.

Notably, this escalation came just hours after Israel and Lebanon signed a framework peace agreement. Analysts note that the Middle East is now caught in a contradictory pattern of "peace in some arenas, war in others," with competing power dynamics growing increasingly complex.

## Analysis

Iran's retaliatory strikes have pushed the US-Iran conflict into an entirely new and dangerous phase. Previously, despite direct US strikes on Iranian soil, Tehran had chosen a relatively restrained response. By directly targeting assets linked to the US military, Iran has signaled an abandonment of "strategic patience" in favor of a tit-for-tat confrontation strategy.

From a military perspective, Iran possesses the largest ballistic missile and drone arsenal in the Middle East, and its "Axis of Resistance" proxy network spans Hezbollah in Lebanon, the Houthis in Yemen, and Shia militias in Iraq. A full mobilization of these forces in coordinated attacks against US and allied targets could create a multi-front conflict across the region.

From an energy market standpoint, the security of Strait of Hormuz navigation is now critically at risk. Approximately 20% of global oil supply transits through this chokepoint, and any substantial disruption could send international oil prices into severe volatility. Should the conflict persist, oil prices exceeding $150 per barrel are not out of the question, dealing a serious blow to the global economic recovery.

From a geopolitical standpoint, this crisis will also test the coordination capacity of major powers. How China and Russia, as Iran's key trading partners, balance their interests while avoiding a full-scale regional war will be a critical factor in determining the trajectory of events.

## Perspectives

**Iran**: The Foreign Ministry spokesperson stated that the US military action seriously violated international law and bilateral agreements, and that Iran has the right to take all necessary measures to protect national sovereignty and security. The IRGC commander announced that armed forces have entered "full combat readiness" and will "firmly repel any act of aggression."

**United States**: A Pentagon official stated that the military is closely monitoring developments and is prepared for all scenarios. The official emphasized that "the US will not tolerate any threat to its personnel" while noting that Washington "remains open to resolving differences through diplomatic channels."

**Regional Response**: Saudi Arabia and the UAE called for maximum restraint from both sides to prevent further escalation. Israel stated it is "closely monitoring developments" and has raised alert levels along its borders.

**International Community**: The UN Secretary-General expressed "extreme concern" over the rapid deterioration of the situation and called on all parties to immediately cease military action and return to the negotiating table. The EU's High Representative urged all parties to "act to cool tensions, not inflame them."
