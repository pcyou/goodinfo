## Core Summary

According to NewsNation, the Trump administration is shifting from a hardline stance to "low keying it" with Iran as a Strait of Hormuz deal approaches. Trump told Axios "We are low-keying it" regarding relations with Iran. This shift suggests both sides may reach some compromise on nuclear issues and regional influence.

## Background

The Strait of Hormuz is one of the world's most important oil transit routes, with approximately 20 million barrels of crude oil passing through daily. The US and Iran have long disagreed over control and freedom of navigation in the strait. Recently, both sides have engaged in indirect contacts, seeking a deal to avoid further escalation.

## Broader Perspective

The Trump administration's shift in attitude toward Iran reflects a pragmatic adjustment in US Middle East strategy. On one hand, the US wants to maintain regional ally security and energy supply stability; on the other hand, continued confrontation may bring higher military and economic costs. A Strait of Hormuz agreement will significantly impact global energy markets, potentially easing recent oil price pressures.

## Multiple Perspectives

**Supportive Position:** Diplomatic negotiations are the best way to resolve differences and avoid military conflict risks.

**Critical Voices:** Concerned the US may make too many concessions on non-proliferation, undermining regional security.

**Market Reaction:** International oil prices fell after the news, showing optimism about a deal.
