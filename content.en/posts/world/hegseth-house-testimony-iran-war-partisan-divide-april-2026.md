## 📰 Body

On April 29, 2026, US Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth appeared before the House of Representatives for his first congressional testimony since the Iran War began, facing pointed questioning from lawmakers of both parties. The hearing became the latest stage for intense debate over military action in American politics.

### Hearing Focus

According to The New York Times, Hegseth mounted a comprehensive defense of the administration's Iran military operations during the hearing, emphasizing the legitimacy and necessity of the actions. He detailed the strategic objectives of the military campaign and responded to questions from both parties regarding war progress, military casualties, and resource allocation.

PBS analysis noted that the hearing "revealed a partisan divide within American politics over the Iran War." Republican lawmakers largely supported the administration's military actions, while Democratic lawmakers raised serious questions about the war's legitimacy, costs, and humanitarian impact.

### Five Key Takeaways

The Wall Street Journal summarized five key takeaways from the hearing:

1. **Strategic Objectives**: Hegseth detailed the scope of the military operation's goals, emphasizing its aim to degrade Iran's military threat capabilities
2. **Casualty Disclosure**: The Defense Department publicly disclosed US casualties from military operations for the first time
3. **Resource Commitment**: Hegseth acknowledged that military operation costs have exceeded initial projections but described them as "necessary investments"
4. **Allied Positions**: The hearing revealed divergent stances among US allies on the Iran issue
5. **Future Plans**: Hegseth suggested military operations may continue for an extended period but did not provide a specific timeline

### Bipartisan Reactions

CBS News reported that Hegseth "took aim at Iran war critics" during the hearing, arguing that their questioning "undermines national security." This rhetoric further intensified partisan divisions.

Democratic lawmakers accused the administration of failing to adequately consult with Congress before launching military operations and of not providing a clear exit strategy. Some lawmakers called for an independent review of the military action's legality.

### International Impact

The hearing comes as international oil prices continue to surge due to the Iran situation. Brent crude has surpassed $126 per barrel, hitting a four-year high. The uncertainty surrounding military operations is not only affecting global energy markets but also sparking widespread discussion among allies about US foreign policy.

Analysts note that Hegseth's testimony is a key effort by the administration to rally more support in Congress, but the partisan divisions exposed during the hearing suggest that the political battle over the Iran War has only just begun.
