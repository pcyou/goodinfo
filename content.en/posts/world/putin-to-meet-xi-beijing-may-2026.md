Russian President Vladimir Putin is set to meet with Chinese leader Xi Jinping in Beijing, just days after President Trump concluded his own visit to the Chinese capital. The intensive diplomatic schedule underscores China's pivotal position in the global balance of power.

## Diplomatic Chain Reaction

CBS News reported that Putin will visit China shortly after Trump departs Beijing. This represents a delicate maneuver in US-China-Russia trilateral relations. Trump described his trip as a "very successful summit," though few substantive agreements were disclosed.

## Strategic Calculations

For Putin, following closely on Trump's visit signals that Russia will not be sidelined in US-China engagement. China and Russia have deepened their strategic coordination in recent years, cooperating closely on energy, trade, and international affairs.

For Xi, hosting both US and Russian leaders in quick succession demonstrates China's diplomatic influence as a global power. Analysts suggest this may herald a new model of great-power balancing diplomacy.

## International Reactions

Washington has responded cautiously to Putin's upcoming visit. Some members of the US Congress expressed concern that China and Russia may use the meeting to coordinate positions, particularly regarding policy toward the United States.
