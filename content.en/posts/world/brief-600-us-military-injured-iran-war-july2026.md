According to BBC News, the latest data released by the US Department of Defense shows that over 600 US military personnel have been injured in action since the US-Iran military conflict began in February 2026. The disclosure of this data has sparked widespread discussion in the US about the cost of the conflict.

The report noted that injured personnel sustained injuries ranging from minor trauma to severe traumatic brain injuries. The Department of Defense emphasized that all injured personnel have received appropriate medical treatment.

The timing of this data release is particularly sensitive—coming just as both US and Iran announced a pause in military operations to create space for diplomatic negotiations. Analysts believe the public release of casualty figures may intensify domestic US calls for resolving disputes through diplomatic channels.
