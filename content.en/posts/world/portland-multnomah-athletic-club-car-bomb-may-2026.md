## Article

On May 2, 2026, the landmark Multnomah Athletic Club in downtown Portland, Oregon, was severely damaged when a former employee drove a car loaded with explosives into the building, according to multiple sources cited by OregonLive.

### The Incident

According to sources, the attacker, a former employee of the club, drove a vehicle packed with a large amount of explosive material directly into the building. The explosion caused catastrophic structural damage, nearly destroying the interior facilities. Emergency responders rushed to the scene to conduct search and rescue operations and firefighting efforts.

### Investigation

Law enforcement agencies have launched an investigation. Preliminary information indicates the attacker harbored personal grievances against the club. Police are currently working to identify the type and quantity of explosives used and to determine whether there are any additional security threats.

### Building Background

The Multnomah Athletic Club is one of Portland's oldest private athletic clubs, founded in 1891 with over 10,000 members. The building is located in the heart of downtown and serves as an important cultural and social landmark. The bombing has not only dealt a devastating blow to the structure itself but has also significantly impacted the sense of security in Portland's city center.

### Security Implications

This incident has sparked widespread concerns about public space safety. In recent years, vehicle-based attacks have increased globally, prompting governments to strengthen related security measures. The Portland city government is expected to announce specific measures to enhance downtown security in the coming days.
