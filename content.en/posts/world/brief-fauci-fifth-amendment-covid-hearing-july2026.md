## Core Summary

Former US chief health official Anthony Fauci invoked his Fifth Amendment rights at a Senate Covid origins hearing on July 29, refusing to answer lawmakers' questions. Fauci expressed concern that Republicans could use his testimony to prosecute him for perjury. The hearing was marked by tense exchanges, including a heated argument with Senator Rand Paul.

## Event Details

According to NBC News, Fauci repeatedly invoked his constitutional Fifth Amendment right against self-incrimination during the Senate HELP Committee hearing, refusing to answer questions about the origins of the pandemic and prevention/control policies.

Fauci issued a statement at the hearing's outset saying he feared the Republican-controlled committee might try to "trip him up" and use his testimony to bring perjury charges against him in the future. He stated he was willing to answer questions related to science and public health, but declined to provide details on specific decision-making processes.

During the hearing, Fauci engaged in a heated exchange with Republican Senator Rand Paul. Paul accused Fauci of "misleading the public" during the pandemic, while Fauci countered by calling Paul "unhinged." This confrontation became one of the hearing's most watched moments.
