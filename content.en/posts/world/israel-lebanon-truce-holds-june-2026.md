categories: [world]

Israel continues military operations in southern Lebanon but has refrained from striking Beirut following a ceasefire deal announced by the US. The development comes after President Trump reportedly clashed with Israeli PM Netanyahu over plans to attack the Lebanese capital. Lebanon stated that Hezbollah has agreed to a reciprocal halt of hostilities. However, ongoing Israeli strikes in the south suggest the ceasefire remains fragile. The situation will directly impact broader US-Iran ceasefire negotiations, as a stable Israel-Hezbollah truce could pave the way for a wider Middle East peace agreement.
