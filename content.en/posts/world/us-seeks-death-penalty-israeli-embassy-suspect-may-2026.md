## [Flash] U.S. Seeks Death Penalty for Suspect in Killing of Israeli Embassy Staff

U.S. federal prosecutors have announced they will seek the death penalty for the suspect accused of killing Israeli embassy staff members. The case stems from a 2024 attack in Washington, D.C. that left multiple casualties. The move reflects the U.S. government's strict stance on crimes involving the safety of diplomatic personnel.
