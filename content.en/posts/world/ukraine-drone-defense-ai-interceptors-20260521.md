After four years of Russia's full-scale invasion, Ukraine has made significant advances in air defense, deploying a combination of AI technology and interceptor systems to counter drone attacks.

Ukrainian military officials say their air defense has evolved from reactive measures to a multi-layered integrated system. AI is being used for target identification and threat assessment, improving interception rates.

Analysts note that Ukraine's drone defense experience is reshaping global air defense strategies, with multiple nations studying tactical data accumulated during the conflict.
