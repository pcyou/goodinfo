## Russian Forces Launch Major Attack on Kyiv

Russian forces launched a new wave of massive missile strikes on Ukraine's capital Kyiv on May 14, hitting multiple residential buildings. Rescue workers are searching through rubble for survivors, with casualties already confirmed.

This attack marks one of the most intense Russian strikes on Ukrainian urban infrastructure in recent months. Kyiv's mayor urged residents to seek shelter in bomb shelters and warned of possible follow-up attacks. Explosions caused power outages across several neighborhoods, and emergency services have been fully mobilized.

The escalation comes amid broader Russian offensive operations across eastern Ukraine. International condemnation has been swift, with Western allies reaffirming military support commitments to Kyiv.
