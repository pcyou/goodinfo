## Event Summary

The BRICS leaders' summit has opened in New Delhi with Indian Prime Minister Modi as host and Russian President Putin and Chinese President Xi in attendance. According to multiple international media reports, Trump administration trade and tariff policies form the implicit main thread of discussions among participants, but there are clear divisions within BRICS on how to respond to Washington, making a unified position highly unlikely.

Observers noted that BRICS countries share common interests on issues such as energy cooperation, payment systems, and local currency settlement, but differ significantly on geopolitical risk exposure and strategic path choices. Some member states lean toward a more confrontational response, while others place greater value on economic ties with Western markets and avoid open confrontation. Such internal divisions have, to some extent, weakened the influence of the BRICS bloc as a unified external voice.

At the same time, host country India played a critical coordinating role during the summit. The Modi government has long maintained a flexible posture on multilateral diplomacy and great power balancing, and the New Delhi meeting has become an important window for all parties to observe the future direction of the BRICS mechanism. The meeting outcomes and the contents of any joint communique will serve as important indicators for assessing the cohesion of the BRICS bloc.
