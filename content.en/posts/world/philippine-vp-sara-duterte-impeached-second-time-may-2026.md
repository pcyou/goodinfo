## Philippines VP Duterte Faces Second Impeachment

Philippine Vice President Sara Duterte has been impeached for the second time, marking an escalation in the country's political power struggle.

## What Happened

The House of Representatives formally filed articles of impeachment against Vice President Sara Duterte. If convicted in a Senate trial, she will be disqualified from running for president.

Duterte, daughter of former President Rodrigo Duterte, was elected vice president in 2022. She previously served as education secretary in President Marcos' cabinet before resigning over policy disagreements.

## Political Context

The impeachment reflects intensifying tensions between two of the Philippines' most powerful political families — the Marcoses and the Dutertes — who were once allies but have become fierce rivals.

Analysts note the timing is significant, with the 2028 presidential election approaching. Duterte is widely viewed as a potential candidate for the top job.

## What Comes Next

The Senate trial outcome remains uncertain. Even if the impeachment fails, the episode will further polarize Philippine politics and could hamper government effectiveness.
