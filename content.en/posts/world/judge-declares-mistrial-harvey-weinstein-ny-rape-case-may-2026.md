## [Flash] Judge Declares Another Mistrial in Harvey Weinstein New York Rape Case

A New York judge has declared another mistrial in the rape case against Harvey Weinstein. This marks yet another significant procedural setback in a case that has been a focal point of the American justice system and public attention since the #MeToo movement. The mistrial will further extend the already lengthy legal proceedings.
