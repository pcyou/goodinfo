---
title: 'Russian Strikes Kill 26 in Ukraine Ahead of Kyiv-Announced Ceasefire'
date: 2026-05-06 11:47:52 +0800
categories: [world]
tags: ["Russia", "Ukraine", "Ceasefire", "War", "Diplomacy"]
---

Russian military strikes killed at least 26 people in Ukraine, according to Ukrainian officials, in an escalation that came just as Kyiv announced its own ceasefire declaration.

The timing of the attacks — coinciding with Ukraine's ceasefire announcement — has raised questions about Moscow's intentions and the prospects for any meaningful de-escalation. Ukrainian officials condemned the strikes as a deliberate attempt to undermine the diplomatic process.

Western allies expressed solidarity with Kyiv while calling on Russia to honor the ceasefire framework. The incident highlights the deep mistrust that continues to complicate peace efforts in the region.
