**Brief**

An overnight cyber attack on 12 October disabled the cold-storage control systems at hundreds of French supermarket chains, spoiling fresh meat, dairy and frozen goods within hours. Preliminary estimates put losses in the millions of euros.

The incident underlines a new supply-chain risk for retail: as heating, ventilation and refrigeration migrate to centralised cloud control, a single breach can propagate across many stores simultaneously rather than staying contained on one site.
