## [Brief] Pope Warns That AI Threatens Human Dignity

## Core Summary

According to BBC News, Pope Francis, currently on a visit to France, has publicly stated that the rapid development of AI could pose a serious threat to human dignity, calling for a stricter global ethical review of AI technology and emphasizing that technological progress must always keep the preservation of humanity as its premise.

### Event Details

Speaking during his series of addresses in France, the Pope noted that the "human-like decision-making capability" of AI, if not bound by clear ethical limits, could erode humanity's agency in labor, privacy, and public life. He called for stronger international cooperation on AI governance and the establishment of clear value and legal frameworks.

### Big Picture

This is the Pope's first relatively systematic public statement on AI in 2026, continuing the Vatican's sustained attention to frontier-technology ethics in recent years. Against the backdrop of the EU AI Act advancing and national AI regulatory frameworks gradually taking shape, a religious leader's warning about technology from the "humanity" dimension has injected a new value dimension into the global AI governance discussion.
