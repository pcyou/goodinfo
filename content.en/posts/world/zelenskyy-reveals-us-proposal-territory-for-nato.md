## Core Summary

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has publicly stated that the US government proposed a new peace plan suggesting Ukraine abandon its territorial claims to the Donbas region in exchange for NATO membership. This proposal marks a significant adjustment in Western countries' approach to resolving the Ukraine conflict, sparking widespread attention and intense debate in the international community.

## Event Details

According to reports from the Associated Press and other media outlets, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy revealed in his latest statement that the US government proposed a new European security framework plan. The core content of this proposal is: Ukraine agrees to give up its territorial sovereignty claims over the Donbas region (including Donetsk and Luhansk), and in exchange, NATO will accept Ukraine as a full member.

Zelenskyy warned that this new framework could allow Russia to weaken NATO and reshape European borders. He emphasized that any changes to European security must be decided by European NATO members after the war ends, not imposed externally. Zelenskyy himself insists that Ukraine will not surrender territory, and stated that Moscow may use these negotiations to change the European security landscape rather than focus on ending the war.

This proposal is considered one of the most substantive peace initiatives put forward by Western countries since the conflict began. Analysts point out that the plan attempts to find a balance between ensuring Ukraine's security and meeting some of Russia's strategic demands. However, it has also triggered strong reactions within Ukraine, with nationalist groups and some lawmakers viewing it as a betrayal of Ukrainian sovereignty.

Currently, Russia has not yet made an official response to this proposal. Kremlin spokespersons have previously stated that Moscow will continue to adhere to its established position, namely that Ukraine must recognize the "new realities" of the Donbas region and Crimea.

## Multiple Perspectives

**Supporting Views**: Some European diplomats believe that this proposal provides a viable path to end the long-standing conflict. They point out that NATO membership will provide Ukraine with strong security guarantees, and while territorial concessions are painful, they may bring lasting peace. Some analysts also emphasize that after Ukraine joins NATO, its economic reconstruction will receive more international support, and people's living standards are expected to recover quickly.

**Opposing Views**: Nationalist groups and some lawmakers within Ukraine strongly oppose this proposal, viewing it as a betrayal of Ukrainian sovereignty. They point out that millions of Ukrainian citizens live in the Donbas region, and abandoning the area means abandoning these compatriots. Additionally, critics worry that this precedent could be emulated by other countries, undermining the fundamental principle of respecting national sovereignty and territorial integrity as stipulated in the UN Charter.

**Neutral Views**: International relations scholars caution that the successful implementation of such proposals requires complex execution mechanisms and international oversight. They note that historical experience shows that agreements trading territory for security often face long-term implementation difficulties. The key lies in establishing credible security guarantee mechanisms to ensure that Ukraine does not face security threats again after making concessions. At the same time, any solution must fully consider the wishes and well-being of residents in the Donbas region.

## Panoramic Perspective

The emergence of this proposal reflects that the Ukraine conflict has entered a new phase. After nearly two years of fierce confrontation, all parties have realized that military means are difficult to achieve decisive victory, and diplomatic resolution has become an inevitable choice. However, how to balance safeguarding national sovereignty with maintaining regional stability remains an extremely complex challenge.

From a geopolitical perspective, Ukraine's accession to NATO will fundamentally change Europe's security architecture. This not only means that NATO's eastward expansion reaches new heights, but may also trigger strong reactions from Russia. Historically, NATO's eastward expansion has been a sensitive issue in Russia-Europe relations, and this proposal may further exacerbate strategic mutual suspicion between the two sides, even triggering a new arms race.

From an international law perspective, the practice of trading territorial concessions for security guarantees is controversial. The UN Charter clearly stipulates that respecting national sovereignty and territorial integrity is a basic principle of international relations. Any arrangement that violates this principle may set a dangerous precedent, affecting the stability of the global order. The international community should treat such proposals cautiously to avoid laying hidden dangers for future territorial disputes.

From a humanitarian perspective, residents of the Donbas region, regardless of their political stance, are the biggest victims of this conflict. Any solution must fully consider the wishes and well-being of local people, avoiding causing new humanitarian crises. The international community should strengthen attention to civilian protection, ensuring that the peace process does not come at the expense of ordinary people's interests.

In the future, the ultimate fate of this proposal will depend on the results of multi-party gaming. The decisions of the Ukrainian government, Russia's response, divisions within NATO, and the attitude of the international community will all influence the development of the situation. In any case, achieving lasting peace requires sincerity and wisdom from all parties, not simple zero-sum games. The international community should promote the establishment of a more inclusive and sustainable security architecture to ensure long-term stability in Europe and even globally.
