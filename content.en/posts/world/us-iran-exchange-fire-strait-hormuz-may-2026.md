## Direct Military Confrontation in the Strait of Hormuz

US and Iranian forces have exchanged fire in the Strait of Hormuz, one of the world's most critical maritime chokepoints. According to Axios, the confrontation occurred as the US military attempted to open a shipping lane through the strategic waterway.

## Iran Fires Missiles at UAE, Targets Tankers

NBC News reported that Iran launched missiles at the United Arab Emirates and fired drones at commercial tankers operating in the strait. The US confirmed it is guiding ships through the area to ensure safe passage.

## Markets React Sharply

Wall Street responded immediately to the escalation. The Dow Jones Industrial Average slipped while oil prices surged on fears of a prolonged disruption to one of the world's most vital petroleum shipping routes. Approximately 20 percent of global oil consumption passes through the Strait of Hormuz.

## Strategic Importance

The Strait of Hormuz, at its narrowest point just 21 miles wide, serves as the only sea passage from the Persian Gulf to the open ocean. Any sustained disruption to traffic through the strait has immediate and severe consequences for global energy markets and economic stability.
