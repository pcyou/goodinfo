## Core Summary

The Washington Post and The New York Times reported on September 13 that U.S. President Donald Trump said during a two-day visit to Ireland that a unified Ireland would be a \"fantastic\" outcome, breaking with decades of U.S. policy on Northern Ireland. Key British allies reacted sharply, with some British political figures describing the remark as a geopolitical bombshell for British domestic politics. The Daily Beast noted that the comments came during a particularly sensitive period for British politics and could widen the strain between the U.K. government and the Democratic Unionist Party in Northern Ireland.
