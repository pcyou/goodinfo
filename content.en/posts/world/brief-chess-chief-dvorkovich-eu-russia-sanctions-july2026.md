## [Brief] World Chess Chief Sanctioned by EU

According to BBC News, World Chess Federation president and former Russian Deputy Prime Minister Arkady Dvorkovich has been hit with a new round of European Union sanctions over the Russia-Ukraine conflict.

Dvorkovich now faces an EU travel ban and asset freeze. This sanction marks a continued escalation of EU pressure on Russia-related figures and has sparked widespread discussion in international sports circles about the relationship between politics and sports.
