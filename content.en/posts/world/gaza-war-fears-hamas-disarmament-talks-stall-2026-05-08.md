Disarmament negotiations involving Hamas have reached an impasse, raising fears of a renewed Gaza war. According to BBC reports, significant disagreements on key terms have stalled the ceasefire process. The international community is calling for all parties to return to negotiations and prevent further deterioration of the humanitarian crisis.
