The United States has confirmed new strikes on Iranian radar facilities. Meanwhile, Kuwait has condemned "repeated" missile and drone attacks on its territory. Tensions around the Strait of Hormuz continue to escalate as both the US and Iran report military actions in the area. Iran has also stated it carried out counter-strikes around the strait.
