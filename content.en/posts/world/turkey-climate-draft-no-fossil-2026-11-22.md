Turkey's final draft submitted during UN climate negotiations contains no mention of fossil fuels, drawing criticism from environmental organizations. This omission is viewed as a significant setback for climate action and may affect the final outcomes of the COP31 summit. Parties are engaged in final-stage consultations on the draft content.
