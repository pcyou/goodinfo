## Core Summary

According to The New York Times, Russian forces have adopted a new drone tactic against Ukraine's capital Kyiv, conducting relentless wave-style attacks. The core objective of this tactic is to gradually disable Ukraine's air defense systems through continuous consumption of defensive resources. This tactical shift marks a new phase in the Russia-Ukraine conflict, posing serious threats to urban infrastructure and civilian safety, and triggering deep international concern over humanitarian crises.

## Event Details

According to multiple authoritative media reports, the pattern of Russian drone attacks on Kyiv has undergone significant changes recently. Previously, Russian forces typically employed single or double attack patterns, but now they conduct multi-wave, continuous drone attacks. This "wave-style" tactic forces Ukrainian air defense systems to operate continuously, gradually depleting interceptor ammunition and operator endurance.

Military analysts point out that this tactical shift reflects a significant improvement in Russian drone production and deployment capabilities. By extensively using relatively inexpensive suicide drones, Russian forces attempt to overwhelm Ukrainian air defense systems with numerical superiority. While this strategy is costly, it has a significant destructive effect on urban infrastructure.

## Panoramic Perspective

The emergence of Russia's new drone tactic marks a major evolution in modern warfare. From a military technology perspective, this tactic fully exploits the low cost and large-scale deployability of drones, posing a severe challenge to traditional air defense systems. This forces militaries worldwide to reassess air defense strategies and accelerates the development of defense technologies against drone swarms.

From a humanitarian perspective, this sustained urban assault has a devastating impact on civilian life. Continuous alerts and attacks cause severe psychological damage to residents, and systematic destruction of urban infrastructure (electricity, water supply, healthcare). The UN Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights has repeatedly called on all parties to comply with international humanitarian law and protect civilians from the effects of war.

From a geopolitical perspective, this tactical escalation may influence Western countries' decisions on military aid to Ukraine. As the conflict continues, aid-giving countries face dual pressures from domestic politics and economic costs. Balancing support for Ukraine's defensive capabilities while avoiding indefinite escalation of the conflict has become a major policy challenge for Western nations.

## Multiple Perspectives

**Ukrainian Official Position**: The Ukrainian Ministry of Defense stated it is accelerating the development of domestic drone interception systems and calling on Western allies to provide more advanced air defense equipment. The Ukrainian side emphasized that despite severe challenges, air defense forces are doing everything possible to protect civilian safety.

**Western Military Analysis**: The Center for Strategic and International Studies (CSIS) points out that while Russia's drone tactic has caused significant damage, it also exposes limitations in its precision strike capabilities. This "saturation attack" strategy reflects shortcomings in Russia's conventional military capabilities.

**Humanitarian Organization Concerns**: The International Committee of the Red Cross warns that sustained urban attacks are creating a severe humanitarian crisis. With winter approaching, damage to energy infrastructure could leave millions facing the threat of severe cold, calling on all parties to establish humanitarian corridors.
