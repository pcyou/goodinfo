Israel announced on May 16 that it killed a senior Hamas military commander in an airstrike on Gaza. Hamas subsequently confirmed the death of the commander.

## Details

The Israeli military described the strike as a targeted operation against Hamas's command structure. This is part of Israel's ongoing campaign to degrade Hamas's military leadership.

## Context

The Israel-Hamas conflict continues to escalate. While the US has announced an extension of the ceasefire agreement, actual fighting has not fully stopped. Lebanon reported that Israeli strikes have killed 6 people.

International calls for restraint and peace negotiations continue amid growing uncertainty in the Middle East.
