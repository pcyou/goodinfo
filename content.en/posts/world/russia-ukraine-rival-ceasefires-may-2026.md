## Russia and Ukraine Announce Contradictory Ceasefire Plans as Fighting Continues

On May 5, 2026, Russia and Ukraine each announced separate ceasefire proposals, but the two plans diverge fundamentally on key terms, and fighting continued on the front lines.

### The Two Plans

Ukraine's proposal demands a full Russian withdrawal from occupied territories and restoration of pre-2014 borders. Russia countered with a conditional ceasefire that would require Ukraine to recognize Russian control over disputed regions.

### Fighting Continues

Despite both sides announcing ceasefire intentions, artillery exchanges and drone strikes persisted along the contact line. International observers noted that neither plan includes a verifiable monitoring mechanism, making an immediate truce unlikely.

### International Response

The UN Secretary-General urged both sides to demonstrate genuine willingness for peace through diplomatic channels. The EU and US reaffirmed their support for Ukraine's sovereignty and territorial integrity.
