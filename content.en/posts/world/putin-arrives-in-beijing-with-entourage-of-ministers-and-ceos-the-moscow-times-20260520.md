## Putin Arrives in Beijing With Ministerial and Business Delegation

Russian President Vladimir Putin has arrived in Beijing, accompanied by a delegation of cabinet ministers and corporate CEOs. The visit comes at a delicate moment in US-China relations, just days after President Trump's phone call with President Xi.

Key areas of focus are expected to include energy cooperation, trade settlement mechanisms, and coordination on international affairs. Against the backdrop of intensifying US-China competition, the trajectory of Sino-Russian relations remains under close global scrutiny.

Analysts note that Putin's visit serves not only to consolidate bilateral ties but also to send a clear signal to the West: Russia is accelerating its "pivot to the East" strategy in the wake of sanctions. Energy, military technology, and financial cooperation are expected to be the core agenda items.
