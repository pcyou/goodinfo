## Core Summary

American actress Hayden Panettiere has passed away at age 36. She was widely known for her roles in the TV series Heroes and Nashville.

## Event Details

According to The Washington Post and other media, Panettiere passed away recently. Born in New York in 1989, she started as a child star and built a career in Hollywood. Her notable roles include cheerleader Claire in NBC's Heroes and country singer Juliette in CMT's Nashville.

## Broader Perspective

Her passing has reignited public discussion about celebrity mental health and Hollywood pressures. Her willingness to openly share her struggles with postpartum depression inspired many facing similar challenges.
