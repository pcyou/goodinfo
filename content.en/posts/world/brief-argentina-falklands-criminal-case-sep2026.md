[Brief] The BBC reported on September 8 that the Argentine government has announced plans to file criminal cases against several international oil companies operating in Falkland Islands waters, accusing them of illegally exploring and extracting oil resources in territory Argentina claims sovereignty over. The move comes after Argentine President Milei recently escalated Argentina's sovereignty claims over the Falklands. The report noted that Argentine judicial authorities are preparing relevant legal documents, potentially involving multiple international energy companies holding exploration permits in Falklands waters. The Argentine government said it will maintain its sovereignty claim over the Malvinas through legal, diplomatic, and economic means. The United Kingdom has previously made clear it has sovereignty over the Falkland Islands and refused to negotiate sovereignty issues with Argentina.
