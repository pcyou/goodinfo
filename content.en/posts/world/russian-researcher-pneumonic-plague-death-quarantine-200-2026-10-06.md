**[Brief]** A Russian researcher has died of pneumonic plague, with around 200 contacts placed under quarantine. US officials are seeking details from Russian authorities and assessing whether the incident poses a cross-border public-health risk. Pneumonic plague is the most contagious form of plague and can spread between humans via respiratory droplets.
