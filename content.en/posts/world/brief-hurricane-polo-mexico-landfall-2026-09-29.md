## [Brief] Hurricane Polo Makes Landfall on Mexico Pacific Coast

### Core Summary

The BBC reports that Hurricane Polo has made landfall on Mexico's Pacific coast, with the Baja California peninsula now on alert. The peninsula is a popular tourist destination, and local authorities and the tourism sector are coordinating emergency response and evacuation measures.

### Event Details

According to the BBC, Hurricane Polo made landfall on Mexico's Pacific coast, with the landfall point on the Baja California peninsula. The peninsula has long been known for its tourism industry, packed with resort hotels, coastal holiday areas and outdoor activity projects. The report notes that local authorities have activated their emergency response mechanisms, and the tourism sector is working with emergency management teams on visitor evacuation and safety guidance.

The report also notes that the precise wind level, rainfall intensity and potential secondary hazards after landfall are still being assessed, but local authorities have pre-positioned rescue resources and triggered evacuation plans for low-lying coastal areas and visitor-dense zones. Over the next several hours, the affected footprint and the detailed damage picture will become clearer.
