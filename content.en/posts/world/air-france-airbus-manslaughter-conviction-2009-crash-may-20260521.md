A French court has found Air France and Airbus guilty of criminal negligence in the 2009 crash of Air France Flight 447, which killed all 228 people aboard. The disaster was one of the deadliest in Atlantic aviation history, when an A330 aircraft crashed en route from Rio de Janeiro to Paris. The court's ruling means both companies will face criminal liability, which is rare in aviation accident cases.
