## Core Summary

The Aspen Acres Fire in Colorado continues to spread, destroying 55 homes in one county and over 100 structures in another. Strong winds and dry conditions are hampering firefighting efforts, with thousands of residents evacuated.

## Brief

According to CBS News, the wildfire has caused damage across multiple Colorado counties. Fire officials say strong winds and low humidity are the main drivers of rapid fire spread. Thousands of residents have received evacuation orders, with additional communities under evacuation warnings.

This is one of the most severe wildfire events in Colorado this year. The governor has declared a state of emergency and requested FEMA assistance.
