## Panoramic Analysis

Read the two main threads of this edition together and a single pattern emerges: control of scarce resources is being repriced. On the artificial intelligence side, Saudi Aramco and the South Korean budget moved within the same week, which says the geography of compute, power and data centres has shifted from a corporate capital spending question into a matter of national budgets and energy capital. In crypto, the Korean central bank survey and the heavy outflows from spot products point to the same judgement, that exchanges are no longer treated merely as trading venues but as quasi-financial institutions. The combined implication is that the next regulatory cycle will watch the infrastructure that carries assets, not only the prices themselves. Whoever controls electricity, chips, ports and clearing rails will hold the new pricing power.
