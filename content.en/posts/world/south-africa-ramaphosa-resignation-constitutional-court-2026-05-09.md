## [Flash] South Africa Ramaphosa Faces Resignation Call After Constitutional Court Ruling

South Africa's Constitutional Court has ruled that MPs were wrong to block impeachment proceedings against President Cyril Ramaphosa. The ruling puts immense political pressure on Ramaphosa, with opposition parties demanding his immediate resignation. This represents a major test for South Africa's democratic institutions.
