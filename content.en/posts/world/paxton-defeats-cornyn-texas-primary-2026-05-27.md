## Paxton Defeats Cornyn in Texas Senate Primary Upset

In a stunning upset in the Texas Republican Senate primary, Attorney General Ken Paxton defeated incumbent Senator John Cornyn, a result widely viewed as a MAGA-aligned victory that could reshape the balance of power in the Senate.

Cornyn, a key Senate Republican figure who previously served as GOP Whip, had deep institutional connections in Washington. His defeat marks the latest sign of a power shift between the party establishment and its populist wing.

Paxton, known for his hardline conservative positions, ran with former President Trump's endorsement. Analysts suggest this victory could embolden more establishment-challenging candidates in future primaries.

Given Texas's strong Republican lean, the primary effectively determines who will hold the seat.
