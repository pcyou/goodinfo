## [Brief] US Imposes New Sanctions on Top Cuban Officials

The United States has announced a new round of sanctions against senior Cuban officials, increasing pressure on the Cuban government. The move has sparked discussion over whether the US is attempting to force regime change in Cuba. The sanctions include financial restrictions and travel bans.
