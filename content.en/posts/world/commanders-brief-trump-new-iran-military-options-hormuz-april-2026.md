## Senior US Commanders to Present New Iran Military Plans to Trump as Hormuz Tensions Escalate

April 30, 2026 — Senior US military commanders are scheduled to brief President Trump on a range of new military options against Iran on Thursday, Axios reported exclusively. The news sent international oil prices sharply higher, as markets feared further escalation in the US-Iran confrontation.

### Details of the New Military Options

According to Axios, the briefing will cover multiple tiers of military scenarios, including plans for precision strikes against Iranian defensive positions along the Strait of Hormuz. CNN subsequently reported that the US military is developing operational plans targeting Iran's Strait defenses in the event that ceasefire negotiations collapse.

Sources familiar with the matter said the central focus of the briefing is how to break the current shipping deadlock in the Strait of Hormuz. The Wall Street Journal reported that the United States is pitching a new coalition framework to allied nations aimed at restoring maritime traffic through the critical waterway.

### Oil Market Reaction

Following the report, Brent crude prices surged. BBC reported that the jump was a direct response to market concerns over potential new US military action against Iran. Oil had already broken above $120 per barrel earlier in the week due to reports of an extended Iranian blockade of the strait.

CNBC data showed that Brent crude climbed above $121 per barrel after news of the Trump briefing emerged.

### Trump Rejects Iran's Offer

In a separate exclusive, Axios reported that Trump has rejected Iran's proposal, stating that the blockade will remain in place until a nuclear deal is reached. This position suggests that a near-term de-escalation of US-Iran tensions is unlikely.

CNN had previously reported that Trump signaled the Hormuz standoff could last months rather than the weeks markets had anticipated, further heightening anxiety in energy markets.

### Putin Warns of "Dire Consequences"

On the international diplomatic front, Russian President Vladimir Putin warned Trump of "dire consequences" from military action against Iran, according to live updates from NewsNation. This signal from Moscow suggests that an escalation of the Iran conflict could trigger broader geopolitical ripple effects.

### Congressional Response

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth has been under intense scrutiny in Congress over the Iran war. During a nearly six-hour hearing, Hegseth criticized lawmakers questioning the feasibility of the military campaign, calling them "defeatist." The Washington Post reported that Hegseth became visibly agitated during the testimony, with some observers describing it as a "dramatic unraveling."

The Independent's analysis suggested that Hegseth's performance before Congress reflects growing political pressure on the Trump administration over the Iran war.
