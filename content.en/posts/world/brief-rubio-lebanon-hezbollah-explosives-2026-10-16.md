**[Summary]**

U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio publicly urged Lebanon on October 16 to resist Hezbollah pressure over recently seized explosives, pledging continued U.S. technical and intelligence support. The episode touches Lebanese domestic politics, Middle East security and the U.S.–Lebanon relationship.

**[Event Details]**

- **Background**: Lebanese security forces recently intercepted suspected terrorism-related explosives in multiple locations, with investigations pointing to an alleged Hezbollah-linked network.
- **U.S. position**: Secretary Rubio publicly urged the Lebanese government to resist Hezbollah pressure and handle the case under due legal process, hinting at continued U.S. intelligence and technical backing.
- **Lebanese position**: The Lebanese government said it will pursue the case through legal channels while expressing concern that escalation could further destabilise domestic politics and security.
- **Hezbollah**: Hezbollah publicly denied any link to the seized explosives, accusing the investigation of being politically motivated.
