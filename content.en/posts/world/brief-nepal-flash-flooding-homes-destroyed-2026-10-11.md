**[Core Summary]**

Flash floods and landslides triggered by heavy rainfall have struck multiple regions of Nepal in recent days, destroying or burying many homes and forcing large numbers of residents to evacuate to temporary shelters set up by the government. Local authorities have activated emergency response, are assessing relief needs and delivering supplies to affected areas, while neighboring countries and international humanitarian agencies have expressed concern and are preparing to provide assistance.

**[Event Details]**

- **Affected areas**: Flash floods and landslides have hit low-lying and mountainous communities across multiple Nepalese provinces. Roads, bridges and water supply facilities in some villages were washed away within hours.
- **Evacuations**: Local authorities organized emergency evacuations to temporary shelters set up in nearby schools and community centers. Power and communications in the hardest-hit areas were interrupted for a period.
- **Infrastructure**: Multiple mountain roads connecting to the outside were blocked. Emergency rescue teams are working to reopen them and telecom and power operators have activated post-disaster recovery plans.
- **Weather context**: Meteorologists say the heavy rainfall was driven by the interaction of an active monsoon and orographic uplift. Strong convective weather remains possible in mountainous areas in the next 24 hours.
- **International attention**: India, Bangladesh and other neighbors have been in contact with Nepalese authorities and have expressed willingness to provide disaster relief supplies and humanitarian assistance once assessment is complete.

**[Multiple Viewpoints]**

- **Nepal's disaster management authorities**: "The emergency response mechanism has been activated. We are continuously assessing the damage extent and needs and urge affected residents to strictly follow evacuation and safety guidelines."
- **Nepal's meteorological department**: "The heavy rainfall was caused by the combined effect of the active monsoon and orographic uplift. Strong convective weather remains possible in mountain areas over the next 24 hours."
- **Community residents' representative**: "Our homes and crops were destroyed within hours. We urge the government and charitable organizations to accelerate the delivery of relief supplies and help repair roads to the mountains."
- **International humanitarian agencies**: "We are in close contact with Nepalese counterparts and will provide emergency relief supplies and medical support as soon as needs assessments are completed."
