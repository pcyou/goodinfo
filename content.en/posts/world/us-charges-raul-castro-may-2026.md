## US Plans to Charge Former Cuban Leader Raul Castro

US authorities are preparing to file criminal charges against former Cuban leader Raul Castro, potentially as early as next week, according to multiple sources. The case reportedly centers on Cuba's 1996 downing of two civilian planes operated by the Brothers to the Rescue group.

**Historical Background**

In February 1996, the Cuban Air Force shot down two civilian aircraft, killing four people. The incident remains one of the most serious military confrontations between the US and Cuba since the Cold War.

**Legal and Political Dimensions**

Prosecuting a nonagenarian former head of state presents enormous practical challenges. Analysts view the move as primarily symbolic, reflecting the current administration's hardline stance on Cuba policy.
