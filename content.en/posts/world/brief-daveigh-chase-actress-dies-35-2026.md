BBC reports that American actress Daveigh Chase has passed away at the age of 35. She was best known for her roles in the film "The Ring" and for voicing the lead character in the animated film "Lilo & Stitch." Her family has not yet disclosed the cause. Police say they are investigating the circumstances.
