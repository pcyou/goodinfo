## [Brief] DR Congo President Signals Term Extension

The president of the Democratic Republic of Congo has hinted at extending his term in office and delaying scheduled elections.

The statement has drawn widespread attention and concern both domestically and internationally. Analysts warn this could further tense the country political situation and trigger strong opposition reactions.

The DR Congo has faced ongoing political instability and security challenges with armed conflict continuing in the eastern regions. A term extension could deepen domestic political divisions.

The international community has urged the Congolese government to abide by constitutional provisions and hold elections on time to preserve the democratic process.
