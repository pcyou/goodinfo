## Core Summary

According to Reuters, a strong earthquake in Japan has injured at least 50 people and severely disrupted power supply and transportation in affected areas. Following the quake, local authorities quickly activated emergency response mechanisms and organized rescue teams for search and rescue operations and post-disaster recovery. There are currently no reports of fatalities, but infrastructure damage is still being assessed.

## Event Details

Reuters reported that a strong earthquake struck Japan during local time, with noticeable shaking felt across multiple regions. The quake has injured at least 50 people, who have been transported to nearby medical facilities for treatment. Some buildings have developed cracks, roads have subsided, and certain areas are experiencing power outages.

The Japan Meteorological Agency has released detailed earthquake information, including epicenter location, magnitude, and aftershock warnings. The government has called on the public to remain vigilant and take precautions against potential aftershocks. In terms of transportation, some railway lines have suspended operations, highways are under temporary restrictions, and airport flights have been delayed.

Japan's Chief Cabinet Secretary stated at an emergency press conference that disaster response has been designated as the highest priority, with full efforts underway to assess damage and organize rescue operations. The Self-Defense Forces are on standby and ready to deploy for disaster relief at any time.

The power company stated it is working全力 to repair damaged power facilities and restore electricity to affected areas as quickly as possible. Currently, tens of thousands of households are without power, and repair work is proceeding intensively.

Transportation authorities stated that high-speed rail lines including the Shinkansen are undergoing comprehensive safety inspections and will gradually resume operations once confirmed safe. Highway restrictions will also be lifted as road conditions permit.

This earthquake occurred during Japan's seismically active period, and meteorological departments had previously reminded the public to prepare for earthquakes multiple times. As a country prone to earthquakes, Japan has a relatively well-developed earthquake early warning and emergency response system, with strict building seismic standards.
