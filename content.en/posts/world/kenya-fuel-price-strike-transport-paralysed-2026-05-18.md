## Kenya Transport Strike Paralysed Over Fuel Prices

A major transport strike has erupted in Kenya over rising fuel prices, paralyzing transportation across multiple regions. The strike has severely disrupted both passenger travel and goods delivery nationwide.

Strike organizers are calling on the government to take measures to control fuel prices and ease the burden on citizens. Kenya has been affected by global energy price volatility, with domestic fuel costs continuing to climb.
