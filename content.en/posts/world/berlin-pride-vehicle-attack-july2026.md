## Core Summary

According to BBC News, CBS News, and AP News, the Berlin Pride vehicle ramming incident has reached a major development: suspect Abdul Ballout was shot dead by police in western Berlin on Saturday evening after rushing at officers with a knife. The incident left one person dead and 29 injured. Investigation reveals that the suspect had previously been imprisoned for attempting to join an extremist organization but was released after serving less than two years. This is one of the most serious security incidents at a major public event in Berlin in recent years, sparking deep reflection on public event security measures and parole policies in Germany.

## Event Details

The incident occurred at the Pride parade in central Berlin. According to a Berlin police spokesperson, a vehicle suddenly drove into the crowd during the event, causing major casualties. The latest confirmed casualty figures show one person killed and 29 injured, many seriously.

Following the incident, Berlin police quickly sealed off the area and launched a large-scale manhunt. Police identified the suspect as 21-year-old Abdul Ballout. According to CBS News, the suspect was located by police in western Berlin on Saturday evening. He rushed at officers with a knife, and police subsequently shot him dead.

Investigation reveals that Ballout had previously been imprisoned for attempting to join an extremist organization but was released after serving less than two years. This background has raised strong questions in German society about parole policies and extremist monitoring mechanisms. The specific motive has not yet been disclosed, and investigators are conducting a comprehensive collection and analysis of evidence from the scene.

Berlin's mayor and Germany's federal interior minister have been briefed. The Berlin city government has activated emergency response mechanisms to coordinate medical, police, and fire departments in rescue and investigation efforts.

Pride parade is one of Berlin's most important annual public events, attracting hundreds of thousands of participants. This year's event was originally expected to proceed peacefully, emphasizing diversity, inclusion, and social equality. The incident has cast a shadow over this annual celebration.

Although the casualty scale of this Berlin Pride parade incident remains limited, its location and timing are highly symbolic.

Pride parade in Europe is not just a cultural event, but a public expression of diversity and inclusion values. As the main driver of Europe's refugee acceptance policy, Germany has seen rising internal social tensions in recent years, with far-right forces and anti-immigrant sentiment gaining ground in some groups.

Despite Germany's heavy investment in major event security, completely preventing such incidents is nearly impossible. This incident is expected to push Germany and other EU countries to reassess security standards for street events, potentially including more physical barriers, vehicle interception systems, and forward-deployed intelligence.
