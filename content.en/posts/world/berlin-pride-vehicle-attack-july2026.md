## Core Summary

According to BBC News, a serious security incident occurred at the Pride parade in central Berlin when a vehicle drove into the crowd, injuring multiple people. Police have cordoned off the area and launched a large-scale manhunt. This is another serious security incident at a major public event in Berlin in recent years, drawing renewed attention to security measures at public events in Germany.

## Event Details

The incident occurred at the Pride parade in central Berlin. According to a Berlin police spokesperson, a vehicle suddenly drove into the crowd during the event, causing injuries to multiple participants. The exact number of casualties and the severity of injuries are still being verified.

Berlin police quickly sealed off the area around the incident and launched a large-scale manhunt. Police say they are working to track down the vehicle and driver. The specific motive has not yet been disclosed, and investigators are collecting and analyzing evidence from the scene.

Berlin's mayor and Germany's federal interior minister have been briefed and are expected to make public statements shortly. The Berlin city government has activated emergency response mechanisms to coordinate medical, police, and fire departments in rescue and investigation efforts.

Pride parade is one of Berlin's most important annual public events, attracting hundreds of thousands of participants. This year's event was originally expected to proceed peacefully, emphasizing diversity, inclusion, and social equality. The incident has cast a shadow over this annual celebration.

A spokesperson for the German federal police said it is currently impossible to confirm whether this was a deliberate attack, and all possibilities are being investigated. Investigators are reviewing surveillance footage and witness statements to reconstruct the full picture of the event.

Multiple hospitals in Berlin have been placed on standby to receive potentially increasing numbers of injured. The German Red Cross and other emergency organizations have also dispatched additional personnel to the area to assist with rescue efforts.

This incident once again highlights the security challenges facing large public events in Europe. In recent years, multiple European countries have frequently faced various security threats at public places and large events. From the Nice truck attack to the Berlin Christmas market incident, vehicle ramming has become a worrying attack pattern. Although the casualty scale of this Berlin Pride parade incident remains to be confirmed, its location and timing are highly symbolic.

Pride parade in Europe is not just a cultural event, but a public expression of diversity and inclusion values. Launching an attack on such an occasion, regardless of motive, will be interpreted as a provocation against a diverse society. As the main driver of Europe's refugee acceptance policy, Germany has seen rising internal social tensions in recent years, with far-right forces and anti-immigrant sentiment gaining ground in some groups.

From a security perspective, open street events inherently face protection difficulties. Despite Germany's heavy investment in major event security, completely preventing such incidents is nearly impossible. This incident is expected to push Germany and other EU countries to reassess security standards for street events, potentially including more physical barriers, vehicle interception systems, and forward-deployed intelligence.

For the German government, how to balance the openness and security of public events will be a continuing policy challenge. Excessive security may weaken the essential meaning of events, but insufficient security could lead to more serious consequences. This balance needs to be found through broad social discussion after the investigation conclusions are released.
