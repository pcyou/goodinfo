[Brief] ## Post-Match Disturbances

After Paris Saint-Germain advanced to the Champions League final, large-scale celebrations erupted in Paris, but some areas turned into riots. Police made 127 arrests, with multiple reports of vandalism.

## What Happened

While most celebrations were peaceful, some fans threw objects, set fires, and clashed with police along the Champs-Elysees and other areas.

## Police Response

Paris police deployed additional forces to maintain order and urged citizens to celebrate within the law. The city government condemned the destructive behavior.
