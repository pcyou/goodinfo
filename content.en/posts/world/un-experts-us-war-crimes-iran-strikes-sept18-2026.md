## Core Summary

Independent UN human rights experts publicly announced on September 17 that there are reasonable grounds to believe that US airstrikes on Iranian territory earlier this year may constitute war crimes. The experts focused particularly on the strike on a primary school in Minab and a sports complex in Lamerd, both of which resulted in significant civilian casualties. To date, the US government has not formally responded to the allegations or acknowledged carrying out the strikes.

## Event Details

According to BBC reporting, multiple independent experts under the UN Human Rights Council reached this conclusion after months of investigation. Based on satellite imagery, local medical system records, and survivor testimony, the expert panel determined that several US pinpoint strikes in February failed to meet the basic requirements of international humanitarian law regarding proportionality and foreseeable civilian harm.

The Minab primary school strike served as the key trigger for the UN investigation. According to public records, the school was operating normally at the time of the strike, with large numbers of children in attendance. The attack caused severe structural damage to the school and affected surrounding civilian residences. The Lamerd sports complex strike also resulted in major casualties, with the facility hosting a local sporting event at the time.

In their statement, UN experts clearly noted that international humanitarian law strictly distinguishes between military objectives and civilian facilities, with strikes against the latter requiring extremely strict necessity conditions. Any strike that cannot clearly demonstrate military necessity may constitute a violation of international law. The experts also criticized the US refusal to publicly explain the target selection logic afterwards, arguing that this lack of transparency has deepened doubts about the strikes' compliance.

Notably, the timing of this UN statement is particularly delicate. US-Iran relations remain highly tense, with both sides maintaining intensive military deployments across multiple Middle Eastern regions. The UN experts' assessment may provide important grounds for the international community to reassess US Middle East military operations and could affect ongoing international criminal accountability discussions.

## Panoramic Perspective

The UN experts' determination carries significant international law implications and far-reaching geopolitical impact. From an international law perspective, while statements by independent experts lack legal binding force, their conclusions typically become important reference points for subsequent judicial proceedings and international investigations. If these determinations are adopted by the International Criminal Court or other judicial institutions, lengthy accountability proceedings could begin, posing a long-term challenge to the international legitimacy of US military operations.

The deeper impact lies in the practical enforceability of the international humanitarian law system. For years, the international community has faced political resistance to legal accountability for major power military actions, and the UN experts' statement highlights the tension between international rule of law and realpolitik. If this determination ultimately fails to translate into concrete legal consequences, it will further weaken international humanitarian law's actual effectiveness in constraining major power behavior.

From a geopolitical analysis perspective, this event may deepen trust rifts between the US and Middle Eastern countries. For years, US military operations in the Middle East have relied on "protecting civilians" as an important moral foundation. The UN experts' determination will force the US government to reassess its operational guidelines and may also affect security cooperation frameworks between the US and Gulf allies.
