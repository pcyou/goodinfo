## Core Summary

The US military conducted airstrikes against Iranian missile launcher positions on Larak Island, marking the first known direct military strike in recent weeks. The strike occurred near the Strait of Hormuz, a critical chokepoint through which approximately 20% of the world's oil supply passes, immediately raising serious concerns about regional security and global energy supply stability. Following the incident, international oil prices surged, and multiple governments called for restraint from all parties.

## Event Details

According to multiple authoritative media reports from BBC and The Guardian, the US military launched airstrikes against missile launchers on Iran's Larak Island. Larak Island is located near the Strait of Hormuz, one of the world's most important energy transportation routes.

This military action represents the first known direct strike against Iranian territory in recent weeks, marking a significant escalation in regional tensions. The specific reasons and context for the strike remain unclear, but analysts suggest it may be related to a series of security incidents in the region recently.

Following the airstrikes, security conditions in the Strait of Hormuz immediately drew widespread attention. As one of the world's most critical energy transportation corridors, any military conflict in the area could have major impacts on international energy markets.

This military strike has profound implications for global geopolitical dynamics and energy security. First, the Strait of Hormuz, as a critical chokepoint for global energy transportation, directly affects the stable operation of the global economy. Any military conflict in this region could lead to shipping disruptions, soaring insurance costs, and consequently driving up global energy prices.

Second, this incident could trigger an escalation in regional arms races and proxy conflicts. Iran and its regional allies may retaliate against this strike, while the US and its regional allies may further strengthen military deployments. This vicious cycle would seriously undermine stability in the Middle East.

Third, this event highlights the vulnerability of international energy security. Global dependence on fossil fuels makes critical transportation routes focal points for geopolitical competition. This incident may accelerate countries' efforts to promote energy diversification and renewable energy development.

Finally, this military action poses challenges to international law and multilateralism. Unilateral military action without UN Security Council authorization may weaken the foundations of international order, sparking more discussions about how to maintain international peace and security.

**Iran's Response**: Iranian state media condemned the airstrikes as "a serious violation of national sovereignty" and warned of a "decisive response." Iran's Revolutionary Guards have entered a state of high alert.

**International Community Concerns**: The UN Secretary-General called for maximum restraint from all parties to avoid further escalation. The EU High Representative for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy expressed "serious concern" and urged resolution of differences through diplomatic channels.

**Energy Market Reaction**: International oil prices immediately rose following the news, with Brent crude futures increasing by over 3%. Analysts warn that if tensions persist, oil prices could climb further.

**Regional Countries' Attitudes**: Gulf Cooperation Council member states called for resolving disputes through dialogue and avoiding military conflict. Israel and other US allies in the region expressed support for the US action.
