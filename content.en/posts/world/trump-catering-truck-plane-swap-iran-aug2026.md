## Core Summary

According to multiple reports including from the BBC, US President Trump recently took an extraordinary security measure to evade a potential threat from Iran: he was secretly transferred from Air Force One to another aircraft under cover of a catering truck. The unusual arrangement has sparked widespread discussion both domestically and internationally.

## Event Details

The secret plane swap reportedly occurred during a presidential trip. With intelligence indicating a potential Iranian threat to his itinerary, the White House security team opted for an unconventional approach. A catering truck was driven alongside Air Force One, through which Trump was quietly transferred to another aircraft, with the entire operation kept strictly classified.

The White House has not issued an official response. However, security experts note that protective details do employ multiple routes and deceptive measures in high-threat environments, though using a catering truck as cover is exceptionally rare.

The incident comes amid heightened US-Iran tensions. Since the Iran war began, American military deployments and diplomatic activities in the Middle East have faced unprecedented security challenges.
