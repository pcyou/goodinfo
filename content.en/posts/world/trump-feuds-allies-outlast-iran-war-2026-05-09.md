## [Flash] Trump Feuds With Allies Likely to Outlast Iran War

According to Reuters, analysts say tensions between the Trump administration and traditional allies such as European nations are unlikely to ease even after the Iran conflict concludes. Trump's unilateral approach to trade, military deployment, and foreign policy has caused structural damage to transatlantic relations.

The report notes that communities in Germany and elsewhere are adapting to life with reduced US troop presence, while allies' trust in American security commitments continues to erode.
