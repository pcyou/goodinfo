## [快讯] 以色列空袭贝鲁特：停火以来首次打击真主党指挥官

据路透社报道，以色列对贝鲁特发动空袭，这是停火协议生效以来首次对贝鲁特进行军事打击。目标为一名真主党高级指挥官。此次行动打破了数周来的停火平静，引发国际社会对黎巴嫩局势再度恶化的担忧。

## Israel Strikes Beirut: First Hit Since Ceasefire, Targeting Hezbollah Commander

According to Reuters, Israel launched an airstrike on Beirut, the first military strike on the city since the ceasefire took effect. The target was a senior Hezbollah commander. The operation breaks weeks of ceasefire calm, raising international concerns about a renewed deterioration in Lebanon's situation.
