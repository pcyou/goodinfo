## Overview

The 2026 Met Gala took place at the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York, with this year's theme being Costume Art, exploring fashion as an embodied art form. The event is one of the most anticipated nights in the fashion calendar.

## Details

Beyonce returned as a co-chair of the Met Gala, marking her first time in the role since 2016. The red carpet featured numerous celebrities showcasing art-inspired looks, deeply integrating fashion with artistic concepts.

Another major focus of this year's gala was the participation of Amazon founder Jeff Bezos. Bezos has increased his involvement in the fashion world in recent years, and his high-profile attendance drew criticism from some figures in the fashion community. Questions were raised about whether commercial capital is exerting too much influence over a cultural event, with some even calling for a boycott.

## Impact and Reactions

On the red carpet, several celebrities chose classic designs with art historical significance, echoing this year's Costume Art theme. Fashion commentators noted that this year's looks collectively presented a deep homage to artistic expression rather than mere displays of luxury.

The Met Gala serves as the primary fundraising event for the Metropolitan Museum of Art's Costume Institute, bringing crucial financial support to the museum each year. This year's event continues the trend of the past few years, connecting fashion with broader cultural conversations.
