## Zelenskyy Condemns Russian Attack During Truce as Five Killed at Gas Facility

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy strongly condemned Russia for launching military strikes while simultaneously seeking a truce, calling the Kremlin actions utterly cynical on May 5, 2026.

### The Attack

Russian overnight strikes on a gas facility in Ukraine central Poltava region killed three employees of the state energy company Naftogaz. Two emergency service workers who responded to the scene were also killed, bringing the total death toll to five.

### Truce and Military Parade

Zelenskyy pointed out that the Kremlin was seeking a truce to stage a military parade in Moscow while continuing to launch deadly attacks. He described this behavior as demonstrating complete cynicism.

### International Reaction

The strike further intensified international doubts about Russia sincerity. Analysts suggested that Russia may be using the truce window for military repositioning and parade preparations rather than genuinely seeking peace.

### Energy Infrastructure at Risk

The Poltava region is an important natural gas producing area in Ukraine, and the attack on this facility highlights the ongoing threats to Ukrainian energy infrastructure during the conflict.
