## Trump Criticizes Pope's Stance on Iran

On May 5, 2026, Trump publicly criticized Pope Francis's position on Iran, claiming the Pope's statements could endanger Catholic communities around the world.

### The Comments

Trump posted on social media, accusing the Pope of not taking a strong enough stance on Iran-related issues and suggesting this could negatively impact Catholic communities in the Middle East.

### International Reaction

The remarks quickly sparked broad international discussion. The Vatican has not issued an official response, but diplomatic observers note that public criticism of the Pope by a national leader is unusual.
