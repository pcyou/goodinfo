## [Flash] Memorial Reveals Death Toll as Thousands of North Koreans Fight for Russia

A memorial has shed light on the heavy casualties among North Korean soldiers fighting in Russia's war against Ukraine. An estimated 11,000 North Korean troops were deployed to the Ukrainian battlefield. The memorial hints at the grim cost of this little-known military involvement.
