**[Core Summary]** Xi Jinping has concluded a two-day visit to Pyongyang, his first official trip to North Korea since 2019. Both leaders pledged to strengthen bilateral ties, while North Korea's economic performance and nuclear status drew international attention.

## Key Outcomes of the Visit

The visit covered bilateral economic cooperation, regional security, and international affairs. Following meetings between the two leaders, a joint statement was issued emphasizing the deepening of traditional friendly relations and advancing practical cooperation in economics and trade.

Notably, several international media outlets have recently published in-depth reports on North Korea's economic situation. The Wall Street Journal characterized North Korea's economy as an "unexpected success story," while the Kyiv Post analyzed how North Korea has been deriving economic benefits from Russia's ongoing situation.

## North Korea's "Economic Transformation" Narrative

The New York Times published an opinion piece titled "Kim Jong Un's Triumph," arguing that North Korea is reshaping its international positioning in ways the outside world did not anticipate. Analysts suggest that by strengthening ties with major powers like Russia, North Korea has gained more diplomatic leverage and economic benefits.

Meanwhile, North Korea's nuclear capability positioning is also shifting. New Statesman commented that Kim Jong Un is enjoying the geopolitical dividends of his newly established status as a nuclear power.

## Analysis and Perspective

The resumption of high-level China-North Korea exchanges marks subtle but profound changes in the East Asian geopolitical landscape. In the current international environment, as a central player in regional security issues, North Korea's diplomatic trajectory will directly affect the power balance in Northeast Asia.

From China's perspective, strengthening ties with North Korea serves multiple strategic purposes. First, stable China-North Korea relations help maintain a security buffer zone in Northeast Asia, preventing uncontrollable situations on the Korean Peninsula. Second, against the backdrop of ongoing U.S.-China competition, North Korea's geopolitical value as a traditional ally is being reassessed.

From North Korea's perspective, this visit provides important diplomatic endorsement. By deepening cooperation with China, North Korea gains additional support when facing international sanctions and diplomatic isolation. At the same time, North Korea is leveraging the space between great power competition to maximize its own interests.

Notably, North Korea's so-called "economic success" narrative requires cautious examination. While there are some signs of activity in certain sectors of the North Korean economy, its overall economic structure still faces severe challenges, including prolonged international sanctions, infrastructure deficits, and livelihood issues. The international community has questioned the transparency of North Korea's economic data, and the accuracy of related reports needs independent verification.

## Market Perspectives

**Supportive Views**: Regional analysts argue that deepening China-North Korea relations helps stabilize the Korean Peninsula situation and reduces the risk of sudden crises. Some economists point out that if North Korea can continue to push economic opening, its geographic location and labor costs could become potential advantages for attracting investment.

**Critical Voices**: Western observers express concern about the China-North Korea rapprochement, arguing it may weaken international pressure on North Korea's nuclear issue. Human rights organizations also urge that any cooperation with North Korea should include conditions for human rights improvement.

**Market Impact**: East Asian capital markets are closely watching the trajectory of China-North Korea relations. Analysts note that if North Korea shows substantive signals of economic opening, it could have ripple effects on trade and investment patterns in neighboring countries.
