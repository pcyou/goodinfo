## 📰 Trump Disapproval Reaches New High

According to the latest poll conducted by The Washington Post, ABC News, and Ipsos, U.S. President Donald Trump's disapproval rating has climbed to its highest level of his term, reflecting widespread public dissatisfaction with his handling of the economy, international conflicts, and domestic policy.

The nationwide survey shows that more than half of respondents disapprove of Trump's performance as president, a significant increase from polls conducted several months ago. The poll assessed the president on overall performance, economic management, foreign policy, and several specific issues.

Analysts note that the rise in disapproval is linked to several factors. First, the ongoing U.S.-Iran conflict and the resulting surge in oil prices have directly impacted the cost of living for American families. The sharp increase in gasoline prices has significantly raised transportation expenses for ordinary households, sparking widespread economic anxiety.

Second, the Trump administration's hardline foreign policy stance has strained relations with transatlantic allies. The recent announcement of a major troop withdrawal from Germany, along with hints that military presence in Italy and Spain could also be reduced, has led many voters to worry that U.S. leadership in global affairs is being undermined.

The poll also shows that dissatisfaction with Trump's direction is rising among Republican and independent voters as well. Some voters who previously firmly supported Trump have begun to question the effectiveness of his policies, particularly as concerns about war costs and living expenses intensify.

However, analysts also caution that the polling data needs to be understood in a broader context. Historically, U.S. presidents experience fluctuations in approval ratings during their terms, and the current complex geopolitical environment — including the U.S.-Iran war, global economic uncertainty, and domestic political divisions — makes the polling results particularly nuanced.

The Washington Post noted that these results could affect Republican performance in the upcoming midterm elections. Some Republican lawmakers have already begun to distance themselves from the White House, seeking to protect their political interests as voter dissatisfaction grows.

Political commentators suggest that how the Trump administration responds to economic pressures and diplomatic challenges in the coming months will directly impact the trajectory of his approval ratings. If oil prices remain persistently high and allied relationships further deteriorate, disapproval could continue to climb.
