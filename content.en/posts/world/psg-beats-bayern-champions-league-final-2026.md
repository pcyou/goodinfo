## PSG Reach Champions League Final After Dembele Sinks Bayern Munich

In the 2025-26 UEFA Champions League semifinal, Paris Saint-Germain advanced to the final after Ousmane Dembele's decisive goal knocked out Bayern Munich.

Dembele scored the crucial goal that secured PSG's victory, sending the French champions through to the championship match. The team has shown formidable form throughout this season's Champions League campaign.

### Match Recap

Bayern Munich put up a resilient defensive display, but Dembele's breakthrough goal ultimately sealed their fate and sent them out of the competition.

PSG will await the winner of the other semifinal in the final. This marks one of PSG's best Champions League runs in recent years.

### Season Outlook

Paris Saint-Germain has invested heavily this season with the goal of winning their first-ever Champions League title. Reaching the final is seen as a significant step toward that ultimate objective.
