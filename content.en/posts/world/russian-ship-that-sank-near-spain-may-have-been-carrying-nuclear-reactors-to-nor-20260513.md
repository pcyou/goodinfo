## Russian Ship Sunk Near Spain May Have Been Carrying Nuclear Reactors to North Korea

A Russian vessel that sank off the coast of Spain may have been transporting nuclear reactors to North Korea, according to reports. The incident has raised serious international concerns about nuclear proliferation and maritime safety. The cause of the sinking remains under investigation.
