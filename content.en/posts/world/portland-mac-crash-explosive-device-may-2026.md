## Incident Overview

On the evening of May 2, 2026, a serious incident occurred in Portland, Oregon: a vehicle crashed at high speed into the historic Multnomah Athletic Club (MAC), triggering a fire. Police discovered an explosive device at the scene, and one person has been confirmed dead.

## What Happened

According to Oregon Public Broadcasting (OPB), the vehicle caught fire after crashing into the club's building. Police quickly cordoned off the surrounding area and evacuated nearby buildings. The Portland Police Bureau (PPB) stated that the explosive device was found at the scene and is being handled by specialized bomb disposal personnel.

KPTV reports that video appears to show the vehicle crashing through the Multnomah Athletic Club's lobby before the explosion. The exact cause and motive of the incident remain under investigation.

## Multnomah Athletic Club

The Multnomah Athletic Club is one of Portland's oldest and most prominent private social and athletic clubs, founded in 1891 with over 10,000 members. The building is located in downtown Portland and is a significant city landmark.

## Investigation Status

KOIN provided live coverage of the developing situation. Portland police have stated there is no immediate additional threat to public safety but are actively investigating the nature of the incident — whether it was a deliberate attack or resulted from other circumstances.

OregonLive reports that footage shows the vehicle approaching the club at high speed before impact, a detail that has heightened investigators' attention to the possibility of intentional action.

## Official Response

The Portland Mayor's office said it is closely monitoring the situation and has pledged to provide timely updates. The club has suspended operations to cooperate with the ongoing police investigation.
