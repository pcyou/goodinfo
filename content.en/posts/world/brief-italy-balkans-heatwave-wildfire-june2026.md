**[Core Summary]** According to Reuters, Italy and the Balkans are experiencing an extreme heatwave, with temperatures breaking historical records in multiple locations. The hot, dry conditions have pushed forest fire risk to the highest level, and fire services across the region have entered emergency status.

Meanwhile, French officials confirmed that a previous heatwave caused approximately 1,000 excess deaths. Europe is facing increasingly frequent extreme heat events as the impacts of climate change become more pronounced.
