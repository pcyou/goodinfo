# Trump Administration Secures New Indictment Against Former FBI Director James Comey Over Instagram Post

> Former FBI Director James Comey faces a new grand jury indictment from the Trump administration over an Instagram post featuring the numbers "8647" on a seashell image, which officials have interpreted as an implicit threat against President Trump.

---

On April 28, 2026, The New York Times reported that the Trump administration has secured a **new grand jury indictment** against former FBI Director James Comey. The charges stem from an Instagram post that federal officials interpreted as constituting a potential threat against President Trump.

## The Controversial "8647" Seashell Post

According to CNBC and multiple other outlets, the key evidence in the indictment is a photo Comey posted on Instagram featuring the numbers **"8647"** superimposed on an image of a seashell. Prosecutors argue that this sequence of numbers, in certain contexts, could be interpreted as a veiled threat.

Comey has previously faced indictment over similar social media activity, but this is a separate indictment obtained under a new investigative framework by the Department of Justice.

## Political Background

The feud between Comey and Trump dates back to 2017, when Trump fired Comey from his position as FBI Director. Since then, Comey has been a vocal critic of Trump's governing style and perceived threats to judicial independence, continuing to speak out on social media.

This indictment comes just two days after the White House Correspondents' Dinner shooting, at a time when American discourse on political violence and social division is at a fever pitch.

## Reactions

- **Democrats**: Some Democratic lawmakers have characterized the case as "political persecution," arguing it represents the use of the justice system to target political opponents.
- **Republicans**: Some Republican lawmakers have supported the DOJ's action, maintaining that any potentially threatening communication should face legal scrutiny regardless of political affiliation.
- **Legal Community**: Constitutional scholars have questioned the legal basis of the indictment, suggesting that classifying cryptic social media expression as a threat may venture into First Amendment gray areas.

## Concerns About Judicial Independence

The Comey case is viewed as the latest manifestation of ongoing tensions between the Trump administration and the American justice system. Critics worry that such prosecutions could be used as tools to suppress dissent, potentially undermining the independence and credibility of the judicial system.

As the case moves through the courts, it is expected to further fuel national debate about presidential power, free speech, and judicial fairness.
