Israel has launched airstrikes on Beirut's southern suburbs, putting an already fragile ceasefire agreement under severe strain. The military action comes at a critical juncture in ceasefire negotiations between Hezbollah and Israel, raising international concerns about a renewed escalation of conflict. The Washington Post reports that the strikes targeted areas in southern Beirut, a key Hezbollah stronghold.
