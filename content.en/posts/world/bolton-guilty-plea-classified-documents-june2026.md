## Summary

Former US National Security Adviser John Bolton has pleaded guilty to mishandling classified documents. He faces up to five years in prison and has agreed to pay a $2.25 million fine, according to prosecutors.

## Details

Bolton served as Trump's National Security Adviser from 2018 to 2020 and later became one of his most prominent Republican critics. The guilty plea relates to charges that he improperly retained classified documents at his private residence after leaving government service.

Under the plea agreement, Bolton will cooperate with prosecutors and pay the fine. Sentencing will be determined in the coming months.

## Analysis

The Bolton case underscores systemic vulnerabilities in how the US manages classified information across administrations. Coming after classified document controversies involving both Trump and Biden, it highlights the need for stronger post-tenure document handling protocols.
