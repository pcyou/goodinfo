## Bodies of Missing Italian Divers Found in Maldives

Divers have discovered the bodies of four missing Italian divers in a sea cave in the Maldives, according to CBS News.

The divers went missing while diving in Maldivian waters. After days of searching, rescue teams found their remains in a complex underwater cave system. The exact cause of the accident is under investigation.
