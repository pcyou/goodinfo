## [Brief]

According to The New York Times and CNN, a dangerous and what meteorologists call "no ordinary" heat dome is gripping the eastern United States. Washington DC, New York, Boston and other major cities have recorded extreme heat for a second consecutive day, with many locations breaking July temperature records. Central Park in New York soared above 100°F (38°C), with even higher heat index readings.

The extreme heat has directly impacted the ongoing World Cup tournament, with multiple match kickoffs pushed to evening hours. New York City's mayor advised residents to set thermostats to 78°F to ease grid strain, but the suggestion drew mockery from political opponents. Meanwhile, heat-related emergency calls and ER visits have surged across the New York area.

The National Weather Service warns the heat dome's impact will continue through the July 4 holiday weekend, advising the public to reduce outdoor activity and watch for heatstroke symptoms. This is the most severe heatwave to hit the eastern US since summer 2026 began.
