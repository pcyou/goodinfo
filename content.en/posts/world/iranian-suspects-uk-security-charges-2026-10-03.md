## Core Summary

According to Al Jazeera, UK prosecutors have formally charged two Iranian nationals over an alleged security plot targeting the Jewish community in Britain. The case traces back to leads obtained by UK counter-intelligence services through multiple international partners; after months of surveillance and investigation, British police arrested the suspects earlier this month and brought the prosecution. Once revealed, the case rapidly escalated into a major diplomatic dispute between London and Tehran: Iran's most senior diplomat in London was summoned, and the Foreign Secretary issued a strongly worded statement. British media underline that the incident highlights the new risks of Middle East tensions spilling into European cities and raises the bar for cross-Atlantic intelligence cooperation and community safety governance.
