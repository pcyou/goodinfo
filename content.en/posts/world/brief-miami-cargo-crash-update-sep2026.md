[Brief] BBC News reported on September 8 that new progress has been made in the investigation into the Boeing 767-300 cargo plane crash at Miami airport. Investigators confirmed that the five people killed were in two vehicles on the ground, and described the scene as "utter devastation." The cargo plane overshot the airport runway during landing, crashed into vehicles on the ground, and caught fire. The accident has already triggered broad discussion about runway safety buffer zone settings, and the US Federal Aviation Administration is reassessing related safety standards.
