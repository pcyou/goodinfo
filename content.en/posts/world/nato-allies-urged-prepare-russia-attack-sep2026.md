## US Urges NATO Allies to Prepare for Possible Russian Attack

**[Core Summary]** The United States formally urged NATO allies on September 2, 2026 to strengthen defensive preparations in response to intelligence suggesting Russia may launch military action against NATO member states. This unprecedented warning came after Germany's power grid was sabotaged and Leipzig airport was hit by drone strikes. EU and NATO leaders vowed to increase pressure on Russia, as European security situation deteriorates at an alarming pace.

According to USA Today, senior US State Department officials warned NATO allies in closed-door meetings that intelligence indicates Russia may take military action against NATO's eastern flank members in the near term. This warning prompted Poland and the Baltic states to urgently raise their alert levels and accelerate deployment of additional military forces.

### Timeline of Events

The crisis was triggered by a series of security incidents on September 1. The German government publicly accused Russian military intelligence (GRU) of deliberately sabotaging Brandenburg's power grid, causing brief power outages for thousands of households. The same day, Leipzig airport was hit by drone strikes, forcing dozens of flights to be delayed or canceled.

On September 2, European Commission President von der Leyen and NATO Secretary General Rutte issued a joint statement condemning Russia's "intimidation tactics" and vowing that "intimidation will not stop support for Ukraine." The US subsequently issued a higher-level security warning, urging all NATO allies to prepare their defenses.

### Military Deployment Developments

The Polish government has announced the second phase of its "Eastern Shield" program, including deploying 20,000 additional troops along the Polish-Russian border, along with more air defense systems and counter-drone equipment. Lithuania, Latvia, and Estonia have also announced extensions to their military service terms and requested permanent US armored brigade deployments.

NATO's Supreme Headquarters Allied Powers Europe is coordinating a large-scale military exercise expected to involve over 50,000 troops, designed to test allies' rapid response capabilities in the event of a Russian attack.

### Panoramic Perspective

The US public urging of allies to prepare for Russian attack marks a major strategic shift from "deterrence" to "combat readiness." The rarity of this statement lies in directly publishing intelligence assessments, breaking with the traditional "strategic ambiguity" approach.

From a military strategy perspective, with Russia bogged down in a protracted war of attrition in Ukraine, the possibility of a limited military operation against NATO's eastern flank does exist. Such a "testing attack" aims to verify the actual effectiveness of NATO's Article 5 collective defense clause while demonstrating to European civilians the vulnerability of the Western alliance. However, this strategic calculation carries extremely high risk—any miscalculation could trigger full-scale conflict.

From the perspective of European internal politics, this crisis is accelerating EU defense integration. French President recently revived proposals for a "European army," while Germany announced raising defense spending to 2.5% of GDP. However, divisions remain within the EU on defense autonomy—Eastern European countries prefer relying on US protection, while Western European nations pursue greater strategic autonomy.

### Multiple Perspectives

**US Position**: State Department spokesperson emphasized that US commitment to NATO Article 5 is "ironclad," but allies must prepare their own defenses. The US will provide necessary intelligence support and military aid but will not substitute for European countries' defense responsibilities.

**EU Stance**: The joint statement by von der Leyen and Rutte shows EU and NATO maintain a unified position on Russia. The statement emphasized "intimidation will not work" and pledged continued support for Ukraine in resisting Russian aggression.

**Russian Response**: Kremlin spokesperson denied all accusations, calling them "baseless hysteria," and counter-claimed that NATO's military buildup along Russia's borders is the "real security threat."

**Expert Analysis**: Former NATO Supreme Allied Commander Europe noted the current situation is the most dangerous since the Cold War ended. All parties are engaged in "brinkmanship" gaming, where any miscalculation could trigger catastrophic consequences. He called for restoring diplomatic dialogue channels and establishing crisis management mechanisms.

This crisis once again highlights the fragility of European security architecture. Under the shadow of nuclear weapons, how to resolve the crisis through diplomatic means and avoid military conflict will be a key test of leaders' wisdom.
