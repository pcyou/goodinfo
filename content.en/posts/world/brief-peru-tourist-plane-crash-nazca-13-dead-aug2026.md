**Analysis**: The Nazca Lines are a UNESCO World Heritage Site attracting tourists for aerial viewing. This accident has reignited debates on tourism aviation safety oversight. Peru's civil aviation authority announced enhanced inspections of older aircraft.

**Perspectives**: Local media reported the aircraft was older with questionable maintenance records. Tourism operators worry about impact on Peru's recovering tourism sector.
