## Lebanon Reports 13 Killed in Israeli Strikes, Including Two Paramedics

**Summary:** Lebanon's health ministry reports that the latest Israeli airstrikes have killed 13 people, including two paramedics, as tensions in the Middle East continue to escalate.

---

According to BBC, Lebanon's health ministry says the latest Israeli airstrikes have killed 13 people, including two paramedics who were on their way to respond to an emergency.

The strikes hit southern Lebanon and represent one of the deadliest single attacks in recent weeks. The paramedics were targeted while en route to the scene, drawing strong condemnation from international humanitarian organizations.

The International Committee of the Red Cross issued a statement calling on all parties to respect international humanitarian law and protect medical and rescue personnel.

The attack comes amid escalating tensions in the Middle East, with the ongoing conflict between Israel and Hezbollah in Lebanon already causing thousands of casualties. The international community has called on both sides to show restraint and prevent further escalation.
