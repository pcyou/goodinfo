## Zelensky's Ex-Chief of Staff in Court as Ukraine Corruption Probe Escalates

Ukraine's former presidential chief of staff appeared in court as part of an escalating corruption investigation, facing money laundering allegations. The case highlights Kyiv's ongoing anti-corruption campaign amid the war and has drawn significant public attention in Ukraine.
