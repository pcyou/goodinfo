[Brief] ## Arrest Operation

Australian authorities have arrested three women returning from Syria who are linked to the Islamic State (ISIS). The case highlights security challenges posed by returning foreign fighters.

## Security Review

The Australian Federal Police and national security agencies conducted long-term monitoring of these women. The arrests were executed upon their entry to prevent potential security threats.

## Policy Impact

The case has reignited debate over how to handle citizens returning from conflict zones. The Australian government emphasized national security priority and will pursue criminal charges against those involved.
