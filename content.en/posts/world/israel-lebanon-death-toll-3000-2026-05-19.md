Israeli airstrikes on Lebanon have killed more than 3,000 people, marking a devastating escalation in the conflict with Hezbollah that has been ongoing since March this year.

**Conflict Background**

The hostilities center on the confrontation between Israel and the Lebanese armed group Hezbollah. Since March, cross-border fire has escalated from initial skirmishes into a large-scale military campaign.

**Civilian Casualties**

Lebanese official statistics show that the death toll includes a significant number of civilians. Airstrikes have destroyed residential neighborhoods, infrastructure, and medical facilities, displacing hundreds of thousands of people.

**Diplomatic Deadlock**

Despite repeated international calls for a ceasefire, negotiations between Israel and Hezbollah remain at an impasse. The two sides have fundamental disagreements on key conditions, including border security arrangements and the disarmament of armed groups.

A UN envoy is mediating between the parties, but no breakthrough is currently visible. Humanitarian organizations warn that civilian casualties could climb further as the conflict continues.
