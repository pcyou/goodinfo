categories: [world]
tags: []
---

Trump has announced a temporary pause to "Project Freedom" — the US effort to guide vessels through the Strait of Hormuz amid escalating tensions with Iran.

The pause may indicate a reassessment of US military deployment strategy in the Middle East. The Strait of Hormuz is one of the world's most critical oil transit routes, with approximately one-fifth of global oil supply passing through daily.

Iran has previously threatened retaliation against US escort operations, adding complexity to the regional situation. Analysts suggest the pause could create a window for diplomatic negotiations.
