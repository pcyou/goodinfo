## Iran Retaliates After US Strikes Kill Four at Wedding Party

Iranian state media has announced that retaliatory missile and drone attacks on US targets have begun, marking a dramatic escalation in tensions between the two nations.

According to BBC reporting, Iranian news agencies issued official statements confirming the start of retaliatory strikes against US military facilities in the Middle East. The statement described the attacks as a legitimate act of self-defense against "American aggression."

The crisis was triggered by US military strikes that reportedly hit a wedding party, killing four people. The incident sparked widespread outrage in Iran, with the country's leadership vowing a "severe and decisive" response.

This development comes just hours after the two nations experienced their first direct military exchange since diplomatic relations were severed in 1979. President Trump had vowed "severe strikes" against Iran, and Iran's retaliatory statement means the situation has escalated at an alarming pace.

Middle Eastern nations are closely monitoring developments, with Gulf states concerned that expanded conflict could affect regional stability and global energy supplies. The UN Secretary-General has called for maximum restraint from both sides.
