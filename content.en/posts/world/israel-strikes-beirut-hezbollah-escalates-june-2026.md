Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has ordered strikes on the Beirut suburb of Dahieh, a Hezbollah stronghold, in response to attacks on Israeli civilians. The conflict between Hezbollah and Israel is further escalating, worsening security conditions across the Middle East. Previous exchanges of fire have already caused significant casualties and infrastructure damage.
