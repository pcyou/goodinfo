## Core Summary

The United Arab Emirates formally accused Iran on August 14 of launching attacks in the Strait of Hormuz, marking the latest escalation in regional tensions. US Vice President Vance immediately responded, stating that ensuring cheap oil and gas supply is 'goal number one.' This accusation comes as US-Iran nuclear negotiations remain stalled, further complicating the security situation in the Middle East. The Strait of Hormuz, as the world's most critical energy transport corridor, handles approximately 21 million barrels of crude oil daily, and any conflict will directly impact global energy supply.

## Event Details

According to multiple international media reports, the UAE government issued a statement accusing Iran of carrying out 'attack behavior' against UAE vessels in the Strait of Hormuz. The statement claims these attacks occurred in international waters, seriously violating international maritime law. The UAE called for an international investigation and demanded that Iran immediately cease such provocative behavior.

US Vice President Vance, in response to the incident, stated that ensuring cheap oil and gas supply is America's 'goal number one.' He warned that the US will take all necessary measures to protect its interests and ally security in the Middle East. The US Fifth Fleet has already increased patrol intensity in the Persian Gulf region.

Iran denied the UAE's accusations, calling them 'baseless.' Iran's Foreign Ministry spokesperson stated that Iran remains committed to maintaining security and stability in the Strait of Hormuz, accusing certain countries of attempting to divert attention by creating tensions.

## Panoramic Perspective

The Strait of Hormuz, as the strategic channel connecting the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman, is the 'choke point' of the global energy supply chain. Approximately 21 million barrels of crude oil pass through this narrow waterway daily, accounting for nearly one-third of global seaborne oil trade. Any attacks or blockades targeting this strait will immediately trigger violent fluctuations in global energy markets.

The background of this incident is the continued deterioration of US-Iran relations. The Trump administration has reimposed a 'maximum pressure' policy on Iran, demanding that Tehran completely abandon uranium enrichment activities. Iran insists its right to peaceful nuclear energy is non-negotiable and warns it will take all necessary measures to safeguard national sovereignty. This confrontation has made the Middle East security situation increasingly tense.

From an economic perspective, any conflict in the Strait of Hormuz will produce cascading effects. International oil prices could surge in the short term, triggering global inflationary pressure. European and Asian energy import countries will bear the brunt, facing serious supply shortage risks. Additionally, shipping insurance costs will rise substantially, further pushing up global trade costs.

Regarding geopolitical landscape, this incident may accelerate camp division in the Middle East. Gulf Arab states may further strengthen military cooperation with the US, while Iran may deepen strategic relations with Russia and other countries. The solidification of this confrontation pattern will make regional peace even more distant.

## Multiple Perspectives

**UAE Position**: The UAE government emphasized that this attack represents a serious challenge to international law and regional security. The UAE calls for decisive international action to stop Iran's 'provocative behavior' and protect the free passage of international waterways.

**US Official Attitude**: Vice President Vance's statement shows Washington prioritizes energy security. The US may intensify sanctions against Iran and increase military presence in the Persian Gulf to deter further attacks.

**Iran Response**: Iran denies all accusations and blames the UAE and US for attempting to create tensions to cover up other issues. Iran emphasizes its military forces are solely for defensive purposes and will not proactively initiate conflict.

**International Energy Market Analysis**: Energy analysts warn that tensions in the Strait of Hormuz have already led to increased volatility in international oil prices. If the situation further deteriorates, oil prices could break through the $100 per barrel mark, causing serious damage to global economic recovery.
