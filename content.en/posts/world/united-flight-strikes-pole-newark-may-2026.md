## United Airlines Flight Involved in Landing Incident

A United Airlines Boeing carrying 221 passengers struck a light pole and a truck on the nearby New Jersey Turnpike while landing at Newark Liberty International Airport on Sunday.

### What Happened

According to officials, the aircraft experienced an anomaly during its approach, with the wingtip hitting a light pole. Debris then fell onto the adjacent turnpike, striking a truck. The airport immediately activated emergency response procedures.

### Passenger Safety

Fortunately, all 221 passengers and crew members were safely evacuated. No serious injuries were reported aboard the aircraft. The condition of the truck driver is being assessed.

### Flight Details

The flight was a United Airlines domestic route operated by a Boeing 737-series aircraft. Following the incident, Newark airport temporarily suspended operations on part of its runway, causing delays to subsequent flights.

### Investigation Launched

The Federal Aviation Administration has opened an investigation into the cause of the incident. Preliminary analysis suggests a possible height misjudgment during the aircraft landing approach.
