Colombia presidential election results have triggered sharply divided reactions domestically. Supporters celebrated while opponents expressed shock and skepticism. The outcome may significantly influence the country future economic policy and social governance. International observers noted the process was orderly overall, though turnout in some areas fell below expectations.
