## Core Summary

Romania's Ministry of Defense announced on August 20 that the military discovered and destroyed a suspicious marine drone in waters near the Neptun Deep natural gas exploration project in the Black Sea. According to Reuters, this is another security incident in Romania's Black Sea waters recently, drawing widespread attention to the protection of critical energy infrastructure.

## Event Summary

Romania's Defense Minister stated that military radar detected an unidentified aircraft moving toward the Neptun Deep project area within Romania's Black Sea exclusive economic zone. The military immediately dispatched fighter jets to destroy it. The source and purpose of the drone remain unclear, and relevant departments are conducting an investigation.

## Background Analysis

Neptun Deep is a major Black Sea natural gas development project cooperation between Romania and OMV, holding strategic significance for Romania's energy security. Security tensions in the Black Sea region have remained high recently, and this incident once again highlights potential threats to critical energy infrastructure.
