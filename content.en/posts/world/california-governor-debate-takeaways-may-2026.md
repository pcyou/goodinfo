## Five Takeaways From California Governor Debate

The final televised California gubernatorial debate featured sharp exchanges between Democratic and Republican candidates over housing affordability and tax policy, the New York Times and CNN reported.

Democratic candidates clashed with each other in what was described as a fiery and at times contentious debate. Indiana and Ohio also held primary elections on the same day.

Latest polls show Democratic and Republican candidates leading the race. The debate is seen as a key indicator ahead of the November general election, with housing costs and taxes emerging as top voter concerns.
