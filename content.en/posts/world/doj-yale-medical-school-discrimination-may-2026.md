## DOJ Says Yale Medical School Discriminated Against Asian, White Applicants

The US Department of Justice has found that Yale School of Medicine discriminated against Asian and White applicants in its admissions process, marking another major federal intervention in university admissions policies following the Supreme Court's ruling on affirmative action.

The DOJ said Yale's admissions practices unfairly disadvantaged Asian and White applicants. The university has not yet issued a detailed response.
