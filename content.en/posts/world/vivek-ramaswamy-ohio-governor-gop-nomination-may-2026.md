## Vivek Ramaswamy Wins GOP Nomination for Ohio Governor

BBC reports that entrepreneur and former presidential candidate Vivek Ramaswamy has won the Republican nomination for governor of Ohio.

Ramaswamy briefly ran for the 2024 Republican presidential nomination before dropping out and endorsing Trump. This Ohio gubernatorial race marks his return to electoral politics.

### Ohio Political Landscape

As a key swing state, Ohio's governor race could influence the 2026 midterm landscape. Ramaswamy's business background and political trajectory make this a closely watched contest.

### Related Race

Meanwhile, Sherrod Brown won the Democratic Senate nomination in Ohio, setting up a closely contested Senate race in the state.
