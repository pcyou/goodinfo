## Brief] Suspected Sabotage Disrupts Dutch Rail Network as Foreign Objects Found on Tracks at Multiple Locations

The incident highlights security threats facing European critical infrastructure and will shape the next round of policy reviews on rail network security across the Netherlands and Europe.
