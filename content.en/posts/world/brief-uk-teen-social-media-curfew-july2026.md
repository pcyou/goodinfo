The UK government recently proposed a new policy to implement a social media curfew for older teenagers, restricting their use of social platforms during certain hours. The policy allows teens to opt out of restrictions, but this flexibility has drawn criticism from campaigners who argue the approach is too piecemeal to effectively protect young people from online harm.
