## Breaking

U.S. Central Command announced early on May 8 that American forces launched "self-defense strikes" against Iranian targets in the Strait of Hormuz, following what it described as attacks on U.S. warships transiting the strategic waterway.

## The Incident

According to CBS News, the strikes came after U.S. naval vessels came under fire while conducting escort operations in the strait. The Strait of Hormuz is one of the world's most critical oil transit chokepoints, handling roughly a fifth of global petroleum shipments.

President Trump stated that the ceasefire between Washington and Tehran "remains in place," characterizing the strikes as a defensive response. Iran, however, accused the U.S. of violating the truce, alleging that American forces targeted an oil tanker and struck coastal areas.

## Market Impact

Oil prices surged following the escalation. Brent crude climbed further, intensifying fears of global energy supply disruption.

U.S. stock futures fluctuated amid the geopolitical uncertainty, with traders also weighing the upcoming April jobs report.

## International Response

China called for the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz and resolution of the Iran issue through dialogue. Analysts warn the clash could further complicate already fragile U.S.-Iran negotiations, increasing pressure on diplomatic mediation efforts worldwide.
