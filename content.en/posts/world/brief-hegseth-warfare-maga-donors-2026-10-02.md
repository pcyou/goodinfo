## Hegseth Puts Future of Warfare in Hands of Major Trump Donors

The Guardian reports that US Defense Secretary Hegseth has placed leadership over several key future-warfare technologies in the hands of companies closely tied to major Trump political donors, drawing concern from military officials and members of Congress. The development reflects a further politicization of the US defense industrial base, with potential implications for military modernization and procurement fairness.

According to the report, priority programs covering drones, AI-enabled combat systems, cyber weapons, and next-generation munitions have skewed toward a small number of firms with close ties to the Trump camp. Some current and former Defense Department officials have expressed concern that this short-term political tilt could erode real combat capability over the medium term and distort competition within the defense industrial base.

Several Democratic members of Congress have begun calling for hearings on the procurement decisions, asking the Pentagon to publicly disclose selection criteria and conflict-of-interest safeguards. The trajectory of the matter merits continued attention.
