## Core Summary

Todd Blanche, Trump's attorney general nominee, has formally withdrawn the controversial $1.8 billion "anti-weaponization" fund plan. The fund could have been used to compensate people prosecuted over the US Capitol riot. The move is seen as a political compromise to secure Senate confirmation, after two Republican senators indicated they would back his nomination.
