## Linda McMahon Defends Dismantling the Education Department

US Education Secretary Linda McMahon defended plans to dismantle the federal Department of Education at a congressional hearing, arguing that education functions should be devolved to state and local governments.

The proposal faces strong opposition from educators and lawmakers who warn it could harm educational equity for disadvantaged students.
