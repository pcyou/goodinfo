## UK Prime Minister Starmer Formally Resigns

UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer formally announced his resignation on June 22, marking the end of his approximately two-year tenure after leading the Labour Party to a landslide victory in 2024 that ended 14 years of Conservative rule.

Starmer delivered a brief statement at 10 Downing Street confirming he would submit his resignation to the King. In the preceding weeks, controversies had mounted around his government, with multiple Labour MPs publicly calling for his departure.

Although Starmer won the 2024 election by a decisive margin, his administration struggled to deliver on campaign promises in economic recovery, NHS reform, and immigration policy. The Labour Party suffered major losses in May local elections, intensifying pressure for his resignation.

The Labour Party is now accelerating leadership election preparations, with several cabinet members seen as potential successors. Starmer will continue as caretaker PM until a new leader is chosen.
