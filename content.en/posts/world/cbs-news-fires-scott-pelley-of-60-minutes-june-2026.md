According to The New York Times, CBS News has announced the firing of veteran 60 Minutes journalist Scott Pelley, a decision that has sparked strong reactions in the American journalism community. As one of the program's core correspondents, Pelley's departure is seen as a sign of major internal personnel changes at CBS. Industry observers speculate that this move may be related to the network's recent strategic adjustments.
