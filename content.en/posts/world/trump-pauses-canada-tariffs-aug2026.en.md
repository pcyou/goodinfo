## Summary

President Trump announced on August 19 a three-day pause on imposing 50% tariffs on Canadian goods, stating that countries are "close to a deal." The decision provides temporary relief amid escalating US-Canada trade tensions, though markets remain cautious about the final terms.

The pause follows emergency consultations between the two countries. Canada has not yet issued an official response, but business leaders have welcomed the extension of negotiation time.
