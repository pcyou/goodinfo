### 1. US GPS Systems Experience Unprecedented Positioning Errors 🛰️

**Significance: ★★★★★**

In November 2025, GPS systems across the United States experienced positioning errors of up to 33 feet (approximately 10 meters), a phenomenon scientists had never previously recorded. After months of investigation, research teams confirmed the anomaly was directly caused by multiple massive coronal mass ejections (CMEs).

**Impact Analysis**: GPS systems are deeply integrated into critical sectors including aviation navigation, financial transaction timestamps, and power grid synchronization. This event exposes the vulnerability of modern technological infrastructure to space weather and will drive countries to accelerate development of advanced space weather warning systems and multi-source fusion positioning solutions.
