A shooting at the Islamic Center of San Diego on May 19, 2026 left three people dead, including a security guard hailed as a hero. Both suspects were also killed in the incident.

According to police, the attack occurred at the Islamic Center of San Diego. The security guard was killed while attempting to stop the gunmen and has been celebrated as a hero by the local community.

The incident has raised concerns about hate crimes. San Diego Muslim community leaders have called for enhanced security at religious sites and demanded a thorough law enforcement investigation.
