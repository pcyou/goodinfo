# London Terror Attack: Two Jewish Men Stabbed in Golders Green, Police Declare Terrorist Incident

**LONDON, April 29, 2026** — A knife attack in the Golders Green area of north London left two Jewish men wounded on Tuesday. Police have declared the incident a "terrorist event" and arrested a 45-year-old male suspect.

## The Attack

According to the BBC, the assault occurred in Golders Green, one of London's largest Jewish communities. CCTV footage shows the suspect suddenly attacking two Jewish men on the street with a knife. Both victims were rushed to hospital with serious but non-life-threatening injuries.

The Metropolitan Police Counter Terrorism Command confirmed the attack is being formally classified as a terrorist incident. A police spokesperson said the suspect attempted to stab officers during his arrest and was ultimately subdued using a Taser.

## Suspect Background

Police stated that the 45-year-old suspect has a history of serious violence and mental health issues. Investigators are conducting a thorough probe into his motives. The extremist group HAYI has claimed responsibility for the attack, though police have not yet confirmed any direct link between the group and the suspect.

## Political Response

The UK Prime Minister issued a statement following the attack, strongly condemning the violence targeting the Jewish community. "There is no place for this kind of hatred and prejudice-fueled violence in Britain," the Prime Minister said. "We will fully support the Metropolitan Police and counter-terrorism departments in their investigation."

The Home Secretary added that the government would review community security measures to ensure Jewish communities and other minority groups receive adequate protection.

## A Broader Wave of Antisemitism

The attack comes amid a wave of incidents targeting Jewish targets across the UK. The Washington Post reports that multiple attacks on Jewish targets are being investigated for possible links to Iran, raising concerns about how geopolitical conflicts are impacting domestic security in Britain.

Data from the Community Security Trust (CST) shows that antisemitic incidents in the UK rose approximately 35% in the first quarter of 2026 compared to the same period last year. Community leaders have called on the government to take stronger action against the rising tide of antisemitism.

## Community Response

Residents of the Golders Green Jewish community expressed shock and anger at the attack. A local rabbi posted on social media: "Our community has been attacked today, but we will not be defeated by fear. We will continue to stand together and protect our faith and homes."

The Mayor of London visited the scene and announced increased police deployment in the area. Police have urged anyone with relevant information to come forward and assist with the investigation.

The incident has reignited discussions about Britain's counter-terrorism capabilities and community security. As international tensions persist, balancing national security with community freedoms remains a critical challenge for the UK government.
