## Core Summary

According to an exclusive report by Axios, the Trump administration is developing a post-war reconstruction plan for the Middle East, covering Gaza reconstruction, regional security architecture, and economic recovery. The plan is still in its early stages and has not been officially announced.

## Event Details

The report indicates the plan is being jointly drafted by the State Department and the Office of the Middle East Envoy. It is expected to provide a framework for infrastructure reconstruction in Gaza while advancing normalization of relations between Israel and Arab states. Sources reveal the plan includes proposals for an international reconstruction fund and deployment of multinational peacekeeping forces.

## Reactions

Israel has not yet issued an official response. The Palestinian side has taken a cautious stance, emphasizing that any reconstruction plan must be based on the Palestinian people's right to self-determination. Multiple European nations have expressed interest, noting that America's role in the Middle East peace process remains critical.
