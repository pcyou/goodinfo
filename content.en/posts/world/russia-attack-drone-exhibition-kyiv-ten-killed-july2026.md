## Core Summary

According to BBC News, Russian forces used ballistic missiles to strike a drone exhibition venue near Kyiv on Thursday, killing at least ten people. The attack occurred during daytime when prominent members of Ukraine's defense industry were visiting the event. This marks another serious attack on defense-related facilities in the ongoing Russia-Ukraine conflict.

## Event Details

The strike hit an exhibition center near Kyiv during a drone technology expo. According to eyewitnesses, multiple ballistic missiles precisely hit the venue around midday, causing massive explosions. Several key figures from Ukraine's defense industry were present at the time.

Ukrainian authorities stated that the attack demonstrates Russian military intelligence capabilities regarding Ukraine's defense industrial facilities. The use of ballistic missiles indicates this was a carefully planned strike operation rather than random bombing. Rescue teams arrived quickly at the scene to conduct search and rescue operations.

## Panoramic Perspective

This attack reflects profound changes in modern warfare. Drone technology has become a critical combat element in the Russia-Ukraine conflict, with both sides increasing investment in drone research and production. Russia's choice to target a drone exhibition aims to undermine Ukraine's defense industrial base and morale.

From a strategic perspective, this attack reflects Russia's attempt to curb Ukraine's defense industry development through precision strikes. However, this tactic may backfire, prompting Ukraine and its Western allies to accelerate defense technology cooperation and capacity building. The international community has condemned attacks on civilian exhibition facilities and called for compliance with international humanitarian law.

## Multiple Perspectives

Ukrainian officials strongly condemned the attack, calling it a "terrorist act" against the defense industry, and vowed to accelerate drone production capacity. Western allies expressed support for Ukraine and promised additional air defense system assistance.

Russia has not issued an official statement on the attack. Some Russian military analysts believe striking Ukraine's defense industrial facilities represents legitimate military targets. International observers worry such attacks could escalate conflict intensity and hinder peace negotiations.

The UN spokesperson reiterated that any attacks on civilians and civilian facilities violate international law, calling on all parties to exercise restraint.
