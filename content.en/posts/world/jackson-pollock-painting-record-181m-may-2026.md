An abstract expressionist masterpiece by Jackson Pollock sold for $181 million on May 19, 2026, setting a new auction record.

The sale far exceeded estimates, reflecting the continued strength of the top-tier art market. Pollock is renowned for his unique "drip painting" technique, and his works are highly sought after in the art market.

Art market experts note that this record-breaking sale demonstrates strong collector demand for 20th-century American abstract expressionist works.
