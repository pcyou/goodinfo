**[Summary]** The United States has launched military strikes against Iranian targets in response to a US military helicopter being shot down over the Strait of Hormuz. President Trump accused Iran of the attack and vowed retaliation. Iran subsequently launched broad counterattacks, sharply escalating tensions in the Middle East.

## The Incident

According to BBC and multiple international media reports, US President Trump accused Iran of shooting down a US military helicopter over the Strait of Hormuz. In response, the US military launched strikes against targets inside Iran.

Iran subsequently launched widespread retaliatory attacks. According to The Guardian's live coverage, Iran's counterattacks covered multiple target areas. CBS News also confirmed the US retaliatory strikes.

The Strait of Hormuz is one of the world's most critical oil transit chokepoints, with massive volumes of crude oil passing through daily. The escalation of military tensions in the region has drawn widespread international concern, particularly regarding potential impacts on global energy supply and shipping security.

This is one of the most serious military confrontations between the US and Iran in recent years. Relations between the two countries have been strained for a long time due to nuclear issues, economic sanctions, and competition for regional influence.

## Broader Perspective

The escalation of US-Iran military conflict has far-reaching implications for global geopolitics. First, the Strait of Hormuz, through which about one-fifth of the world's oil passes, directly affects the stability of global energy markets. If the conflict continues to escalate, international oil prices could face severe volatility, impacting global economic growth.

From a regional security perspective, the Middle East is inherently complex — Israel, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, and other regional powers each have different strategic interests. Direct US-Iran military confrontation could trigger chain reactions, prompting regional allies to reassess their security strategies.

For the global economy, energy price uncertainty is the most direct transmission mechanism. If Iran blocks or threatens freedom of navigation in the Strait of Hormuz, global oil supply chains will face severe challenges. Additionally, heightened risk aversion could drive gold and the US dollar higher while pressuring emerging market assets.

International diplomatic mediation will be key to de-escalating tensions. The United Nations, European Union, and regional organizations may need to play more active mediation roles to prevent the conflict from spiraling out of control.

## Different Viewpoints

**The US government** stated that the military strikes were a legitimate response to Iranian provocation, aimed at maintaining US military presence and freedom of navigation in the Gulf region.

**Iran** accused the US strikes of being a blatant violation of international law and vowed to continue defending its interests and regional influence.

**International analysts** broadly agree that military means cannot fundamentally resolve the structural contradictions between the US and Iran. Only through diplomatic channels to restore dialogue can long-term regional stability be achieved.

**Regional countries** expressed varying levels of concern about the escalation, with some calling for both sides to exercise restraint and prevent the conflict from spreading further and affecting peace and prosperity across the Middle East.
