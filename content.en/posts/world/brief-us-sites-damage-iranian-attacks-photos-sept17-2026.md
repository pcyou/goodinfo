## Executive Summary

Satellite imagery obtained jointly by the BBC and CBS News shows significant damage to multiple U.S. military bases in the Middle East from recent Iranian strikes. Losses include a fully destroyed Air Force aircraft, several severely damaged buildings, and large areas of runway damage.

## Key Details

The satellite photos reveal damage more extensive than previously disclosed. Several buildings show clear penetration damage, at least one fixed-wing aircraft on the tarmac is a total loss, and parts of some facilities may take months to repair. Analysts say the public release of the imagery is likely aimed at Congress and allies to illustrate the real security pressures on U.S. forces in the region and to add a new variable to ongoing defense budget negotiations.
