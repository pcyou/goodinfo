## Core Summary

On the 10th anniversary of the UK Brexit referendum, multiple media outlets published retrospective analyses. Economists point out that Brexit has significantly hampered UK economic growth over the past decade.

## Event Details

According to The New York Times, UK economic growth has clearly slowed since the 2016 referendum. Trade volume with the EU has fallen approximately 15 percent compared to pre-Brexit levels.

## Panoramic Analysis

The Brexit decade review offers important lessons for all economies facing major policy shifts. The UK case demonstrates that the costs of leaving a large economic union far exceeded expectations during the referendum.
