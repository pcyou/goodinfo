According to Deadline, Mortal Kombat II is projected to gross over $40 million in its opening weekend, going head-to-head with The Devil Wears Prada 2.

As a sequel to the classic fighting game adaptation, Mortal Kombat II has drawn significant interest from gaming fans and action movie audiences. Early reviews praise its visual effects and fight choreography.

The Devil Wears Prada 2, with its returning cast and buzz, is performing well among female demographics. The contrasting films sharing the same release window reflect the diversification of current cinema market demand.
