## Summary

Ukraine's former defence minister, who was recently dismissed by President Zelensky, publicly called for presidential elections on August 19, in a rare move that has sent shockwaves through the country's political establishment. The statement is seen as a direct challenge to the Zelensky government and reflects growing political divisions amid the ongoing conflict.

The former minister argued that Ukrainians deserve the right to choose their leadership as the war enters a new phase, and questioned the transparency of the government's military and political decision-making.

Ukraine's parliament was divided in response, with some members supporting the call while others criticized it as "ill-timed" during wartime. Under Ukrainian law, elections during martial law require parliamentary approval and legal amendments.
