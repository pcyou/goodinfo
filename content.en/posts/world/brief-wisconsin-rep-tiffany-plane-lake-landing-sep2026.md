## Core Summary

NBC News reported on September 13 that the small plane carrying Wisconsin Republican Representative Tom Tiffany suffered a mechanical failure in flight and was forced to make an emergency landing on a lake. The congressman sustained only minor injuries in the incident and was transferred by emergency responders to a nearby hospital for observation. After the event, Tiffany's office publicly thanked first responders and the local community for their rapid response and emphasised that he would continue to perform his duties. The specific technical cause of the incident has been referred to the U.S. Federal Aviation Administration for investigation.
