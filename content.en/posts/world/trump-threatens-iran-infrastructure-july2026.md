## Core Summary

According to CNN and BBC, US President Donald Trump issued a stark warning that the United States will strike Iran's bridges and power plants each time Iranian forces fire on ships in the Strait of Hormuz. This statement marks an escalation from diplomatic confrontation to direct military threats, raising international concerns about a broader Middle East conflict.

## Event Details

### Presidential Statement

President Trump made clear in a public statement that the US will implement proportional retaliation for any Iranian attacks on international shipping in the Strait of Hormuz. He emphasized that every attack on global energy routes will be met with corresponding consequences, and the US will not tolerate threats to energy supply security.

### Strait Crisis Background

The Strait of Hormuz is one of the world's most critical oil transit chokepoints, with approximately one-fifth of global oil supply passing through daily. Recent Iranian military actions against international commercial vessels have caused shipping insurance costs to surge and forced multiple oil tankers to reroute.

### Military Deployment Adjustments

The US Department of Defense has announced additional military deployments to the region, including extra carrier strike groups and air defense systems. US Central Command stated it will take all necessary measures to protect international shipping freedom and regional ally security.

## Panoramic Perspective

This crisis has direct impacts on global energy markets. International oil prices have risen over 15 percent in the past week, with analysts warning that further escalation could push prices above $120 per barrel. European and Asian energy-importing nations face supply disruption risks, with governments urgently assessing alternative supply routes.

From a geopolitical perspective, this event is reshaping Middle East security architecture. Gulf Arab states face pressure to choose sides, while Israel closely monitors developments. China, as the largest energy importer from the region, faces scrutiny over its diplomatic stance.

International law experts have questioned Trump's threats, arguing that strikes on civilian infrastructure may violate international humanitarian law. The UN Secretary-General has called for restraint and diplomatic resolution.

## Multiple Perspectives

**US Government Position**: White House spokespersons emphasized this is a necessary deterrent to maintain international shipping freedom, stating the US will not stand by while global energy supplies are threatened.

**Iran's Response**: Iran's Foreign Ministry condemned the US threats as "war provocation," reaffirming its legitimate rights in international waters and stating readiness to respond to any aggression.

**European Allies' Stance**: French and German Foreign Ministers issued a joint statement calling on all parties to avoid actions that could lead to loss of control, emphasizing the importance of diplomatic solutions.

**Market Reaction**: The International Energy Agency convened an emergency meeting to discuss releasing strategic petroleum reserves. The global shipping industry called for an international escort mechanism in the Strait of Hormuz.

**Regional Concerns**: Saudi Arabia and the UAE expressed serious concern, worried that escalation will affect regional stability and economic development.
