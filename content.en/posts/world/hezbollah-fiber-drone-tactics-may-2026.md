## BBC Analysis Reveals Hezbollah's Evolving Drone Tactics

BBC Verify has analyzed dozens of videos showing Hezbollah's use of fiber-optic guided drones against Israeli targets, revealing significant evolution in the group's drone warfare capabilities.

**Tactical Evolution**

Fiber-optic guided drones are connected to operators via physical cables, making them immune to electronic jamming. Analysis shows Hezbollah is not only deploying these drones but continuously improving their operational methods and attack patterns — from simple reconnaissance to precision strikes on specific targets, evolving faster than Israeli military intelligence anticipated.

**Ceasefire Impact**

Despite the US-announced ceasefire extension, exchanges between Israel and Hezbollah continue. Six people were killed in an Israeli strike on Lebanon in the past 24 hours. Hezbollah's enhanced drone capabilities mean the security situation remains fragile even within the ceasefire framework.
