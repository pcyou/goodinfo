## Brief

According to Reuters, Denmark has stated that under a recently concluded Greenland-related agreement, NATO shares responsibility for Arctic security. The remarks further clarify NATO's strategic role and multilateral coordination framework in the Arctic, while injecting new political momentum into the trilateral cooperation mechanism among Denmark, the United States and NATO on Arctic affairs. Analysts have noted that the Arctic's strategic value in geopolitics, shipping-lane security, resource development and military deployment continues to rise, and the agreement and the related remarks are an important reference point for the Arctic governance landscape and for transatlantic security coordination. The development once again highlights the continued evolution of Arctic issues from a regional matter to an item on the international security agenda, and the related trends warrant continued attention.
