## Core Summary

A bomb blast at a cafe in Damascus, Syria's capital, has killed nine people. The explosion occurred near the Ministry of Justice, a major government building. Syrian state media reported the incident, but no group has claimed responsibility so far.

## Event Details

According to Syrian state news agency, the explosion occurred at a cafe in central Damascus. The cafe is located just hundreds of meters from the Ministry of Justice, in an area dense with government institutions. After the blast, Syrian security forces quickly cordoned off the scene, and rescue personnel transported the injured to nearby hospitals.

Syrian officials have not yet released findings on the cause of the explosion. Preliminary reports suggest the explosive device may have been placed inside the cafe in advance. Witnesses described the blast as powerful enough to shatter windows in nearby buildings.

No group has claimed responsibility for the incident. Syrian security departments have launched a comprehensive investigation and strengthened security measures around government buildings throughout the capital.
