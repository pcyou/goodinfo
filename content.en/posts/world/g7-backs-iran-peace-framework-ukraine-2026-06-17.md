## Core Summary

The Group of Seven leaders issued a joint communique at their Canadian summit formally endorsing the peace framework agreement being negotiated between the United States and Iran. The move provides crucial international backing for a deal facing significant domestic skepticism in Washington. Ukraine was reintroduced as a core summit topic on the final day.

## Event Details

**Joint Statement**: G7 leaders declared support for the "core principles" of the US-Iran framework, emphasizing diplomatic resolution serves global interests. The Canadian Prime Minister stated the deal aligns with all member nations' vision for Middle East stability.

**Framework Details**: A 14-point draft memorandum obtained by Bloomberg outlines restrictions on nuclear activities, a sanctions relief timeline, and regional security arrangements. Reports indicate eight unresolved issues remain including verification mechanisms and sequencing of sanctions removal.

**Ukraine Returns**: The Guardian reports multiple European leaders insisted on bringing Ukraine back into discussions. President Trump described his meeting with Zelenskyy as "productive" and hinted at pushing new peace talks. Reuters quoted sources saying Trump emphasized "I am the boss" in closed sessions.

**Domestic Pressure**: Despite G7 endorsement, the deal faces headwinds at home. The Wall Street Journal noted congressional approval remains uncertain with some lawmakers questioning enforceability.

## Analytical Perspective

The G7 endorsement reflects deep anxiety among Western allies about Middle East instability's impact on global energy markets. Brent crude has traded above eighty dollars per barrel during heightened tensions, directly affecting energy-import-dependent economies in Europe and Japan.

For the Trump administration, the joint statement serves as diplomatic ammunition against domestic critics. However, it also means G7 members have staked political capital on the deal's success. Ukraine's return to the agenda signals European allies' successful diplomatic push to prevent the conflict from being overshadowed.

## Diverse Viewpoints

**Supporters**: Canada and France emphasized diplomacy over confrontation. France's Foreign Ministry stated any agreement reducing regional tensions deserves support.

**Skeptics**: The Wall Street Journal argued the enforcement mechanism lacks sufficient constraints.

**Regional View**: Al Jazeera analysis noted the statement overlooked other stakeholders' concerns, particularly Lebanon and Syria.
