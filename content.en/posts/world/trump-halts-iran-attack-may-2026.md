Donald Trump announced on May 19, 2026 that he is halting plans for a "major attack" on Iran, but stated the U.S. remains prepared to strike if Tehran does not abandon its nuclear weapons program.

The decision marks the latest turn in U.S.-Iran tensions. The Trump administration has repeatedly warned Iran to cease nuclear weapons-related activities. The pause is seen as leaving room for diplomatic negotiations.

Analysts suggest Trump's statement demonstrates military pressure while preserving negotiation possibilities. The international community is watching closely, concerned about potential further escalation in the region.
