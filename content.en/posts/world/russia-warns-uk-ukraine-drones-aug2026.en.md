## Core Summary

Russia has issued a formal warning to the UK over its continued drone supplies to Ukraine. The UK Defence Ministry swiftly responded, reaffirming that Britain stands "shoulder to shoulder" with Ukraine.

## Event Details

According to BBC, a Russian spokesperson made a strong statement regarding Britain's transfer of drone technology to Ukraine, warning such actions could be seen as hostile. The UK Defence Ministry immediately reaffirmed solidarity with Ukraine.

Drones have become critical weapons in the Russia-Ukraine conflict, with both sides extensively using various types for reconnaissance, strikes, and suicide attacks.

## Broader Perspective

Russia's warning reflects its anxiety over escalating Western military aid. As relatively low-cost but highly effective weapons, drones are reshaping modern warfare. NATO countries providing advanced drones to Ukraine not only affects the current battle but also reshapes European security architecture.
