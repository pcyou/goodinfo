## [Brief] Redistricting Pressure Mounts in Southern States After Indiana GOP Map

According to Politico, following successful congressional redistricting by Republicans in Indiana, several Southern states are facing similar pressures. Analysts note this could have profound implications for the partisan landscape in future midterm elections.

Trump's success in purging Republican dissenters has accelerated the reshaping of political maps.
