President Trump recently stated that due to receiving threat information, the United States has withdrawn B-1 bombers from RAF Fairford. Trump did not share details on the threat source, but stated this is a precautionary measure. The base recently experienced security incidents, drawing external attention to European security.

US Department of Defense officials stated the bomber withdrawal is a precautionary adjustment and does not signal changes to commitments to European allies. Multiple European governments have expressed high concern over base security incidents, with evaluations judging this decision highlights NATO internal security coordination complexity.
