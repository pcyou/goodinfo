## Summary

The death toll from Venezuela's devastating earthquake sequence has climbed above 1,400, up from the previous count of 920. Continuous aftershocks are hampering rescue operations in the critical 72-hour window. International rescue teams are accelerating their arrival while survivors recount emotional stories of hearing family members crying beneath collapsed buildings.
