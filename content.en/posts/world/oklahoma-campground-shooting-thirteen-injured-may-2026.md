A shooting at a campground party in Oklahoma has left at least thirteen people injured. Local law enforcement authorities have launched an investigation into the incident.

The shooting occurred during a gathering at the campground. The injured have been transported to nearby hospitals for treatment. Full details on the condition of those wounded have not yet been released.

Police are at the scene working to determine the cause of the shooting and identify the suspect. The local community has expressed shock and concern over the event.

The incident has renewed discussions about gun violence in the United States. Oklahoma has experienced several similar public safety incidents in recent years.
