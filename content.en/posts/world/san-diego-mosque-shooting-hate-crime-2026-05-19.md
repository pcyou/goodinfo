## [Brief] Three Killed in San Diego Mosque Shooting, Investigated as Hate Crime

A shooting at a mosque in San Diego, California, has left three people dead. Police have arrested multiple teenage suspects and are investigating the case as a hate crime. Authorities are examining whether "hate rhetoric" motivated the attackers. The incident has shocked the local Muslim community, with civil rights organizations calling for a thorough investigation.
