## [Brief] Trump Orders Smithsonian History Warnings

According to BBC News, the Trump administration has ordered the Smithsonian Institution to post warning labels on its museum exhibits, claiming some contain "inaccurate" narratives of American history.

The Trump administration has repeatedly accused the world's largest museum and research complex of harboring "anti-American bias." This move has sparked widespread controversy in academic and museum circles. Critics view it as interference with academic freedom, while supporters say it is a necessary step to correct historical narrativedeviations.
