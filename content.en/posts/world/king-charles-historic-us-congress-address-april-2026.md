# King Charles III Delivers Historic Speech to US Congress, Calls for 'Reconciliation and Renewal'

On April 28, 2026, King Charles III delivered a historic address to a joint session of the US Congress, becoming the first British monarch to address the American legislature. In a world marked by international uncertainty, the King's speech aimed to repair and strengthen the special relationship between the United Kingdom and the United States.

## "In Times of Great Uncertainty"

In his opening remarks, Charles III stated that his address came "in times of great uncertainty," and that the relationship between the UK and the US was "more important than ever." The 77-year-old monarch repeatedly referenced the shared history, values, and contemporary challenges that bind the two nations.

Upon concluding his speech, the King received a standing ovation from the assembled members of Congress — an unprecedented moment in British royal diplomatic history.

## Strains and Repair in the Anglo-American Relationship

Current US-UK relations are strained, reflecting British reluctance to fully back the joint US-Israeli military campaign against Iran. The UK government's hesitancy has created friction in the bilateral relationship.

The King's goal with this visit has been to ease those tensions through diplomatic engagement. Analysts note that while disagreements exist between the two nations, "a strain is not a rupture." Toward the end of his speech, Charles III spoke of the "reconciliation and renewal" that he said characterized centuries of interactions between the two nations.

## Trump's Response

Earlier in the day, following a White House military welcoming ceremony, President Trump held a brief meeting with the King. In subsequent remarks, Trump praised the monarch highly: "He's a fantastic person. They're incredible people and it's a real honour."

Analysts suggest that Trump's mercurial personality makes the trajectory of Anglo-American relations unpredictable — warm relations can turn chilly in a flash, but former antagonists can also be rapidly rehabilitated. Whether the King's words, both publicly and almost certainly in private diplomacy, will be sufficient to reinforce the alliance remains to be seen.

## Historical Significance

This marks the first time a British monarch has addressed the US Congress, and its symbolic significance cannot be understated. The "special relationship" between the UK and the US has been a cornerstone of global geopolitics since World War II, but in recent years, it has faced unprecedented challenges due to Brexit, shifts in US foreign policy, and accelerating global multipolarization.

The King's state visit — encompassing the White House military ceremony, the Congressional address, and bilateral meetings with Trump — is viewed as a significant diplomatic effort to restore this critical bilateral relationship.
