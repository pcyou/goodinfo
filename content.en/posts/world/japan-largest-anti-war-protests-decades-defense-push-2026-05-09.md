## [Flash] Japan's Largest Anti-War Protests in Decades as PM Pushes Defense Buildup

Japan has seen its largest anti-war protests in decades, with citizens opposing the Prime Minister's plan to amend the pacifist constitution. Protesters fear remilitarization, while supporters argue defense strengthening is necessary amid regional security threats. The divide over defense policy is becoming increasingly pronounced.
