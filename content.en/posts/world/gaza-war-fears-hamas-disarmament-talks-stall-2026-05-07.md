[Brief] ## Stalled Negotiations

Hamas disarmament talks have stalled, raising fears of renewed conflict in Gaza. International mediators are working to bring both sides back to the negotiating table.

## Background

Since the ceasefire took effect, the question of Hamas disarmament has been one of the most challenging issues. Israel insists on complete disarmament, while Hamas views this as an existential threat and refuses to concede.

## International Response

Multiple diplomatic forces are urgently coordinating to prevent escalation. The UN has called on all parties to exercise restraint and resolve differences through dialogue.
