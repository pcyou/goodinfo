## Trump Approves Emergency Declaration for California Chemical Leak

Trump signed an emergency declaration for a chemical leak in Orange County, California, providing federal assistance. Governor Newsom had requested federal intervention. The leak caused evacuations of nearby residents, and environmental agencies are assessing contamination scope.
