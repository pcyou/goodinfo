NPR reports that President Trump has nominated former SEC Chairman Jay Clayton to serve as Director of National Intelligence. Clayton brings a long legal resume from Trump's first term, where he led the Securities and Exchange Commission. The move is seen as the latest personnel shakeup in the intelligence community, following recent controversy over the Pulte nomination.
