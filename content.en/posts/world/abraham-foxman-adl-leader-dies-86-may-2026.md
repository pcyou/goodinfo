Abraham Foxman, former national director of the Anti-Defamation League (ADL), has died at age 86. Foxman was one of the organization's most influential leaders, dedicating decades to fighting antisemitism and advancing civil rights. Both The Washington Post and The New York Times published obituaries reflecting on his advocacy career.
