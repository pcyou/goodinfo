## Core Summary

A severe landslide occurred at an unlicensed gold mine in Colombia's Antioquia province on August 20, trapping and ultimately killing 13 miners. According to BBC reports, the mine did not have legal extraction permits and had extremely poor safety conditions.

## Event Summary

Following the incident, Colombia's civil defense department immediately launched rescue operations, but faced enormous challenges due to the extremely unstable mine structure. All 13 people were ultimately confirmed dead. Colombia's president issued a statement pledging to intensify crackdowns on illegal mining activities.

## Background Analysis

Illegal mining has long existed in certain parts of Colombia, concentrated primarily in Antioquia and Chocó provinces. These mines typically lack basic safety facilities, and accidents such as collapses and poisonings occur frequently. It is estimated that illegal mining causes economic losses exceeding hundreds of millions of dollars annually in Colombia, accompanied by severe environmental damage.
