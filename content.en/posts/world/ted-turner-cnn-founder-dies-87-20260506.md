## CNN founder Ted Turner dies at 87

Media mogul Ted Turner, who pioneered the modern 24-hour news culture when he launched the Cable News Network in 1980, has died at age 87.

Turner's media empire also included TBS and TNT. His creation of CNN fundamentally transformed how news is consumed worldwide, establishing the template for round-the-clock global news coverage.
