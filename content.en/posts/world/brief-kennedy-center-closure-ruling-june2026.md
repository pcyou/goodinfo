## Summary

A federal judge issued a temporary injunction blocking the Kennedy Center's planned two-year closure. However, the center's management stated that the ruling only suspends the closure order and does not obligate them to reschedule previously cancelled performances. Critics argue the closure decision was politically motivated to remove artistic programs inconsistent with the current administration's positions.
