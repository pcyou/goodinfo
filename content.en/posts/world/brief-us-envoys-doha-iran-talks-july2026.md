## Core Summary

US envoys have arrived in Doha, Qatar to consult with mediators on the Iran nuclear issue. However, Qatar's Foreign Ministry confirmed that no Iranian representatives are present for direct talks — only indirect communication is underway.

## Brief

According to BBC and CNN, the US negotiation team arrived in Doha prepared to relay messages to Iran through Qatari and other intermediaries. Qatar's Foreign Ministry spokesman stated clearly that "no high-level meetings or direct talks between the US and Iran are scheduled."

The arrangement reflects the complexity and sensitivity of US-Iran negotiations. Iran has insisted on sanctions relief as a precondition, while the US demands Iran halt uranium enrichment first. The gap between positions makes direct dialogue difficult to achieve.
