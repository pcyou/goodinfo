## Overview

Ukraine is considering legislation that would effectively ban foreigners from accessing surrogacy services in the country. Ukraine has been one of the world's leading surrogacy destinations, particularly for international intended parents.

## Impact

The proposed law, if passed, would have far-reaching consequences for the global surrogacy industry and the many international families who rely on Ukrainian clinics. The move comes amid the country's ongoing geopolitical challenges and shifting social policies. The legislation's timing reflects broader debates around reproductive rights and national sovereignty.
