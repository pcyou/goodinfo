[Core Summary] Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky called Russian President Vladimir Putin a killer after former US President Donald Trump referred to the Russian leader as a friend, escalating tensions in US-Russia-Ukraine relations.

**Event Details:**

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky responded strongly after former US President Donald Trump called Russian President Vladimir Putin a friend. In an interview with media, Zelensky explicitly stated that Putin is a killer, directly challenging Trump's efforts to ease relations with Russia.

Trump has recently stated in multiple occasions that he maintains a good personal relationship with Putin and claims this relationship helps promote peace negotiations. However, Zelensky's remarks indicate that the Ukrainian government does not agree with this pro-Russia diplomatic strategy.

**Panorama Analysis:**

This verbal conflict between Zelensky and Trump reflects deep contradictions in the current international political landscape. Trump attempts to ease the Russia-Ukraine conflict through personal diplomacy, but his friendly attitude towards Putin is seen by Ukraine as neglecting its sovereignty and security interests. Zelensky's strong response is not only a direct rebuttal to Trump's remarks but also a signal to the international community that Ukraine will not compromise on core interests.

In the long term, this public diplomatic divergence may affect US military aid policy to Ukraine, thereby changing the battlefield situation. At the same time, it exposes divisions within the Western alliance on Russia strategy, and European countries may need to find a balance between the US and Russia. For global energy markets and geopolitical stability, the direction of the Russia-Ukraine conflict remains a key variable.

**Multiple Perspectives:**

Views supporting Zelensky's position argue that any attempt to normalize Putin would weaken support for Ukraine and potentially encourage Russia to continue its military operations. Leaders of several EU countries have also expressed firm support for Ukrainian sovereignty.

On the other hand, Trump's supporters believe that dialogue rather than confrontation is the only way to achieve peace. They point out that prolonged military conflict has brought huge humanitarian and economic costs to both sides, and seeking diplomatic solutions is necessary.

Neutral observers warn that such public diplomatic disputes may complicate peace negotiations, and all parties need to find a balance that upholds principles while promoting dialogue.
