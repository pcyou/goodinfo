## 📰 Article

### US Seeks China's Help on Iran

Al Jazeera reports that Trump will discuss the Iran crisis with President Xi Jinping during his upcoming visit to China. Washington is hoping to leverage Beijing's position as one of Iran's key trading partners to push Tehran back to the negotiating table. Analysts suggest that if the US and China can reach a consensus on Iran, it could help ease tensions across the broader Middle East.
