## [Brief] She Was First Lady at 19; Now Making Fourth Attempt at Peru's Presidency

According to CNN, a Peruvian politician who became first lady at the age of 19 is making her fourth attempt at winning the country's presidency. The race is highly competitive, with security and instability emerging as the top concerns for voters. Analysts note that voter dissatisfaction in Peru has created opportunities for multiple candidates, but the country's long-standing political turbulence remains the greatest source of uncertainty.
