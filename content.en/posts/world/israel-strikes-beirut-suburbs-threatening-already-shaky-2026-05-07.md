## 摘要

以色列对贝鲁特郊区发动空袭，威胁到本已脆弱的停火协议。《华盛顿邮报》报道，此次袭击发生在黎巴嫩真主党与以色列停火协议签署后不久，国际社会对此表示关切。袭击造成平民伤亡，联合国呼吁各方保持克制。此次军事行动引发了人们对中东局势再度升级的担忧。

## Summary

Israel launched airstrikes on Beirut suburbs, threatening an already fragile ceasefire agreement. The Washington Post reports that the strikes came shortly after the ceasefire between Hezbollah and Israel was established, drawing international concern. The attacks caused civilian casualties, and the UN has called for restraint from all parties. The military operation raises fears of renewed escalation in the Middle East.
