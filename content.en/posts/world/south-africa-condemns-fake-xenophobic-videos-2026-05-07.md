[Brief] ## Government Statement

The South African government has strongly condemned recent videos allegedly showing xenophobic attacks, calling them fabricated. Authorities urged the public not to spread unverified information.

## Background

Xenophobic violence is a long-standing sensitive issue in South Africa. Videos circulating on social media claiming to document new attacks have been found to be inaccurate upon investigation.

## Social Impact

The government stated that fake videos aim to inflame racial tensions and undermine social stability. An investigation has been launched to hold those responsible accountable.
