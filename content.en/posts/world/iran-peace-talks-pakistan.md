## US Envoys Witkoff and Kushner Head to Pakistan for Iran Diplomacy

On April 25, 2026, President Trump dispatched senior envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner to Islamabad, Pakistan, for diplomatic efforts aimed at addressing the ongoing tensions with Iran. This marks the most significant diplomatic initiative on the Iran situation in recent weeks.

### Diplomatic Mission and Positions

According to live reports from CBS News and CNN, the US delegation is expected to arrive in Pakistan today. Iran's foreign minister has also arrived in Islamabad in preparation for possible indirect dialogue. However, Iranian officials have made it clear that there are currently no plans for direct negotiations with the United States.

The Washington Post reported that Iran is approaching the indirect dialogue cautiously, emphasizing that any talks must respect Iran's national sovereignty and nuclear rights. The US delegation hopes to use Pakistan as an intermediary to facilitate communication on key points of disagreement.

### Pakistan's Role as Mediator

Pakistan occupies a unique geopolitical position, maintaining diplomatic relations of varying degrees with both Iran and the United States. Islamabad has previously played a mediating role in regional conflicts, and its selection as the venue for these talks reflects the trust that all parties place in Pakistan's diplomatic channels.

Analysts note that the Pakistani Prime Minister and diplomatic team are expected to provide a secure and neutral environment for dialogue, though they will not directly engage in the substantive content of the negotiations.

### Background and Implications

This diplomatic mission takes place against a backdrop of sustained tensions between Iran and Western nations. Previous rounds of maneuvering have centered on Iran's nuclear program and regional security issues. The Trump administration's decision to send Kushner — a former senior White House adviser deeply involved in Middle East diplomacy — alongside Witkoff signals the importance the US places on advancing dialogue.

The international community has broadly expressed hope that this trip to Pakistan could create opportunities for de-escalating the Iran situation. The United Nations and several European nations have already expressed support for resolving differences through diplomatic channels.
