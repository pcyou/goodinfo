## 📰 Article

### Death Toll Rises in Lebanon

Israeli strikes on Lebanon have killed at least 51 people in the past 24 hours, including medical workers, Al Jazeera and The New York Times report. Eight members of a single family were killed in one attack. Reports also indicate that Israeli forces have used weapons firing tiny metal cubes in Lebanon, similar to tactics deployed in Gaza. The humanitarian situation in Lebanon continues to deteriorate.
