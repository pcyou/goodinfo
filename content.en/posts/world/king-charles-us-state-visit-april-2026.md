## 📰 Article Body

On April 28, 2026, King Charles III and Queen Camilla arrived in Washington, D.C., beginning a highly anticipated state visit to the United States. The visit is the latest formal diplomatic engagement by a British monarch to the US, during which Charles III will deliver a historic address to the US Congress and hold talks with President Trump.

According to the BBC, Charles III and Queen Camilla received a warm welcome upon their arrival at the White House. The British Embassy subsequently hosted a grand garden reception, inviting hundreds of political and business leaders to attend.

The Guardian noted that the visit is shrouded in a delicate political atmosphere. Charles III's meeting with Trump will be held off-camera, reflecting potential disagreements between the two leaders on various international issues. Meanwhile, Charles III's address to the US Congress will be a significant historic moment for a British monarch addressing the American legislative body.

NBC News analyzed that Charles III's visit carries the important mission of repairing the UK-US relationship. Over the past year, the two countries have experienced varying degrees of friction on trade, climate change, and international security. The King hopes to use this visit to reinforce the "special relationship" between the two nations.

Reports indicate that during the visit, Charles III will also engage in extensive exchanges with business leaders, environmental organization representatives, and cultural figures, showcasing Britain's achievements in innovation, sustainable development, and cultural soft power.

Analysts believe that against the backdrop of rapidly changing global dynamics, the importance of the UK-US relationship is self-evident. Charles III's visit is not merely a ceremonial diplomatic event, but also an important exploration of the future direction of bilateral cooperation.
