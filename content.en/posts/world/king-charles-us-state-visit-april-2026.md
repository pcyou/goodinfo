# King Charles Begins US State Visit: First in Nearly 20 Years

> April 28, 2026 | Source: BBC, The Washington Post

King Charles III and Queen Camilla arrived in the United States on April 27 for a four-day state visit — the first by a British monarch since Queen Elizabeth II's visit in 2007, marking a significant diplomatic milestone in US-UK relations.

## White House Welcome

The royal couple arrived at Joint Base Andrews in Maryland before being greeted at the White House by President Donald Trump and First Lady Melania Trump. Security is exceptionally tight following Saturday's shooting incident in Washington, D.C.

At the White House, the royals were served tea in the Green Room and then taken to the garden to view a newly expanded beehive — rebuilt in the shape of a miniature White House. This carefully orchestrated diplomatic gesture was designed to appeal to King Charles, a well-known advocate for beekeeping.

## Congress Address to Call for "Reconciliation and Renewal"

King Charles will address the US Congress on Tuesday. According to royal sources, he is expected to express sympathy over Saturday's gun attack and call for unity in defending democratic values amid significant international challenges.

The King will say that despite current disagreements between the US and the UK, "time and again, our two countries have always found ways to come together." His address will call for "reconciliation and renewal" of the US-UK partnership, advocating for shared values of tolerance, liberty, and equality.

He is expected to urge the defence of these beliefs through support for NATO and the protection of Ukraine. The King will tell US lawmakers that the transatlantic alliance is built on a "generosity of spirit and a duty to foster compassion, to promote peace, to deepen mutual understanding and to value people of all faiths and none."

## Diplomacy in the Details

The state visit, carried out on behalf of the UK government, is a soft-power effort to strengthen bilateral ties during a year in which the US marks its 250th anniversary of independence.

Queen Camilla wore a brooch combining the British and US flags, originally presented to the late Queen Elizabeth II by the mayor of New York during a 1957 state visit. That trip was itself a diplomatic mission to rebuild the US-UK partnership after the Suez Crisis of 1956 strained relations over a Middle East conflict.

The royal couple then attended a garden party at the UK's Washington embassy, with more than 600 guests in attendance. Even the menu carried diplomatic significance — beef sandwiches featured meat from the first tariff-free batch of British beef imported following a recently negotiated trade deal.

Among the political attendees were former Speaker Nancy Pelosi, Senator Ted Cruz, and UK Foreign Secretary Yvette Cooper. Queen Camilla spent time speaking with women representing domestic abuse advocacy organizations, a cause close to her heart.
