## Brief

Al Jazeera reported on September 20 that Israeli forces operating near the West Bank city of Jenin killed a Palestinian. Israel said the operation targeted an individual suspected of involvement in security threat activities. The incident occurred in the northern West Bank, a region that has long been a flashpoint in the Israeli-Palestinian conflict. The incident again highlights the ongoing tension of the Israeli-Palestinian conflict in the West Bank. The international community has expressed concern and called on all sides to exercise restraint to prevent further escalation.
