## Core Summary According to ABC News, a powerful nor'easter will strike the US Northeast this weekend, bringing heavy rain, strong winds, and potential power outages to New York, Boston, and other major cities. The National Weather Service has issued multiple warnings, urging millions of residents to prepare for emergencies and adjust weekend travel plans.

## Event Details

Reports indicate the storm will begin affecting New England late Friday and move up the East Coast through the weekend. New York City and Boston are forecast to see the heaviest rain and strongest gusts, with some areas possibly seeing gusts over 60 mph. Emergency management officials cited in the coverage urged residents to prepare for possible outages, stock up on drinking water, emergency lighting, and backup batteries, and avoid non-essential travel during the storm's peak.

## Comparison of Viewpoints

The National Weather Service emphasized that uncertainty remains high in the storm's track and intensity, with some weather models showing the storm center tracking closer to the coast, raising the risk of significant coastal flooding; local emergency management departments urged residents to follow local warnings and avoid underestimating the storm's impact.
