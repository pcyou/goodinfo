Turkiye and Pakistan recently reached a consensus on Saudi Arabia military deployment, signing a Mecca pact three-party protocol. The agreement focuses on combined training, joint execution, equipment interoperability, and across other dimensions, evaluating to enhance the three countries' joint capability in regional security affairs. The protocol is viewed externally as recognition of Pakistan's participation in multilateral security mechanisms.

A Saudi Ministry of Defense spokesperson stated the three-party protocol marks a new milestone in regional security cooperation, expecting more countries to join in the future. Turkish and Pakistani military high-level officials have conducted intensive mutual visits recently, marking an initial coordination framework.
