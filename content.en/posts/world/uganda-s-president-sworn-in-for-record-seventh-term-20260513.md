## Uganda's President Sworn In for Record Seventh Term

Uganda's President Yoweri Museveni has been sworn in for a record seventh term in office. Museveni has been in power since 1986, making him one of Africa's longest-serving leaders. Opposition groups have questioned the legitimacy of his continued rule.
