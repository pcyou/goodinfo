## Overview

At least 10 people were injured in a shooting at a lake party near Oklahoma City, according to local authorities.

## Emergency Response

Emergency responders were dispatched to the scene after reports of gunfire at a gathering near the lake. Multiple victims were transported to area hospitals with varying degrees of injury. Local hospitals activated emergency protocols to receive and treat all casualties.

## Investigation

Law enforcement agencies have secured the area and launched an investigation into the circumstances of the shooting. No suspect has been publicly identified at this time. Police are urging witnesses to come forward with information.
