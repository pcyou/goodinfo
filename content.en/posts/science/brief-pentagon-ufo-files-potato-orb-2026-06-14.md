## Core Summary

According to The New York Times, the US Department of Defense has released its third batch of Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) files, including records of a mysterious potato-shaped object hovering over Colorado and multiple reports of glowing red orbs. This is the latest step in the White House's push for transparency on extraterrestrial phenomena.

## Details

The newly declassified files were published on the Department of Defense website WAR.GOV/UFO. The documents record multiple unidentified flying object sightings in the northeastern United States, including oddly shaped flying objects and unexplained luminous phenomena.

According to CBC, these files represent the third major release of UAP documents conducted at the White House's request. Axios noted that the Pentagon's unsealed reports cover apparent UFO sightings in the northeastern United States.

These newly released files have renewed public and researcher interest in the UFO phenomenon. While most sightings can ultimately be explained as known phenomena or equipment errors, some cases remain unexplained.
