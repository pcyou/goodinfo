## Perspective

From the perspective of space exploration, crew selection and mission planning for major space missions involve not just technical considerations but also social equity, scientific representation, and public perception. As space exploration transitions from government-led to increasingly commercialized, astronaut team composition and selection criteria will face renewed scrutiny. Future deep-space missions will require more diverse team compositions to ensure complementary perspectives in extreme environments.

## Multiple Viewpoints

Supporters argue that crew selection for scientific missions should be based on professional capability and mission fit, with mission success as the top priority. Meanwhile, society calls for increased diversity in future space exploration. Science communication experts note that such discussions help raise public awareness of space endeavors and inspire the next generation in STEM fields.
