## Massive Alaska megatsunami was second largest ever recorded

A massive megatsunami struck Alaska, confirmed as the second largest ever recorded. New research suggests glacier melt driven by climate change is increasing the risk of giant waves in mountainous coastal regions.

Scientists warn that as Arctic and sub-Arctic glaciers continue to retreat rapidly, similar extreme events could become more frequent, posing growing threats to coastal communities.
