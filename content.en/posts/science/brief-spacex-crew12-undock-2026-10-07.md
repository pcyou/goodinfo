**[Core Summary]**
Space.com and NASA reported that the four astronauts of SpaceX's Crew-12 mission have officially undocked from the International Space Station to return to Earth after nearly ten months in space, with splashdown recovery expected in the designated ocean area.

**[Event Details]**
- **Mission Overview**: The four Crew-12 astronauts officially undocked after nearly ten months aboard the International Space Station.
- **Handover**: The replacement crew has arrived at the station and will conduct the subsequent six-month on-orbit mission.
- **Return Profile**: The Crew Dragon spacecraft will proceed through deorbit, reentry and splashdown recovery in accordance with procedures, with SpaceX and NASA coordinating each phase.
