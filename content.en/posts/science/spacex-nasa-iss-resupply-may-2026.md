SpaceX launched a Dragon spacecraft from Cape Canaveral, Florida on May 13, carrying supplies to the International Space Station.

## Mission Overview

The launch is part of SpaceX's Commercial Resupply Services contract with NASA. The Dragon spacecraft carries scientific experiments, crew provisions, and technical equipment to the orbiting laboratory.

## Background

SpaceX has been a key NASA commercial cargo partner since 2012, regularly resupplying the ISS. This mission represents another routine delivery under the long-standing partnership.
