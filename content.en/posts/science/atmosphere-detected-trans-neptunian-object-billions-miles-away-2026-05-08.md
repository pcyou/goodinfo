The New York Times reports that scientists have detected surprising signs of an atmosphere on a trans-Neptunian object billions of miles from Earth. The finding challenges scientists' understanding of how bodies in the outer solar system evolve.

Trans-Neptunian objects are small icy worlds orbiting beyond Neptune, typically thought to be too cold and small to maintain atmospheres. This discovery could reshape our understanding of astrophysical processes in the solar system's outer regions.
