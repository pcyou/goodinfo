## [Flash] Five Italian Divers Die During Cave Scuba Dive in Maldives

Five Italian divers have died during a cave scuba diving expedition in the Maldives. Local search and rescue authorities say operations to recover the remaining divers have been temporarily suspended due to bad weather conditions. Cave diving is widely recognized as one of the most dangerous water sports, requiring specialized training and equipment.
