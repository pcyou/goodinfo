## Core Summary

The SpaceX Dragon cargo capsule successfully splashed down in waters off the California coast after completing its resupply mission to the International Space Station. The mission successfully delivered science equipment and supplies to the orbital laboratory.

## Details

According to U.S. space news, the Dragon cargo capsule undocked from the ISS and, after re-entry through the atmosphere, safely splashed down in the designated recovery zone. Recovery teams immediately began operations to transfer returned cargo and scientific results to land.

This mission was one of SpaceX's regular Commercial Resupply Services missions for NASA, further validating the Dragon system's reliability and reusability.
