## Core Summary

The World Meteorological Organization (WMO) has issued a warning that a strong El Nino event will develop in the coming months. This climate phenomenon could bring extreme weather to many regions globally, including heavy rainfall, droughts, and heat waves, with significant impacts on agriculture, water resources, and public health.

## Event Details

El Nino refers to the abnormal warming of sea surface temperatures in the central and eastern equatorial Pacific, typically occurring every 2-7 years. Strong El Nino events are rarer and have more significant impacts on the global climate system. WMO indicates that current oceanic and atmospheric indicators show El Nino is developing rapidly and may reach strong intensity.

## Panoramic Perspective

A strong El Nino has far-reaching economic and social impacts. Agriculture is most directly affected, as droughts and floods may reduce yields in major grain-producing regions, pushing up global food prices. Water resource management faces challenges, with some regions potentially experiencing water supply stress.The compounding effect with global warming may make extreme weather events more frequent and intense.
