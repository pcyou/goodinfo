[Brief] For the first time on record, sea turtles have successfully nested and hatched on a California beach. Dozens of baby turtles made their way to the Pacific Ocean. Scientists attribute this historic event to rising ocean temperatures caused by El Niño and climate change, which attracted tropical species to northern waters.
