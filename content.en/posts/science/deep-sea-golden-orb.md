## Marine Biologists Solve Mystery of Deep-Sea 'Golden Orb'

On April 25, 2026, Sci.News reported that marine biologists have finally identified a mysterious "golden orb" discovered during a 2023 deep-sea expedition in the Gulf of Alaska, after more than two years of intensive research. This discovery provides important clues for deep-sea biological research.

### The Discovery

In 2023, during a deep-sea expedition by the US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), researchers collected this mysterious golden spherical object from the waters of the Gulf of Alaska. The object displayed a unique golden luster and spherical structure, which is extremely rare in deep-sea environments. Since its discovery, scientists have been working to identify its true nature.

### Research Breakthrough

After years of analysis, researchers used advanced molecular biology techniques and deep-sea ecological research methods to finally confirm the object's origin. According to an official NOAA announcement, the research team revealed the true identity of this mysterious biological specimen, adding a significant chapter to deep-sea ecosystem research.

The New York Times reported that this discovery not only answers a long-standing scientific mystery but also provides scientists with new perspectives for understanding deep-sea biodiversity.

### Scientific Significance

The deep sea is one of the least explored regions on Earth, with only about 5% of the ocean floor having been mapped in detail. Every deep-sea discovery has the potential to bring new scientific breakthroughs. The identification of this "golden orb" will further advance research in deep-sea biological taxonomy and ecology.

Scientists say this discovery reminds us that even in the 21st century, the ocean depths still harbor a vast number of undiscovered species worthy of further exploration and study.
