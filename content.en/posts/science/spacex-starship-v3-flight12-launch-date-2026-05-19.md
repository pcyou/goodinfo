## [Brief] SpaceX Starship V3 First Test Gets New Launch Date

SpaceX's Starship V3 first flight test (Flight 12) has received a new launch date. Starship V3 represents a major upgrade to SpaceX's next-generation heavy-lift rocket, featuring enhanced payload capacity and full reusability.
