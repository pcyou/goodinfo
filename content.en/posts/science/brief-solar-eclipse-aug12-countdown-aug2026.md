## [Brief] Total Solar Eclipse August 12: 6 Days to Go

According to Space.com, there are just 6 days remaining until the total solar eclipse of August 12, 2026. The eclipse will cross parts of North America, Europe, and North Africa, making it one of the most anticipated astronomical events in recent years.

Unlike the 2024 total solar eclipse that crossed the central United States, this eclipse path will traverse Spain, northern France, southern UK, Iceland, Greenland, eastern Canada, and the northeastern United States. The path of totality will be approximately 250 km wide at its broadest, with maximum totality lasting about 4 minutes 30 seconds.

Astronomical institutions and tourism departments worldwide have entered final preparations. Many locations expect large numbers of astronomy enthusiasts and tourists, with hotels and viewing sites long since booked out. Scientists remind observers to use professional eclipse glasses to avoid permanent eye damage from looking directly at the sun.

A total solar eclipse, when the Moon completely covers the Sun, is a rare phenomenon where the sky briefly darkens, temperatures drop, and the Sun's corona becomes visible to the naked eye. This provides scientists with valuable opportunities to study the solar atmosphere.
