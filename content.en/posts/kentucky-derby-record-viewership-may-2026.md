According to the Associated Press, this years Kentucky Derby set an alltime viewership record. Golden Tempos victory attracted a record number of television viewers.

The race averaged 19.6 million viewers, making it one of the mostwatched Kentucky Derbys in history. This figure surpassed all previous editions of the race.

The Kentucky Derby, as one of Americas most prestigious horse racing events, attracts a large audience every year. This years high viewership was partly attributed to Golden Tempos outstanding performance and the excitement of the race itself.

The Kentucky Derby traditionally takes place on the first Saturday of May each year and remains an important fixture on the American sports calendar. This viewership record further cements the races place in American sports culture.
