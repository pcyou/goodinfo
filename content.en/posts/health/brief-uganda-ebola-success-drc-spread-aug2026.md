**Summary**: Uganda has successfully contained its Ebola outbreak with no new hospitalizations in two weeks. In contrast, DR Congo's outbreak continues to spread.

**Analysis**: Uganda employed rapid response strategies including border screening, contact tracing, and vaccination campaigns. WHO praised Uganda's effectiveness while warning DR Congo remains a regional threat. Experts note the divergent outcomes highlight the importance of early intervention and community engagement.

**Perspectives**: International health experts praised Uganda's system as a model for other African nations. Critics note Uganda's smaller scale versus DR Congo's complex challenges of armed conflict and infrastructure, making simple replication difficult.
