A football friendly between DR Congo and Chile scheduled in Spain has been cancelled by the local mayor over Ebola outbreak concerns. The decision reflects heightened international vigilance against Ebola spread. The WHO has previously issued warnings about the Ebola situation in DR Congo, and countries are strengthening border quarantine measures.
