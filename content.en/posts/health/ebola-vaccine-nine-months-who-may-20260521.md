The World Health Organization (WHO) has warned that a vaccine for the current Ebola outbreak could take up to nine months to develop, as the death toll continues to rise. The Washington Post previously reported that the White House had resisted letting an Ebola-infected doctor return to the US for treatment, sparking controversy. The outbreak is primarily spreading across African regions, with the international community ramping up aid efforts.
