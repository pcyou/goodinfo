## [Brief] TrumpRx Platform Adds Hundreds of Generic Drugs

Trump's TrumpRx prescription drug platform has announced the addition of hundreds of generic medications, aiming to lower drug prices in the US. The expansion is part of the administration's effort to reduce healthcare costs.
