The Ebola outbreak in Congo is spreading in ways not fully understood, according to a Washington Post opinion piece. The article emphasizes that Congo currently lacks sufficient testing capacity to track the virus's spread patterns. Experts are calling on the international community to increase investment in African testing infrastructure to prevent further spread.
