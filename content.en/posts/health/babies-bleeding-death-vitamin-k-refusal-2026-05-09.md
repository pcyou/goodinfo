## [Flash] Babies Dying from Bleeding as Parents Refuse Birth Vitamin K Shot

CNN reports that multiple infants have died from preventable bleeding disorders after parents refused the routine vitamin K injection given at birth. Vitamin K shots are standard newborn care that effectively prevent hemorrhagic disease. Medical experts urge parents not to reject this critical preventive measure based on misinformation.
