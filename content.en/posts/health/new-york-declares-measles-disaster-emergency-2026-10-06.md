New York State recently declared a measles disaster emergency, the first such declaration in the US mainland since 2025. New York State health officials stated the outbreak spread exceeds expectations and requires immediate emergency response. Multi-state outbreak linkage has drawn external attention to vaccination rates.

US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention data shows national measles cases have recently exceeded one thousand, with some states reporting concentrated outbreaks. Expert evaluations identify declining vaccination rates as main factor, urging timely vaccination of children.
