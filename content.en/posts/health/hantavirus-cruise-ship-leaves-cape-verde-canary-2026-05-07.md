## [快讯] 汉坦病毒疫情游轮离开佛得角驶向加那利群岛

据美联社和BBC报道，一艘爆发汉坦病毒疫情的游轮已离开佛得角，驶向加那利群岛。此前已有3名重症患者被紧急疏散。该游轮上多名乘客出现汉坦病毒感染症状，引发国际关注。汉坦病毒主要通过啮齿动物排泄物传播，人际传播罕见，致死率较高。当局正在对游轮进行全面消毒，并为其余乘客安排医学检查。

## Cruise Ship with Hantavirus Outbreak Leaves Cape Verde for Canary Islands

According to AP News and BBC, a cruise ship experiencing a hantavirus outbreak has left Cape Verde heading for the Canary Islands. Three severely ill patients were previously evacuated. Multiple passengers aboard showed symptoms of hantavirus infection, drawing international attention. The virus spreads primarily through rodent droppings, with rare human-to-human transmission but a high fatality rate. Authorities are conducting full disinfection and arranging medical examinations for remaining passengers.
