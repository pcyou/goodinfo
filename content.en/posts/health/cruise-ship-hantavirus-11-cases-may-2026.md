The hantavirus outbreak aboard a cruise ship in the Andes region has grown to 11 cases. France's health ministry confirmed the virus matches a known South American hantavirus strain. One French passenger is in critical condition, and the CDC has tightened home monitoring guidance for high-risk contacts.
