## Core Summary

Taylor Farms, a major US produce supplier, has launched a product recall after its iceberg lettuce was linked to a multi-state parasitic infection outbreak.

## Event Details

The Washington Post reports that Taylor Farms initiated the recall after its iceberg lettuce products tested positive for connection to the spreading cyclosporiasis outbreak. The CDC is investigating cases across multiple states with thousands confirmed. Several restaurants in Lincoln, Nebraska have temporarily removed related menu items.

## Analysis

This food safety incident highlights the vulnerability of America's industrialized food supply chain. When products from large agricultural suppliers become contaminated, the impact can span multiple states and affect thousands of consumers. Cyclosporiasis spreads through contaminated food and water, causing severe diarrhea, nausea, and abdominal pain.
