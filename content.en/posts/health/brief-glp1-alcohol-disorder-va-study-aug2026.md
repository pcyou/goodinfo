## Core Summary

The US Department of Veterans Affairs has initiated a clinical study to explore whether GLP-1 receptor agonist drugs can effectively treat alcohol use disorder, according to The Washington Post. These drugs have already achieved breakthrough results in weight loss and diabetes treatment, and their application scope may now extend further into addiction medicine.
