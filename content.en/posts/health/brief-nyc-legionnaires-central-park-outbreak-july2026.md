# [Brief] NYC Health Officials Issue Legionnaires' Disease Warning Near Central Park

New York City health officials have issued a warning to visitors and residents near Central Park following a growing outbreak of Legionnaires' disease on the Upper East Side.

Legionnaires' disease is a severe respiratory infection caused by Legionella bacteria, spread through inhalation of contaminated water mist. The NYC Health Department says multiple confirmed cases are linked to a specific area on the Upper East Side, and investigators are examining potential sources including cooling towers and fountain systems.

Health officials urge anyone experiencing fever, cough, or shortness of breath to seek medical attention immediately. While most patients recover with timely treatment, the disease poses serious risks to elderly individuals and those with weakened immune systems.
