**[Core Summary]** According to Axios, the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) stated it is continuously monitoring risks related to a recent plague situation in Russia and is prepared to respond. This is the CDC's first formal response after a Russian researcher died from pneumonic plague and approximately 200 people were placed under quarantine observation.

**[Event Details]**
- **Monitoring Target**: CDC is tracking the potential international public health impact of the Russian plague outbreak.
- **Response Measures**: CDC has activated information-sharing mechanisms with Russia and WHO, and is assessing the importation risk to US domestic territory.
- **Official Stance**: CDC leadership said it is "prepared for any threat" but has not issued travel warnings.
