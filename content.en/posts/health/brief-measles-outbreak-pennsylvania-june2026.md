## Summary

The measles outbreak in Lancaster County, Pennsylvania continues to grow, with confirmed cases reaching 72, all among children. Health officials say vaccine hesitancy and public distrust in the healthcare system are seriously hindering containment efforts.

## Details

According to the Pittsburgh Post-Gazette, the Lancaster County cluster has become the largest measles outbreak in Pennsylvania in recent years. Doctors warn that adult measles cases tend to be more severe than in children, and low vaccination rates have created significant gaps in community immunity.

Pennsylvania health officials state they "will not slow down until all cases are contained." Local health agencies are increasing community outreach to rebuild vaccine trust through face-to-face communication.
