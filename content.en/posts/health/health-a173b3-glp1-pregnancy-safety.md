[Brief] Medical professionals are raising concerns about the use of GLP-1 receptor agonists (such as Ozempic and Wegovy) during pregnancy. Recent studies involving 750,000 pregnancies and animal trials suggest these drugs may reduce oxygen supply to fetuses. Most doctors recommend pregnant women avoid these medications until more safety data is available.
