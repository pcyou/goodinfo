[Brief] The World Health Organization has reported a hantavirus cluster linked to cruise ship travel, involving passengers across multiple countries. Hantavirus is primarily transmitted through rodents and typically causes fever and hemorrhagic symptoms. The WHO has recommended enhanced health monitoring at ports and aboard vessels.
