[Brief] Five US states are monitoring passengers from an Atlantic cruise ship following a hantavirus outbreak. Health authorities are tracing close contacts and issuing health advisories. Hantavirus can cause severe respiratory illness. No deaths have been reported so far, but officials advise passengers experiencing fever or breathing difficulties to seek medical attention immediately.
