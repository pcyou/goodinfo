## Core Summary

Michigan has reported two deaths linked to a cyclosporiasis outbreak, marking the first fatalities associated with the illness. Cyclosporiasis is an intestinal illness caused by a parasitic infection, typically spread through contaminated food or water, with symptoms including severe diarrhea and dehydration. While the disease is generally not life-threatening, immunocompromised individuals may face higher risks.
