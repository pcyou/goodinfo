## Summary

A new study suggests that reducing protein intake may be an important factor in achieving healthy aging for most adults. The research indicates that moderate protein restriction could help extend healthspan, though experts emphasize that dietary adjustments should be personalized.

## Details

The study indicates that excessive protein intake may accelerate cellular aging processes. Researchers recommend that adults beyond middle age should appropriately reduce the proportion of protein in their diet while increasing fruit and vegetable consumption.
