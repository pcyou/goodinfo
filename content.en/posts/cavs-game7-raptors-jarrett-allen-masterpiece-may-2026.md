## Overview

The Cleveland Cavaliers have advanced to the next round of the NBA playoffs after defeating the Toronto Raptors in a hard-fought Game 7. Center Jarrett Allen delivered a dominant performance in the decisive match, answering his critics with clutch rebounds and scoring.

## Details

Allen showcased his inside dominance throughout Game 7, controlling the glass on both ends of the floor. After the game, he directly addressed the Toronto crowd, telling them to go home — a moment that quickly went viral on social media.

ESPN's post-game analysis highlighted Allen's rebounding as one of the key factors in Cleveland's victory. Role players also stepped up in critical moments, providing essential support for the team's success.

## Impact

The Cavaliers have cleared a difficult series and continue their push toward the championship. Allen's Game 7 performance is widely seen as having temporarily quieted doubts about his abilities and value, though the team will need to maintain this level of play in the rounds ahead.

The Raptors have ended their playoff run, and the team's front office is expected to evaluate and adjust the roster during the offseason.
