## Overview

A TikTok creator has rallied crowd-sourced pledges to purchase Spirit Airlines following the carrier abrupt collapse over the weekend.

## Background

Spirit Airlines, a major ultra-low-cost carrier in the United States, ceased operations unexpectedly, leaving thousands of passengers stranded across the country. The sudden shutdown sent shockwaves through the aviation industry.

## Public Response

The social media campaign quickly gained traction, with thousands of users pledging financial support for a potential buyout. The effort highlights growing public frustration with airline reliability and consolidation in the aviation industry. Aviation experts have expressed skepticism about the feasibility of a crowd-funded acquisition, noting the complex regulatory and financial hurdles involved.
