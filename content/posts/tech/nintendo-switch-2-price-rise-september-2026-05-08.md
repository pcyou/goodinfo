## 事件概述

任天堂公司宣布，其新一代游戏主机Switch 2的价格将于2026年9月起正式上调。这是Switch 2发布后的首次官方价格调整。

## Nintendo Switch 2 price rise takes effect from September

Nintendo has announced that the price of its next-generation Switch 2 console will increase starting in September 2026. This marks the first official price adjustment since the console's launch.

The price hike is attributed to rising global supply chain costs and tariff pressures affecting the electronics industry. Nintendo noted that the adjustment reflects broader industry trends, with several competitors facing similar cost increases.

The timing of the increase — ahead of the holiday shopping season — is expected to impact consumer demand. Analysts suggest that early adopters who purchased at launch pricing avoided the increase, while new buyers will face higher costs.
