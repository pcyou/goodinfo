## [快讯] Valve上调Steam Deck售价超40%

据BBC Technology报道，游戏公司Valve宣布将Steam Deck掌上游戏机的价格上调超过40%，理由是全球供应链成本持续攀升。这是Steam Deck发售以来最大幅度的价格调整。

Valve在声明中表示，半导体元器件、物流和制造成本的综合上涨迫使公司重新定价。此次调价覆盖了所有Steam Deck型号，包括LCD和OLED版本。

分析人士指出，随着全球消费电子需求回暖和贸易政策不确定性，硬件制造商面临的成本压力正在加剧。此次涨价可能影响Steam Deck在掌游市场的竞争力，尤其是在Nintendo Switch 2等竞品即将上市的背景下。

## Valve Raises Steam Deck Prices by Over 40% Citing Rising Costs

Gaming company Valve has announced a price increase of over 40% for its Steam Deck handheld, citing rising semiconductor, logistics, and manufacturing costs. The adjustment covers all models including LCD and OLED variants. Analysts note the move reflects broader cost pressures facing hardware manufacturers amid recovering consumer electronics demand and trade policy uncertainty.
