## [快讯] 商务部长Lutnick因与Epstein关联遭议员质询

## Commerce Secretary Lutnick Grilled by Lawmakers Over Epstein Ties

According to The New York Times, US Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick faced intense questioning from lawmakers in Congress over his ties to Jeffrey Epstein. Lutnick was pressed during the hearing to clarify the details of his relationship with Epstein, sparking broad discussion about ethical vetting of cabinet officials.
