## 多国追踪汉坦病毒邮轮乘客

美国至新加坡等多个国家的卫生部门正在紧急追踪从汉坦病毒邮轮下船的乘客。此前该邮轮已出现首例死亡病例。

世卫组织表示当前情况不构成大流行威胁，但各国仍在全力排查潜在风险。

## Multiple Countries Track Hantavirus Cruise Contacts

Health authorities from the U.S. to Singapore are urgently tracking passengers who disembarked from a cruise ship after the first hantavirus fatality. The WHO says the situation does not constitute a pandemic threat, but countries are racing to assess potential risks.
