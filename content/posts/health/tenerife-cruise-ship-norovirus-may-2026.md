## [快讯] 公主邮轮暴发诺如病毒，特内里费医疗人员准备接应

据BBC和CBS News报道，一艘从佛罗里达州劳德代尔堡出发的公主邮轮上暴发诺如病毒疫情。船上一名美国肿瘤科医生在疫情期间担任了"临时主治医生"，协助应对疫情。西班牙特内里费岛医疗团队已做好充分准备，迎接该邮轮靠岸并对患病乘客进行医疗处置。

此前，另一艘从劳德代尔堡出发的公主邮轮也报告了诺如病毒疫情，显示邮轮群聚感染风险持续存在。

## Norovirus Outbreak on Princess Cruise Ship, Tenerife Medics on Standby

According to BBC and CBS News, a norovirus outbreak has occurred on a Princess cruise ship that departed from Fort Lauderdale, Florida. An American oncologist aboard the ship served as the de facto head doctor during the outbreak. Medical teams on Spain's Tenerife island are on standby to receive the vessel and provide medical care to affected passengers.

A separate Princess cruise ship departing from Fort Lauderdale also reported a norovirus outbreak, highlighting ongoing cluster infection risks on cruise vessels.
