## [快讯] 汉坦病毒游轮事件 5州监测乘客

一艘大西洋游轮上出现汉坦病毒疫情后，美国5个州正在对船上乘客进行健康监测。相关部门正在追踪所有密切接触者，并向可能暴露于病毒的乘客发布健康警告。

汉坦病毒是一种由啮齿类动物传播的病毒，感染后可能导致严重的呼吸系统疾病。此次游轮疫情引发了公共卫生部门的密切关注，各国正在协调乘客追踪工作。

目前尚无死亡报告，但卫生官员提醒出现发热、呼吸困难等症状的乘客应立即就医。

## Hantavirus Cruise Ship: 5 US States Monitor Passengers

Following a hantavirus outbreak on an Atlantic cruise ship, five US states are monitoring passengers for potential infections. Health authorities are tracing all close contacts and issuing health advisories to those potentially exposed.

Hantavirus, typically spread by rodents, can cause severe respiratory illness. The cruise ship incident has prompted close monitoring by public health departments, with international coordination underway for passenger tracking.

No deaths have been reported so far, but health officials advise passengers experiencing fever or breathing difficulties to seek immediate medical attention.
