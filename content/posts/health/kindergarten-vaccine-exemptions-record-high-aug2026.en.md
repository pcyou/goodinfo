## Core Summary

U.S. kindergarten vaccine exemption rates have reached another all-time high, according to The Washington Post. The trend has raised serious concerns among public health experts, who warn that declining vaccination rates could allow diseases like measles to resurge in communities.

## Details

Data shows a growing number of parents seeking vaccine exemptions for their children on religious, philosophical, or personal belief grounds. This phenomenon has intensified over recent years, with exemption rates rising most sharply in certain politically conservative states.

Public health officials note that maintaining herd immunity requires at least a 95% vaccination rate. Multiple regions have already fallen below this critical threshold, creating conditions for disease outbreaks.
