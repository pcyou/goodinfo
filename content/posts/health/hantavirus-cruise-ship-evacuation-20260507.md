## [快讯] 3 Patients Evacuated from Cruise Ship with Hantavirus Outbreak

美联社报道，一艘大西洋游轮爆发汉坦病毒疫情，3名患者正被紧急撤离至欧洲。经确认，此次爆发为罕见安第斯型（Andes strain）汉坦病毒，该毒株可在人际传播。目前游轮上其余乘客正在接受健康监测。

## 3 Patients Evacuated from Cruise Ship with Hantavirus Outbreak

Three patients are being evacuated to Europe from a cruise ship experiencing a hantavirus outbreak. The rare Andes strain, which can spread between humans, has been confirmed.
