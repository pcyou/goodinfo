## 中文

一架原定飞抵美国底特律的国际航班在飞行途中紧急转降加拿大，原因是机上一名乘客在疾病控制与预防中心的筛查程序中出现了疑似埃博拉症状。

据消息，该航班在例行健康筛查中发现一名乘客出现发热等埃博拉典型症状后，立即启动应急协议，与加拿大卫生部门协调将航班转降至就近机场。目前该乘客已被隔离并接受进一步检测。

此次事件发生之际，刚果民主共和国的埃博拉疫情正在加速蔓延。该国已取消了世界杯训练营计划，多个国际组织报告称西方援助削减严重削弱了当地的疫情防控能力。

世界卫生组织警告称，埃博拉病毒的潜伏期可达二十一天，国际社会呼吁恢复对非洲公共卫生体系的资金支持。

## English

An international flight bound for Detroit was diverted to Canada after a passenger displayed potential Ebola symptoms during routine health screening, underscoring the real risk of the Central African outbreak spreading beyond the continent.

The passenger exhibited fever and symptoms consistent with Ebola infection. Emergency protocols were activated, and the aircraft was redirected to a Canadian airport. The passenger has been placed in isolation pending further testing.

This incident comes as the Ebola outbreak in the Democratic Republic of Congo continues to accelerate. The DRC has canceled its World Cup training camp, and international organizations report that cuts to Western foreign aid have severely weakened local disease containment capabilities.

The WHO warns that Ebola has an incubation period of up to twenty-one days. The international community is calling for restored funding for African public health infrastructure.
