## [快讯] 汉坦病毒游轮疫情：两名乘客为德州居民

卫生官员确认，游轮上爆发汉坦病毒疫情，其中两名受影响乘客为德克萨斯州居民。汉坦病毒主要通过啮齿动物传播，可引起严重的呼吸系统疾病。

## [Brief] Hantavirus Cruise Ship Outbreak: Two Texas Residents Affected

Health officials confirmed a hantavirus outbreak on a cruise ship, with two affected passengers being Texas residents. Hantavirus is primarily transmitted through rodents and can cause severe respiratory illness.
