## [快讯] UnitedHealthcare to Remove Prior Authorization for 30% of Services

UnitedHealthcare announced a major shift in healthcare administration, removing prior authorization for 30% of its services.

联合健康宣布取消30%医疗服务的预授权要求，这是美国医疗管理领域的重大转变。预授权制度长期被医生和患者批评为延误治疗，此次改革有望简化就医流程。
