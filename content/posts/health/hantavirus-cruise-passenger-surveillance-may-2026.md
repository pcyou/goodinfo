## [快讯] 汉坦病毒乘客监测

据《纽约时报》报道，美国多个州已下令对曾乘坐爆发汉坦病毒疫情的邮轮并已返回各自住所的乘客进行健康监测。

汉坦病毒是一种可通过啮齿动物传播的严重呼吸道疾病，此前一艘远洋邮轮上出现多例感染病例，引发国际社会关注。

卫生官员表示，监测措施包括定期体温检查和症状报告，旨在防止病毒在社区中进一步扩散。截至目前，尚未发现大规模社区传播迹象。

世界卫生组织已发布相关旅行建议，提醒公众注意防范。

## [Brief] Hantavirus Cruise: Multiple US States Monitor Passengers Who Return Home

Multiple US states have ordered health monitoring for passengers who sailed on a cruise ship hit by a hantavirus outbreak and have since returned to their homes, the New York Times reports.

Hantavirus is a severe respiratory disease transmitted by rodents. Several cases emerged on an ocean cruise, drawing international attention.

Health officials say monitoring measures include regular temperature checks and symptom reporting to prevent further community spread. So far, no large-scale community transmission has been detected.

The World Health Organization has issued related travel advisories.
