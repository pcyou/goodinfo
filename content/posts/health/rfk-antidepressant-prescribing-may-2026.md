## RFK Jr Moves to Curb Antidepressant Prescribing in US

### 中文

美国卫生部长小罗伯特·肯尼迪正采取措施限制抗抑郁药物的处方，这是其更广泛的精神卫生政策改革的一部分。该举措引发了医学界的广泛讨论。

### English

US Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr is taking steps to curb antidepressant prescribing, part of a broader mental health policy reform that has sparked wide debate in the medical community.
