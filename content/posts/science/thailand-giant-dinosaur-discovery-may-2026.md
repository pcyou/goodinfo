## 泰国出土巨型恐龙新物种

研究人员在泰国发现了一种新的巨型恐龙物种化石。该发现为了解东南亚地区白垩纪时期的生态系统提供了重要线索。研究团队正在对化石进行详细分析，以确定其分类和生存年代。

## New Giant Dinosaur Species Discovered in Thailand

Researchers have unearthed fossil remains of a new giant dinosaur species in Thailand. The discovery provides important insights into the Cretaceous-era ecosystems of Southeast Asia. The research team is conducting detailed analysis to determine its classification and geological period.
