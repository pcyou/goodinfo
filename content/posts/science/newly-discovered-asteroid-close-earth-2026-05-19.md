## [快讯] 新发现小行星将飞掠地球

NASA确认，一颗新发现的小行星将于周一从距离地球比月球更近的位置飞掠而过。天文学家表示该小行星不会对地球构成威胁，但此次近距离飞行为研究提供了难得机会。

## [Brief] Newly Discovered Asteroid to Pass Closer to Earth Than the Moon

NASA has confirmed that a newly discovered asteroid will pass Earth at a closer distance than the Moon on Monday. Astronomers say the asteroid poses no threat to Earth, but the close approach provides a rare opportunity for scientific study.
