## NASA Releases Thousands of New Artemis II Mission Photos

### 中文

NASA 近日公布了阿尔忒弥斯 II 任务期间拍摄的数千张新照片，并精选出 9 张最佳作品向公众展示。阿尔忒弥斯 II 是 NASA 重返月球计划的关键一步，此次发布的照片包括地球全景、月球背面影像以及宇航员在舱内的工作场景。这些图像不仅记录了历史性时刻，也为未来阿尔忒弥斯 III 登月任务提供了宝贵的参考资料。

### English

NASA has released thousands of new photos from the Artemis II mission, highlighting nine of the best images for public viewing. Artemis II is a critical step in NASA's plan to return humans to the Moon. The newly published images include panoramic views of Earth, far-side lunar imagery, and astronauts working inside the capsule. These photographs document a historic milestone and provide valuable reference material for the upcoming Artemis III lunar landing mission.
