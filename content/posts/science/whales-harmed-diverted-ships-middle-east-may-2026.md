## [快讯] 科学家警告：中东冲突导致航线改道或危害鲸鱼

BBC报道，科学家发出警告，由于中东冲突，自2023年以来大量船只改道绕行好望角，这一航线变化可能对鲸鱼种群造成伤害。

船舶改道增加了在南大西洋和印度洋航线上的航运密度，这些区域是多种鲸鱼的重要栖息地和迁徙路线。研究人员指出，船只撞击和噪音污染是主要威胁。

## Whales Could Be Harmed by Diverted Ships Avoiding Middle East, Scientists Warn

BBC reports that scientists have warned whales could be harmed by shipping diversions caused by Middle East conflicts. Since 2023, vessels have rerouted around the Cape of Good Hope to avoid the region, increasing maritime traffic in areas that serve as critical habitats and migration routes for multiple whale species. Ship strikes and noise pollution are the primary concerns.
