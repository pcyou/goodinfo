## [快讯] SpaceX NASA补给任务

SpaceX于5月13日从佛罗里达卡纳维拉尔角发射龙飞船，执行向国际空间站运送补给的任务。

### 任务详情

此次发射是SpaceX为NASA执行的国际空间站商业补给服务任务之一。龙飞船将携带实验设备、生活物资和技术补给前往空间站。

### 背景

SpaceX自2012年以来一直是NASA商业补给服务的关键合作伙伴，定期向国际空间站运送物资。此次任务是该合作框架下的又一次常规补给行动。

## SpaceX NASA ISS Resupply Mission

SpaceX launched a Dragon spacecraft from Cape Canaveral, Florida on May 13, carrying supplies to the International Space Station.

### Mission Details

The launch is part of SpaceX's Commercial Resupply Services contract with NASA. The Dragon spacecraft carries scientific experiments, crew provisions, and technical equipment to the orbiting laboratory.

### Background

SpaceX has been a key partner in NASA's Commercial Resupply Services since 2012, regularly delivering cargo to the ISS. This mission continues the ongoing partnership between the two organizations.
