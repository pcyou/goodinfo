## [快讯] Gene Mutation Behind Human Speech Capability Replicated in Mice

纽约时报报道，科学家成功在实验小鼠身上复制了赋予人类语言能力的基因突变。这一研究为理解人类语言的生物学基础提供了新视角。该突变被认为在人类进化过程中扮演了关键角色，使人类获得了独特的语言表达能力。

## Gene Mutation Behind Human Speech Capability Replicated in Mice

Scientists have replicated in mice the gene mutation that gave humans the gift of speech, offering new insights into the biological foundations of human language.
