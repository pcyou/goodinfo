## 大都会博物馆扩建

纽约大都会博物馆宣布将与附近的Neue Galerie合并，雅诗兰黛继承人将画廊交由大都会博物馆管理。这是该博物馆近年来最大规模的扩建计划，将进一步巩固其作为全球顶级艺术机构的地位。

---

## The Met to Expand Through Merger With Neue Galerie

New York's Metropolitan Museum announced it will expand by merging with the nearby Neue Galerie, with an Estée Lauder heir transferring the gallery to the Met. The move represents the museum's most significant expansion in recent years.
