## [快讯] Pornhub to Become Accessible Again for UK Users Who Complete Age Checks

### 中文

BBC报道，完成苹果设备端年龄检查的英国iPhone用户将不再被屏蔽访问Pornhub成人内容，标志着英国互联网年龄验证政策的新进展。

### English

BBC reports that UK iPhone users who have completed Apple's device-based age checks will no longer be blocked from accessing Pornhub's adult content, marking new progress in the UK's internet age verification policy.
