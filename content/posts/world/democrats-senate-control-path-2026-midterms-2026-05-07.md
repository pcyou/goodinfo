## [快讯] Democrats Eye Senate Majority Despite Hurdles

《华盛顿邮报》报道，民主党在2026年中期选举中看到了重新控制参议院的希望，但仍需跨越多个重大障碍。分析人士指出，尽管共和党在某些选区面临民调劣势，但民主党仍需攻克多个保守州席位才能实现翻盘。

Democrats see a path to Senate control in the upcoming elections, but face significant structural hurdles including unfavorable electoral maps and incumbent advantages in key swing states. Political analysts note that while national polling trends favor Democrats, individual race dynamics remain highly competitive in the districts most likely to determine control.
