## [快讯] 五角大楼公布大量UFO文件

美国国防部（五角大楼）周五公布了一批与不明空中现象（UAP）相关的机密文件。这批文件是持续进行的UFO调查计划的一部分，进一步丰富了公众可获取的相关记录。

此次解密行动反映了美国政府在空中现象透明度方面的持续推进。此前，五角大楼已多次发布与不明飞行物相关的报告和视频。

文件内容包括军方飞行员目击事件的详细记录和分析报告，但尚未包含任何确认外星来源的证据。

## Pentagon Releases Batch of UFO Files

The US Department of Defense (Pentagon) released a significant batch of classified documents related to unidentified aerial phenomena (UAP) on Friday. The release is part of an ongoing government effort to increase transparency around military encounters with unidentified objects.

The documents include detailed records and analysis reports from military pilot sightings, though they contain no confirmed evidence of extraterrestrial origins. This follows a pattern of gradual declassification efforts by the US government on the UAP topic.
