据路透社报道，特朗普表示他将很快决定是否接受一项伊朗协议，并明确强调霍尔木兹海峡必须向国际航运开放。

霍尔木兹海峡是全球最重要的石油运输通道之一，约五分之一的全球石油供应经过该海峡。近期中东局势紧张导致油价大幅上涨，全球经济承压。

分析人士指出，特朗普的这一表态可能预示着美国对伊朗政策的重大转变，但具体协议细节尚未公开。国际市场密切关注事态发展。

---

According to Reuters, Trump said he will soon decide whether to accept an Iran deal and emphasized that the Strait of Hormuz must be open to international shipping.

The Strait of Hormuz is one of the world's most critical oil transit routes, with roughly one-fifth of global petroleum supply passing through it. Recent Middle East tensions have driven oil prices sharply higher, putting pressure on the global economy.

Analysts note that Trump's statement could signal a significant shift in US Iran policy, though details of any potential agreement have not been made public. International markets are closely watching developments.
