## 以色列空袭黎巴嫩南部

据黎巴嫩卫生部消息，以色列于5月13日对贝鲁特南部地区发动一系列空袭，造成22人死亡，其中包括8名儿童。

### 事件详情

此次空袭发生在黎巴嫩南部多个地点，是近期该区域最严重的军事升级之一。以色列军方表示行动针对真主党基础设施，但具体目标尚未公开确认。

### 国际反应

联合国秘书长对此表示严重关切，呼吁各方保持克制。多个阿拉伯国家发表声明谴责空袭造成平民伤亡。

### 背景

自伊朗战争以来，中东局势持续紧张。以色列与真主党之间的冲突虽有所缓和，但零星交火时有发生。此次空袭的规模表明紧张局势可能出现新一轮升级。

## Israeli Strikes in Southern Lebanon Kill 22, Including 8 Children

Lebanon's health ministry reported that Israeli airstrikes hit multiple locations in southern Beirut on May 13, killing 22 people, including eight children.

### Details

The strikes targeted multiple sites across southern Lebanon, marking one of the most significant military escalations in the area in recent weeks. The Israeli military stated the operations targeted Hezbollah infrastructure, though specific targets have not been publicly confirmed.

### International Response

The UN Secretary-General expressed grave concern and called on all parties to exercise restraint. Several Arab nations issued statements condemning the civilian casualties.

### Context

Tensions in the Middle East have remained elevated since the Iran conflict. While Israel-Hezbollah hostilities had somewhat de-escalated, sporadic clashes have continued. The scale of this strike suggests a potential new round of escalation in the region.
