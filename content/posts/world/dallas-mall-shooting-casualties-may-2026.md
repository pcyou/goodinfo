## Two Killed, Three Injured in Shootings Near Dallas Mall

### 中文

达拉斯北部一购物中心附近发生枪击事件，造成 2 人死亡、3 人受伤。警方正在调查作案动机并搜寻嫌疑人。

### English

Two people were killed and three others injured in a shooting near a shopping mall north of Dallas. Police are investigating the motive and searching for suspects.
