## Canada's Carney Appoints Former War Crimes Prosecutor as Governor General

### 中文

加拿大总理马克·卡尼任命前战争罪检察官担任总督，这一罕见选择表明加拿大正试图在国际法和人权领域发挥更积极的作用。该任命被视为卡尼政府外交政策转向的重要信号。

### English

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney has appointed a former war crimes prosecutor as governor general, a rare choice signaling Canada's intent to play a more active role in international law and human rights.
