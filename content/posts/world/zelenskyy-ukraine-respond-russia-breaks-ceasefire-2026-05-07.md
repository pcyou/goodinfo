## [快讯] 泽连斯基：俄罗斯违反停火协议，乌克兰将对等回应

据Euronews报道，乌克兰总统泽连斯基表示，俄罗斯方面违反了停火协议，乌克兰将采取对等措施回应。此前俄罗斯对乌克兰多处目标发动无人机袭击，打破了双方各自宣布的停火安排。

## Zelenskyy: Russia Breaks Ceasefire, Ukraine Will Respond in Kind

According to Euronews, Ukrainian President Zelenskyy said Russia violated the ceasefire agreement and Ukraine will respond in kind. Russia previously launched drone strikes against multiple Ukrainian targets, breaking the ceasefire arrangements both sides had announced.
