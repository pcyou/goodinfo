## 摘要

中国宣布对前国防部长判处死刑，缓期两年执行。据BBC报道，这一判决与腐败调查有关。这是中国军方反腐行动的最新进展，显示出对国防领域腐败问题的零容忍态度。此前中国已对多位军方高级官员展开调查。

## Summary

China has announced suspended death sentences for former defence ministers, according to BBC. The sentencing is related to a corruption investigation. This marks the latest development in China's military anti-corruption campaign, demonstrating a zero-tolerance approach to corruption in the defense sector.
