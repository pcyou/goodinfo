# 以色列对黎巴嫩南部发动新一轮打击，划定新战斗区域

## Israel Launches Fresh Strikes on Southern Lebanon, Declares New Combat Zone

### 中文报道

以色列军方于5月28日对黎巴嫩南部发动新一轮空袭，并宣布将扎赫拉尼河以南地区划定为"战斗区域"。此前以军已对该地区发布撤离令。

据BBC报道，以军此次行动是对黎巴嫩境内目标的持续打击，军事行动集中在扎赫拉尼河以南地带。以军声明称，该地区现已被正式指定为战斗区，军事行动将进一步扩大。

黎巴嫩南部居民对长期冲突已习以为常。尽管国际社会多次呼吁停火，但该地区战事从未真正停止。自以军3月初重新进入黎巴嫩南部以来，多个村庄遭摧毁，以方占领了约十公里的黎巴嫩领土。

此次新一轮打击发生在美伊冲突升级的同一天，中东地区多条战线同时恶化，引发国际社会对地区全面失控的担忧。

### English Report

The Israeli military launched fresh airstrikes on southern Lebanon on May 28, declaring areas south of the Zahrani River as "combat zones." The strikes followed evacuation orders issued to residents in the targeted areas.

According to BBC, the operation represents an intensification of Israel's ongoing campaign against targets in Lebanon. The military stated that the region south of the Zahrani River has been formally designated as a combat zone, with operations expected to expand.

Residents of southern Lebanon have grown resigned to the prolonged conflict. Despite repeated international calls for ceasefire, fighting in the region has never truly stopped. Since Israeli forces re-entered southern Lebanon in early March, multiple villages have been destroyed and Israel has occupied approximately ten kilometers of Lebanese territory.

The fresh strikes coincided with escalating US-Iran tensions on the same day, with multiple fronts across the Middle East deteriorating simultaneously — raising international concerns about the risk of a broader regional conflagration.
