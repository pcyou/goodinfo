## 俄罗斯胜利日阅兵首次无坦克展示，乌克兰战事不顺利信号

BBC报道，今年莫斯科胜利日阅兵近二十年来首次不展示任何军事重装备，仅有士兵方阵行进。分析人士认为，这一异常安排反映出俄军在乌克兰战场上的装备损耗严重，也暗示克里姆林宫对战争前景的信心不足。

## Russia's Victory Day Parade Features No Tanks for First Time, Signal of Ukraine War Struggles

BBC reports that Moscow's Victory Day parade will feature no military hardware for the first time in nearly two decades, with only soldier formations marching. Analysts view this as a sign of severe Russian equipment losses in Ukraine and suggest the Kremlin lacks confidence in the war's trajectory.
