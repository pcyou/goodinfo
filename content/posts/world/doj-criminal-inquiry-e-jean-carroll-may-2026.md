# [快讯] 美国司法部对E·琼·卡罗尔展开刑事调查

## DOJ Opens Criminal Inquiry into E. Jean Carroll

据《纽约时报》报道，美国司法部已对E·琼·卡罗尔(E. Jean Carroll)展开刑事调查。卡罗尔此前曾对特朗普提起民事诉讼并获胜。

据《纽约时报》5月27日报道，司法部已启动刑事调查程序。目前调查的具体性质和范围尚不清楚。

According to The New York Times, the US Department of Justice has opened a criminal inquiry into E. Jean Carroll, who previously won a civil case against Donald Trump. The scope and nature of the investigation remain unclear at this time.
