## 普京誓言报复，指称乌克兰袭击学生宿舍

俄罗斯总统普京周四誓言将对乌克兰进行报复，指控乌方袭击了位于莫斯科占领区东部的一栋学生宿舍。

据BBC报道，乌克兰方面承认发动了此次袭击，但表示其打击目标是驻扎在该区域的俄罗斯精锐"鲁比孔"（Rubicon）无人机军事单位，学生宿舍并非预定目标。

此次事件再次凸显了俄乌冲突中民用基础设施面临的持续威胁。普京的报复威胁可能预示着新一轮军事升级，为已经紧张的局势增添更多不确定性。

自冲突爆发以来，双方多次互相指责对方攻击民用设施。国际社会对此类事件表示严重关切，呼吁冲突各方遵守国际人道主义法。

## Putin Vows Retaliation After Accusing Ukraine of Hitting Student Dormitory

Russian President Vladimir Putin vowed retaliation against Ukraine on Thursday, accusing Kyiv of striking a student dormitory in a Moscow-occupied area of eastern Ukraine.

According to BBC, Ukraine acknowledged carrying out the strike but stated that its intended target was Russia's elite "Rubicon" drone military unit stationed in the area, and the dormitory was not the intended target.

The incident once again highlights the ongoing threat to civilian infrastructure in the Russia-Ukraine conflict. Putin's retaliation threat could signal a new round of military escalation, adding further uncertainty to an already volatile situation.

Since the conflict began, both sides have repeatedly accused each other of attacking civilian facilities. The international community has expressed grave concern over such incidents, calling on all parties to comply with international humanitarian law.
