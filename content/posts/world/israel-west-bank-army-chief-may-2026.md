## Israeli Army Chief: West Bank Trops 'Killing Like Haven't Since 1967'

### 中文

以色列军队最高指挥官发表惊人言论，称驻扎在约旦河西岸的部队正在进行"自196年以来最大规模的军事击杀行动"，引发国际社会对军事升级和人道主义危机的严重担忧。

这一表态在以色列国内外引起了广泛争议。人权组织呼吁联合国立即介入调查，多个国家政府发表声明谴责这种军事行动。

约旦河西岸的局势近年来持续恶化，以色列的军事行动和定居点扩建不断引发巴勒斯坦人的抗议和国际社会的批评。此次军方高层的公开表态，进一步加剧了地区的紧张局势。

### English

Israel's army chief has stated that troops in the West Bank are "killing like we haven't killed since 1967", raising serious international concerns about military escalation and humanitarian crisis.

The statement has sparked widespread controversy both within Israel and internationally. Human rights organizations have called for an immediate UN investigation, and several governments have issued statements condemning the military operations.

The situation in the West Bank has deteriorated in recent years, with Israeli military operations and settlement expansion drawing repeated protests from Palestinians and criticism from the international community.
