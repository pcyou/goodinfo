## [快讯] 特朗普撤销IRS诉讼

特朗普同意撤销对国税局(IRS)的诉讼，以换取司法部设立18亿美元的"反武器化"基金。此举引发关于司法独立性和行政权力平衡的讨论。

## [Brief] Trump Drops IRS Lawsuit in Exchange for $1.8B DOJ Fund

Trump has agreed to drop his lawsuit against the IRS in exchange for the Department of Justice establishing a $1.8 billion "anti-weaponization" fund. The deal has sparked discussions about judicial independence and the balance of executive power.
