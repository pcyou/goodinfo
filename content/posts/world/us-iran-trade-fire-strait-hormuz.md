## 美伊霍尔木兹海峡交火：谁先开的枪？

美国与伊朗在霍尔木兹海峡发生交火，双方均声称是对方率先开火。这场冲突进一步升级了本已紧张的局势，特朗普表示战争将"很快结束"，而德黑兰方面则试图巩固其对这一战略水道的控制权。

霍尔木兹海峡是全球最重要的石油运输通道之一，每天约有五分之一的全球石油供应经过此处。美伊双方在该海域的军事对峙已经持续数周，此次交火标志着冲突进入了一个新的危险阶段。

据CNBC报道，双方在交火后各自发布了声明，指责对方违反了停火协议。美国方面称伊朗船只首先向美军舰艇发射了警告射击，而伊朗革命卫队则声称美军无人机侵犯了其领空。

## U.S.-Iran Clash in Strait of Hormuz

The United States and Iran exchanged fire in the Strait of Hormuz, with both sides claiming the other initiated the engagement. The clash escalates already tense relations as Trump says the war will "be over quickly" while Tehran seeks to assert control over the strategic waterway.

The Strait of Hormuz is one of the world's most critical oil transit chokepoints, with roughly one-fifth of global petroleum supplies passing through daily. The military standoff between the two nations has been building for weeks, and this exchange marks a dangerous new phase in the conflict.

According to CNBC, both sides issued statements after the clash accusing the other of violating ceasefire terms. U.S. officials claimed Iranian vessels fired warning shots at American warships first, while Iran's Revolutionary Guard stated that U.S. drones had violated its airspace.

Pakistan officials have suggested that a temporary truce may be close, though neither Washington nor Tehran has confirmed this. The international community is watching closely as the situation remains volatile.
