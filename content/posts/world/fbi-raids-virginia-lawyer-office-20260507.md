## [快讯] FBI Raids Office of Virginia Democratic Lawmaker

Politico报道，FBI周三突袭了弗吉尼亚州一名民主党州议员的办公室。该议员此前主导了该州的选区重划工作。目前尚不清楚调查的具体原因，但此举引发了政治层面的广泛关注。

## FBI Raids Office of Virginia Democratic Lawmaker

The FBI raided the office of a Virginia Democratic state lawmaker who led redistricting efforts, Politico reported. The reason for the investigation remains unclear.
