# [快讯] 吉尔·拜登称2024年辩论表现令她"感到恐惧"

## Jill Biden Says 2024 Debate Performance Was Frightening

吉尔·拜登(Jill Biden)近日表示，乔·拜登在2024年总统辩论中的表现令她"感到恐惧"(frightened)，她当时担心丈夫可能是"中风了"(having a stroke)。

吉尔·拜登承认辩论表现确实存在问题，但仍坚持认为乔·拜登本应继续参选。

Jill Biden recently said that Joe Biden's performance in the 2024 presidential debate was "frightening," and that she was worried he might have been "having a stroke" at the time. She acknowledged the debate performance was problematic but maintained that Joe Biden should have continued his campaign.
