## [快讯] 苏格兰前领导人丈夫承认挪用54万美元政党资金

据美联社报道，苏格兰前首席大臣尼古拉·斯特金（Nicola Sturgeon）的丈夫彼得·穆雷尔（Peter Murrell）承认挪用苏格兰民族党（SNP）资金约54万美元。

法庭文件显示，穆雷尔将政党资金用于购买咖啡机、高档钢笔以及《侠盗猎车手》（Grand Theft Auto）游戏等个人物品。穆雷尔已于此前辞去SNP首席执行官职务。

此案引发了对SNP财务管理的广泛质疑，也对其政治声誉造成打击。斯特金本人否认与此事有关联。

## Former Scottish Leader's Husband Admits $540K Party Fund Embezzlement

Peter Murrell, husband of former Scottish First Minister Nicola Sturgeon, has admitted to embezzling approximately $540,000 from the Scottish National Party (SNP), according to AP News.

Court documents revealed that Murrell used party funds for personal purchases including coffee machines, luxury fountain pens, and Grand Theft Auto video games. Murrell had previously resigned as SNP chief executive.

The case has raised widespread questions about SNP financial management and dealt a blow to its political reputation. Sturgeon has denied any involvement in the matter.
