## 印度与巴基斯坦冲突一周年：停火仍在维持，但和平遥遥无期

距离印度与巴基斯坦爆发军事冲突已满一年，尽管双方停火协议仍在维持，但实质性进展微乎其微。

### 停火现状

一年来，两国边境基本保持平静，没有发生重大军事冲突。但军事集结仍在继续，双方在克什米尔地区的对峙态势未有任何缓解。

### 外交僵局

尽管国际社会多次呼吁双方重启对话，但新德里和伊斯兰堡之间的外交渠道几乎完全关闭。双方互相指责对方破坏停火协议。

### 地区影响

南亚地区的紧张局势对全球经济产生了连锁反应，能源价格波动和供应链中断仍在持续。

### 国际调停

联合国和多个大国正在努力促成新一轮谈判，但目前尚未取得突破性进展。

## One Year After India-Pakistan Conflict: Ceasefire Holds, But Little Else Does

It has been one year since the military conflict between India and Pakistan erupted. While the ceasefire agreement remains in place, substantive progress has been minimal.

### Ceasefire Status

Over the past year, the border between the two countries has remained largely calm, with no major military clashes. However, military buildups continue, and the standoff posture in Kashmir has not eased at all.

### Diplomatic Deadlock

Despite multiple calls from the international community for both sides to restart dialogue, diplomatic channels between New Delhi and Islamabad remain virtually closed. Both sides accuse each other of undermining the ceasefire.

### Regional Impact

The tense situation in South Asia has created ripple effects on the global economy, with energy price volatility and supply chain disruptions continuing.

### International Mediation

The United Nations and several major powers are working to facilitate a new round of negotiations, but no breakthrough has been achieved so far.
