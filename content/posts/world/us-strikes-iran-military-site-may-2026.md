# 美国对伊朗军事设施发动新一轮打击，中东局势再度升级

## US Launches New Strikes on Iranian Military Sites, Escalating Middle East Tensions

### 中文报道

据多方消息证实，美国军队于5月27日对伊朗境内的多处军事设施实施了新一轮空袭。这是近期中东地区最重大的军事升级行动之一。

此次打击 targeted 伊朗的军事基础设施，美方官员确认行动已执行，但尚未公布具体的打击目标和受损程度评估。这是美国在近期对伊朗军事行动基础上的又一次升级。

目前伊朗方面尚未对此轮打击做出正式回应。分析人士指出，此举可能进一步加剧中东地区的紧张局势，特别是在美伊关系本就脆弱的背景下。

国际社会对此高度关注。联合国及多个地区国家呼吁各方保持克制，避免局势进一步升级。有分析认为，军事行动可能对正在进行的外交斡旋产生影响。

### English Report

The United States has carried out new military strikes against Iranian military sites, according to multiple sources confirming the operation on May 27, 2026.

US officials confirmed the execution of the strikes but have not yet disclosed specific targets or damage assessments. This represents a significant escalation in the ongoing military posturing between the two nations.

Iran has not yet issued an official response. Analysts warn that the strikes could further destabilize an already volatile Middle East, potentially undermining ongoing diplomatic efforts in the region.

The international community has called for restraint, with the UN and several regional nations urging all parties to de-escalate tensions and return to diplomatic channels.
