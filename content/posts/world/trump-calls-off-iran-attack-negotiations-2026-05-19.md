## 特朗普临时叫停对伊朗军事打击，称海湾国家请求推迟

美国总统特朗普表示已取消原定的对伊朗军事打击行动，称海湾国家盟友请求推迟。特朗普在同一天内先威胁伊朗随后又撤回，政策反复引发国际社会关注。伊朗方面正在评估如何回应此前美以联合打击。分析人士认为，当前各方正处于"严肃谈判"阶段，和平协议可能性增加。

## Trump Calls Off Planned Iran Strike, Says Gulf Allies Requested Delay

US President Trump announced the cancellation of planned military strikes on Iran, stating that Gulf state allies requested the delay. In a single day, Trump first threatened Iran and then pulled back, with the policy reversal drawing international attention. Iran is assessing how to respond to previous US-Israeli strikes. Analysts believe parties are currently in "serious negotiations," increasing the possibility of a peace deal.
