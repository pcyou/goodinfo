## [快讯] 委内瑞拉囚犯抗议遭暴力镇压 狱警向示威者开火

## Venezuelan Prisoners Protest Alleged Mistreatment as Security Forces Open Fire

Venezuelan prisoners held a protest over alleged mistreatment, insisting it was peaceful. However, security forces deployed to quell the demonstration opened fire on the protesters, BBC reported. Details of the incident are still being verified.
