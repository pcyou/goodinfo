## [快讯] Tennessee Republicans Propose Gerrymandered House Map

Axios报道，田纳西州共和党人周三提出了一项新的联邦众议院选区地图，被批评为严重偏向共和党。新地图拆分了该州多数黑人聚居的选区，引发公平性争议。此举预计将面临法律诉讼。

## Tennessee Republicans Propose Gerrymandered House Map

Tennessee Republicans unveiled a new U.S. House map that carves up the state's majority-Black district, drawing criticism and expected legal challenges.
