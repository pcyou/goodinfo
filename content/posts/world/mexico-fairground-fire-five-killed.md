## 墨西哥游乐场火灾

墨西哥东南部城市比利亚埃尔莫萨一处游乐场发生大规模火灾，造成五人死亡。火灾发生在一场音乐会期间，据报道当时现场约有13.5万人。

## Mexico Fairground Fire Kills Five

A massive fire broke out at a fairground in Villahermosa, southeastern Mexico, killing five people. The blaze erupted during a concert reportedly attended by approximately 135,000 people.
