## 日本爆发反战抗议：民众反对首相加强国防计划

日本多地爆发大规模反战抗议活动，民众反对首相推动的国防扩张计划。

### 抗议背景

在全球地缘政治局势紧张的背景下，日本首相提出加强国防力量的计划，包括增加军费开支和扩大自卫队活动范围。此举引发部分民众强烈反对。

### 抗议活动

数千名示威者聚集在东京等地，举着"反对战争"、"守护和平宪法"等标语。抗议者认为，日本的和平宪法是战后最重要的制度遗产，不应轻易修改。

### 国际关注

此次抗议引起国际社会广泛关注。周边国家对日本军事扩张计划保持高度警惕，担忧可能引发地区军备竞赛。

### 政治影响

抗议活动反映了日本社会在国防政策上的深刻分歧。支持者认为需要应对日益严峻的安全环境，反对者则坚持和平发展道路。

## Anti-War Protests Rock Japan as PM Pushes for Stronger Defence

Large-scale anti-war protests have erupted across Japan, with citizens opposing the Prime Minister's defence expansion plans.

### Background

Against the backdrop of escalating global geopolitical tensions, Japan's PM has proposed strengthening the country's defence capabilities, including increased military spending and expanding the Self-Defense Forces' operational scope. This has triggered strong opposition from segments of the public.

### Protests

Thousands of demonstrators gathered in Tokyo and other cities, holding signs reading "No to War" and "Protect the Peace Constitution." Protesters argue that Japan's pacifist constitution is the most important institutional legacy of the post-war era and should not be easily amended.

### International Attention

The protests have drawn significant international attention. Neighboring countries remain highly vigilant about Japan's military expansion plans, concerned they could trigger a regional arms race.

### Political Impact

The protests reflect a deep divide in Japanese society over defence policy. Supporters argue the need to respond to an increasingly severe security environment, while opponents insist on maintaining the path of peaceful development.
