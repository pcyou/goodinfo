## 伊朗指责美国违反停火协议

伊朗政府正式指责美国犯下"严重违反"停火协议的行为，使本就脆弱的中东和平进程蒙上新的阴影。

与此同时，美国总统特朗普对正在进行的和平谈判发出了矛盾信号。他一方面表示寻求达成协议，另一方面又强硬表态称"要么是好协议，否则就没有协议"。

霍尔木兹海峡地区的紧张局势持续升级，美国方面仍在对外释放达成伊朗协议的积极信号。这种外交辞令与实地局势之间的落差，令国际社会对和平前景感到担忧。

分析人士指出，当前的和平谈判正处于关键窗口期，任何一方的误判都可能导致局势进一步恶化。

## Iran Accuses US of Grave Ceasefire Violation

Iran has formally accused the United States of a "grave violation" of the ceasefire agreement, casting new doubt on the fragile Middle East peace process.

Meanwhile, President Trump has sent conflicting signals on the peace talks, saying he seeks a "good deal or no deal."

Tensions continue to rise around the Strait of Hormuz even as US officials tout prospects for an Iran deal. The gap between diplomatic optimism and deteriorating ground conditions has raised concerns internationally.

Analysts say the peace negotiations are at a critical juncture, with any miscalculation risking further escalation.
