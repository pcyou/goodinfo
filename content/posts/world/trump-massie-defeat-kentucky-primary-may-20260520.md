## [Brief] Trump Consolidates Republican Control

In the Kentucky Republican primary, a Trump-backed candidate defeated incumbent Thomas Massie. This result further consolidates Trump's influence over the Republican Party. Analysts describe it as a continuation of Trump's intra-party purge, demonstrating his nearly unchallenged political authority within the GOP.
