## 卢比奥会见教皇修好美梵关系

美国国务卿卢比奥与教皇利奥举行友好会晤，旨在修复美国与梵蒂冈之间的外交关系。双方讨论了存在分歧的议题，但助手称会议气氛融洽。

## Rubio Meets Pope Leo to Mend U.S.-Vatican Ties

U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio held a friendly meeting with Pope Leo, aimed at repairing diplomatic relations between the United States and the Vatican. Aides described the meeting as cordial despite discussions of areas of disagreement.
