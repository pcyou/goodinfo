## 特朗普政府点名12名移民拟撤销公民身份

据《纽约时报》报道，特朗普政府已启动法律程序，拟撤销12名已归化移民的美国公民身份。这一举措被认为是本届政府移民执法行动的最新升级。

### 行动细节

此次被点名的人员涉及不同背景，但政府声明中暗示这些人在最初申请入籍时存在"虚假信息"或"隐瞒关键事实"。具体指控细节尚未完全公开。

撤销已归化公民身份在美国法律中被称为"剥夺国籍"（denaturalization），属于极为罕见的执法行动。根据美国法律，只有在证明入籍过程中存在欺诈或严重虚假陈述的情况下，才能剥夺已取得的公民身份。

### 法律争议

移民权益组织对此举提出强烈批评。美国公民自由联盟（ACLU）表示，此类行动可能被政治化，针对特定族裔或政治立场的移民群体。法律专家指出，剥夺国籍案件在美国历史上数量极少，通常仅限于涉及恐怖主义或严重战争罪行的极端案例。

### 政策背景

这一行动与特朗普政府持续的移民收紧政策一脉相承。此前，政府已取消了一项针对新ICE探员的快速培训项目，并在多个方向上加强移民执法力度。

被点名的12名移民有权在法律程序中进行抗辩。由于剥夺国籍案件需要经过联邦法院审理，预计相关法律程序将持续数月甚至数年。

## Trump Administration Moves to Revoke Citizenship of 12 Immigrants

The Trump administration has initiated proceedings to strip 12 naturalized U.S. citizens of their citizenship, according to the New York Times. The government alleges these individuals provided false information or concealed material facts during their naturalization process.

Denaturalization is an extremely rare action in the United States. Under U.S. law, citizenship can only be revoked if it is proven that fraud or willful misrepresentation occurred during the naturalization process. Historically, such cases have been reserved for extreme circumstances involving terrorism or serious war crimes.

Civil rights organizations have condemned the move. The ACLU warned that denaturalization efforts could become politicized and target immigrant communities based on ethnicity or political views. Legal experts note that the number of denaturalization cases in U.S. history is exceptionally small.

The 12 named individuals have the right to contest the proceedings in federal court. Given the complexity of denaturalization cases, the legal process is expected to span months or years.

This action aligns with the administration's broader immigration enforcement agenda, which has also included canceling a fast-track training program for new ICE agents and expanding enforcement operations across multiple fronts.
