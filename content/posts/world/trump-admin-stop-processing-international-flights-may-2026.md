# [快讯] 特朗普政府计划停止处理国际航班入境手续

## Trump Administration Drawing Up Plans to Halt International Flight Processing

据多家媒体报道，特朗普政府正在制定计划，拟停止处理国际航班的入境手续。此举可能大幅改变美国现有的边境管理和移民审查流程。

该计划的具体实施方案和时间表尚未公布，但消息人士称政府正在认真考虑这一选项。航空业界和移民权益组织对此表示担忧，认为这可能导致国际旅行和贸易的严重中断。

According to multiple reports, the Trump administration is developing plans that could halt the processing of international flights entering the United States. The specific implementation timeline has not been disclosed, but sources say the option is being seriously considered. Aviation industry groups and immigration advocates have expressed concerns about potential disruptions to international travel and trade.
