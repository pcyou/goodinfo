## [快讯] 特朗普将赴沃尔特·里德医院接受第二任期第三次体检

据NBC报道，特朗普将前往沃尔特·里德国家军事医疗中心接受体检，这是其第二任期内的第三次例行健康检查。

此次访问引发了外界对总统健康状况的关注。分析人士指出，频繁的健康检查可能暗示存在未公开的健康问题，也可能只是例行预防性措施。白宫尚未公布此次体检的具体内容和预计持续时间。

特朗普此前曾在沃尔特·里德医院因感染新冠接受治疗。作为总统的常规医疗检查通常在该医院进行。

## Trump to Visit Walter Reed for Third Check-up of Second Term

Trump is scheduled to visit Walter Reed National Military Medical Center for a health check-up, his third routine examination of the second term, NBC reported.

The visit has raised questions about the president's health status. Analysts note that frequent medical visits could either indicate undisclosed health concerns or simply be routine preventive measures. The White House has not yet released details about the scope and expected duration of this examination.

Trump previously received treatment at Walter Reed during his COVID-19 infection. Routine presidential medical examinations are typically conducted at this facility.
