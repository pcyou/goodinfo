## 司法部指控耶鲁医学院招生歧视

美国司法部宣布，经调查发现耶鲁大学医学院在招生过程中对亚裔和白人申请者存在歧视行为。这是继最高法院限制高校平权行动裁决后，联邦政府对高校招生政策的又一重大干预。

司法部表示，耶鲁医学院的招生政策导致亚裔和白人申请者的录取机会受到不公正影响。校方尚未对此事作出详细回应。

## DOJ Says Yale Medical School Discriminated Against Asian, White Applicants

The US Department of Justice has found that Yale School of Medicine discriminated against Asian and White applicants in its admissions process. This follows the Supreme Court's ruling limiting affirmative action in college admissions.

The DOJ said Yale's admissions policies unfairly reduced the chances of Asian and White applicants being accepted. The university has not yet issued a detailed response.
