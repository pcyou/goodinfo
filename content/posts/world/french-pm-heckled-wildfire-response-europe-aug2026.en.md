## Core Summary

France's Prime Minister was confronted by residents and opposition lawmakers over inadequate wildfire response measures, according to the BBC. Meanwhile, wildfires continue to spread across multiple European countries, testing firefighting capabilities amid sustained high temperatures and drought conditions.

## Details

Southern France has been hit by severe wildfires in recent days, with thousands of hectares of forest destroyed and multiple residential areas evacuated. Local residents have expressed dissatisfaction with the government's emergency response speed, citing significant shortcomings in early warning mechanisms and firefighting resource deployment.

The Prime Minister was surrounded and questioned by residents during a visit to affected areas, while opposition lawmakers publicly criticized the government's inadequate preparedness for climate-related disasters. Meanwhile, Spain, Portugal, Greece, and other Southern European countries face equally severe wildfire threats, and the EU has activated cross-border firefighting assistance mechanisms.
