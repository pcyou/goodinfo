## 美军向伊朗籍油轮开火，海湾局势骤然升级

**U.S. Navy Fires on Iranian-Flagged Tanker as Gulf Tensions Escalate Sharply**

据《卫报》报道，美国海军向一艘伊朗籍油轮开火，与此同时美国总统特朗普在社交媒体上发布帖子威胁进行更多轰炸。这一事件标志着美伊冲突的进一步升级，尽管双方据报正就停火协议进行谈判。

According to The Guardian, the U.S. Navy has fired on an Iranian-flagged tanker, while President Trump posted on social media threatening further bombing. The incident marks a further escalation in the U.S.-Iran conflict, even as both sides are reportedly negotiating a ceasefire agreement.

此次事件发生在霍尔木兹海峡附近海域，该海峡是全球最重要的石油运输通道之一。美军此举被视为对伊朗海上活动的直接军事回应，可能使正在进行的停火谈判面临新的变数。

The incident occurred near the Strait of Hormuz, one of the world's most critical oil shipping routes. The U.S. military action is seen as a direct military response to Iranian maritime activities and could introduce new complications to ongoing ceasefire negotiations.

与此同时，特朗普表示战争将"很快结束"，称伊朗正在审阅美方提出的和平提案。巴基斯坦作为调解方表示正"努力将此次停火转化为永久结束这场战争"。

Meanwhile, Trump stated the war would be "over quickly," saying Iran was reviewing a U.S. peace proposal. Pakistan, acting as a mediator, said it was "endeavouring to convert this ceasefire into a permanent end to this war."

分析人士指出，美军对伊朗籍船只的开火行动与外交谈判同时进行，反映出美国"以打促谈"的策略——通过军事压力迫使伊朗在谈判桌上做出更多让步。

Analysts note that the U.S. military action against Iranian vessels concurrent with diplomatic negotiations reflects a "fight-to-talk" strategy—using military pressure to force Iran to make more concessions at the negotiating table.
