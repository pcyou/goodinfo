## 事件概述

《星球大战》中卢克·天行者的扮演者马克·哈米尔（Mark Hamill）近日在特朗普家族墓地前发布了一张照片，这一举动引发了白宫的强烈反应。白宫发言人称该行为"令人不安"，批评哈米尔利用已故儿童进行政治表态。

## White House calls Mark Hamill 'sick' after Trump cemetery post

Actor Mark Hamill sparked controversy after posting a photo at the Trump family cemetery. The White House condemned the act, with a spokesperson calling it "sick" and criticizing the use of a deceased child for political messaging. The incident has reignited debates about the boundaries of political expression and celebrity involvement in politics.

Hamill, known for his role in Star Wars and his active social media presence, has been an outspoken critic of Trump. The post quickly went viral, drawing both support and condemnation from across the political spectrum.
