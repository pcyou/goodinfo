## 中文

美国众议院共和党领袖于5月22日突然撤回了一项旨在限制特朗普总统对伊朗军事行动的决议案投票，此举在华盛顿引发广泛讨论。

此前，已有部分共和党议员表示支持该决议案，试图通过立法手段约束总统对伊朗采取军事行动的权限。然而，共和党领导层在最后一刻决定取消投票，被外界视为党内对外交政策分歧的又一次公开信号。

 Axios 报道指出，这一决定反映了共和党内部在对伊政策上的复杂博弈。部分议员担忧过度军事升级可能带来的地区风险，而另一些议员则坚定支持总统的外交与军事策略。

此次事件凸显了美国国会在外交和军事事务上的制衡作用，同时也展现了执政党内部的政策张力。

## English

House Republican leaders abruptly pulled a vote on Thursday aimed at curtailing President Trump's military actions against Iran, sparking widespread discussion in Washington.

Several Republican lawmakers had previously indicated support for the resolution, which sought to limit the president's authority to take military action against Iran through legislative means. However, leadership's last-minute decision to cancel the vote is being seen as yet another public signal of intra-party divisions on foreign policy.

Axios reports that the decision reflects complex maneuvering within the Republican Party over Iran policy. Some lawmakers worry about regional risks from further military escalation, while others firmly support the president's diplomatic and military strategy.

The episode highlights Congress's role in checks and balances on foreign and military affairs, while also exposing policy tensions within the ruling party.
