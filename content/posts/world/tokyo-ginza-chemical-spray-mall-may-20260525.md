## [快讯] 东京银座高档商场发生化学喷洒事件 19人送医

据BBC报道，东京银座六丁目豪华购物中心发生一起化学喷洒事件。警方称一名男子在ATM机附近喷洒了某种物质，至少19人因不适被送往医院。警方正在调查喷洒物质的性质和事件动机。

## Chemical Spray Incident at Tokyo Ginza Mall Sends 19 to Hospital

At least 19 people were taken to hospital after a man sprayed a substance at an ATM inside the luxury Ginza 6 shopping centre in Tokyo, BBC reported. Police are investigating the nature of the substance and the motive behind the incident.
