## 圣地亚哥清真寺枪击案凶手曾被FBI标记

圣地亚哥清真寺致命袭击事件的一名枪手此前曾被联邦调查局（FBI）标记，这一消息引发了对情报失误和国内恐怖主义监控体系的严重质疑。

据《卫报》报道，在袭击发生后的紧张两小时内，警方在应对过程中遭遇了重重困难。事件暴露出执法部门在跟踪和预防潜在威胁方面存在的漏洞。

此次袭击再次引发了关于美国国内极端主义威胁的讨论。国会议员和社区领袖呼吁加强情报共享机制，提高对潜在威胁的预警能力。

圣地亚哥穆斯林社区对此事件表示震惊和悲痛，呼吁团结和反恐行动。当地社区组织正在为受害者家属提供支持。

## San Diego Mosque Shooter Was Previously Flagged by FBI

One of the shooters in the deadly attack at a San Diego mosque had been previously flagged by the FBI, raising serious questions about intelligence failures and domestic terrorism monitoring.

According to The Guardian, police faced significant difficulties during a frantic two-hour response following the attack. The incident exposed vulnerabilities in law enforcement's ability to track and prevent potential threats.

The attack has reignited discussions about the threat of domestic extremism in the United States. Members of Congress and community leaders are calling for enhanced intelligence-sharing mechanisms and improved early warning capabilities.

San Diego's Muslim community expressed shock and grief over the incident, calling for unity and anti-terrorism action. Local community organizations are providing support to victims' families.
