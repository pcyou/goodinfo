## 古巴称CIA局长访问哈瓦那

古巴政府表示，美国中央情报局局长已访问哈瓦那，正值该国面临数十年来最严重的能源危机。古巴全岛因柴油和石油短缺导致大面积停电，民众生活受到严重影响。

分析人士认为，此次访问可能与美国对古巴制裁政策的调整以及能源援助谈判有关。古巴此前指责美国加剧了其能源困境。

## Cuba Says CIA Chief Visited Havana Amid Worsening Energy Crisis

Cuba's government said the CIA director visited Havana as the island nation faces its worst energy crisis in decades, with widespread blackouts caused by diesel and oil shortages.

Analysts suggest the visit may relate to discussions about US sanctions policy and potential energy assistance. Cuba has previously blamed US sanctions for exacerbating its energy crisis.
