## 情报报告：伊朗冲突中中国获重大优势

据《华盛顿邮报》报道，一份最新情报评估显示，在持续的伊朗冲突背景下，中国正获得相对于美国的重大战略优势。该报告指出，中国利用美国在中东的军事投入，在亚太地区扩大了经济和外交影响力。

路透社分析认为，特朗普希望借助北京的力量协助解决伊朗问题，但中国可能有自己的战略考量。分析人士指出，中国近年来持续强化与中东产油国的能源和基础设施合作，伊朗局势进一步凸显了美国战略重心的分散问题。

情报官员表示，中国正通过多边外交渠道扩大在联合国及区域安全机制中的话语权，同时加速推进"一带一路"沿线国家的合作。这份评估加剧了美国决策层对于"双线作战"战略困境的担忧。

---

China gains a major strategic edge over the U.S. amid the ongoing Iran conflict, according to a new intelligence assessment reported by The Washington Post. The report indicates that China is expanding its economic and diplomatic influence in the Asia-Pacific while the U.S. remains militarily engaged in the Middle East.

Reuters notes that while Trump seeks Beijing's help on Iran, China may have its own strategic calculus. Analysts point to China's deepening energy and infrastructure partnerships with Middle Eastern oil producers, which the Iran situation has further highlighted as a strategic concern amid America's divided focus.

Intelligence officials say China is amplifying its voice in the UN and regional security mechanisms through multilateral diplomacy while accelerating cooperation along Belt and Road corridors. The assessment intensifies concerns among U.S. policymakers about the strategic dilemma of maintaining commitments on two fronts.
