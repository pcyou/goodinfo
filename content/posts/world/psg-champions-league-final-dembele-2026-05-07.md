## 欧冠半决赛：PSG淘汰拜仁晋级决赛

巴黎圣日耳曼在欧冠半决赛第二回合中凭借奥斯曼·登贝莱的关键表现击败拜仁慕尼黑，成功晋级本赛季欧冠决赛。

登贝莱在比赛中发挥决定性作用，帮助PSG在激烈的对决中脱颖而出。这场胜利标志着巴黎俱乐部在欧洲顶级赛事中的又一次重要突破。

拜仁慕尼黑未能逆转首回合的劣势，结束了本赛季的欧冠征程。PSG将在决赛中等待另一场半决赛的胜者。

本赛季欧冠联赛竞争激烈，PSG的表现展现了球队在欧战中的持续进步。登贝莱的发挥被认为是本场比赛的转折点。

## Champions League Semifinal: PSG Advance to Final After Defeating Bayern

Paris Saint-Germain reached the Champions League final after a decisive performance from Ousmane Dembélé helped defeat Bayern Munich in the semifinal second leg.

Dembélé played a pivotal role in the match, helping PSG emerge victorious from a fiercely contested tie. The victory marks another significant milestone for the Parisian club in European competition.

Bayern Munich were unable to overturn the first-leg deficit, ending their Champions League campaign for this season. PSG will await the winner of the other semifinal in the final.

The Champions League has been highly competitive this season, and PSG's performance demonstrates the club's continued progress in European football. Dembélé's contribution was seen as the turning point of the match.
