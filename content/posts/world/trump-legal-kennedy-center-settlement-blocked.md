特朗普近期面临两项重大法律裁决。据 Axios 报道，一名法官下令必须从肯尼迪中心移除特朗普的名字。

与此同时，另一家法院暂时阻止了特朗普17.76亿美元"反武器化"和解协议的款项支付。这笔巨额和解金此前引发广泛争议。

两项裁决反映出特朗普在法律层面持续面临挑战。分析人士指出，这些判决可能对其政治影响力产生进一步影响。

Trump faces two significant legal rulings. According to Axios, a judge has ordered that Trump's name must be removed from the Kennedy Center.

Separately, another court has temporarily blocked payouts from Trump's $1.776 billion "anti-weaponization" settlement, which had drawn widespread controversy.

Both rulings reflect ongoing legal challenges for Trump. Analysts suggest these decisions could further impact his political influence.
