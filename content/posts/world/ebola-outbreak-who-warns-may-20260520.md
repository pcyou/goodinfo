## 世卫组织警告：埃博拉疫情持续恶化

世界卫生组织（WHO）就乌干达埃博拉疫情发出警告，称确诊病例和死亡人数可能继续上升。据报道，已有更多人因疑似埃博拉病毒感染死亡，疫情形势令人担忧。

世卫组织表示正在与当地政府和国际合作伙伴协调应对，包括派遣医疗专家团队、提供防护设备和建立隔离治疗设施。但该地区医疗基础设施薄弱，疫情控制面临重大挑战。

国际社会呼吁加强边境监测和快速响应机制，防止疫情进一步扩散至周边国家。

## WHO Warns: Ebola Outbreak Worsening

The World Health Organization (WHO) has issued a warning about the Ebola outbreak in Uganda, saying confirmed cases and deaths are likely to continue rising. Reports indicate more deaths from suspected Ebola infections, raising concerns about the epidemic's trajectory.

The WHO says it is coordinating with local authorities and international partners on response efforts, including deploying medical expert teams, providing protective equipment, and establishing isolation treatment facilities. However, the region's weak health infrastructure poses significant challenges to containment.

The international community is calling for strengthened border monitoring and rapid response mechanisms to prevent the outbreak from spreading further to neighboring countries.
