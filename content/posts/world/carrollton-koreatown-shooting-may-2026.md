## 2 Dead, 3 Injured After Shooting at Carrollton Koreatown Shopping Center

### 中文

CBS News 报道，得克萨斯州 Carrollton 市一处韩国城购物中心发生枪击事件，造成2人死亡、3人受伤。警方表示嫌疑人已被控制。事件发生在 Dallas 郊区的亚裔商业聚集区，具体作案动机仍在调查中。

### English

CBS News reports that a shooting at a Koreatown shopping center in Carrollton, Texas, left 2 people dead and 3 injured. Police say the suspect is in custody. The incident occurred in a suburban Dallas area with a significant Asian-American business community. The motive is under investigation.
