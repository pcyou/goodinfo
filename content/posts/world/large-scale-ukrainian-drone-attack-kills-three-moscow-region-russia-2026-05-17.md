俄称乌克兰大规模无人机袭击致莫斯科州三人死亡

俄罗斯方面表示，乌克兰发动了一次大规模无人机袭击，造成莫斯科州三人死亡。这是近几个月来对俄罗斯首都地区最具破坏性的打击之一。

此次袭击凸显了无人机作战能力的升级，以及冲突影响范围已远远超出乌克兰东部前线。乌克兰近年来大力发展无人机技术，已具备对俄罗斯纵深目标进行精确打击的能力。

莫斯科州官员表示，防空系统拦截了多架无人机，但仍有部分突破防线造成人员伤亡和财产损失。袭击发生后，当地加强了防空部署和安保措施。

分析人士指出，无人机正成为俄乌冲突中改变战场格局的关键武器。乌克兰频繁使用无人机对俄罗斯军事设施和能源基础设施发动攻击，迫使俄方分散防空资源。

Russia Says Large-Scale Ukrainian Drone Attack Kills Three in Moscow Region

Russian authorities reported that a large-scale Ukrainian drone attack has killed three people in the Moscow region, marking one of the most significant strikes on the capital area in recent months.

The attack underscores the escalation of drone warfare capabilities and the expanding reach of the conflict far beyond the front lines in eastern Ukraine. Ukraine has heavily invested in drone technology in recent years and has demonstrated the ability to strike deep into Russian territory.

Moscow region officials said air defense systems intercepted multiple drones, but some broke through, causing casualties and property damage. Security measures have been tightened in the area following the attack.
