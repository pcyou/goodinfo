## 事件概述

美国一名前副警长被陪审团裁定过失杀人罪成立。案件涉及该警长在一名黑人男子进入其祖母家中时将其枪杀，案件引发了关于执法暴力和种族问题的持续讨论。

## Ex-deputy found guilty of reckless homicide

A former US deputy sheriff has been found guilty of reckless homicide in the shooting of a Black man who was entering his grandmother's home. The verdict comes after a trial that drew attention to issues of police use of force and racial justice.

The case has been closely watched by civil rights advocates, who argue it highlights ongoing concerns about law enforcement accountability. The former deputy now faces sentencing, with prosecutors seeking a substantial prison term.
