# [快讯] 特朗普宣布俄乌5月9日起三日停火

美国总统特朗普宣布，俄罗斯和乌克兰将从5月9日（二战胜利纪念日）起实施为期三天的停火。此前双方曾互相指责对方违反各自的停火协议，以掩盖在纪念活动期间的军事行动。

这一宣布发生在美伊之间也发生交火之后。特朗普同时表示，美国与伊朗之间的停火仍然有效，尽管此前出现了暴力冲突。伊朗方面则指责美国进行"鲁莽的军事冒险"，称美国每次在外交解决方案即将达成时便发动攻击。

停火的具体执行细节和各方监督机制尚未公布。

# [Brief] Trump Announces Three-Day Russia-Ukraine Ceasefire Starting May 9

U.S. President Trump announced that Russia and Ukraine will observe a three-day ceasefire starting May 9, coinciding with Victory Day commemorations of the Soviet Union's defeat of Nazi Germany. The announcement comes as both sides accused each other of violating separate ceasefires to cover military operations during the celebrations.

Trump also stated that the U.S.-Iran ceasefire remains intact despite a recent burst of violence. Iran accused the U.S. of "reckless military adventure," claiming American attacks come each time a diplomatic solution is on the table.
