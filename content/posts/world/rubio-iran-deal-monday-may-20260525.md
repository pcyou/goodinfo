## [快讯] 鲁比奥：美伊"实质性"协议可能周一达成 油价跌破关键位

据BBC最新报道，美国国务卿鲁比奥表示，美伊之间可能于周一达成"实质性"和平协议。此前特朗普曾指示谈判代表"不要急于"签约，市场情绪出现反复。

受此消息影响，国际油价进一步走低，跌幅扩大至约5%。亚洲股市普遍走高，投资者押注中东地缘政治风险将显著缓解。不过共和党内部强硬派仍对协议条款提出质疑，最终结果尚存变数。

分析人士指出，若协议如期签署，霍尔木兹海峡通航将逐步恢复，全球能源供应链紧张局面有望缓解。

*Source: BBC World, Reuters*

## Rubio: 'Solid' Iran Deal May Come Monday as Oil Extends Losses

US Secretary of State Rubio said a 'solid' peace deal with Iran could be reached as early as Monday, BBC reported. This comes after President Trump earlier told negotiators 'not to rush' into an agreement, creating market whipsaw.

Oil prices extended losses, sliding about five percent. Asian equities broadly rose as investors bet on a significant reduction in Middle East geopolitical risk. However, hardline Republicans continue to question the terms, leaving the outcome uncertain.

If the deal is signed, shipping through the Strait of Hormuz is expected to gradually resume, easing global energy supply chain tensions.

*Source: BBC World, Reuters*
