# 肯尼亚一学校发生火灾致十六名学生遇难

## School Fire in Kenya Kills Sixteen Pupils, Rescue Operations Ongoing

### 中文报道

肯尼亚一所学校于5月28日发生严重火灾，造成至少十六名学生遇难。当地警方确认了这一消息，并表示搜救行动仍在进行中，伤亡人数可能进一步上升。

据BBC报道，火灾发生在学校宿舍区域，火势迅速蔓延导致多名学生被困。救援人员已赶赴现场开展搜救工作，但目前尚未确认最终的伤亡数字。

这起悲剧再次引发了人们对东非地区学校消防安全的关注。该地区许多学校的建筑设施老旧，缺乏基本的消防设备和应急疏散通道，一旦发生火灾后果往往十分严重。

肯尼亚教育部门已宣布将对此事展开全面调查，并承诺加强全国学校的消防安全标准。

### English Report

A severe fire at a school in Kenya has killed at least sixteen students on May 28, local police confirmed. Search-and-rescue operations are ongoing, and the casualty toll is expected to rise.

According to BBC, the fire broke out in the school's dormitory area and spread rapidly, trapping multiple students. Emergency responders have been deployed to the scene, but final casualty figures have not yet been confirmed.

The tragedy has reignited concerns about fire safety in East African schools. Many educational institutions in the region operate with aging infrastructure, lacking basic fire suppression equipment and emergency evacuation routes — making fires particularly deadly.

Kenya's education department has announced a full investigation into the incident and pledged to strengthen fire safety standards across all schools nationwide.
