## [快讯] 乍得空袭博科圣地，数十名尼日利亚渔民恐遇难

BBC报道，乍得军队对博科圣地据点发动空袭后，数十名尼日利亚渔民可能已遇难。一名渔民领袖告诉BBC，约40人恐在空袭中死亡，或在试图逃离时溺水。

事件发生在乍得湖地区，该区域长期受到博科圣地武装活动影响。目前尚无官方确认的伤亡数字。

## Dozens of Nigerian Fishermen Feared Dead After Chad Air Strikes on Boko Haram

BBC reports that dozens of Nigerian fishermen may have been killed following Chad air strikes on Boko Haram strongholds. A fishermen's leader told the BBC that around 40 people are feared dead — either in the strikes or drowning as they tried to flee. The incident occurred in the Lake Chad region, an area long affected by Boko Haram insurgency. Official casualty figures have not yet been confirmed.
