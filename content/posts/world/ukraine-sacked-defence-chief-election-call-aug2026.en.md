## Summary

Ukraine's sacked defence minister has publicly called for presidential elections, posing a direct challenge to wartime leadership. The rare statement has sparked widespread discussion about potential shifts in Ukraine's political landscape, reflecting accumulating domestic political pressure amid the ongoing war.

## Details

The former defence minister stated in a public declaration that President Zelenskyy should hold presidential elections, arguing that the country needs democratic procedures to reaffirm the legitimacy of its leadership during the continuing war. This statement is seen as a direct challenge to wartime governance.

Ukraine has maintained martial law since the full-scale invasion in 2022, suspending electoral activities. However, as the war becomes protracted, discussions about political legitimacy and democratic processes have intensified.

## Analysis

This event reveals the deep political challenges Ukraine faces under prolonged war pressure. While wartime unity is necessary for crisis response, long-term suspension of democratic processes may undermine government legitimacy and public support.

Politically, the former minister's statement may represent dissatisfaction among some political elites and citizens with the current situation. War fatigue, economic difficulties, and reconstruction pressures are accumulating, and calls for political change may grow stronger.

From an international perspective, Ukraine's political stability is crucial for Western allies. Any internal political turbulence could affect the continuity of military aid and diplomatic coordination efficiency. Balancing democratic legitimacy with national unity during wartime has become a major challenge for Ukraine.

## Perspectives

**Election Advocates**: Argue that elections are needed to confirm government legitimacy during prolonged conflict, ensuring public trust and support for leadership.

**Current Government**: Emphasizes practical difficulties of holding elections before war ends, and concerns it could divert national focus from war efforts.

**International Observation**: Western allies generally support Ukraine's sovereign decisions but monitor how political stability affects war efforts.

**Domestic Opinion**: Polls show rising public anxiety about war outcomes, but views on election timing remain divided.
