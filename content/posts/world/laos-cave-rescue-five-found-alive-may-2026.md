## Five Found Alive After Week Trapped in Flooded Laos Cave

### 中文

救援人员在老挝一处被洪水淹没的洞穴中发现了5名被困超过一周的村民，全部生还。参与救援的潜水员中包括曾参与2018年泰国少年足球队洞穴救援的专业人员。仍有2人失踪，搜救继续中。

### English

Rescuers have found five villagers alive after they were trapped for over a week in a flooded cave in Laos. The rescue team includes divers who participated in the 2018 Thai soccer team cave rescue. Two more villagers remain missing as search operations continue.
