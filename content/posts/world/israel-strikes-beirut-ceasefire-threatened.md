## 以色列空袭贝鲁特郊区

以色列对贝鲁特郊区发动空袭，称击毙了一名真主党高级指挥官。此次军事行动对本已脆弱的黎巴嫩停火协议构成严重威胁。

据《华盛顿邮报》报道，以军战机在凌晨时分对贝鲁特南郊目标实施了精确打击。爆炸声在数公里外清晰可闻，当地居民被迫紧急疏散。真主党尚未对此次袭击作出正式回应。

这是近数周以来以色列在黎巴嫩境内发动的最严重军事行动，国际社会对此表示深切关注。

## Israel Strikes Beirut Suburbs, Threatening Fragile Ceasefire

Israel conducted airstrikes on Beirut suburbs, saying it killed a senior Hezbollah commander near the city. The attack poses a serious threat to an already fragile ceasefire agreement in Lebanon.

According to the Washington Post, Israeli fighter jets carried out precision strikes on targets in southern Beirut in the early morning hours. The explosions were audible from several kilometers away, forcing local residents to evacuate. Hezbollah has not yet issued a formal response to the attack.

This is the most significant Israeli military operation inside Lebanese territory in recent weeks, drawing deep concern from the international community.
