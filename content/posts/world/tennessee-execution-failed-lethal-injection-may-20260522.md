## 中文

[快讯] 美国田纳西州一次死刑执行因监狱工作人员无法找到囚犯静脉而被迫中止。州长李（Bill Lee）随后给予该囚犯为期一年的暂缓执行。此次事件再次引发了对注射死刑程序人道性的争议。

## English

[Flash] A Tennessee execution was called off after prison staff were unable to locate the inmate's vein for lethal injection. Governor Bill Lee subsequently granted the prisoner a one-year reprieve. The incident has renewed debate over the humaneness of lethal injection procedures.
