## [快讯] Pussy Riot Protest Forces Russian Pavilion to Close at Venice Biennale

卫报报道，Pussy Riot成员周三在威尼斯双年展上发起抗议，导致俄罗斯馆短暂关闭。这是俄罗斯重返双年展后遭遇的首次公开抗议行动，反映了围绕俄罗斯参与国际文化活动的持续争议。

## Pussy Riot Protest Forces Russian Pavilion Closure at Venice Biennale

A Pussy Riot protest at the Venice Biennale forced the Russian pavilion to briefly close, marking the first public protest since Russia's return to the exhibition.
