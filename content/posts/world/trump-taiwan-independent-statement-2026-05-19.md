## [Brief] Trump Tells Taiwan Not to "Go Independent"

US President Trump has publicly stated that Taiwan should not "go independent." However, analysts note that Taiwan's public opinion on this issue is complex, and independence is not the mainstream view. The statement has sparked new discussions in the cross-strait-US relationship.
