## Sherrod Brown Wins Democratic Senate Nomination in Ohio

### 中文

NBC News 报道，资深参议员 Sherrod Brown 赢得俄亥俄州民主党参议员提名，为关键 battleground 州的参议院选举奠定基础。

Brown 是俄亥俄州现任参议员，以劳工权益保护和制造业政策著称。俄亥俄州作为摇摆州，其参议院席位归属将对参议院控制权产生影响。

### English

NBC News reports that veteran Senator Sherrod Brown has won the Democratic Senate nomination in Ohio, setting the stage for a key battleground Senate race.

Brown, the incumbent senator from Ohio, is known for his focus on labor rights and manufacturing policy. Ohio's Senate seat could be pivotal in determining control of the Senate.
