## US Sues New York Times Claiming Discrimination Against White Male Employee

### 中文

美国司法部对纽约时报提起诉讼，指控该媒体机构在就业中歧视一名白人男性员工。这是联邦政府对知名媒体组织采取的罕见法律行动。

### English

The US Department of Justice filed a lawsuit against The New York Times, alleging employment discrimination against a white male employee — an unusual federal action against a major media organization.
