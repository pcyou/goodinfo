## 伊朗战争以来政治处决激增 "这可能是你最后一次听到我的声音"

**Political Executions Surge in Iran Since Start of War**

### 中文报道

据BBC独家报道，自2月28日美国和以色列对伊朗发动军事打击以来，伊朗境内的政治处决数量急剧上升。联合国已核实至少32名政治囚犯被处决，这一数字令人震惊。

报道称，多名政治犯在被处决前留下了遗言。其中一名囚犯说："这可能是你最后一次听到我的声音。"这句话揭示了伊朗内部人权状况的急剧恶化。

战争不仅带来了外部的军事打击，也加剧了伊朗内部的政治镇压。反对派人士、记者和活动人士成为主要目标。国际社会对此表示严重关切，但受限于战争局势，外部干预能力有限。

人权组织呼吁国际社会关注伊朗国内的人权危机。联合国人权事务高级专员办事处表示，他们正在收集相关证据，并呼吁伊朗政府立即停止处决政治犯。

### English Report

According to an exclusive BBC report, political executions inside Iran have surged dramatically since the US and Israel launched military strikes on February 28. The UN has verified at least 32 political prisoners have been executed, a staggering figure that underscores the deteriorating human rights situation.

Multiple prisoners left final messages before their executions. One prisoner stated: "This may be the last time you hear my voice," revealing the rapidly worsening human rights conditions within Iran.

The war has not only brought external military strikes but also intensified internal political repression. Opposition figures, journalists, and activists have become primary targets. The international community has expressed grave concern, but external intervention capabilities remain limited due to the ongoing conflict.

Human rights organizations are calling for international attention to Iran's domestic human rights crisis. The UN Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights stated it is collecting evidence and urging the Iranian government to immediately halt the execution of political prisoners.
