## [快讯] 内塔尼亚胡秘密访问阿联酋

以色列方面表示，总理内塔尼亚胡曾在伊朗战争最激烈时期秘密访问阿联酋。但阿联酋方面否认曾接待内塔尼亚胡。

### 争议

以色列外交部发布声明称内塔尼亚胡进行了此次秘密外交行程，旨在协调地区安全策略。然而阿联酋官方予以否认，称没有记录显示内塔尼亚胡曾到访。

### 背景

伊朗战争期间，中东各国围绕安全与能源利益展开了密集的外交活动。内塔尼亚胡此前已推动与多个阿拉伯国家的关系正常化。

## Netanyahu's Alleged Secret UAE Visit

Israel claims Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu made a secret trip to the UAE during the height of the Iran war. The UAE has denied receiving him.

### The Dispute

Israel's foreign ministry stated Netanyahu conducted the covert diplomatic mission to coordinate regional security strategy. The UAE officially denied the visit, saying no records show Netanyahu was in the country.

### Context

The Iran war period saw intense diplomatic maneuvering among Middle Eastern nations over security and energy interests. Netanyahu has previously pushed for normalization agreements with several Arab states.
