## [快讯] 英国大选或暴露政治体系裂痕

**[Flash] UK Elections May Expose Fraying Political System**

《纽约时报》报道，英格兰、苏格兰和威尔士数百万选民将参与即将到来的选举，分析人士警告这可能暴露英国日益脆弱的政治体系。报道称"全面崩溃"的风险正在上升，地方与中央政府之间的分歧持续扩大。

The New York Times reports that millions of voters across England, Scotland and Wales will participate in upcoming elections, with analysts warning this could expose Britain's increasingly fragile political system. The report warns of rising risk of "total collapse" as divisions between local and central government continue to widen.
