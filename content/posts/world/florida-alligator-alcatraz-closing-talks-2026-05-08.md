## 佛州"鳄鱼监狱"移民设施或关闭，联邦与州官员展开磋商

《纽约时报》报道，联邦与佛州官员正在讨论关闭位于该州著名的"鳄鱼监狱"移民拘留设施。该设施因关押条件恶劣而饱受争议。双方正就设施的后续处理和被拘留人员的安置方案进行磋商。

## Florida's "Alligator Alcatraz" Migrant Facility May Close as Officials Negotiate

The New York Times reports that federal and Florida officials are discussing the closure of the notorious "Alligator Alcatraz" migrant detention facility. The site has faced heavy criticism over detention conditions. Both sides are negotiating the facility's decommissioning and detainee relocation.
