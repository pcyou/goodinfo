## [快讯] 白宫枪手此前多次与特勤局发生冲突 法庭文件披露

据BBC报道，此前在白宫附近与特勤局发生枪击事件的嫌疑人，在2025年6月就曾阻碍白宫入口车道，并向特勤局特工声称自己是"耶稣基督"。

法庭文件显示，该嫌疑人并非首次与特勤局发生接触。此前的事件中，他试图强行进入白宫区域，并向安保人员发表混乱言论。此次披露引发了对美国特勤局筛查和预警机制的质疑。

目前尚不清楚该嫌疑人在枪击事件中的具体动机和是否造成人员伤亡。联邦调查局已介入调查。

## White House Gunshot Suspect Had Previous Encounters with Secret Service

Court documents reveal the suspect involved in a recent White House-area shooting incident had previous encounters with the Secret Service. In June 2025, the individual obstructed a White House entry lane and told agents he was "Jesus Christ."

The filings show the suspect was no stranger to Secret Service encounters, having previously attempted to access restricted White House areas while making incoherent statements to security personnel. The disclosure raises questions about US Secret Service screening and early warning protocols.

The FBI has taken over the investigation. Details on casualties and the suspect's precise motives remain under investigation.
