## Andrew Tate Civil Trial Postponed in Britain

### 中文

有争议的网络红人Andrew Tate在英国的民事诉讼被延期。据报道，他正在寻求英国刑事检控专员的保证，确保返回英国时不会被逮捕。

### English

Controversial internet personality Andrew Tate's civil trial in Britain has been postponed. He is reportedly seeking assurance from the Crown Prosecution Service that he would not be arrested upon returning to the UK.
