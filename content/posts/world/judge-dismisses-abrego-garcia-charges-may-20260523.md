## [快讯] 法官驳回人口贩卖指控

一位法官周四驳回了针对Kilmar Abrego Garcia的人口贩卖刑事指控。这是围绕移民执法持续法律斗争中的又一重要进展。

此案与近期美国移民政策的争议性举措密切相关，反映了执法机构与司法系统之间的紧张关系。

## Judge Dismisses Human Trafficking Charges

A judge dismissed criminal human trafficking charges against Kilmar Abrego Garcia on Thursday. The case is tied to ongoing controversial immigration enforcement measures and reflects tensions between law enforcement agencies and the judicial system.
