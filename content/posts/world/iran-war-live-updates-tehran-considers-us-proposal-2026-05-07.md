## 伊朗局势更新：德黑兰考虑美国提案，特朗普施压结束冲突

据《纽约时报》和路透社报道，伊朗方面正在认真考虑美国提出的结束冲突提案。与此同时，特朗普总统加大对德黑兰的外交压力，要求尽快达成和平协议。

据报道，美国特使正在与伊朗代表进行幕后谈判，讨论结束持续数月的霍尔木兹海峡对峙。此前美军在海峡区域执行"自由计划"行动，与伊朗船只发生多次交火。

伊朗外交部长表示，德黑兰愿意在尊重伊朗主权的前提下进行对话。但伊朗同时强调，任何协议必须包括解除制裁和保障其地区安全利益。

特朗普在社交媒体上表示，"伊朗知道他们无法永远封锁海峡，和平是最好的选择。"但他也警告，如果伊朗不认真对待谈判，美国将采取进一步行动。

欧洲国家也对局势表示关注，法国已派遣戴高乐号航母前往红海区域，以保护国际航运自由。中国外交部呼吁各方保持克制，通过对话解决问题。

市场方面，投资者对和平协议前景持乐观态度。道琼斯指数今日大涨600点，标准普尔500指数首次突破7,300点。

## Iran Situation Update: Tehran Considers US Proposal as Trump Ramps Up Pressure

According to The New York Times and Reuters, Iran is seriously considering a US proposal to end the ongoing conflict. Meanwhile, President Trump is increasing diplomatic pressure on Tehran to reach a peace deal quickly.

US envoys are reportedly conducting behind-the-scenes negotiations with Iranian representatives, discussing an end to the months-long standoff in the Strait of Hormuz. The US military had been executing "Project Freedom" operations in the strait area, with multiple engagements against Iranian vessels.

Iran's foreign minister stated that Tehran is willing to engage in dialogue provided Iranian sovereignty is respected. However, Iran emphasized that any agreement must include sanctions relief and guarantees for its regional security interests.

Trump said on social media, "Iran knows they can't block the strait forever, and peace is the best option." But he also warned that further actions would follow if Iran does not take negotiations seriously.

European nations have also expressed concern. France has dispatched the Charles de Gaulle aircraft carrier to the Red Sea area to protect freedom of international navigation. China's foreign ministry called on all parties to exercise restraint and resolve issues through dialogue.

Markets responded optimistically to the peace deal prospects, with the Dow surging 600 points and the S&P 500 closing above 7,300 for the first time.
