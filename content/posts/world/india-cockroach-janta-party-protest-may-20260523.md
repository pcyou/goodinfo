## [快讯] 印度网络抗议运动崛起

据CBS报道，印度Z世代领导的网络抗议运动"蟑螂人民党"（Cockroach Janta Party）迅速获得势头，令该国政治领导人感到不安。这场运动将蟑螂这一卑微的生物转变为政治抗议的象征，在社交媒体上引发广泛共鸣。

## India's Online Protest Movement Gains Momentum

According to CBS, India's Gen Z-led online protest movement, dubbed the "Cockroach Janta Party," has rapidly gained momentum and appears to be unsettling the country's political leadership. The movement has transformed the lowly cockroach into a symbol of political protest, resonating widely on social media.
