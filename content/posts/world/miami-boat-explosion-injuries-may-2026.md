## [快讯] 迈阿密疑似船只爆炸致11人受伤送医

据BBC和《卫报》报道，美国迈阿密发生一起疑似船只爆炸事故，至少11人被送往医院接受治疗。当地应急部门已赶赴现场，事故具体原因仍在调查中。

## Suspected Boat Explosion Injures 11 in Miami

According to BBC and The Guardian, a suspected boat explosion occurred in Miami, with at least 11 people sent to hospital for treatment. Emergency services have responded to the scene and the exact cause of the incident is under investigation.
