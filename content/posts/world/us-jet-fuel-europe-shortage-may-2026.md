## [快讯] 美国航空燃料或输往欧洲缓解短缺

美国正在考虑允许航空燃料出口至欧洲，以帮助缓解可能影响航空业的供应短缺。这一措施反映出全球能源供应链正面临新的压力。

据BBC报道，欧洲部分地区可能面临航空燃料供应紧张的局面，美国方面的援助将有助于稳定航空市场。中东地区的局势紧张也可能是推动这一决策的因素之一。

## US Jet Fuel Could Flow to Europe to Ease Potential Shortages

The United States is considering allowing jet fuel exports to Europe to help ease potential supply shortages affecting the aviation industry. The move reflects new pressures on global energy supply chains.

According to BBC, parts of Europe may face jet fuel supply constraints, and US assistance would help stabilize aviation markets. Tensions in the Middle East may also be a factor driving this consideration.
