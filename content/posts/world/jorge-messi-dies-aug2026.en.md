## Core Summary

Jorge Messi, the father and former agent of football icon Lionel Messi, died on August 8 at the age of 68 from a heart attack in Rosario, Argentina. He was widely regarded as the most influential figure in shaping his son's extraordinary career, from discovering his talent as a young boy to managing his business affairs throughout his rise to becoming one of the greatest footballers in history.

## Event Details

According to NBC News, Jorge Messi suffered a fatal heart attack in his hometown of Rosario, Argentina. Tributes poured in from across the football world following the announcement.

Born in Rosario in 1958, Jorge was an amateur footballer who introduced Lionel to the sport at age four, enrolling him in Newell's Old Boys youth academy. When Lionel was diagnosed with growth hormone deficiency at age 11, Jorge tirelessly sought treatment options, ultimately facilitating the legendary move to Barcelona's La Masia academy.

Jorge served as his son's agent for many years, managing commercial contracts and brand partnerships including deals with Adidas and Pepsi. Despite his son's global fame, Jorge remained notably private, rarely appearing in public.

## Panoramic Perspective

Jorge Messi's passing marks the end of an era in football history. His journey from the streets of Rosario to the Camp Nou represents one of the most remarkable father-son partnerships in sports. He exemplified the "family agent" model in modern professional athletics, where relatives manage players' careers to avoid conflicts of interest with external agents.

The football community has responded with widespread condolences. Barcelona, the Argentine Football Association, and Inter Miami all issued statements. Messi, currently playing for Inter Miami in Major League Soccer, is expected to handle family arrangements in the coming days.
