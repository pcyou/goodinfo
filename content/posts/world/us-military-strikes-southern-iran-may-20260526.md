## 美军宣布对伊朗南部发动新一轮打击

美国军方今日宣布，已对伊朗南部目标发动新一轮军事打击。此次行动发生在伊朗与美国就结束冲突进行谈判的关键时刻，使地区局势进一步紧张。

据美军发言人透露，打击行动针对伊朗南部多个军事设施。此次打击的规模和具体目标尚未完全公开，但表明美军在中东地区的军事行动仍在持续升级。

这一军事行动与此前报道称伊朗与美国正在就停火框架达成协议形成鲜明对比。伊朗方面此前表示，尽管谈判取得一定进展，但双方在关键问题上仍存在重大分歧，协议达成并非迫在眉睫。

### 地区局势持续紧张

与此同时，以色列总理内塔尼亚胡宣布计划加大对黎巴嫩真主党的军事打击力度，进一步加剧了中东地区的紧张局势。

霍尔木兹海峡作为全球最重要的石油运输通道之一，近期的军事活动引发了国际社会对全球能源供应安全的担忧。分析人士指出，任何影响该海峡通航能力的冲突都将对全球油价产生重大影响。

### 市场反应

受伊朗局势影响，国际油价出现剧烈波动。在有关伊美协议可能达成的消息传出后，油价一度大幅下跌，但随后因军事打击消息再度回升。

## US Military Announces New Strikes on Southern Iran

The US military has announced new strikes on targets in southern Iran, escalating tensions even as negotiations between Iran and the United States over ending the conflict continue.

According to a military spokesperson, the strikes targeted multiple military facilities in southern Iran. The exact scale and targets have not been fully disclosed, but the operation indicates ongoing escalation of US military activity in the Middle East.

The strikes come despite reports that Iran and the US have been negotiating a framework agreement to end hostilities. Iranian officials have cautioned that while progress has been made, significant differences remain on key issues and a deal is not imminent.

### Regional Tensions Escalate

Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu has also announced plans to intensify military operations against Hezbollah in Lebanon, further heightening tensions across the Middle East.

The Strait of Hormuz, one of the world's most critical oil shipping routes, has seen increased military activity, raising international concerns about global energy supply security. Analysts note that any disruption to navigation in the strait would have significant impacts on global oil prices.
