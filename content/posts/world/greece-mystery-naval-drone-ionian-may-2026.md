## [快讯] 希腊在爱奥尼亚海发现神秘海军无人机

希腊当局正在对爱奥尼亚海发现的一架不明海军无人机展开调查。该无人机的来源和用途尚不清楚，其出现在这一敏感海域引发了各方关注。爱奥尼亚海位于希腊西部，是地中海的重要航道。目前希腊海军已介入调查，无人机的归属尚未确认。

## [Brief] Greece Investigates Mystery Naval Drone in Ionian Sea

Greek authorities are investigating an unidentified naval drone discovered in the Ionian Sea. The origin and purpose of the drone remain unclear. Its appearance in the strategically sensitive waters has drawn attention. The Greek navy has been called in, and the drone's ownership has yet to be determined.
