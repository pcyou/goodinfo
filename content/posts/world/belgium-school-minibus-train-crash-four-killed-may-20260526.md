比利时发生一起校车与火车相撞事故，造成四人遇难，其中包括两名学生、一名带队老师和校车司机。比利时部长确认了这起悲剧事故，具体原因正在调查中。

A school minibus collided with a train in Belgium, killing four people including two schoolchildren, their chaperone, and the driver. A Belgian minister confirmed the crash; the cause is under investigation.
