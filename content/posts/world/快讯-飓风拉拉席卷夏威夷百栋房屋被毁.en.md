## Event Summary

According to BBC, Hurricane Lala has swept across the Hawaiian Islands, with about 100 homes destroyed by waves and flooding. Hawaii's governor stated that "amazingly" there has been no loss of life besides a car crash.

Weather authorities report that Lala is currently moving southwest of the smaller Hawaiian Isles, expected to bring more rain and wind. Residents of the smaller islands have been asked to remain vigilant against flooding and landslides.

Local authorities have activated emergency evacuation procedures, with hundreds of residents relocated to safety. The Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) has dispatched teams to assess damage in the disaster area.
