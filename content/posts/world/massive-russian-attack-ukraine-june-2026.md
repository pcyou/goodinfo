## 俄语大规模空袭乌克兰 基辅高层公寓被击中

2026年6月2日，俄罗斯对乌克兰多个城市发动大规模导弹和无人机袭击，造成至少9人死亡，多人受伤。

基辅市长维塔利·克利钦科表示，两栋高层公寓楼被直接击中，目前仍有人员被困在废墟下，救援工作正在进行中。

此次袭击是近几个月来俄罗斯对乌克兰基础设施最猛烈的一次打击。乌克兰防空部队启动了紧急防御机制，但部分导弹仍突破了防线。

国际方面，欧盟和美国对此次袭击表示强烈谴责。联合国秘书长呼吁立即停止针对平民设施的军事行动。

## Russia Launches Massive Attack on Ukraine, Killing at Least 9

Russia has launched a massive missile and drone attack on multiple Ukrainian cities on June 2, 2026, killing at least 9 people and injuring dozens.

Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko reported that two high-rise apartment buildings were directly hit, with fears that people remain trapped under the rubble. Emergency rescue operations are underway.

This marks one of the most intense strikes on Ukrainian civilian infrastructure in recent months. Ukraine's air defense forces activated emergency protocols, but some missiles penetrated the defensive line.

The EU and US condemned the attack. The UN Secretary-General called for an immediate halt to military operations targeting civilian facilities.
