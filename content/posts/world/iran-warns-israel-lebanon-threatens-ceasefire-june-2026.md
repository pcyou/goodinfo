## 伊朗警告以色列行动威胁停火谈判

据BBC报道，伊朗警告称以色列在黎巴嫩的军事行动威胁到与美国的停火谈判。伊朗已暂停与美国的双边谈判，并誓言要介入黎巴嫩局势。BBC分析指出，特朗普急于结束冲突，但伊朗方面不愿退让，局势仍然复杂。

## Iran Warns Israeli Actions Threaten US Ceasefire Talks

Iran has warned that Israeli military actions in Lebanon threaten ceasefire negotiations with the US. Iran has suspended bilateral talks and vowed to intervene in the Lebanon situation. BBC analysis notes Trump is pushing to end the conflict but Iran is not backing down, keeping the situation complex.
