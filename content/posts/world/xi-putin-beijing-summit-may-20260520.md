## 习近平普京北京峰会：中俄深化战略协作

2026年5月20日，中国国家主席习近平在北京会晤到访的俄罗斯总统普京，这是特朗普与普京会晤后不久的又一次高层外交活动。习近平在会晤中警告称，国际秩序正面临"丛林法则"的威胁，呼吁建立更加公平的多边体系。

此次峰会被视为中俄两国在美国特朗普政府推行"美国优先"政策背景下的战略协调。双方预计将签署一系列经贸和能源合作协议，并在地缘政治议题上发出共同声音。

分析人士指出，中俄元首在北京会晤的时机意味深长——就在特朗普完成对国内政治对手的清洗后不久，全球权力格局正在重新洗牌。习近平强调反对单边制裁，呼吁通过对话解决国际争端，这一立场与普京的外交路线高度一致。

此次会晤也释放出一个信号：面对西方持续的压力，中俄两国将继续深化在能源、贸易和技术领域的合作。

## Xi-Putin Beijing Summit: Strategic Coordination Deepens

Chinese President Xi Jinping met with Russian President Vladimir Putin in Beijing on May 20, 2026, marking another high-level diplomatic encounter shortly after Trump's own meeting with Putin. Xi warned at the summit that the international order faces the threat of "law of the jungle," calling for a more equitable multilateral system.

The summit is seen as strategic coordination between China and Russia against the backdrop of the Trump administration's "America First" policy. The two sides are expected to sign a series of economic, trade, and energy cooperation agreements while sending a unified voice on geopolitical issues.

Analysts note the timing is significant — coming shortly after Trump consolidated his grip on the Republican Party, the global power landscape is being reshuffled. Xi emphasized opposition to unilateral sanctions and advocated resolving international disputes through dialogue, a stance closely aligned with Putin's diplomatic approach.

The meeting signals that China and Russia will continue deepening cooperation in energy, trade, and technology in the face of ongoing Western pressure.
