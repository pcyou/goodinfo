## 田纳西州共和党通过新选区地图，拆分唯一多数黑人选区

田纳西州共和党议员通过了一项新的国会选区地图，将该州唯一的多数黑人选区拆分。此举被批评为旨在削弱少数族裔投票影响力的党派重划选区行为。NBC新闻报道，新地图预计将在联邦法院面临法律挑战，民权组织已表示将提起诉讼。该州目前在国会众议院有9个席位，此次重划可能影响2026年中期选举的席位分布。

## Tennessee GOP Passes Map Splitting State's Only Majority-Black District

Tennessee Republican legislators passed a new congressional district map that splits the state's only majority-Black district. Critics denounce the move as partisan gerrymandering aimed at diluting minority voting power. NBC News reports the map is expected to face legal challenges in federal court, with civil rights organizations already preparing lawsuits.
