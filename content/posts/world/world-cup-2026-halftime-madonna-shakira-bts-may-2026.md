## [快讯] 麦当娜、夏奇拉与BTS将领衔2026世界杯决赛中场秀

据Billboard和《纽约时报》报道，国际足联（FIFA）宣布麦当娜、夏奇拉和韩国组合BTS将共同担任2026年世界杯决赛中场表演嘉宾。这是世界杯历史上首次由多位国际巨星联合呈现中场演出，预计将吸引全球数亿观众。

---

FIFA has announced that Madonna, Shakira, and K-pop group BTS will jointly headline the 2026 World Cup Final halftime show. This marks the first time in World Cup history that multiple global superstars will share the halftime stage, expected to draw hundreds of millions of viewers worldwide.
