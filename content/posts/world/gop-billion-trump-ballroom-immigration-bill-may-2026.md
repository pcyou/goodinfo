## GOP Proposes $1 Billion for Trump Ballroom Project in Immigration Bill

### 中文

共和党议员在一项移民法案中提议拨款 10 亿美元，用于资助特朗普宴会厅建设项目，此举因将移民政策与个人建设资金混在一起而遭到批评。

### English

Republican lawmakers proposed allocating $1 billion in an immigration bill to fund a Trump ballroom construction project, drawing criticism for mixing immigration policy with personal construction funding.
