## 事件概述

英国地方选举结果陆续出炉，首相基尔·斯塔默领导的工党遭遇重大挫折，而纳齐姆·扎赫awi领导的改革党（Reform UK）取得了显著的选举胜利。面对党内要求辞职的压力，斯塔默明确表示不会辞职，将完成首相任期。

## Starmer refuses to quit after Labour election setbacks

UK Prime Minister Keir Starmer has refused to resign after Labour suffered significant losses in local elections across England, with Reform UK making major gains. Starmer said he would "not be walking away" and would focus on delivering for the British people.

The election results are being seen as a major test of public confidence in the Labour government. Reform UK's surge reflects growing voter dissatisfaction with both major parties.
