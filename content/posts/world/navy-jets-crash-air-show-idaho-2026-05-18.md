## 爱达荷州航展两架海军战机空中相撞 4名机组人员弹射逃生

**2 Navy Jets Crash During Air Show at Mountain Home Air Force Base in Idaho**

### 中文报道

据ABC7洛杉矶报道，爱达荷州芒廷霍姆空军基地在举办航展期间发生严重事故：两架F-18战斗机在空中相撞后坠毁。幸运的是，4名机组人员全部成功弹射逃生，目前情况稳定。

事故发生时，现场有大量观众正在观看飞行表演。相撞瞬间的画面被多段视频记录下来，在社交媒体上迅速传播。航展主办方立即启动了紧急预案，暂停了后续飞行表演。

美国海军已成立调查小组，对事故原因展开全面调查。初步分析认为，事故可能与飞行表演中的近距离编队动作有关。军方表示，机组人员的安全是首要任务，调查将查明事故的全部细节。

芒廷霍姆空军基地是美国空军重要的战斗机训练基地，每年举办的航展吸引了大量航空爱好者。此次事故将对未来航展的安全管理产生重要影响。

### English Report

According to ABC7 Los Angeles, a serious incident occurred at Mountain Home Air Force Base in Idaho during an air show: two F-18 fighter jets collided mid-air and crashed. Fortunately, all four crew members successfully ejected and are currently in stable condition.

The crash happened while a large audience was watching the aerial demonstration. Video footage of the collision was captured by multiple recordings and spread rapidly on social media. Air show organizers immediately activated emergency protocols and suspended subsequent flight performances.

The US Navy has established an investigation team to conduct a comprehensive inquiry into the cause of the accident. Initial analysis suggests the crash may be related to close-formation maneuvers during the air show. The military stated that crew safety is the top priority and the investigation will determine all details of the incident.

Mountain Home Air Force Base is an important US Air Force fighter training facility, and its annual air show attracts numerous aviation enthusiasts. This incident will have significant implications for future air show safety management.
