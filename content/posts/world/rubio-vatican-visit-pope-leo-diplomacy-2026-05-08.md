## 鲁比奥访问梵蒂冈，寻求修复白宫与教皇利奥的关系

美国国务卿鲁比奥访问罗马梵蒂冈，旨在修复白宫与教皇利奥之间的外交关系。据《华盛顿邮报》报道，此前美梵关系因政策分歧出现紧张，此次访问被视为缓和双边关系的重要外交举措。

## Rubio Visits Vatican to Repair White House Ties with Pope Leo

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio visited the Vatican in Rome, aiming to repair diplomatic relations between the White House and Pope Leo. The Washington Post reports that US-Vatican ties had strained over policy differences, making this visit a significant diplomatic outreach.
