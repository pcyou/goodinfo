## US Agency Sues New York Times, Accusing It of Bias Against White Male Employee

### 中文

《华盛顿邮报》报道，美国联邦机构对纽约时报提起诉讼，指控该报在雇佣和晋升中对白人男性员工存在系统性偏见。这是近年来少数针对主流新闻机构的就业歧视诉讼之一。纽约时报尚未对此诉讼作出回应。此案可能引发关于新闻行业多元化和公平雇佣实践的更广泛讨论。

### English

The Washington Post reports that a US federal agency has filed a lawsuit against The New York Times, alleging systematic bias against white male employees in hiring and promotion decisions. This is one of the few employment discrimination lawsuits against a major news organization in recent years. The New York Times has not yet responded to the suit. The case could spark broader discussions about diversity and fair hiring practices in the journalism industry.
