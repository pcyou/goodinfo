## 中文

[快讯] 民主党全国委员会（DNC）发布了备受瞩目的2024年大选"尸检"报告，重新揭开了哈里斯败选带来的党内创伤。报告详细指出了竞选策略、信息传递和选民动员方面的多项关键失误，引发了新一轮党内反思与争论。

## English

[Flash] The Democratic National Committee released its long-awaited autopsy report on the 2024 election loss, reopening wounds within the party. The report details multiple critical failures in campaign strategy, messaging, and voter mobilization, sparking a new round of internal reflection and debate.
