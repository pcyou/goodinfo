## Event Summary

According to CBS News, nearly 1,000 aftershocks have been recorded following a powerful earthquake in Indonesia, with the death toll rising to at least 53. The earthquake caused widespread building collapses and severe infrastructure damage. Rescue teams are searching the rubble for survivors, but continuous aftershocks are challenging relief efforts.

Indonesia's National Disaster Management Agency stated that the government has activated an emergency response mechanism, mobilizing national resources to support disaster areas. The international community has also expressed condolences and pledged assistance. Seismologists warn that aftershocks may continue for weeks and advise the public to remain vigilant.
