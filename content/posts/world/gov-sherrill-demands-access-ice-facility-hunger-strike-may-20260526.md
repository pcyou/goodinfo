## 新泽西州长要求进入ICE设施，绝食抗议扩大 [快讯]

新泽西州长谢里尔要求进入ICE拘留设施，原因是该设施内绝食抗议正在扩大。州长表示关注被拘留者的待遇和健康状况。

New Jersey Governor Sherrill demanded access to an ICE detention facility as a hunger strike widens. The governor expressed concern about detainees' treatment and health conditions.
