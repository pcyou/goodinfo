## UK Counter-Terror Police Investigate Arson at Former London Synagogue

### 中文

英国反恐警察已对伦敦一座前犹太教堂的纵火事件展开调查。英国首相就此事件发出警告，并对伊朗局势表达了关注。

据BBC报道，警方已将此次火灾定性为恐怖主义相关事件，正在调查火灾的起因和动机。该犹太教堂此前已不再用于宗教活动，但此次事件仍在英国犹太社区引发了强烈不安。

首相在公开声明中谴责了这起袭击行为，表示政府将采取一切必要措施保护宗教场所和少数群体社区的安全。内政部表示将加强全国宗教场所的安保措施。

这起事件发生在英国反恐警戒级别维持较高等级的背景下，凸显了当前欧洲面临的宗教极端主义威胁。

### English

UK counter-terrorism police have launched an investigation into arson at a former synagogue in London. The Prime Minister issued a warning regarding the incident and expressed concern about the broader situation involving Iran.

According to BBC reports, police have classified the fire as a terrorism-related incident and are investigating its cause and motive. Although the synagogue was no longer in active use for religious services, the incident has caused significant alarm within the UK's Jewish community.

The Prime Minister condemned the attack in a public statement, saying the government would take all necessary measures to protect religious sites and minority communities. The Home Office said it would strengthen security measures at religious sites nationwide.

The incident occurs against the backdrop of the UK maintaining an elevated counter-terrorism alert level, highlighting the ongoing threat of religious extremism in Europe.
