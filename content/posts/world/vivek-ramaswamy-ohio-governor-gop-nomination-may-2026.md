## Vivek Ramaswamy Wins GOP Nomination for Ohio Governor

### 中文

BBC 报道，企业家兼前总统候选人 Vivek Ramaswamy 赢得俄亥俄州州长共和党提名。

Ramaswamy 曾在 2024 年短暂参选共和党总统提名，后退出并支持特朗普。此次俄亥俄州长竞选标志着他重新进入选举政治。俄亥俄州作为关键摇摆州，其州长选举结果将对 2026 年中期选举格局产生影响。

与此同时，Sherrod Brown 赢得了俄亥俄州民主党参议员提名，预计将在该州展开一场激烈的参议院角逐。

### English

BBC reports that entrepreneur and former presidential candidate Vivek Ramaswamy has won the Republican nomination for governor of Ohio.

Ramaswamy briefly ran for the 2024 Republican presidential nomination before dropping out and endorsing Trump. This Ohio gubernatorial race marks his return to electoral politics. As a key swing state, Ohio's governor race could influence the 2026 midterm landscape.

Meanwhile, Sherrod Brown won the Democratic Senate nomination in Ohio, setting up a closely contested Senate race in the state.
