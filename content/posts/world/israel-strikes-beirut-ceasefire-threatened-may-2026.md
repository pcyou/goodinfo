## 以色列空袭贝鲁特郊区，脆弱停火面临严峻考验

据《华盛顿邮报》报道，以色列对贝鲁特郊区发动空袭，这是自真主党停火协议生效以来以色列首次对该地区实施打击。此次军事行动严重威胁了本就脆弱的停火框架，引发国际社会对中东局势再度升级的担忧。

空袭目标位于贝鲁特南郊，该区域长期以来是真主党的重要据点。分析人士指出，此次打击可能标志着以色列对黎巴嫩政策的重大转变，也可能反映出对停火条款执行情况的分歧。

目前，黎巴嫩方面尚未对此次空袭作出正式回应。联合国驻黎巴嫩临时部队（UNIFIL）已呼吁双方保持克制，避免局势进一步恶化。中东地区的和平进程正面临近年来最严峻的考验之一。

## Israel Strikes Beirut Suburbs, Threatening Fragile Ceasefire

Israel has launched airstrikes on Beirut's southern suburbs, marking the first such attack on the area since the Hezbollah ceasefire agreement came into effect, according to The Washington Post. The military action severely threatens an already fragile ceasefire framework, raising international concerns about renewed escalation in the Middle East.

The strikes targeted areas in southern Beirut that have long served as key Hezbollah strongholds. Analysts suggest this operation may signal a significant shift in Israel's Lebanon policy and could reflect disagreements over the implementation of ceasefire terms.

Lebanon has not yet issued an official response to the strikes. The UN Interim Force in Lebanon (UNIFIL) has called on both sides to exercise restraint and avoid further deterioration of the situation. The Middle East peace process is facing one of its most severe tests in recent years.
