## 卢旺达歌手在获释途中去世，曾为政府批评者

一名卢旺达前大学讲师兼歌手在被释放从监狱途中去世。该歌手曾是卢旺达爱国阵线政府和执政党的公开批评者。其死因尚待调查，人权组织呼吁进行独立调查。

## Rwandan Singer Dies During Prison Release, Was Government Critic

A Rwandan former university lecturer and singer died while being released from prison. The singer had been a vocal critic of the government and the ruling Rwandan Patriotic Front. The cause of death is under investigation, with human rights groups calling for an independent inquiry.
