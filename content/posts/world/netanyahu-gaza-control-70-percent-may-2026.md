## 内塔尼亚胡：以军将扩大加沙控制至70%

以色列总理内塔尼亚胡公开表示，已指示以色列国防军（IDF）将其对加沙地带的控制范围扩大至70%。

这一表态与2025年10月以色列和哈马斯达成的停火协议条款直接矛盾。该协议原本规定了以色列军队从加沙撤出的阶段性安排。

分析人士认为，这一决定可能会加剧中东地区的紧张局势，并对正在进行的停火谈判产生负面影响。目前国际社会尚未对此作出正式回应。

## Netanyahu: IDF to Expand Gaza Control to 70%

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu announced he has directed the Israel Defense Forces (IDF) to expand Israeli control over the Gaza Strip to 70% of the territory.

The move directly contradicts the terms of the October 2025 ceasefire agreement between Israel and Hamas, which outlined a phased Israeli military withdrawal from Gaza.

Analysts warn the decision could escalate tensions across the Middle East and undermine ongoing ceasefire negotiations. The international community has not yet issued an official response.
