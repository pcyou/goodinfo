## 伊朗正在审查美方提案，双方接近达成结束战争备忘录

据CNN报道，伊朗正在认真审查美国提出的一项和平提案，消息人士称双方正朝着达成一份结束战争的备忘录方向推进。这是自伊朗-美国冲突升级以来最具实质性的外交进展之一。

据报道，美方提案涉及霍尔木兹海峡通航安全、制裁放松以及核问题等核心议题。伊朗方面的审查过程被视为对美方诚意的关键测试。分析人士指出，如果谈判成功，将大幅降低中东地区的军事对抗风险，并对全球能源市场产生积极影响。

与此同时，国际市场已对和平前景作出反应。原油价格出现回落，全球股市普遍上涨，反映出投资者对伊朗局势缓和的乐观预期。然而，外交官们警告称，最终协议仍面临诸多挑战，包括国内政治阻力和执行细节的分歧。

## Iran Reviews US Proposal, Both Sides Move Toward War-End Memorandum

Iran is actively reviewing a US peace proposal, with sources indicating both sides are moving toward a memorandum to end the ongoing conflict, CNN reports. This marks one of the most substantive diplomatic developments since the Iran-US escalation began.

The US proposal reportedly addresses core issues including Strait of Hormuz navigation security, sanctions relief, and nuclear concerns. Iran's review process is seen as a critical test of American sincerity. Analysts note that successful negotiations would significantly reduce military confrontation risks in the Middle East and positively impact global energy markets.

International markets have already responded to the peace prospects. Oil prices have retreated and global stocks have risen broadly, reflecting investor optimism about de-escalation. However, diplomats warn that a final agreement still faces numerous challenges, including domestic political resistance and disagreements over implementation details.
