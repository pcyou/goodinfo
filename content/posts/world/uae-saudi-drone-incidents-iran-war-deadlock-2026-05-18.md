## 阿联酋和沙特报告无人机袭击事件 伊朗战争僵局持续

**UAE and Saudi Arabia Report Drone Incidents as Iran War Deadlock Drags On**

### 中文报道

随着美国与伊朗之间的冲突持续陷入僵局，战火已经开始蔓延至海湾地区其他关键国家。据路透社报道，阿联酋和沙特阿拉伯均报告了无人机袭击事件，表明伊朗战争正在演变为更广泛的地区性冲突。

阿联酋方面报告称，阿布扎比核电厂附近遭到袭击。这一消息引发了国际社会对核设施安全的严重担忧。沙特阿拉伯也报告了类似的无人机事件，但具体细节仍在核实中。

自2月28日美国和以色列对伊朗发动军事行动以来，这场冲突已经持续近三个月。尽管双方进行过多次停火谈判，但始终未能达成持久和平协议。战争导致的油价飙升已经对全球经济产生深远影响，国际原油价格持续攀升。

联合国方面表示，冲突的扩大化趋势令人担忧。海湾国家作为全球重要石油产区，其安全稳定直接关系到全球能源供应。分析人士指出，如果战争继续向周边国家蔓延，后果将不堪设想。

### English Report

The Iran-US-Israel conflict continues to deadlock, with hostilities now spreading to other key Gulf nations. According to Reuters, both the UAE and Saudi Arabia have reported drone incidents, signaling an escalation of the war into a broader regional conflict.

The UAE reported a strike near the Abu Dhabi nuclear power plant, raising serious international concerns about nuclear facility safety. Saudi Arabia also reported similar drone incidents, with details still being verified.

Since the US and Israel launched military operations against Iran on February 28, the conflict has persisted for nearly three months. Despite multiple ceasefire negotiations, no lasting peace agreement has been reached. Oil prices have soared due to the war, with crude prices continuing to climb and creating deep impacts on the global economy.

The UN has expressed concern over the expanding scope of the conflict. As major oil-producing regions, the security and stability of Gulf states directly affect global energy supplies. Analysts warn that if the war continues to spread to neighboring countries, the consequences could be catastrophic.
