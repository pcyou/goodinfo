## Event Summary

According to Reuters, international oil prices are maintaining recent gains as peace talks between the United States and Iran have stalled. Meanwhile, shipping through the Strait of Hormuz has noticeably slowed, raising concerns about global oil supply security.

The Strait of Hormuz is the world's most important oil transit corridor, with approximately one-fifth of global oil supply passing through it. The slowdown in shipping may be related to escalating regional tensions, or may reflect insurers' reassessment of navigation risks.

Market analysts point out that if the talks remain deadlocked, oil prices could rise further, putting pressure on global inflation and economic growth.
