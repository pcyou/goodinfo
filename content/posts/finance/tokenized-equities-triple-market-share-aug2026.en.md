## Core Summary

Tokenized equities have tripled their market share, according to The Block, with Ondo Finance, Binance, and xStocks leading the sector. This trend indicates a significant acceleration in the migration of traditional financial assets onto blockchain infrastructure.

## Details

Tokenized equities represent digital ownership of traditional stocks via blockchain technology, enabling investors to trade conventional securities on-chain. The sector has experienced explosive growth over recent months, with market size expanding substantially.

Ondo Finance focuses on tokenizing U.S. Treasury bonds and fixed-income products, Binance provides users access to tokenized stocks through its platform, and xStocks is building decentralized securities trading infrastructure. Their collective rise reflects strong market demand for the digitization of traditional financial assets.
