## 全球长期债券收益率达近20年峰值

全球长期债券收益率已升至近二十年来的最高水平。这一趋势反映出市场对长期通胀和财政赤字的深切担忧，可能对全球资产定价产生深远影响。

分析人士指出，长端收益率的持续上升将增加政府借贷成本，挤压财政空间，并对股票和房地产等风险资产的估值构成压力。

---

## Global Long Bond Yields at 20-Year Peak

Global long-term bond yields have risen to their highest level in nearly two decades. The trend reflects deep market concerns about long-term inflation and fiscal deficits, with potential profound implications for global asset pricing.

Analysts note that sustained rises in long-end yields will increase government borrowing costs, squeeze fiscal space, and put pressure on valuations of risk assets including stocks and real estate.
