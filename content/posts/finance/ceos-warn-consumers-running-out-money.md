## CEO警告消费者资金紧张

多位大型企业CEO警告称，消费者正在"耗尽资金"并削减开支，暗示经济可能出现疲软信号。这一表态引发了市场对消费支出放缓的担忧。

## CEOs Warn Consumers Running Out of Money

Multiple major company CEOs are warning that consumers are "running out of money" and cutting back on spending, signaling potential economic weakness. The warnings raise concerns about slowing consumer expenditure.
