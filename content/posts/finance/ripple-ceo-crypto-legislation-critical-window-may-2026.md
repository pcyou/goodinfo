## [快讯] Ripple CEO：未来两周是加密立法关键窗口期

Ripple首席执行官Brad Garlinghouse表示，未来两周对通过更广泛的加密货币政策立法至关重要。参议院需在本月举行关键听证会，加密市场结构法案《Clarity Act》才有合理机会推进。Garlinghouse称"明确规则优于混乱"，行业正处于立法突破的关键时刻。

Garlinghouse在Consensus Miami 2026大会上强调，当前是加密行业推动联邦监管框架的最佳时机。参议院的下一步行动将决定美国能否在全球加密监管竞争中保持领先地位。

<!--more-->

Ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse said the next two weeks will be pivotal in passing broader cryptocurrency legislation. The Senate needs to hold a key hearing this month for the crypto market structure Clarity Act to have a reasonable chance of advancement. "Clarity is better than chaos," Garlinghouse stated, noting the industry is at a critical moment for legislative breakthrough.

Speaking at Consensus Miami 2026, Garlinghouse emphasized that now is the best opportunity for the crypto industry to push for a federal regulatory framework. The Senate's next steps will determine whether the U.S. can maintain its leading position in the global crypto regulatory race.
