## 亚洲市场表现分化

5月14日亚洲股市呈现分化走势。日本和中国科技股受人工智能板块推动表现强劲，但其他市场因投资者观望中美北京峰会结果而趋于谨慎。分析人士指出，峰会贸易谈判的任何信号都可能对全球市场产生连锁反应。

## Asian Markets Show Mixed Performance

Asian stock markets traded mixed on May 14. Japanese and Chinese tech shares rallied on AI momentum, while broader markets remained cautious as investors awaited signals from the Trump-Xi summit in Beijing. Analysts note that any trade negotiation developments could trigger ripple effects across global markets.
