## 摘要

美国财政部表示，今年将需要借款2万亿美元以维持政府正常运转。这一庞大的借款需求反映出联邦赤字持续扩大的严峻形势。随着减税政策延续和支出增加，美国债务水平持续攀升。市场对美国国债的需求和可持续性引发广泛讨论。

## Summary

The U.S. Treasury says it will need to borrow $2万亿 this year just to continue functioning. This massive borrowing requirement reflects the growing federal deficit. With tax cuts extended and spending rising, U.S. debt levels continue to climb, raising questions about Treasury market sustainability.
