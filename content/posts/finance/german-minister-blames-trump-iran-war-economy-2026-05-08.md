## 德国财长指责特朗普"不负责任的战争"拖累德国经济

德国财政部长拉斯·克林贝尔公开指责特朗普"对伊朗的不负责任战争"损害了德国经济。他指出，中东紧张局势推高了能源价格，对德国这一能源密集型经济体造成严重冲击。此言论反映了欧洲盟友对美国中东政策的日益不满。

## German Finance Minister Blames Trump's "Irresponsible War" for Economic Slowdown

German Finance Minister Lars Klingbeil publicly blamed Trump's "irresponsible war in Iran" for harming Germany's economy. He noted that Middle East tensions have driven up energy prices, severely impacting Germany's energy-intensive economy. The remarks reflect growing European frustration with US Middle East policy.
