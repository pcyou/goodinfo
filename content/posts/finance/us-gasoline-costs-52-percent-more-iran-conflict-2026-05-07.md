## [快讯] 美国汽油价格较伊朗冲突前上涨52%

据美联社报道，受伊朗冲突和霍尔木兹海峡局势影响，美国汽油价格较冲突爆发前上涨了52%。这一涨幅对低收入家庭造成显著冲击，部分地区的油价已创下历史纪录。分析师指出，若伊朗局势缓和，油价有望回落，但短期内的供应紧张仍将持续。

## US Gasoline Costs 52% More Than Before Iran Conflict

According to AP News, US gasoline prices have risen 52% compared to pre-conflict levels due to the Iran situation and Strait of Hormuz tensions. This increase has significantly impacted lower-income families, with some areas seeing record-high prices. Analysts note that oil prices could fall if Iran tensions ease, but short-term supply constraints will persist.
