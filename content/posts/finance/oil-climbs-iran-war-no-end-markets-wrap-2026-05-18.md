## 伊朗战争无结束迹象 国际油价持续攀升

**Oil Climbs as Iran War Shows No Sign of Ending: Markets Wrap**

### 中文报道

据彭博社报道，随着特朗普对伊朗发出新的警告信号，显示这场冲突短期内没有结束的可能，国际油价继续攀升。亚太市场开盘走势分化，投资者密切关注中东局势的进一步发展。

战争已经持续近三个月，期间双方多次尝试谈判但均未取得实质性进展。油价的持续上涨对全球经济造成了巨大压力，多个国家的通胀预期被大幅上调。能源成本的上升已经开始传导至消费品价格，普通消费者感受到了实实在在的经济负担。

金融市场方面，投资者在能源股和避险资产之间来回切换。分析师警告称，如果战争继续扩大至海湾其他国家，油价可能进一步飙升，届时将对全球经济造成更严重的冲击。

### English Report

According to Bloomberg, international oil prices continue to climb as Trump's new warnings on Iran signal that the conflict shows no signs of ending in the near term. Asia-Pacific markets opened with mixed performance, with investors closely monitoring further developments in the Middle East situation.

The war has persisted for nearly three months, with multiple negotiation attempts yielding no substantive progress. The continued rise in oil prices has created enormous pressure on the global economy, with inflation expectations significantly raised in multiple countries. Rising energy costs have begun to flow through to consumer goods prices, and ordinary consumers are feeling the real economic burden.

In financial markets, investors are switching between energy stocks and safe-haven assets. Analysts warn that if the war continues to expand to other Gulf nations, oil prices could surge further, delivering an even more severe impact on the global economy.
