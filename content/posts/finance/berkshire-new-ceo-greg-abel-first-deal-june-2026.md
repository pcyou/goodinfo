## 伯克希尔新CEO完成首笔交易

据AP News报道，伯克希尔·哈撒韦新任CEO格雷格·阿贝尔（Greg Abel）自接替沃伦·巴菲特以来，完成了其任内首笔收购交易。这标志着伯克希尔进入新的管理时代。阿贝尔此前负责伯克希尔的非保险业务，以其稳健的经营风格著称。市场将密切关注其后续投资决策方向。

## Berkshire CEO Abel Completes First Deal

New Berkshire Hathaway CEO Greg Abel has completed his first acquisition since taking over from Warren Buffett, marking a new era for the conglomerate. Abel, previously in charge of Berkshire's non-insurance operations, is known for his steady management style. Markets will closely watch his subsequent investment decisions.
