## 中文

Fundstrat 分析师 Tom Lee 表示，SpaceX、Anthropic 和 OpenAI 等公司即将带来的数万亿 IPO 规模最终可被市场吸收，不会导致标普500指数下跌。当前道琼斯指数已上涨300点至历史新高。

## English

Fundstrat's Tom Lee argues that trillions in upcoming IPO supply from SpaceX, Anthropic, and OpenAI can ultimately be absorbed by the market without crashing the S&P 500. The Dow Jones has surged 300 points to a record high this week.
