## [快讯] Citi Stock Falls as Return Target Disappoints

花旗集团（Citigroup）股价今日下跌，原因公司公布的长期回报目标未能达到分析师预期。据彭博社报道，尽管花旗在成本削减和业务重组方面取得进展，但管理层设定的回报率指引低于华尔街最乐观的预测。

Citi shares declined after the bank's return-on-tangible-equity target fell short of analyst expectations, despite ongoing cost-cutting efforts and business restructuring. The miss highlights investor skepticism about CEO Jane Fraser's turnaround strategy, which has yet to deliver the profitability levels seen at rival banks.
