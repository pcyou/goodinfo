## [快讯] FCC委员告诉迪士尼：该机构正发起审查运动

《华尔街日报》报道，一名联邦通信委员会（FCC）委员致信迪士尼，称该机构正对该公司发起审查运动。报道称，特朗普政府的目标涉及更广泛的媒体领域。

此事件引发业界对政府干预媒体独立性的担忧。迪士尼方面尚未正式回应。

## FCC Commissioner Tells Disney the Agency Is on a Campaign to Censor It

The Wall Street Journal reports that an FCC Commissioner has written to Disney, stating the agency is engaged in a campaign against the company. The Trump administration's targets reportedly span a broader range of media outlets. The move raises concerns about government interference in media independence. Disney has not yet issued a formal response.
