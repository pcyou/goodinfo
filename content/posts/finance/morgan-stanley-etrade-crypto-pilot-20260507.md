## [快讯] Morgan Stanley Pilots Crypto Trading on E*Trade

据彭博社报道，摩根士丹利正在E*Trade平台上试点加密交易服务，费率为50个基点。此举是该公司在推出比特币ETF后进一步扩大数字资产业务的又一步。试点标志着传统券商在加密资产领域的持续渗透。

## Morgan Stanley Pilots Crypto Trading on E*Trade

Morgan Stanley is piloting crypto trading on E*Trade at 50 basis points, expanding its digital asset push weeks after launching a bitcoin ETF, Bloomberg reported.
