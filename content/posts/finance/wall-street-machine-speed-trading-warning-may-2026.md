## [快讯] Wall Street Warns Legacy Markets Can't Keep Up With Machine-Speed Trading

### 中文

在Consensus Miami大会上，金融专家指出，随着代币化和实时交易的发展，传统金融市场正面临向全天候自动化基础设施转型的压力，现有系统可能无法满足未来需求。

### English

At Consensus Miami, financial experts warned that as tokenization and real-time trading advance, legacy financial markets face pressure to transition to always-on automated infrastructure, with existing systems potentially unable to meet future demands.
