## 中文

零售巨头沃尔玛最新发出警告称，随着汽油价格持续上涨，美国消费者正在削减非必需品开支。这一信号暗示美国普通家庭正面临日益增长的经济压力。

沃尔玛表示，中低收入消费者的支出意愿明显下降，尤其是汽车相关支出和非必需食品类别。汽油价格上升直接挤压了可支配收入，导致消费频率和单次消费金额双双下滑。

经济分析师认为，消费者支出占美国国内生产总值的近七成，沃尔玛作为美国最大零售商，其销售数据被视为衡量经济健康状况的重要先行指标。

此外，汽油价格上涨也推高了物流和供应链成本，可能进一步传导至消费品终端价格，形成通胀压力。

## English

Walmart has warned that American consumers are cutting back on discretionary spending as rising gasoline prices squeeze household budgets, signaling growing economic pressure on everyday Americans.

The retail giant reported a noticeable decline in spending among low- to middle-income consumers, particularly in automotive-related categories and non-essential food items. Higher fuel costs are directly reducing disposable income.

Economists note that consumer spending accounts for nearly seventy percent of US GDP. Walmart serves as a key barometer of economic health, and a sustained pullback could significantly drag on overall growth.

Additionally, rising fuel costs are pushing up logistics and supply chain expenses, which may cascade into higher consumer prices and renewed inflationary pressure.
