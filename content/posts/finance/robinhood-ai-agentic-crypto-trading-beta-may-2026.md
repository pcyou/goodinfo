## Robinhood Launches AI-Powered Agentic Trading, Crypto Coming Next

### 中文

零售交易平台Robinhood宣布推出AI驱动的代理交易功能，首个测试版本面向股票交易。平台计划将功能扩展至加密货币交易。代理交易利用AI智能体代表用户执行交易策略。

### English

Retail trading platform Robinhood announced AI-powered agentic trading features, with the first beta limited to equities. The platform plans to expand to cryptocurrency trading. Agentic trading uses AI agents to execute strategies on behalf of users.
