## US Gasoline Costs 50% More Than Before the Iran War

### 中文

美联社报道，美国汽油价格较伊朗战争爆发前上涨了百分之五十。持续的霍尔木兹海峡紧张局势导致全球原油供应受阻，能源价格居高不下。尽管美国宣布伊朗冲突已终止，但海峡通航恢复正常仍需时间。高油价正对美国家庭消费和企业运营成本造成显著压力。

### English

The Associated Press reports that US gasoline prices have risen 50% compared to levels before the Iran war. Ongoing tensions in the Strait of Hormuz have disrupted global crude oil supplies, keeping energy prices elevated. Although the US has declared the Iran conflict terminated, normal navigation through the strait will take time to restore. High fuel prices are putting significant pressure on household spending and business operating costs across America.
