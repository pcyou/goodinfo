# [快讯] 美国司法部指控Google员工利用内幕信息在Polymarket获利120万美元

## DOJ Charges Google Employee Over Polymarket Trades Netting $1.2 Million

据NPR报道，美国司法部已对一名Google员工提起刑事指控，称其利用职务获取的内幕信息在预测市场平台Polymarket上进行了交易，获利约一百二十万美元。

该案是监管机构对预测市场内幕交易的首批执法行动之一，标志着对加密预测平台监管的进一步收紧。

According to NPR, the US Department of Justice has charged a Google employee with using insider information to make trades on the prediction market platform Polymarket, netting approximately one point two million dollars in profits. This case represents one of the first enforcement actions targeting insider trading on prediction markets, signaling increased regulatory scrutiny of crypto prediction platforms.
