## [快讯] 比特币重回7.7万美元上方 油价大跌推动亚洲股市走高

据CoinDesk报道，比特币周一交易价格回升至7.7万美元以上。受国际油价约5%大幅下跌推动，亚洲股市普遍走高，风险资产情绪回暖。

与此同时，纳斯达克宣布即将推出比特币期权交易，标志着传统金融市场对加密货币的接纳程度进一步提升。此举有望为机构投资者提供更规范的加密资产配置工具。

分析人士指出，油价下跌降低了通胀预期，为风险资产提供了更有利的宏观环境。加密市场近期表现与科技股关联度有所上升。

*Source: CoinDesk, The Block*

## Bitcoin Holds Above $77,000 as Oil Slide Lifts Asian Equities

Bitcoin traded above $77,000 on Monday, CoinDesk reported. Asian equities rose broadly following a roughly 5% drop in oil prices, boosting risk appetite across markets.

Separately, Nasdaq announced plans to launch Bitcoin options trading, marking another step in mainstream financial adoption of crypto assets. The move is expected to provide institutional investors with more regulated tools for crypto portfolio management.

Analysts note that lower oil prices have reduced inflation expectations, creating a more favorable macro backdrop for risk assets. Crypto markets have shown increased correlation with tech stocks recently.

*Source: CoinDesk, The Block*
