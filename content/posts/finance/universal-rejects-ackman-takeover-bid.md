据 BBC 报道，环球音乐集团（Universal Music Group）拒绝了亿万富翁投资者比尔·阿克曼的收购要约。作为全球最大音乐公司之一，环球音乐旗下拥有众多知名艺人和音乐版权。

---

According to the BBC, Universal Music Group has rejected billionaire investor Bill Ackman's takeover bid. As one of the world's largest music companies, Universal represents numerous major artists and holds extensive music catalog rights.
