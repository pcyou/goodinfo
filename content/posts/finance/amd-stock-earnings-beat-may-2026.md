## AMD Stock Jumps on Solid Earnings

### 中文

Barron's 报道，AMD 股价在发布强劲财报后大幅上涨。

AMD 作为全球主要半导体芯片制造商之一，其财报表现受到市场广泛关注。在当前 AI 芯片需求持续增长的背景下，AMD 的数据中心和 AI 加速器业务成为推动业绩增长的关键因素。

### English

Barron's reports that AMD stock surged following strong quarterly earnings results.

AMD, one of the world's leading semiconductor manufacturers, has been closely watched by investors. Against the backdrop of sustained AI chip demand, AMD's data center and AI accelerator businesses have been key drivers of its revenue growth.
