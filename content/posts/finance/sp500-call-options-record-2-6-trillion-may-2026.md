## S&P 500 Call Options Volume Surges to Record $2.6 Trillion

[快讯] 华尔街追逐上行空间，标普500看涨期权成交量飙升至创纪录的2.6万亿美元。分析师认为这对比特币走势暗示利好，但存在重要不确定性因素。

Wall Street's chase for upside has pushed S&P 500 call options volume to a record $2.6 trillion. Analysts say the implications for bitcoin appear bullish, though with an important caveat about sustainability.
