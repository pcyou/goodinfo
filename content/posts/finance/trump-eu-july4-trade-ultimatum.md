## 特朗普给欧盟下达7月4日最后通牒

特朗普向欧盟发出最后通牒，要求其于7月4日前批准贸易协议，并将对美商品的关税降至零。

据BBC报道，特朗普表示欧盟必须执行去年达成的贸易协议，并取消对美国商品的所有关税。这一表态进一步加剧了跨大西洋贸易紧张局势，尤其在美贸易法院刚刚裁定其全球关税违法之后。

欧盟方面尚未对这一最后通牒作出正式回应。分析人士认为，欧盟不太可能在特朗普设定的时限内做出让步，贸易谈判前景不容乐观。

## Trump Gives EU July 4 Ultimatum on Trade Deal

Trump has issued an ultimatum to the European Union, demanding it approve a trade deal by July 4 and reduce tariffs on American goods to zero.

According to BBC, Trump said the EU must enact the trade deal agreed upon last year and drop all tariffs on U.S. goods. This stance further escalates transatlantic trade tensions, coming just after a U.S. trade court ruled his global tariffs illegal.

The EU has not yet responded formally to the ultimatum. Analysts believe the EU is unlikely to concede within Trump's timeline, making the outlook for trade negotiations bleak.
