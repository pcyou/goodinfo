英国石油公司（BP）宣布罢免董事长阿尔伯特·曼福德（Albert Manifold），理由是对其行为存在"严重担忧"。这一人事变动在国际能源行业引发广泛关注，BP 目前正面临伊朗战争导致的油价波动和能源市场不确定性。

BP removes chair Albert Manifold over 'serious concerns' about his conduct. The leadership change comes at a critical time for the energy giant amid oil price volatility driven by Middle East tensions.
