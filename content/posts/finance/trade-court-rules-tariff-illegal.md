## 美国贸易法院裁定全球关税违法

美国国际贸易法院作出裁决，认定特朗普政府实施的10%全球关税违法。这是特朗普政府在关税问题上遭遇的第二起重大诉讼失败，可能对美国贸易政策产生深远影响。

该裁决意味着美国政府可能被迫退还已征收的部分关税款项，并重新评估其贸易策略。特朗普政府已表示将提起上诉，但法律专家认为法院的裁决依据充分，上诉成功的可能性不高。

这一判决恰逢特朗普向欧盟发出7月4日最后通牒，要求批准贸易协议并将对美商品关税降至零，贸易局势更加复杂。

## U.S. Trade Court Rules Trump's 10% Global Tariff Illegal

The U.S. Court of International Trade has ruled that the Trump administration's 10% global tariff is illegal, marking the second major tariff case the administration has lost. The ruling could have significant implications for U.S. trade policy worldwide.

The decision means the U.S. government may be forced to refund some of the tariff revenues already collected and reassess its trade strategy. The Trump administration has said it will appeal, but legal experts believe the court's reasoning was solid and the appeal has a low chance of success.

This ruling comes as Trump simultaneously issues a July 4 ultimatum to the EU to approve a trade deal, adding further complexity to an already volatile trade landscape.
