## 中文

印度监管机构对预测市场展开 crackdown，Polymarket 已在该国停止服务。据 CoinDesk 报道，此举可能波及 Kalshi 等其他平台。印度此举反映了对预测市场监管的收紧趋势。与此同时，Polymarket 正寻求进入日本市场，任命了区域代表。

---

## English

Indian regulators have cracked down on prediction markets, with Polymarket ceasing operations in the country. CoinDesk reports that other platforms like Kalshi could face similar restrictions. The move reflects a tightening regulatory trend for prediction markets globally. Meanwhile, Polymarket is eyeing entry into the Japanese market and has appointed a regional representative.
