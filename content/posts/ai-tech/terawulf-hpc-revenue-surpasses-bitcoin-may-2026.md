## [快讯] TeraWulf HPC收入首超比特币挖矿

美国矿企TeraWulf在2026年第一季度报告了2100万美元的高性能计算（HPC）收入，首次超过其比特币挖矿业务收入。这一里程碑标志着公司从传统加密货币挖矿向AI算力基础设施转型取得了实质性进展。

随着AI产业对算力的需求持续激增，越来越多原本从事比特币挖矿的公司开始将基础设施转向为AI模型训练和推理提供服务。TeraWulf的转型代表了这一行业趋势的最新案例。

## TeraWulf HPC Revenue Surpasses Bitcoin Mining for First Time

US mining company TeraWulf reported $21 million in high-performance computing (HPC) revenue in Q1 2026, surpassing its Bitcoin mining income for the first time. This milestone marks a significant step in the company's pivot from traditional cryptocurrency mining to AI compute infrastructure.

As AI demand for compute capacity continues to surge, an increasing number of Bitcoin mining companies are repurposing their infrastructure to serve AI model training and inference workloads. TeraWulf's transition represents the latest example of this industry-wide trend.
