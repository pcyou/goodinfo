## 微软担忧过度依赖OpenAI

在埃隆·马斯克与山姆·奥特曼的庭审中，一份关键证词揭示了微软内部对过度依赖OpenAI的深层担忧。

### 庭审披露

庭审记录显示，微软高管曾在内部讨论中表达了对OpenAI关系的风险评估。微软作为OpenAI的最大投资者，已向其投入数百亿美元，但其核心产品如Bing搜索和Copilot助手都深度依赖OpenAI的技术。

### 微软的顾虑

证词指出，微软意识到这种依赖关系可能带来的战略风险：如果OpenAI改变合作方向或与其他公司建立更紧密的关系，微软的AI竞争力将受到严重冲击。

### 行业影响

这一披露引发了科技行业对AI供应链集中度的广泛讨论。随着AI技术成为科技公司的核心竞争力，如何平衡合作与自主开发成为行业共同面临的挑战。

### 后续发展

庭审仍在进行中，更多内部文件可能被披露。市场正在密切关注两家公司的关系走向，以及微软是否会加速发展自有AI技术以降低依赖风险。

## Microsoft Feared Over-Reliance on OpenAI, Musk-Altman Trial Reveals

Court testimony in the Elon Musk vs. Sam Altman trial has revealed that Microsoft internally worried about becoming too dependent on OpenAI.

### Trial Disclosure

Testimony showed that Microsoft executives held internal discussions assessing the risks of their relationship with OpenAI. As OpenAI's largest investor with tens of billions committed, Microsoft's Bing search and Copilot assistant both deeply rely on OpenAI's technology.

### Strategic Concerns

The testimony indicated Microsoft recognized that this dependency posed strategic risks: should OpenAI shift its partnerships or forge closer ties with competitors, Microsoft's AI competitiveness could be severely undermined.

### Industry Implications

This revelation has sparked broader discussions about AI supply chain concentration in the tech industry. As AI becomes core to competitive advantage, balancing partnerships with in-house development is an industry-wide challenge.

### What Next

The trial is ongoing, and more internal documents may emerge. Markets are watching closely for signs of whether Microsoft will accelerate development of its own AI capabilities to reduce dependency risks.
