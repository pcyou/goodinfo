## [快讯] 苹果iOS 26.5发布

苹果于5月13日发布iOS 26.5更新，正式向iPhone和Android用户推送RCS端到端加密功能。

### 新功能

RCS（富通信服务）端到端加密将从今日起开始向iPhone和Android用户滚动推送。该功能将大幅提升跨平台消息通信的安全性。

### 意义

这是苹果首次在iOS与Android之间实现加密消息互通，标志着两大平台在通信标准上的重要进展。

## Apple iOS 26.5 with RCS Encryption

Apple released iOS 26.5 on May 13, rolling out end-to-end encrypted RCS messaging for both iPhone and Android users.

### New Feature

RCS end-to-end encryption begins rolling out today across iPhone and Android, significantly enhancing cross-platform messaging security.

### Significance

This marks Apple's first implementation of encrypted messaging interoperability between iOS and Android, representing a notable advance in cross-platform communication standards.
