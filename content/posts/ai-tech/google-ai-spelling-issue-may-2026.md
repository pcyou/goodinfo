# [快讯] Google最新AI模型连"Google"都拼不对

## Google's Latest AI Model Can't Even Spell "Google"

据TechCrunch报道，Google最新发布的AI模型在生成内容时出现了令人尴尬的拼写错误——连"Google"这个品牌名称都无法正确拼写。

这一问题引发了业界对AI模型质量控制和测试流程的质疑。尽管AI在复杂任务上表现出色，但基础拼写错误仍然频发，凸显了当前AI系统在某些基本能力上的不足。

According to TechCrunch, Google's latest AI model has been producing embarrassing spelling errors — including being unable to correctly spell the brand name "Google" itself. The issue has sparked questions about AI model quality control and testing processes, highlighting gaps in basic capabilities despite impressive performance on complex tasks.
