## 法官当庭质问马斯克代表：「事情不是这样运作的」

在一场备受瞩目的法庭听证会上，一名联邦法官与马斯克方的法律代表展开了激烈交锋，就OpenAI的非营利使命和企业转型问题进行了深入质询。法官多次对马斯克方面的论点表示质疑，并在庭上直言：「事情不是这样运作的」（"That's not how it works"）。

听证会聚焦于Sam Altman的管理风格以及OpenAI从非营利组织向商业实体的转型过程。马斯克此前多次公开批评OpenAI背离了其创立时的非营利初衷，并向法院提起诉讼。然而法官在庭上对马斯克方的部分法律论证提出了严厉质疑，认为其部分主张缺乏充分的事实依据。

BBC报道指出，这场庭审标志着AI行业最引人注目的法律纠纷之一进入关键阶段。法官的态度可能对未来AI治理框架和企业结构转型产生深远影响。

## Judge Crosses Swords With Musk in Court: "That's Not How It Works"

A federal judge engaged in a heated exchange with Elon Musk's legal representatives during a high-profile court hearing focused on OpenAI's transition from a non-profit organization to a commercial entity. The judge repeatedly questioned arguments from Musk's side, at one point stating bluntly: "That's not how it works."

The hearing centered on Sam Altman's management style and the structural transformation of OpenAI. Musk has filed lawsuits alleging that OpenAI has abandoned its original non-profit mission. However, the judge expressed skepticism toward several of Musk's legal arguments, noting that some claims lacked sufficient factual grounding.

BBC reports that this trial marks a critical phase in one of the AI industry's most prominent legal disputes. The judge's stance could have far-reaching implications for future AI governance frameworks and corporate restructuring precedents.
