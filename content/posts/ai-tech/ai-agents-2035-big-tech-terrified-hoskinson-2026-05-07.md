## [快讯] Cardano创始人：到2035年AI代理将比人类更重要，科技巨头"感到恐惧"

Cardano创始人兼Input Output CEO查尔斯·霍斯金森（Charles Hoskinson）在Consensus Miami会议上表示，到2035年，AI代理的重要性将超过人类。

他指出，谷歌、亚马逊和Facebook等科技巨头对此"感到恐惧"，因为AI代理在面对广告时的行为模式与人类完全不同——它们不会点击广告，这将彻底颠覆互联网现有的广告商业模式。

这一观点与Coinbase工程师此前关于"AI代理可能终结互联网广告模式"的预测相呼应。

## [Brief] Cardano Founder: By 2035 AI Agents Will Be More Relevant Than Humans, Big Tech Is "Terrified"

Charles Hoskinson, founder of Cardano and CEO of Input Output, stated at Consensus Miami that by 2035, AI agents will be more relevant than humans.

He noted that tech giants like Google, Amazon, and Facebook are "terrified" because AI agents behave completely differently from humans when confronted with ads — they won't click on them, which will fundamentally disrupt the internet's existing advertising business model.

This aligns with earlier predictions from a Coinbase engineer suggesting that AI agents could end the internet's advertising model.
