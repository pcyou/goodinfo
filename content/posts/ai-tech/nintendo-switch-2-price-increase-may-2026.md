## [快讯] 任天堂Switch 2涨价

任天堂周五宣布上调Switch 2游戏机价格，成为继索尼PS5和微软Xbox之后第三家涨价的主要主机厂商。公司表示，价格上涨主要是由于制造成本上升。

此前，索尼和微软已因零部件成本和关税压力分别上调了各自主机的售价。任天堂此前一直努力维持价格稳定，但最终也不得不跟进。

分析人士指出，主机涨价可能对销量产生一定影响，但在当前成本环境下，厂商的利润空间已受到显著挤压。

## Nintendo Raises Switch 2 Prices, Following PS5 and Xbox

Nintendo announced price increases for the Switch 2 console on Friday, becoming the third major hardware maker to raise prices after Sony's PS5 and Microsoft's Xbox. The company cited rising manufacturing costs as the primary driver.

Sony and Microsoft had previously increased console prices due to component costs and tariff pressures. Nintendo had attempted to hold prices steady but ultimately followed suit.

Analysts note that while price hikes could affect sales volumes, hardware makers' profit margins have been significantly squeezed in the current cost environment.
