## 中文

SpaceX和OpenAI两家公司的估值正在接近甚至超越沃伦·巴菲特旗下的伯克希尔·哈撒韦公司（Berkshire Hathaway），这一里程碑标志着私人科技公司的资本实力已达到前所未有的高度。

据市场分析，SpaceX的最新估值已超过三千五百亿美元，而OpenAI在最新一轮融资中的估值也突破三千亿美元。伯克希尔·哈撒韦当前市值约为三千六百亿美元。如果两家科技公司按当前趋势完成下一轮融资，将双双超越这家传奇投资巨头。

这一变化反映了资本市场对太空探索与人工智能领域的极度看好。SpaceX凭借星舰项目的持续推进和星链服务的全球扩张，估值不断攀升。OpenAI则凭借GPT系列模型的商业化成功，成为人工智能行业最炙手可热的标的。

分析人士指出，这种现象意味着全球资本正在从传统价值投资向科技前沿领域大规模转移。

## English

SpaceX and OpenAI are on track to surpass Warren Buffett's Berkshire Hathaway in market valuation, marking a historic shift in how capital allocates between traditional value investments and frontier technology companies.

SpaceX's latest valuation exceeds three hundred fifty billion dollars, while OpenAI has crossed three hundred billion dollars in its most recent funding round. Berkshire Hathaway's current market capitalization stands at approximately three hundred sixty billion dollars.

This milestone underscores the extraordinary capital flowing into space exploration and artificial intelligence. SpaceX continues to advance its Starship program and expand Starlink satellite internet services globally, while OpenAI's GPT models have become the backbone of enterprise AI adoption.

Market analysts see this as a fundamental reallocation of global investment from traditional value companies to high-growth technology platforms.
