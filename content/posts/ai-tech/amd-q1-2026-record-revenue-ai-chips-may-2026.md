## AMD Reports Record Q1 2026 Revenue Driven by AI Chip Demand

### 中文

AMD 公布了 2026 年第一季度财务业绩，创下单季收入历史新高，主要受人工智能加速器和数据中心处理器的强劲需求推动。

财报显示，AMD 的数据中心业务表现尤为亮眼，AI 加速器产品销售大幅增长，继续在 AI 芯片市场挑战英伟达的主导地位。随着全球科技公司持续加大 AI 基础设施投资，AMD 的 MI 系列加速处理器获得了越来越多的大型客户订单。

AMD CEO 苏姿丰表示，公司对 AI 市场的长期增长前景充满信心，并计划继续加大研发投入，推出下一代 AI 芯片产品。

分析师指出，AMD 正在 AI 芯片领域取得实质性进展，其市场份额持续提升，虽然仍远落后于英伟达，但差距正在缩小。

### English

AMD reported first quarter 2026 financial results showing record revenue, driven by strong demand for AI accelerators and data center processors.

The company's data center segment performed particularly well, with AI accelerator sales surging as AMD continues to challenge Nvidia's dominance in the AI chip market. With global tech companies ramping up AI infrastructure spending, AMD's MI series accelerators are winning more large-scale customer deployments.

AMD CEO Lisa Su expressed confidence in the long-term growth of the AI market and announced plans to increase R&D investment for next-generation AI chips.

Analysts note that AMD is making meaningful progress in the AI chip space, steadily gaining market share. While still trailing Nvidia by a wide margin, the gap is narrowing.
