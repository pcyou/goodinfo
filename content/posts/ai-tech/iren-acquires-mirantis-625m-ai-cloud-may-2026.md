## [快讯] IREN以6.25亿美元全股票交易收购Mirantis

比特币矿企IREN宣布以6.25亿美元全股票交易收购云计算公司Mirantis，加速向AI云平台转型。分析师认为，该交易对 privately held Mirantis 的估值约为其收入的四到五倍。

IREN此前已开始从纯挖矿模式向AI数据中心租赁模式转型，此次收购旨在完善其AI云平台的技术能力。Mirantis在容器管理和私有云领域拥有深厚积累，将帮助IREN为企业客户提供完整的AI基础设施服务。

<!--more-->

Bitcoin mining company IREN announced a $625 million all-stock deal to acquire cloud computing company Mirantis, accelerating its pivot toward an AI cloud platform. Analysts said the deal implies roughly four to five times revenue valuation for the privately held Mirantis.

IREN has been transitioning from a pure-play mining model toward leasing infrastructure to AI and cloud providers. This acquisition aims to complete its AI cloud platform's technical capabilities. Mirantis brings deep expertise in container management and private cloud, helping IREN offer comprehensive AI infrastructure services to enterprise clients.
