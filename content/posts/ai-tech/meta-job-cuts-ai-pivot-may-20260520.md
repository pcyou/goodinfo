据 NPR 报道，Meta（原 Facebook）宣布新一轮裁员计划，将裁减约 8000 名员工，这是该公司持续推进 AI 战略转型的最新举措。

此次裁员涉及多个部门，但核心逻辑清晰：Meta 正在将资源从传统业务转向人工智能领域。此前，CEO 扎克伯格已将 2025 年定义为"效率之年"，强调 AI 优先的公司战略。

裁员消息公布后，科技行业反应分化。一方面，投资者对 Meta 的成本控制举措表示认可；另一方面，裁员规模的扩大引发了对硅谷持续"瘦身"趋势的关注。

Meta 在 AI 领域的投入正在加速。从 Meta AI 助手到开源大模型 Llama 系列，再到 AI 驱动的广告推荐系统，公司正在将人工智能渗透到每一个核心产品中。此次人员调整旨在精简组织结构，将更多资源集中到 AI 研发团队。

值得关注的是，这并非 Meta 首次大规模裁员。2023 年以来，公司已经历了多轮组织优化，累计裁员超过 2 万人。分析认为，Meta 正在重塑为一家"AI-first"公司，传统的产品和运营团队将面临持续的结构性调整。

## English

Meta is cutting approximately 8,000 jobs as part of its accelerating pivot toward artificial intelligence, NPR reports, marking the latest phase of the company's strategic transformation.

The cuts span multiple departments but follow a clear pattern: resources are being redirected from legacy operations to AI-focused teams. CEO Mark Zuckerberg has described 2025 as a "year of efficiency," emphasizing an AI-first approach across all business lines.

Meta's AI investments are expanding rapidly. From its Meta AI assistant to the open-source Llama model family and AI-driven ad recommendation systems, the company is embedding artificial intelligence into every core product. The restructuring aims to streamline operations and channel more resources into AI research and development.

This is not Meta's first major workforce reduction. Since 2023, the company has undergone multiple rounds of layoffs, cutting over 20,000 positions in total. Analysts see the pattern as Meta reshaping itself into an "AI-first" company, with traditional product and operations teams facing ongoing structural adjustments.

Investors have responded positively to the cost-control measures, though the scale of the cuts has renewed concerns about the broader trend of Silicon Valley downsizing.
