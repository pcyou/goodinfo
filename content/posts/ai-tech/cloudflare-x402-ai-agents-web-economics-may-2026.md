## AI Agents Are Breaking Web Economics, but Cloudflare Says x402 Can Help

### 中文

CoinDesk 报道，随着 AI 代理（AI agents）大量访问互联网内容，传统网络经济模式正面临挑战。Cloudflare 提出 x402 协议作为解决方案。

x402 协议旨在为 AI 代理与内容创作者之间建立付费机制，使 AI 系统在抓取和使用网络内容时能够进行微支付。这一方案有望解决当前 AI 大量消耗网络资源却未向内容创作者补偿的问题。

在 AI 代理数量快速增长的背景下，如何平衡技术创新与内容生态可持续性成为行业关注焦点。x402 协议的推出标志着支付基础设施正在为 AI 时代进行重新设计。

### English

CoinDesk reports that as AI agents increasingly access internet content, traditional web economic models are facing challenges. Cloudflare proposes the x402 protocol as a solution.

The x402 protocol aims to establish a payment mechanism between AI agents and content creators, enabling micro-payments when AI systems scrape and use web content. This approach could address the current imbalance where AI consumes significant web resources without compensating content creators.

As the number of AI agents grows rapidly, balancing technological innovation with content ecosystem sustainability has become an industry focus. The x402 protocol marks a redesign of payment infrastructure for the AI era.
