## Dune裁员25%

加密数据分析平台Dune本周裁员25%，CEO Fredrik Haga在社交媒体X上表示，公司在AI生产力工具方面的大量投资是裁员的部分原因。

这一消息引发了科技行业对AI替代人工的新一轮讨论。Dune是加密领域重要的链上数据分析平台，为投资者和开发者提供区块链数据洞察。

## Dune Cuts 25% of Staff Citing AI Efficiencies

Crypto data firm Dune cut a quarter of its workforce this week. CEO Fredrik Haga said on X that the company's heavy investment in AI-powered productivity tools was a contributing factor, sparking fresh debate about AI-driven workforce reduction in the tech sector.
