## 中文

[快讯] 英国国家犯罪局（NCA）和警察局长协会（NPCC）呼吁，应禁止16岁以下未成年人访问未能有效阻止裸露内容传播和陌生人联系的社交平台。该建议呼应了英国正在推进的在线安全立法方向。

## English

[Flash] The UK's National Crime Agency and National Police Chiefs' Council say children under 16 should be blocked from platforms that fail to prevent exposure to explicit content and contact from strangers. The recommendation aligns with the direction of the UK's advancing online safety legislation.
