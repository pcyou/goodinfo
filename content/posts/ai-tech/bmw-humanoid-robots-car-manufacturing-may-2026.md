## 宝马引入人形机器人到汽车生产线

据BBC报道，宝马公司（BMW）宣布人形机器人（humanoid robots）将是汽车制造业的未来发展方向。该公司已在欧洲的一家汽车工厂引入人形机器人进行生产作业。

此举延续了美国汽车制造商类似项目的趋势。此前，多家美国车企已开始测试人形机器人在生产线上的应用，用于执行重复性高、劳动强度大的装配任务。

宝马表示，人形机器人能够在传统自动化设备难以覆盖的柔性装配环节发挥重要作用。不过，公司尚未公布具体的部署规模和预期替代人工的比例。

分析人士指出，这一趋势标志着汽车制造业正从传统机械自动化向更加智能化的方向演进。

## BMW: Humanoid Robots Are 'the Future' of Car Manufacturing

BMW has announced that humanoid robots represent the future of car manufacturing, introducing them at a European car plant — building on similar projects underway in the US.

According to BBC, BMW stated that humanoid robots can play an important role in flexible assembly tasks where traditional automation equipment falls short. The company has not yet disclosed the scale of deployment or expected workforce impact.

Analysts say this trend marks the automotive industry's shift from traditional mechanical automation toward more intelligent, adaptive manufacturing systems.
