## IREN股价飙升：英伟达支持AI扩张，3000万股认股权证引关注

加密货币矿企转型AI计算的IREN周四股价大幅上涨，消息面上英伟达通过认股权证形式支持其AI业务扩展计划。

### 交易细节

根据披露，英伟达获得了与3000万股IREN股票挂钩的认股权证，作为对其AI基础设施投资的战略支持。这标志着英伟达正加强与AI算力提供商的深度合作。

### 背景分析

IREN此前主要从事比特币挖矿，近年来积极向AI计算转型。英伟达的认股权证安排表明其对IRENAI业务前景的认可。

- IREN正在扩大GPU集群规模
- 计划建设多个AI数据中心
- 目标成为主要AI算力服务商

### 行业趋势

比特币矿企转型AI已成为行业趋势。随着比特币减半后挖矿收益下降，大量矿企寻求利用现有电力和基础设施资源转向利润更高的AI计算服务。

### 市场反应

IREN股价当日大幅上涨，交易量显著放大。

## IREN Stock Surges as Nvidia Backs AI Expansion with Warrants Tied to 30 Million Shares

Cryptocurrency miner-turned-AI-computing company IREN saw its stock surge on Thursday after Nvidia backed its AI business expansion through a warrant arrangement tied to 30 million shares.

### Deal Details

Under the disclosed arrangement, Nvidia received warrants linked to 30 million IREN shares as part of strategic support for its AI infrastructure investment. This marks Nvidia's deepening cooperation with AI compute providers.

### Background

IREN, previously focused on Bitcoin mining, has been actively pivoting toward AI computing. Nvidia's warrant arrangement signals confidence in IREN's AI business prospects.

- IREN is expanding its GPU cluster capacity
- Plans to build multiple AI data centers
- Aims to become a major AI compute service provider

### Industry Trend

The transition from Bitcoin mining to AI has become an industry-wide trend. As mining profitability declines post-halving, mining companies are leveraging existing power and infrastructure resources to shift to higher-margin AI computing services.

### Market Reaction

IREN shares rose sharply on the day with significantly elevated trading volume.
