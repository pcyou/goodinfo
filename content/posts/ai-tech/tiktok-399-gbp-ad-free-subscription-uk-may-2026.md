## [快讯] TikTok在英国推出3.99英镑无广告订阅

BBC报道，TikTok在英国推出每月3.99英镑的无广告订阅服务。选择订阅的用户可以免除个性化广告，而不订阅的用户仍可继续免费使用平台，但会默认看到个性化推荐广告。

此举是TikTok在欧洲市场探索多元化收入模式的最新尝试，也反映出社交媒体平台在广告收入之外寻求新增长点的趋势。

## TikTok Launches £3.99 Ad-Free Subscription in UK

BBC reports that TikTok has launched a £3.99 per month ad-free subscription option in the UK. Subscribers can skip personalized ads, while non-subscribers can continue using the platform for free with default personalized advertising. The move represents TikTok's latest attempt to diversify revenue streams in the European market.
