## 中文

SpaceX的星舰V3（Starship V3）Flight 12首次发射于北京时间5月22日上午在最后时刻因地面系统问题被迫取消。这是有史以来最强大的火箭的首次飞行尝试。

星舰V3是SpaceX在原有星舰基础上的重大升级版本，拥有更大的推力与载荷能力。本次发射计划从德克萨斯州博卡奇卡的星际基地发射台升空，但在发射倒计时最后阶段，地面系统出现异常，任务控制中心决定中止发射程序。

SpaceX表示，团队正在排查地面系统问题，将在确认安全后重新安排发射窗口。此次Flight 12任务旨在验证V3版本的飞行性能和星舰的回收能力。

星舰项目是SpaceX的核心战略，承载着火星殖民和NASA阿尔忒弥斯登月计划的运输需求。V3版本的推出标志着该火箭系统的又一次重大迭代。

## English

SpaceX's Starship V3 Flight 12 launch was scrubbed at the last minute due to a ground system issue on Thursday morning Beijing time. This was the first flight attempt of the most powerful rocket ever built.

Starship V3 represents a major upgrade over the previous Starship iteration, with significantly increased thrust and payload capacity. The launch was planned from the Starbase facility in Boca Chica, Texas, but an anomaly in the ground systems during the final countdown led mission control to abort the launch sequence.

SpaceX said the team is investigating the ground system issue and will reschedule the launch once safety is confirmed. Flight 12 was intended to validate the V3 version's flight performance and Starship recovery capabilities.

The Starship program is central to SpaceX's strategy, supporting both Mars colonization ambitions and NASA's Artemis lunar program. The V3 version marks another major iteration in the rocket system's development.
