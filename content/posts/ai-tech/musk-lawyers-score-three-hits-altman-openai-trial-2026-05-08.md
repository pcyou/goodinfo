## 马斯克律师在OpenAI诉讼中得分：对Sam Altman的三次关键打击

Elon Musk针对OpenAI提起的诉讼迎来新进展。其律师团队在最新庭审中对Sam Altman实现了三次关键打击，进一步质疑OpenAI向营利性转型的合法性。

### 庭审焦点

Musk的律师团队围绕以下核心问题展开质询：
- OpenAI最初的非营利使命是否被违背
- 向GPT-4等闭源模型转变的决策过程
- Altman在OpenAI治理结构变更中的角色

### 法律背景

Musk作为OpenAI联合创始人之一，于2024年提起诉讼，指控OpenAI背离了最初的非营利使命，转向纯粹的营利模式。此案被视为AI行业最具影响力的法律纠纷之一。

### 行业影响

案件结果将深远影响AI行业的治理模式和非营利研究机构的运作方式。

### 后续进展

案件将继续审理，双方预计将提交更多证据和证词。

## Elon Musk's Lawyers Score Points Against Sam Altman in OpenAI Trial

Elon Musk's lawsuit against OpenAI has reached a new milestone, with his legal team achieving three key hits against Sam Altman in the latest court hearing, further questioning the legitimacy of OpenAI's transition to a for-profit model.

### Trial Focus

Musk's legal team centered their questioning on:
- Whether OpenAI's original non-profit mission has been violated
- The decision-making process behind the shift to closed-source models like GPT-4
- Altman's role in changes to OpenAI's governance structure

### Legal Background

Musk, as a co-founder of OpenAI, filed the lawsuit in 2024, alleging that OpenAI has strayed from its original non-profit mission toward a purely for-profit model. The case is considered one of the most impactful legal disputes in the AI industry.

### Industry Impact

The outcome will profoundly influence AI industry governance models and how non-profit research organizations operate.

### Next Steps

The trial will continue, with both sides expected to submit additional evidence and testimony.
