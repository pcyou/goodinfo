## [快讯] Google Cloud and Solana Foundation Launch Pay-as-You-Go System for AI Agents

### 中文

Solana基金会的Pay.sh平台为AI机器人提供了发现、访问和支付Google Cloud API使用费用的新方式，标志着区块链技术在AI经济中迈出了重要一步。

### English

The Solana Foundation's Pay.sh platform provides a new way for AI bots to discover, access, and pay for API usage on Google Cloud, marking a significant step for blockchain technology in the AI economy.
