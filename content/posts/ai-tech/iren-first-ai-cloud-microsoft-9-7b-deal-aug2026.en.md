## Core Summary

IREN has officially delivered its first AI cloud deployment project under a $9.7 billion contract with Microsoft. This milestone signals that large-scale cooperation between tech giants and specialized compute providers is entering the execution phase, accelerating the AI infrastructure outsourcing model.

## Details

IREN previously signed a long-term contract worth $9.7 billion with Microsoft to provide AI cloud computing infrastructure services. The first deployment delivered is part of this contract, with three more major deployments expected to follow.

IREN, formerly a cryptocurrency mining company, has aggressively pivoted to become an AI compute provider. This delivery to Microsoft validates the strategic feasibility of its transition from crypto mining to AI infrastructure.
