## 核心摘要

英国皇家天文台馆长帕迪·罗杰斯（Paddy Rodgers）警告称，AI即时提供答案的能力可能会"贬低人类智慧"。他表示，天文台丰富的历史展示了人类知识的力量，社会需要避免对AI的过度依赖。

## English Summary

Paddy Rodgers, head of the Royal Observatory, has warned that instant AI answers can "trivialise human intelligence." He said the Observatory's rich history demonstrates the power of human knowledge and the need to avoid over-dependence on AI for learning and discovery.
