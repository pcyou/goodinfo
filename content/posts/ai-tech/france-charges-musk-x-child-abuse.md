## 法国拟对马斯克和X提起刑事指控

法国检方正在寻求对埃隆·马斯克及X社交平台提起刑事指控，原因是该平台上被发现存在大量儿童性虐待材料。

据Yahoo新闻报道，法国检察官经过数月调查后认为，X平台在内容审核方面存在严重疏漏，未能有效阻止非法内容的传播。这一指控如果成立，马斯克和X平台可能面临巨额罚款甚至更严厉的处罚。

自马斯克收购Twitter并更名为X以来，该平台的内容审核政策引发了全球多国的关注和批评。法国此次行动标志着欧洲对社交媒体平台监管的进一步收紧。

## French Prosecutors Seek Charges Against Musk and X Over CSAM

French prosecutors are seeking to bring criminal charges against Elon Musk and X (formerly Twitter) over the distribution of child sexual abuse material on the platform.

According to Yahoo, French prosecutors concluded after months of investigation that X had serious gaps in its content moderation, failing to effectively prevent the spread of illegal content. If the charges are upheld, Musk and X could face substantial fines or even more severe penalties.

Since Musk's acquisition of Twitter and its rebranding to X, the platform's content moderation policies have drawn scrutiny from multiple countries worldwide. France's action signals a tightening of social media regulation across Europe.
