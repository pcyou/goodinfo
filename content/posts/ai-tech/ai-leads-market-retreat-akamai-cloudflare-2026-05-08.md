## AI股领跌市场：Akamai和Cloudflare成盘中最大波动股

周四，人工智能相关股票领跌美国股市，Akamai Technologies和Cloudflare成为盘中波动最大的个股。

### 市场概况

AI板块在连续数月上涨后出现回调。投资者获利了结情绪升温，叠加伊朗局势升级和关税政策不确定性，科技股整体承压。

### 个股表现

- **Akamai**：股价大幅下跌，市场担忧其CDN和边缘计算业务增长放缓
- **Cloudflare**：盘中波动剧烈，尽管公司基本面强劲，但高估值使其在市场避险情绪中首当其冲

### 分析师观点

多位分析师指出，AI基础设施投资的长期逻辑并未改变，但短期估值已反映过多乐观预期。当前回调可能是健康的盘整。

"AI仍然是未来十年最重要的技术趋势，但投资者需要区分真正的AI受益者和跟风炒作。"一位华尔街分析师表示。

### 后市展望

市场关注即将发布的美联储会议纪要和通胀数据，这些将决定科技股下一步走势。

## AI Leads Market Retreat; Akamai and Cloudflare Are Biggest Movers

Artificial intelligence-related stocks led a US market retreat on Thursday, with Akamai Technologies and Cloudflare emerging as the most volatile names of the session.

### Market Overview

The AI sector pulled back after months of strong gains. Profit-taking accelerated, compounded by escalating tensions with Iran and tariff policy uncertainty, putting pressure on tech stocks broadly.

### Individual Stock Performance

- **Akamai**: Shares fell sharply, with market concerns about slowing growth in its CDN and edge computing business
- **Cloudflare**: Experienced sharp intraday swings; despite strong fundamentals, its premium valuation made it vulnerable in a risk-off environment

### Analyst Perspectives

Several analysts noted that the long-term thesis for AI infrastructure investment remains intact, but short-term valuations have priced in excessive optimism. The current pullback could represent a healthy consolidation.

"AI remains the most important technology trend of the next decade, but investors need to distinguish genuine AI beneficiaries from hype-driven names," said one Wall Street analyst.

### Outlook

The market is focused on the upcoming Fed minutes and inflation data, which will determine the next direction for tech stocks.
