据《华尔街日报》独家报道，OpenAI 正在准备正式提交首次公开募股（IPO）申请，标志着这家全球最炙手可热的 AI 公司距离上市仅一步之遥。

OpenAI 自推出 ChatGPT 以来迅速成长为 AI 行业的标杆企业。知情人士透露，公司正在与承销商敲定最终细节，预计最早将于今年内递交申请文件。若顺利推进，这将成为近年来规模最大的科技 IPO 之一。

分析人士指出，OpenAI 的最新估值可能超过 3000 亿美元，远超其此前私募轮次的定价。推动上市的动力来自于持续的算力投入和商业化需求——训练更大规模的模型需要巨额资金，而公开市场能提供更充裕的融资渠道。

然而，挑战同样显著。OpenAI 目前尚未实现盈利，其庞大的基础设施支出与收入之间的差距仍是投资者关注的焦点。此外，公司在治理结构上的特殊性——非营利组织与商业实体的混合模式——也需要在招股书中得到清晰说明。

与此同时，AI 行业的竞争格局正在快速演变。Anthropic、Google DeepMind 等竞争对手也在加速追赶，OpenAI 需要通过 IPO 巩固其领先地位并获取更多资源。

OpenAI IPO 不仅是一家公司的上市，更是 AI 行业从实验室走向资本市场的标志性事件。

## English

OpenAI is preparing to file for an initial public offering, according to an exclusive report by The Wall Street Journal, bringing the world's most prominent AI company one step closer to going public.

Sources familiar with the matter say OpenAI is finalizing details with underwriters and could submit its registration documents as early as this year. The IPO would rank among the largest tech listings in recent history, with estimates placing the company's valuation above $300 billion.

The push for public markets is driven by the enormous capital requirements of training ever-larger AI models. While OpenAI's revenue has grown substantially since ChatGPT's launch, its infrastructure spending continues to outpace income. An IPO would provide access to deeper pools of capital needed to maintain its competitive edge.

Key challenges remain. OpenAI is not yet profitable, and its unusual governance structure — a hybrid of nonprofit and commercial entities — will require careful explanation in its prospectus. Meanwhile, rivals including Anthropic and Google DeepMind are racing to close the gap.

An OpenAI IPO would mark a watershed moment: AI's transition from research labs to the public markets.
