## [快讯] 英国税务海关总署将采用AI系统识别税务欺诈

据BBC Tech报道，英国税务海关总署（HMRC）已与金融数据平台Quantexa签约，斥资1.75亿英镑部署AI系统，用于识别税务欺诈和申报表错误。该合作标志着英国政府在政务科技领域最大规模的AI应用之一。

UK tax authority HMRC has signed a £175 million deal with financial data platform Quantexa to deploy AI systems for detecting fraud and tax return errors. The partnership represents one of the largest government AI deployments in UK history.
