## Ripple, JPMorgan Use XRP Ledger for Tokenized Treasury Transfer

Ripple and JPMorgan jointly executed a cross-border transfer of a tokenized Treasurys fund on the XRP Ledger, demonstrating 24/7 settlement capabilities between crypto and Wall Street.
