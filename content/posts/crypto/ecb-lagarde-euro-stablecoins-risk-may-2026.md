## ECB's Lagarde Warns Euro Stablecoins Pose Financial Stability Risk

[快讯] 欧洲央行行长拉加德警告称，欧元计价稳定币对金融稳定和货币政策构成风险，主张推进数字欧元而非私营稳定币。这与德国联邦银行的立场出现分歧。

ECB President Christine Lagarde warned that euro-denominated stablecoins pose risks to financial stability and monetary policy, advocating for a digital euro over private stablecoins. Her stance diverges from that of the Bundesbank.
