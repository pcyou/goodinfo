## [快讯] Western Union's Solana-Based Stablecoin Could Reshape Cross-Border Payments

### 中文

西联汇款在Solana上推出了美元支持的稳定币USDPT，由Anchorage Digital Bank发行，用于与合作代理和合作伙伴进行全天候结算，分析师称此举可能重塑传统汇款模式。

### English

Western Union launched USDPT, a dollar-backed stablecoin on Solana issued by Anchorage Digital Bank, for 24/7 settlement with agents and partners. Analysts say this move could reshape traditional remittance models.
