## Block Q1业绩超预期：全年指引上调，录得1.73亿美元比特币重估收益

由Jack Dorsey创立的金融科技公司Block公布第一季度财报，业绩超出华尔街预期，并上调了全年业绩指引。

### 核心数据

- Q1录得1.73亿美元的比特币重估收益
- 营收同比增长，主要得益于Cash App业务的持续增长
- 全年业绩指引上调，反映管理层对公司前景的信心

### 业务亮点

Block旗下两大核心业务均表现强劲：
- **Square**：商户支付处理量持续增长，AI驱动的商户工具受到欢迎
- **Cash App**：用户活跃度和交易规模双双提升，Bitcoin业务收入稳定

### 战略方向

Block继续推进其"比特币优先"战略，同时加大对AI和金融科技创新的投入。公司在AI代理支付等前沿领域也有所布局。

### 市场反应

财报公布后，Block股价上涨，投资者对业绩超预期和上调指引表示认可。

## Dorsey's Block Raises Full-Year Guidance After Strong Q1, Records $173M Bitcoin Remeasurement Gain

Financial technology company Block, founded by Jack Dorsey, reported first-quarter results that beat Wall Street expectations and raised full-year guidance.

### Key Figures

- Q1 recorded a $173 million Bitcoin remeasurement gain
- Revenue grew year-over-year, primarily driven by continued Cash App business growth
- Full-year guidance was raised, reflecting management's confidence in the company's outlook

### Business Highlights

Both of Block's core businesses performed strongly:
- **Square**: Merchant payment processing volume continues to grow, AI-powered merchant tools are gaining traction
- **Cash App**: Both user activity and transaction volume increased, with stable Bitcoin business revenue

### Strategic Direction

Block continues to advance its "Bitcoin-first" strategy while increasing investment in AI and fintech innovation. The company is also exploring frontier areas such as AI agent payments.

### Market Reaction

Block shares rose following the earnings release, with investors applauding the beat and raised guidance.
