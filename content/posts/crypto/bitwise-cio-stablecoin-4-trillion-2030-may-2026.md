# Bitwise首席投资官：稳定币供应量2030年或达4万亿美元

据The Block报道，Bitwise首席投资官Matt Hougan表示，随着DoorDash和Meta等科技公司探索稳定币支付方案，到2030年稳定币总供应量可能达到4万亿美元。这一预测反映了稳定币从加密货币交易工具向主流支付基础设施的转变趋势。

## Brief: Stablecoin Supply Could Hit $4T by 2030

Bitwise CIO Matt Hougan projects stablecoin supply could reach $4 trillion by 2030, driven by tech companies like DoorDash and Meta exploring stablecoin payout solutions, signaling a shift from trading tools to mainstream payment infrastructure.
