## 摘要

加密货币交易所Kraken的母公司Payward宣布以6亿美元收购香港稳定币公司Reap。这是加密行业近期最大规模的并购交易之一，标志着大型交易所加速布局稳定币和支付领域。Reap专注于亚洲市场的稳定币支付解决方案，此次收购将帮助Kraken拓展亚太地区的业务版图。交易预计将在2026年下半年完成。

## Summary

Cryptocurrency exchange Kraken's parent company Payward announced a $600 百万 acquisition of Hong Kong-based stablecoin firm Reap. This is one of the largest M&A deals in the crypto sector recently, signaling major exchanges' push into stablecoin and payments. Reap focuses on stablecoin payment solutions in Asian markets. The deal is expected to close in the second half of 2026.
