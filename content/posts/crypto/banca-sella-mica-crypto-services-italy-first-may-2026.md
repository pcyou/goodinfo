## Banca Sella First Italian Bank Approved Under MiCA for Crypto Services

### 中文

意大利私营银行Banca Sella获意大利央行在欧盟MiCA框架下批准，成为首家获准提供数字资产托管和转账服务的意大利银行，是欧洲银行业加密合规服务的重要里程碑。

### English

Italian private bank Banca Sella received Bank of Italy approval under the EU's MiCA framework, becoming the first Italian lender authorized to offer digital asset custody and transfer services — a key milestone for European banking in compliant crypto services.
