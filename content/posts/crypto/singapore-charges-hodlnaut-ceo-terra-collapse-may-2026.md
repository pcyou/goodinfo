## Singapore Charges Ex-Hodlnaut CEO Over Terra Collapse Misstatements

### 中文

新加坡当局起诉Hodlnaut前CEO朱俊涛，指控其在2022年TerraUSD崩盘期间通过Telegram和邮件发布虚假信息，声称公司未受影响。若罪名成立最高可面临20年监禁，是新加坡加强加密监管执法的重要案例。

### English

Singapore authorities have charged former Hodlnaut CEO Zhu Juntao for allegedly publishing false statements during the 2022 TerraUSD collapse, claiming the company was unaffected. If convicted, he faces up to 20 years in prison, marking another significant enforcement action in Singapore's crypto regulatory efforts.
