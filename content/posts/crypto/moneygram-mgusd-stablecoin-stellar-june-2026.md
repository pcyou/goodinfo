## MoneyGram Launches MGUSD Stablecoin on Stellar

MoneyGram has officially launched its MGUSD stablecoin on the Stellar blockchain, integrating it into its global payments network. The move positions the remittance giant alongside a growing list of financial institutions embracing digital dollar infrastructure for cross-border transactions.

The stablecoin is designed to facilitate faster and cheaper international money transfers, leveraging Stellar's low-cost transaction network. MoneyGram's existing global payment infrastructure will serve as on-ramps and off-ramps for the digital asset, potentially reaching millions of users worldwide.

The launch comes amid a broader trend of traditional payment companies entering the stablecoin space, as digital dollar adoption accelerates globally.

## 快讯

MoneyGram正式在Stellar区块链上推出MGUSD稳定币，整合至其全球支付网络。该稳定币旨在促进更快速、更廉价的国际汇款，利用Stellar的低成本交易网络。此举标志着传统支付公司进入稳定币领域的趋势加速。
