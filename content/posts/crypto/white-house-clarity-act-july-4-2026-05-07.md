## [快讯] 白宫瞄准7月4日通过《清晰法案》，加密监管框架即将落地

白宫数字资产顾问帕特里克·维特表示，目标在7月4日前通过《清晰法案》（Clarity Act），该法案旨在为加密货币行业提供明确的监管框架。

维特透露，参议院银行委员会将在本月举行关于市场结构的听证会。Coinbase首席法律官保罗·格鲁瓦尔也支持该法案将在今年夏天通过，并呼吁银行接受稳定币妥协方案。

这标志着美国加密监管立法进程正在加速推进。

## [Brief] White House Targets July 4 for Clarity Act Passage, Crypto Regulatory Framework Nears

White House digital assets adviser Patrick Witt said the goal is to pass the Clarity Act by July 4, which aims to provide a clear regulatory framework for the crypto industry.

Witt revealed that the Senate Banking Committee will hold a hearing on market structure this month. Coinbase CLO Paul Grewal also backed the bill's passage this summer and urged banks to accept the stablecoin compromise.

This marks an acceleration in the US crypto regulatory legislative process.
