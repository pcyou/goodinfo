## [快讯] Sequans Sells 1,025 Bitcoin for Debt Redemption as Revenue Drops

### 中文

Sequans出售了1,025枚比特币，将其财库储备降至1,114枚BTC。该公司第一季度营收下降24.8%至610万美元，出售所得将用于债务赎回和股票回购。

### English

Sequans sold 1,025 bitcoin, reducing its treasury reserves to 1,114 BTC. The company's Q1 revenue fell 24.8% to $6.1 million, with proceeds used for debt redemption and share buybacks.
