## [快讯] Wormhole Bridges Canonical TAO Token to Solana

### 中文

Wormhole通过其Sunrise平台将Bittensor的TAO代币"经典"版本桥接至Solana生态，该功能作为"首日资产网关"支持原生资产的跨链转移。

### English

Wormhole has bridged the 'canonical' version of Bittensor's TAO token to the Solana ecosystem via its Sunrise platform, which acts as a "day-one asset gateway" for cross-chain transfers of native assets.
