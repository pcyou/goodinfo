## [快讯] 新比特币战略储备法案调整

一项新的比特币战略储备法案已取消此前设定的100万枚BTC购买目标，但增加了20年锁定期。该法案还要求对政府持有的比特币进行季度公开储备证明披露和第三方审计。

## New Strategic Bitcoin Reserve Bill Adjusted

A new strategic Bitcoin reserve bill has dropped the previous 1 million BTC purchase target but added a 20-year lockup period. The bill also requires quarterly public proof-of-reserve disclosures and third-party audits of government Bitcoin holdings. The adjustment reflects lawmakers' cautious approach to establishing a national Bitcoin reserve while strengthening transparency and accountability.
