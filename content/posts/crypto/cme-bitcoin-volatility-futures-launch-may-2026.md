## CME Group to Launch Cash-Settled Bitcoin Volatility Futures

### 中文

CME 集团宣布计划推出现金结算的比特币波动率期货，允许机构交易者通过受监管的衍生品市场对冲和投机 BTC 价格波动。这是传统金融市场加密产品线的又一重要扩展。

### English

CME Group announced plans to launch cash-settled bitcoin volatility futures, enabling institutional traders to hedge and speculate on BTC price swings through regulated derivatives markets.
