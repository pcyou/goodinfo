## [快讯] 白宫瞄准7月4日通过加密货币清晰法案

白宫加密货币顾问Patrick Witt表示，政府的目标是在7月4日前通过《清晰法案》（Clarity Act）。该法案旨在为加密货币行业建立明确的监管框架，消除当前的监管不确定性。The Block和CoinDesk均报道了这一消息。此举被视为美国加密监管史上的重要里程碑。

## White House Targets July 4 for Clarity Act Passage

White House crypto adviser Patrick Witt said the administration aims to pass the Clarity Act by July 4. The bill aims to establish a clear regulatory framework for the crypto industry, eliminating current regulatory uncertainty. Both The Block and CoinDesk reported the news, calling it a significant milestone in US crypto regulation history.
