## [快讯] 加密衍生品与华尔街加速融合，股票永续合约或成下一座桥梁

加密货币与传统金融的融合正在加速，永续合约（perpetual futures）被认为是连接两个世界最有力的桥梁。分析师指出，股票永续合约可能很快成为下一个突破点，让传统投资者能够以加密货币市场的交易机制参与股票市场。这一趋势反映了华尔街对加密衍生品基础设施日益增长的接纳度。

Crypto and traditional finance are converging rapidly, with perpetual futures emerging as the strongest bridge between the two worlds. Analysts suggest equity perpetual contracts could be the next breakthrough, allowing traditional investors to access stock markets through crypto trading mechanisms. This trend reflects Wall Street's growing acceptance of crypto derivatives infrastructure.
