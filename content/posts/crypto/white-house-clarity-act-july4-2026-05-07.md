## 白宫为加密立法设定7月4日目标期限

白宫数字资产顾问委员会执行主任Patrick Witt周二表示，白宫正瞄准7月4日作为通过里程碑式加密货币监管法案的目标日期。这是迄今美国政府为加密立法设定的最明确时间表。

Witt透露，参议院银行委员会将于本月就市场结构法案举行听证会，标志着立法进程进入关键阶段。该法案旨在为数字资产市场建立清晰的监管框架，结束多年来监管不确定性的局面。

此举被视为美国加密行业的重要转折点。在过去数年中，加密企业一直面临来自SEC和CFTC双重监管的模糊地带，而《加密清晰法案》有望明确各监管机构的管辖边界。

参议院银行委员会的听证会将聚焦市场结构、稳定币监管和数字资产分类等核心议题。如果法案按计划在7月4日前通过，美国将成为全球主要经济体中首个建立全面加密监管框架的国家。

行业观察者指出，这一时间表与当前国会两党对加密立法的积极态度相吻合，但也需考虑到立法流程中可能出现的变数和修订需求。

## White House Sets July 4 Target for Landmark Crypto Legislation

Patrick Witt, executive director of the President's Council of Advisors for Digital Assets, said Tuesday that the White House is targeting July 4 as the deadline to pass landmark cryptocurrency regulation legislation. This marks the clearest timeline yet set by the U.S. government for crypto lawmaking.

Witt revealed that the Senate Banking Committee will hold hearings on market structure legislation this month, signaling a critical phase in the legislative process. The bill aims to establish a clear regulatory framework for digital asset markets, ending years of regulatory uncertainty.

This move is seen as a major turning point for the U.S. crypto industry. For years, crypto businesses have operated in a gray area of overlapping SEC and CFTC jurisdiction, and the Clarity Act would define the boundaries of each regulator's authority.

The Senate Banking Committee hearings will focus on market structure, stablecoin regulation, and digital asset classification. If passed by July 4 as planned, the U.S. would become the first major economy to establish a comprehensive crypto regulatory framework.

Industry observers note that this timeline aligns with the current bipartisan enthusiasm for crypto legislation in Congress, though potential amendments and revisions during the legislative process remain a variable.
