## 核心摘要

比特币价格跌破77000美元，受伊朗局势升级和国债收益率上升影响。分析人士指出，投资者担心高油价可能推高通胀至迫使美联储加息的水平。加密市场多头已清算超过5.63亿美元。

## English Summary

Bitcoin has dropped below $77,000 as geopolitical tensions over Iran and rising Treasury yields weigh on risk assets. Analysts warn that soaring oil prices could push inflation high enough to force the Federal Reserve to raise interest rates. Crypto traders betting on a rally have seen $563M in liquidations.
