## [快讯] Solana's 'Alpenglow' Upgrade Could Arrive Next Quarter

### 中文

Solana联合创始人在Consensus Miami 2026大会上表示，Alpenglow升级预计最早将于下一季度上线。该升级被Solana社区视为提升网络性能和安全性的重要里程碑。

### English

Solana's co-founder said at Consensus Miami 2026 that the 'Alpenglow' upgrade could arrive as early as next quarter. The upgrade is seen by the Solana community as a key milestone for improving network performance and security.
