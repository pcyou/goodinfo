# [快讯] SEC主席Atkins信号将制定链上市场与AI金融新规则

美国证券交易委员会（SEC）主席Paul Atkins周五表示，SEC将考虑为链上市场结构和软件应用制定新的监管规则。Atkins将AI驱动的金融系统与区块链市场基础设施的需求增长联系起来，认为自动化结算和基于区块链的市场需要明确的监管框架。

Atkins指出，SEC需要明确其监管框架如何适用于软件应用程序。这一信号表明，在加密货币和AI金融交叉领域，美国监管机构正在寻求建立更清晰的规则体系。

与此同时，Kraken母公司Payward已申请联邦OCC信托牌照，Ripple和Coinbase也此前提出了类似申请，显示加密货币行业正积极寻求联邦层面的监管合规路径。

# [Brief] SEC Chair Atkins Signals New Rules for Onchain Markets and AI Finance

SEC Chair Paul Atkins said on Friday that the agency is considering new rulemaking for onchain market structures and software applications. Atkins linked the rise of AI-powered financial systems with growing demand for blockchain-based market infrastructure and automated settlement.

Atkins said the SEC needs to clarify how its regulatory framework applies to software applications. The signal suggests U.S. regulators are seeking clearer rules at the intersection of crypto and AI finance.

Meanwhile, Kraken parent Payward has applied for a federal OCC trust charter, following similar applications from Ripple and Coinbase, indicating the crypto industry's push for federal regulatory compliance.
