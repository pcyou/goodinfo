## [快讯] 加密托管商 Taurus 获得塞浦路斯 MiFID 牌照进军欧盟资本市场

加密资产托管公司 Taurus 已获得塞浦路斯证券交易委员会颁发的 MiFID 投资 firm 牌照，可直接进入欧盟资本市场。该牌照允许 Taurus 向欧盟银行提供代币化金融工具服务，包括代币化债券、基金份额、股票和结构化产品的二级交易。此举标志着传统金融与加密基础设施融合的重要一步，为机构级代币化资产在欧盟范围内的流通铺平了道路。

Crypto custodian Taurus has obtained a MiFID investment firm license from the Cyprus Securities and Exchange Commission, enabling direct access to EU capital markets. The license allows Taurus to offer tokenized financial instruments to EU banks, including secondary trading for tokenized bonds, fund shares, equities, and structured products. This marks a significant step in the convergence of traditional finance and crypto infrastructure.
