## [快讯] SEC委员回应加密规则争议

SEC委员Hester Peirce就该机构加密规则提案可能助长合成代币的担忧作出回应。她试图澄清现已推迟的提案的真实意图，以平息市场的误解。

Peirce作为SEC加密工作组背后的委员，其言论对加密监管方向具有重要影响。

## SEC Commissioner Addresses Crypto Rule Concerns

SEC Commissioner Hester Peirce addressed concerns that the agency's crypto rule proposal could foster synthetic tokens. She attempted to clarify the intent of the now-delayed proposal to quell market misconceptions. As the commissioner behind the SEC's Crypto Task Force, her statements carry significant weight for the direction of crypto regulation.
