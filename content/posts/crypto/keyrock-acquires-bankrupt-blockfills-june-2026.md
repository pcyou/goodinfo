## Keyrock收购Blockfills

据CoinDesk报道，布鲁塞尔数字资产服务公司Keyrock正在收购已破产的芝加哥公司Blockfills。Blockfills此前申请破产，Keyrock的计划收购显示加密行业在经历市场调整后仍在进行整合。

## Keyrock Acquires Bankrupt Blockfills

Brussels-based digital asset services firm Keyrock is acquiring bankrupt Chicago-based company Blockfills, signaling continued consolidation in the crypto industry despite market headwinds.
