## 韩亚银行入股Upbit母公司

韩国韩亚银行（Hana Bank）宣布收购Upbit母公司Dunamu的228万股股份，交易价值约6.7亿美元。收购完成后，韩亚银行将持有Dunamu 6.55%的股份，成为其第四大股东。

此举标志着韩国传统金融机构对加密货币行业的投资持续加深。Upbit是韩国最大的加密货币交易平台，Dunamu则是其运营母公司。

## Hana Bank Acquires $670M Stake in Upbit Parent

South Korea's Hana Bank has acquired a 2.28 million share stake in Dunamu, the parent company of Upbit — South Korea's largest cryptocurrency exchange. The $670 million deal gives Hana Bank a 6.55% stake, making it the fourth-largest shareholder in Dunamu.

The investment signals deepening ties between traditional Korean financial institutions and the crypto industry.
