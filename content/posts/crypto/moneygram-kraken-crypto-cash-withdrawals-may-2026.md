## [快讯] MoneyGram Supports Crypto-to-Cash Withdrawals for Kraken Users

### 中文

MoneyGram开始支持Kraken用户将加密货币提取为现金，双方计划将合作扩展至本地银行存款和跨境汇款服务。

### English

MoneyGram now supports Kraken users withdrawing crypto as cash. The firms plan to expand the partnership beyond cash withdrawals to support local bank deposits and cross-border remittance flows.
