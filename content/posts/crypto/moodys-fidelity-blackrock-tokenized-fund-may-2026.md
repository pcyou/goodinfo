## 穆迪给予代币化基金最高信用评级

穆迪投资者服务公司授予富达和贝莱德联合推出的代币化货币基金最高信用评级。此举被视为传统金融机构对区块链代币化资产认可的重要标志。

## Moody Awards Top Rating to Tokenized Fund

Moody's Investors Service has awarded its highest credit rating to a tokenized money market fund jointly launched by Fidelity and BlackRock. This marks a significant milestone in traditional finance's recognition of blockchain-based tokenized assets.
