## [快讯] Robinhood Crypto首席运营官离职

Robinhood Crypto首席运营官Tanya Denisova在任职五年多后离职。此时该交易平台正面临加密收入的大幅下滑。

Denisova的离开是加密交易平台近期人事变动中的最新一例，反映了行业在当前市场环境下面临的挑战。

## Robinhood Crypto COO Departs

Robinhood Crypto COO Tanya Denisova is leaving after more than five years with the firm. The departure comes as the trading platform navigates a sharp decline in crypto revenue. Denisova's exit is the latest in a series of personnel changes at crypto trading platforms, reflecting challenges the industry faces in the current market environment.
