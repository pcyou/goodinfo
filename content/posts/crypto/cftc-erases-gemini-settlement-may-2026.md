# [快讯] 美国CFTC请求撤销与Gemini的和解协议

## US CFTC Files Request to Erase Gemini Settlement

据CoinDesk报道，美国商品期货交易委员会(CFTC)已提交请求，要求撤销此前与加密货币交易所Gemini达成的和解协议，理由是CFTC认为该协议"不再被视为公平"。

这是加密监管领域的一个重大变化，可能对Gemini及其相关业务产生深远影响。

According to CoinDesk, the US Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) has filed a request to void its previous settlement with cryptocurrency exchange Gemini, stating that the agreement is "no longer considered fair." This represents a significant shift in crypto regulation that could have far-reaching implications for Gemini and its related operations.
