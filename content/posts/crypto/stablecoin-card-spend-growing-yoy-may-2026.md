## Stablecoin Card Spending Growing 100% Year Over Year

[快讯] Rain公司高管表示，稳定币卡消费同比增速达100%。稳定币结算支持周末和假日处理，将资金锁定时间缩短超过40%，显著改善了卡支付的经济模型和资金灵活性。

Stablecoin card spending is growing 100% year over year, according to a Rain executive. Stablecoin settlement enables weekend and holiday processing, reducing trapped capital by over 40% and improving card economics.
