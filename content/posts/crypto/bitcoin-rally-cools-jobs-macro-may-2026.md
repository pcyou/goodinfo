## [快讯] 比特币涨势降温

比特币价格在4月就业数据公布后涨势放缓。尽管就业数据超出预期，但未能突破加密货币市场面临的宏观经济阻力位。

伊朗局势的紧张以及比特币ETF出现资金外流也给市场带来了下行压力。分析人士认为，短期内比特币可能需要在当前水平进行整理，等待更明确的宏观信号。

市场参与者正在密切关注美联储的政策立场以及地缘政治局势的演变，这些因素将对加密货币的下一步走势产生重要影响。

## Bitcoin Rally Cools as Jobs Data Fails to Break Macro Ceiling

Bitcoin's rally cooled after April jobs data, while geopolitical tensions surrounding Iran and Bitcoin ETF outflows added downward pressure. Despite the employment report beating expectations, it failed to break through the macroeconomic resistance levels facing crypto markets.

Analysts suggest Bitcoin may need to consolidate at current levels while awaiting clearer macro signals. Market participants are closely watching Federal Reserve policy positioning and geopolitical developments as key drivers for the next directional move.
