## 白宫顾问：美国比特币储备更新将在"未来几周"公布

据CoinDesk报道，白宫顾问表示，美国比特币储备战略的更新将在"未来几周"内公布。这一表态引发了加密市场的广泛关注和讨论。

白宫顾问在Consensus Miami会议上表示，政府正在认真考虑比特币在国家战略储备中的角色。尽管目前没有透露具体细节，但这一信号被市场解读为美国对加密货币政策持续转向友好的标志。

自特朗普政府上台以来，美国对加密货币的监管态度发生了显著变化。此前白宫已设定7月4日为通过《清晰法案》（Clarity Act）的目标日期，该法案旨在为加密货币行业提供明确的监管框架。

加密行业领袖在Consensus Miami会议上表示，加密货币的主流化时刻已经到来。随着机构投资者的持续入场和监管环境的改善，加密资产正逐步融入传统金融体系。

VanEck分析师此前预测，比特币有望在五年内达到100万美元，并将比特币的采用曲线与电子游戏的普及过程进行了类比。

## White House Adviser: US Bitcoin Reserve Update Coming in 'Next Few Weeks'

According to CoinDesk, a White House adviser stated that an update on the US Bitcoin reserve strategy will be announced within the "next few weeks." This statement has sparked widespread attention and discussion in the crypto market.

The White House adviser spoke at the Consensus Miami conference, noting that the government is seriously considering Bitcoin's role in the national strategic reserve. While no specific details were disclosed, the signal is interpreted by the market as a continuation of the US shift toward crypto-friendly policies.

Since the Trump administration took office, US regulatory attitudes toward cryptocurrency have changed significantly. The White House has previously set a July 4 target date for passing the Clarity Act, which aims to provide a clear regulatory framework for the crypto industry.

Crypto industry leaders at Consensus Miami said the mainstream moment for cryptocurrency has arrived. With continued institutional investor entry and an improving regulatory environment, crypto assets are gradually integrating into the traditional financial system.

VanEck analysts previously predicted that Bitcoin could reach $1 million within five years, comparing Bitcoin's adoption curve to the popularization of video games.
