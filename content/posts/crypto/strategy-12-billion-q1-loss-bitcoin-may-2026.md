## Strategy Posts $12.54 Billion Q1 Loss as Bitcoin Price Declines

### 中文

加密货币企业 Strategy（前 MicroStrategy）公布了 2026 年第一季度财报，报告显示公司录得高达 125.4 亿美元的季度亏损，主要受比特币价格持续下跌影响。这是该公司历史上最大的单季度亏损之一，也是企业持有大量加密货币资产所面临风险的典型案例。

Strategy 的巨额亏损并非来自运营层面的问题，而是由于其持有的大量比特币资产在会计上需要按公允价值计价。当比特币市场价格下跌时，公司必须确认相应的未实现损失，这直接反映在利润表中。

截至第一季度末，Strategy 仍持有超过 40 万枚比特币，总成本约 250 亿美元。由于比特币价格从高位回落，这些持仓的未实现损失直接导致了季度账面亏损。

尽管财报显示巨额亏损，Strategy 管理层重申其长期持有比特币的战略没有改变。公司认为，随着全球通胀压力持续和法定货币贬值趋势，比特币作为"数字黄金"的价值存储属性将得到进一步验证。

然而，这一立场也面临华尔街分析师的质疑。多位分析师指出，企业将资产负债表过度集中于单一加密资产，在价格波动剧烈的市场环境下风险极高。

财报公布后，Strategy 股价盘后下跌约 5%，反映出投资者对其资产负债表风险的担忧。

### English

Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy) reported a staggering $12.54 billion loss in its first quarter of 2026, driven primarily by declining bitcoin prices. The loss ranks among the largest quarterly losses in corporate history tied to cryptocurrency holdings.

The massive loss stems from accounting rules requiring Strategy to mark its bitcoin holdings to fair value. When bitcoin's market price falls, the company must recognize unrealized losses directly on its income statement.

As of Q1 2026, Strategy holds over 400,000 BTC with a total cost basis of approximately $25 billion. The decline in bitcoin prices from recent highs resulted in significant unrealized losses reflected in the quarterly report.

Despite the reported losses, Strategy's management reaffirmed its long-term bitcoin accumulation strategy. The company maintains that bitcoin's role as "digital gold" will be validated as inflation pressures persist and fiat currencies continue to depreciate.

However, Wall Street analysts have raised concerns about the company's concentrated exposure to a single, highly volatile asset on its balance sheet.

Following the earnings release, Strategy shares fell approximately 5% in after-hours trading, reflecting investor concerns about its balance sheet risk.
