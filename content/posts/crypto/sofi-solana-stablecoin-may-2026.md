## SoFi to Launch Stablecoin on Solana

### 中文

金融科技公司SoFi计划在Solana区块链上推出自己的稳定币， citing该网络的速度和低交易成本优势。这是传统金融机构拥抱加密货币基础设施的最新案例。

### English

Financial technology company SoFi plans to launch its own stablecoin on Solana, citing the network's speed and low transaction costs. This is the latest case of traditional finance embracing crypto infrastructure.
