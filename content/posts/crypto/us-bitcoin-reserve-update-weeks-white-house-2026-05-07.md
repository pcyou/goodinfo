## [快讯] 白宫顾问：美国比特币储备更新将在"未来几周"公布

白宫数字资产顾问帕特里克·维特（Patrick Witt）在Consensus Miami会议上表示，美国比特币储备战略的更新将在"未来几周"内公布。

维特引用了美国法警局持有资产近期遭遇的一次安全漏洞事件，证明联邦政府加密资产需要更安全的保管方案。这一表态被视为美国对加密货币政策持续转向友好的最新信号。

## [Brief] White House Adviser: US Bitcoin Reserve Update Coming in "Next Few Weeks"

White House digital assets adviser Patrick Witt said at Consensus Miami that an update on the US Bitcoin reserve strategy will come in the "next few weeks."

Witt cited a recent exploit involving assets held by the US Marshals as proof that federal crypto holdings need better safeguarding. This statement is seen as the latest signal of the US government's continued shift toward a friendlier crypto policy.
