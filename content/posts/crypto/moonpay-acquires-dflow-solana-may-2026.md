## [快讯] MoonPay Acquires Solana Execution Layer Firm DFlow

### 中文

MoonPay收购了Solana执行层公司DFlow，该平台被Coinbase和Phantom使用，累计处理交易量超过500亿美元。

### English

MoonPay has acquired Solana execution layer firm DFlow, which is used by both Coinbase and Phantom and has processed over $50 billion in cumulative trading volume.
