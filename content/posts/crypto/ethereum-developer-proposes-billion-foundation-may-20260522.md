## 中文

以太坊基金会前核心开发者提出了一项大胆计划：募集十亿美元成立一个全新的独立基金会，以应对当前以太坊生态面临的人才流失和方向争议。

这一提议发生在以太坊基金会多名高级开发人员相继离职之后。提议者认为，现有的基金会架构已无法有效推进以太坊的技术路线图，需要一个全新的、资金更充裕的组织来领导生态发展。

消息人士透露，该计划已获得部分加密投资机构的初步兴趣。如果成功实施，这将是加密货币领域最大规模的一次基金会融资。

以太坊当前正面临多重挑战：Layer 2扩容方案的竞争日益激烈，其他公链的崛起分流了开发者和用户，而基金会内部的治理分歧也引发了社区对未来发展方向的担忧。

## English

A former Ethereum Foundation core developer has proposed an ambitious plan to raise one billion dollars for a new independent foundation to lead Ethereum development, responding to an ongoing talent drain and growing disagreements about the network's direction.

The proposal follows the departure of several senior developers from the Ethereum Foundation. The proposer argues that the existing foundation structure can no longer effectively advance Ethereum's technical roadmap.

Sources indicate the plan has generated preliminary interest from several crypto investment firms. If successful, this would be the largest foundation fundraising effort in crypto history.

Ethereum currently faces multiple challenges: intensifying competition among Layer 2 scaling solutions, the rise of alternative chains drawing developers away, and internal governance disputes raising community concerns about the network's future.
