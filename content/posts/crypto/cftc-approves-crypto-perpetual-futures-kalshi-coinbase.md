美国商品期货交易委员会（CFTC）已批准首批加密货币永续合约交易，Kalshi 和 Coinbase 获得上线许可。这标志着美国加密货币衍生品市场迈出重要一步，为机构投资者提供更多对冲工具。

---

The US Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) has approved the first cryptocurrency perpetual futures contracts, with Kalshi and Coinbase receiving authorization to list them. This marks a significant step for US crypto derivatives markets, offering institutional investors more hedging tools.
