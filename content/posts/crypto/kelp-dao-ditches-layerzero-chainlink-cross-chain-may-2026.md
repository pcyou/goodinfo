## Kelp DAO 弃用 LayerZero 转投 Chainlink

### 中文

The Block 报道，Kelp DAO 宣布放弃 LayerZero 跨链协议，转而采用 Chainlink 的跨链基础设施。此前 LayerZero 关联的桥接协议遭黑客攻击，损失达 2.92 亿美元。LayerZero 曾将责任归咎于 Kelp 的配置设置，但 Kelp 反驳称该配置已获得 LayerZero 批准。此次迁移标志着 DeFi 领域对跨链安全性的重新审视。

### English

The Block reports that Kelp DAO is migrating from LayerZero to Chainlink's cross-chain infrastructure following a major bridge exploit valued at two hundred ninety-two million dollars. LayerZero initially blamed Kelp's configuration, but Kelp said the setup was approved by LayerZero. The move highlights growing concerns about cross-chain security in decentralized finance.
