## Blockchain.com向SEC提交上市申请

加密金融服务公司Blockchain.com已向美国证券交易委员会（SEC）秘密提交S-1上市申请文件，正式探索在公开市场挂牌。该公司成为继Circle、Gemini和BitGo之后最新一家寻求上市的加密行业企业。

Blockchain.com成立于2011年，是全球最早的加密货币钱包服务提供商之一。公司旗下产品涵盖加密货币钱包、交易所、机构借贷平台及NFT市场，用户遍布全球100多个国家和地区。

此次上市申请正值加密行业监管环境逐步明朗化的关键时期。随着美国SEC对加密资产的态度趋于开放，多家头部加密企业纷纷加速资本化进程。Circle已于2025年完成上市，Gemini和BitGo也在准备相应的公开市场计划。

分析人士指出，加密行业集中赴上市反映了该行业正从早期的风险投资驱动阶段，转向成熟的公开市场融资阶段。这一趋势意味着机构投资者将有更多合规渠道参与加密市场，同时也标志着行业整体向传统金融体系的进一步融合。

Blockchain.com此前已获得多轮大规模融资，估值超过数十亿美元。若上市成功，将成为加密钱包服务领域首家公开交易的主要企业。

## Blockchain.com Files for U.S. IPO as Crypto Listing Wave Accelerates

Crypto financial services firm Blockchain.com has confidentially filed an S-1 registration statement with the U.S. SEC, joining a growing wave of crypto companies pursuing public listings. It follows Circle, Gemini, and BitGo in the public market push.

Founded in 2011, Blockchain.com is one of the earliest cryptocurrency wallet providers globally. Its products include a crypto wallet, exchange, institutional lending platform, and NFT marketplace, serving users across more than 100 countries.

The filing comes as the regulatory environment for crypto in the U.S. becomes increasingly favorable. With the SEC adopting a more open stance toward digital assets, several leading crypto firms are accelerating their capitalization strategies. Circle completed its IPO in 2025, while Gemini and BitGo are preparing their own plans.

Analysts note that the concentration of crypto IPOs reflects the industry's transition from early-stage venture capital funding to mature public market financing. This trend gives institutional investors more compliant avenues to participate in the crypto market and marks the sector's deeper integration with traditional finance.
