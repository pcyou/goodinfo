## Mastercard Secures New York BitLicense for Stablecoin Push

### 中文

支付巨头万事达卡已获得纽约金融服务部颁发的BitLicense，成为2026年第三家获此牌照的机构。该批准支持万事达卡拓展稳定币和区块链结算基础设施业务，是传统支付网络加速拥抱数字资产的最新案例。

### English

Payments giant Mastercard has secured a BitLicense from New York's DFS, becoming the third institution to receive the license in 2026. The approval supports Mastercard's expansion into stablecoin and blockchain-based settlement infrastructure, marking the latest case of traditional payment networks embracing digital assets.
