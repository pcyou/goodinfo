## TD Cowen Says Stablecoin Yield Fight Could Delay US Crypto Bill

### 中文

TD Cowen 分析师警告，围绕稳定币收益条款的分歧可能推迟美国全面加密立法的通过，立法者在如何监管收益型稳定币方面仍存在分歧。

### English

TD Cowen analysts warn that disagreements over stablecoin yield provisions could delay passage of comprehensive US crypto legislation, as lawmakers remain divided on regulating yield-bearing stablecoins.
