## [快讯] Coinbase加码Centrifuge，作为Base链代币化首选平台

Coinbase扩大对代币化平台Centrifuge的投资，将其指定为Base链上发行代币化资产的首选合作伙伴。Centrifuge将专注于在Base链上推出代币化ETF、信贷和结构化产品。

该合作包括Coinbase对Centrifuge的战略性股权投资。此前State Street与Galaxy已联合推出链上现金管理基金，显示传统金融机构对代币化资产的兴趣持续增长。Coinbase此举进一步巩固了Base链在代币化基础设施领域的地位。

<!--more-->

Coinbase expanded its investment in tokenization platform Centrifuge, designating it as the preferred partner for launching tokenized assets on Base, its Ethereum layer-2 blockchain. Centrifuge will focus on launching tokenized ETFs, credit, and structured products on Base.

The partnership includes a strategic equity investment by Coinbase in Centrifuge. Following State Street and Galaxy's joint launch of an onchain cash management fund, this move signals growing institutional interest in tokenized assets. Coinbase's move further solidifies Base's position in tokenization infrastructure.
