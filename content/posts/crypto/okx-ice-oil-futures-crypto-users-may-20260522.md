## 中文

OKX 与美国洲际交易所（ICE）达成合作，将面向1.2亿加密用户推出"永不 expire"的石油期货合约。这一合作借鉴了 Hyperliquid 平台上 oli 永续期货合约的成功经验，后者交易量已超过10亿美元。

## English

OKX and the Intercontinental Exchange (ICE) are partnering to bring "never-expiring" oil futures contracts to 120 million crypto users. The partnership draws on the success of Hyperliquid's oli perpetual oil futures, which have seen over $1 billion in trading volume.
