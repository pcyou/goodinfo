## 律师瞄准Tether冻结资产

律师Charles Gerstein此前曾成功推动Arbitrum加密资产追回，现在他向联邦法官提出申请，要求命令Tether将OFAC（美国海外资产控制办公室）冻结的USDT转移给持有未支付恐怖主义判决的受害者。

这批被冻结的USDT价值约3.44亿美元，据信与伊朗伊斯兰革命卫队（IRGC）有关。Gerstein代表的是恐怖主义受害者的家属，他们多年来一直在寻求获得赔偿。

此案如获法院支持，将是美国执法机构首次通过司法程序将冻结的加密资产直接分配给受害者，可能开创一个重要先例。

## Lawyer Targets Tether for $344M in Frozen USDT

Charles Gerstein, the lawyer who previously led efforts to recover Arbitrum crypto assets, has filed a motion asking a federal judge to order Tether to transfer OFAC-frozen USDT — allegedly linked to Iran's Revolutionary Guard — to victims holding unpaid terrorism judgments.

The frozen USDT is valued at approximately $344 million. If granted by the court, this would mark the first time frozen crypto assets are directly distributed to victims through judicial proceedings, potentially setting a significant legal precedent.
