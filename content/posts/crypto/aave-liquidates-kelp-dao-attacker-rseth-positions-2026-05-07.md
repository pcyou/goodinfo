## [快讯] Aave清算Kelp DAO攻击者剩余rsETH头寸

据The Block报道，去中心化借贷协议Aave已清算了Kelp DAO攻击者的剩余rsETH头寸。此前该攻击者利用协议漏洞盗取资金，Aave通过清算机制回收了部分被窃资产。

## Aave Liquidates Kelp DAO Attacker's Remaining rsETH Positions

According to The Block, the decentralized lending protocol Aave has liquidated the attacker's remaining rsETH positions from the Kelp DAO exploit. The attacker previously used a protocol vulnerability to steal funds, and Aave recovered some of the stolen assets through its liquidation mechanism.
