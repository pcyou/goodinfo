## Kraken Unveils Bitcoin Vault for BTC Yield Generation

### 中文

加密货币交易所Kraken推出Bitcoin Vault产品，允许用户通过DeFi策略生成BTC计价收益，同时保持对比特币价格敞口。该产品是主流交易平台在加密收益领域进一步扩展的标志。

### English

Crypto exchange Kraken has launched Bitcoin Vault, an earn product letting users generate BTC-denominated rewards through DeFi strategies while maintaining price exposure. The product marks further expansion of mainstream platforms into the crypto yield space.
