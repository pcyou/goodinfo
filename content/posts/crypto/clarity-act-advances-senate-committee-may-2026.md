## 参议院银行委员会通过Clarity Act

美国参议院银行委员会周四投票通过了备受关注的加密市场结构法案《Clarity Act》（清晰度法案）。该法案获得了两党议员的支持，标志着美国加密监管框架迈出了历史性一步。

法案通过后，将提交参议院全体投票进行最终表决。该法案旨在为数字资产行业建立清晰的联邦监管框架，解决长期以来困扰加密行业的监管不确定性问题。

在听证会上，两党议员就法案细节进行了深入讨论。尽管一些参议员对未能获得更广泛的两党共识表示遗憾，但法案的推进本身已被视为加密行业的重要胜利。

分析人士认为，如果该法案最终获得通过，将为加密企业在美国的合规运营提供明确指引，同时可能推动更多机构投资者进入数字资产领域。加密市场对该消息反应积极，比特币价格回升至82000美元上方。

## Senate Banking Committee Advances Landmark Crypto Clarity Act

The U.S. Senate Banking Committee voted Thursday to advance the Clarity Act, a landmark crypto market structure bill, sending it to a full Senate vote.

The legislation, which received bipartisan support, aims to establish a clear federal regulatory framework for the digital asset industry, ending years of regulatory uncertainty that has hampered crypto businesses in the United States.

During the hearing, senators from both parties engaged in lengthy discussions on the bill's provisions. While some lawmakers lamented the failure to secure broader bipartisan consensus, the bill's advancement was widely viewed as a significant win for the crypto industry.

The crypto market responded positively, with Bitcoin climbing above $82,000 as investors bet on clearer regulatory pathways for digital assets.
