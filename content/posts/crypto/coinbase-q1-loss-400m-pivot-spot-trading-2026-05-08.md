## Coinbase Q1亏损近4亿美元：CEO寻求减少对现货加密交易的依赖

美国最大加密货币交易所Coinbase公布第一季度财报，录得近4亿美元的净亏损，引发市场对加密行业前景的担忧。

### 核心数据

Coinbase一季度营收同比下滑，主要受现货交易量萎缩影响。交易收入占比过高一直是该公司的结构性风险，CEO在财报电话会上明确表示需要加速业务多元化。

### 战略转型

公司计划减少对现货加密交易的依赖，重点发展以下业务：

- **机构服务**：为对冲基金和资产管理公司提供托管和交易基础设施
- **链上业务**：拓展Base链生态，增加DeFi和NFT相关收入
- **国际扩张**：寻求在欧洲和亚洲市场获得监管许可

### 行业背景

加密市场近期波动加剧，比特币价格在关键支撑位附近震荡。监管环境的不确定性也给行业带来压力。

分析师指出，Coinbase的转型反映了整个加密行业从"交易驱动"向"基础设施驱动"的趋势转变。

### 市场反应

财报公布后，Coinbase股价在盘后交易中下跌。

## Coinbase Loses Nearly $400 Million in Q1 as CEO Seeks to Reduce Dependence on Spot Crypto Trading

America's largest cryptocurrency exchange Coinbase reported first-quarter results, posting a net loss of nearly $400 million, raising concerns about the outlook for the crypto industry.

### Key Figures

Coinbase's revenue declined year-over-year, primarily driven by shrinking spot trading volumes. Over-reliance on trading revenue has long been a structural risk for the company, and the CEO explicitly stated the need to accelerate business diversification during the earnings call.

### Strategic Pivot

The company plans to reduce its dependence on spot crypto trading, focusing on:

- **Institutional Services**: Providing custody and trading infrastructure for hedge funds and asset managers
- **On-Chain Business**: Expanding the Base chain ecosystem, increasing DeFi and NFT-related revenue
- **International Expansion**: Seeking regulatory licenses in European and Asian markets

### Industry Context

The crypto market has seen heightened volatility recently, with Bitcoin trading near key support levels. Regulatory uncertainty continues to weigh on the sector.

Analysts note that Coinbase's pivot reflects a broader industry shift from "trading-driven" to "infrastructure-driven" business models.

### Market Reaction

Coinbase shares declined in after-hours trading following the earnings release.
