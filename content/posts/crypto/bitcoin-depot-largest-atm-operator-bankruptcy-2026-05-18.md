## 核心摘要

北美最大的比特币ATM运营商Bitcoin Depot已申请Chapter 11破产保护。该公司在纳斯达克上市，曾是美国最大的比特币自动柜员机网络运营商。公司表示，不断变化的监管环境使其难以维持现有商业模式。

## English Summary

Bitcoin Depot, North America's largest bitcoin ATM operator and a Nasdaq-listed company, has filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy. The company cited changing regulatory environments as making its current business model unsustainable.
